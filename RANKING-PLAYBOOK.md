# 🚀 WEEK 1 — THE SEVEN THINGS THAT MATTER

Do these in order. Total time: about two hours.

## 1. Deploy the site ⏱️ 15 min
Nothing below works until `https://ekgurulearning.github.io/EkGuru/` is live.
Follow `DEPLOY.md`.

## 2. Google Search Console ⏱️ 20 min
1. **https://search.google.com/search-console** → Add property → URL prefix
2. Enter your URL, verify with the HTML tag method
3. **Sitemaps** → submit `sitemap.xml`
4. **URL Inspection** → paste each of these → **Request indexing**:
   ```
   /EkGuru/
   /EkGuru/find-tutors.html
   /EkGuru/tutor/sushila-g/
   /EkGuru/tutor/hemlata/
   /EkGuru/tutor/tara/
   ```
   This alone saves 2–4 weeks of waiting.

## 3. Bing Webmaster Tools ⏱️ 5 min
**https://www.bing.com/webmasters** → **Import from Google Search Console** → one click.
Bing feeds DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, and ChatGPT's web browsing. Your keyword meta tags are read
here in full, unlike on Google.

## 4. 🔴 The Preply backlink ⏱️ 5 min — HIGHEST VALUE ACTION
Open your Preply tutor profile → edit your description → add a line such as:

> I also teach through EkGuru: https://ekgurulearning.github.io/EkGuru/

**Why this outranks everything else here:** Preply has enormous domain authority and is
about the exact same subject. One relevant link from a site like that carries more weight
than ten thousand keywords. Ask Sushila to do the same on hers.

## 5. YouTube ⏱️ 10 min
* Channel → About → Links → add the site
* Every video description, first line: `Book a lesson: <your URL>`
* Add `tutor/sushila-g/` to her intro video description specifically

YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine and these links get crawled quickly.

## 6. Your own profiles ⏱️ 15 min
LinkedIn (headline: *Founder, EkGuru · MNIT Jaipur CSE 2022–26*), Instagram bio,
Facebook, X, WhatsApp Business, your email signature.

## 7. Google Business Profile ⏱️ 20 min

# ⚠️ WHAT NOT TO DO

Things that look like shortcuts and cause real damage:

* ❌ **Buying backlinks** — a manual penalty, very hard to recover from
* ❌ **Comment spam** on blogs and forums
* ❌ **Hidden text** or white-on-white keywords
* ❌ **Doorway pages** — near-identical pages per city
* ❌ **Fake reviews** — Google detects the pattern and removes your stars
* ❌ **Copying competitor bios** — duplicate content
* ❌ **Claiming ratings you do not have** — this is why `reviewsCount: 0` shows no stars

The site is deliberately built with none of these. Every phrase it publishes is a true
description of what you offer, so there is nothing that can be penalised.
