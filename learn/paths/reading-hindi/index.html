Reading Hindi

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Beginner — no script knowledge needed · 4–5 hours · free, self-paced · updated 2026-09-11

From first letters to reading signs and short texts.

Devanagari is phonetic and finite: forty-four letters, each with one sound. Two or three focused weeks and you can read anything — this path compresses that into a few sessions.

Reading is the one skill a page can genuinely help you build, because it is visual. Work the alphabet, then move from single words to sentences, and check yourself against the numbers and signs around you.

What's in this path

  1. The letters
    • The Hindi alphabet, explained · 20 min
    • Alphabet explorer — click every letter · 20 min
    • The vowels and matras · 10 min
  2. Sound out real words
    • Your first words — day 12 of Daily Hindi · 10 min
    • Reading drill in the listening lab (read the Devanagari) · 10 min
    • Type it yourself · 15 min
  3. Numbers, time and the world around you
    • Numbers 1 to 100 · 20 min
    • Days, months and telling the time · 15 min
    • Numbers tool — sound out any number · 10 min
