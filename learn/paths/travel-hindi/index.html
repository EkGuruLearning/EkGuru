Travel Hindi

Beginner — useful with zero grammar · 2–3 hours · free, self-paced · updated 2026-09-11

The phrases that get you fed, housed and pointed the right way.

You do not need grammar to travel — you need the right phrases and the nerve to say them. This path is situational: each module covers one place a visitor ends up, with the exact words that work there.

Bargaining, autos, trains, food and being unwell — these are the phrases written by people who use them, not copied from a phrasebook. Practise each set before you need it.

    • Phrasebook with search · 10 min
  2. Food, shopping and directions
    • Food and restaurant phrases · 10 min
    • Topic vocabulary — food and travel · 10 min

  3. Time and money
    • Days, months and telling the time · 15 min
    • Numbers tool — sound out any price · 10 min
