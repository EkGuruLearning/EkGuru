Learning paths

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Six goal-based tracks · free, self-paced · updated 2026-09-11

A path is a sequence: read a guide, use a tool, practise, check yourself, review. Pick the goal that matches where you are — not the one that sounds most impressive. You can always switch.

Hindi From Zero

The complete beginner track, in the order that actually works.

Complete beginner · 6–8 hours of focused study · 15 lessons

Speaking Starter

Stop studying. Start saying things out loud.

Beginner — knows some words · 3–4 hours · 10 lessons

Reading Hindi

From first letters to reading signs and short texts.

Beginner — no script knowledge needed · 4–5 hours · 9 lessons

Travel Hindi

The phrases that get you fed, housed and pointed the right way.

Beginner — useful with zero grammar · 2–3 hours · 8 lessons

Everyday Hindi

Small talk, plans, opinions — the language of normal days.

High beginner · 4–5 hours · 9 lessons

Grammar Foundations

The rules that stop people guessing.

High beginner to intermediate · 4–6 hours · 9 lessons

Not sure which path? Take the placement check — a rough guide, not a certified test.
