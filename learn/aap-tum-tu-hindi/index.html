Aap, Tum or Tu — Getting Politeness Right in Hindi

A free guide from EkGuru · updated 2026-09-11

A note on the Hindi spellings

These guides are written and typed by hand, and Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong — a missing matra, the wrong nukta, a halant in the wrong place. If a word here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page, and please tell us so it gets fixed. The romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard — Hindi has no single agreed romanisation, so you will see shukriya and shukriyaa both used.

English has one word for "you". Hindi has three, and the choice says something about the relationship every single time you speak. Getting it wrong is not a small error — it can be genuinely offensive, or unintentionally cold.

The three

WordUse it forVerb form
आप aapelders, strangers, teachers, anyone senior, anyone you are meeting firstआप कैसे हैं?
तुम tumfriends, siblings, people your own age you know well, younger colleaguesतुम कैसे हो?
तू tuvery close friends, small children, and God in prayerतू कैसा है?

The safe default

Always start with आप. Nobody has ever been offended by being addressed too respectfully. Being addressed too casually by a stranger, on the other hand, lands badly.

The other person will usually tell you when to relax it — "aap mat kaho, tum kaho" means "don't say aap, say tum", and it is a small compliment.

तू is the one to be careful with

Between close friends it is affectionate and completely normal. From a stranger, or in anger, it is an insult. In parts of north India men use it freely with each other; in others it would start an argument.

As a learner, the honest advice is: do not use तू until someone uses it with you first, and even then only with them. There is nothing you can say with तू that you cannot say with तुम.

How the verb changes

This is the part that catches people out — the whole sentence shifts, not just the pronoun:

आपतुमतू
How are you?आप कैसे हैं?तुम कैसे हो?तू कैसा है?
Comeआइएआओआ
Sitबैठिएबैठोबैठ
Tell meबताइएबताओबता
Listenसुनिएसुनोसुन

Those आप imperatives — आइए, बैठिए, बताइए — are the ones worth memorising first. They are how you speak to almost everyone you meet as a visitor.

The word that makes anything polite

जी — add it to almost anything and it becomes respectful.

हाँ जी (yes) · नहीं जी (no) · जी हाँ (yes indeed) · after a name: प्रकाश जी

It costs one syllable and completely changes how you are received. If you learn nothing else from this page, learn जी.

A note on family

Within a family the rules are not about age alone. Many people use आप for parents and in-laws throughout their lives, तुम for siblings, and तू only with the closest childhood friends. If you marry into an Indian family, ask — the convention varies by region and by household, and asking is far better than guessing.

Quick check

How many words for “you” does Hindi have?

Three — Three — आप (aap, respectful), तुम (tum, familiar) and तू (tu, intimate).

Which word for “you” is the safe default with strangers?

आप (aap) — आप (aap) is the respectful default; तुम (tum) is for friends and तू (tu) for very close intimates.

What can choosing the wrong “you” do?

Sound genuinely rude — Choosing wrong can be genuinely rude — the three words carry real social weight.
