Marathi grammar

Word order, verbs, pronouns and the mistakes everyone makes — explained, not listed.

1. The verb goes last

Marathi is subject–object–verb: मी भात खातो (mi bhat khato) — मैं चावल खाता हूँ — “I eat rice (a man speaking — a woman says खाते; word-for-word: I · rice · eat)”. Learn this once and every sentence parses.

2. Three genders, not two

Marathi has masculine, feminine AND neuter — one more than Hindi. Adjectives and verbs agree: चांगला मुलगा (good boy), चांगली मुलगी (good girl), चांगले पुस्तक (good book). Learn every noun with its gender.

4. Present tense — one full verb

“To eat” (खाणे) in the present. The verb agrees with WHO is speaking and their gender: a man says खातो, a woman says खाते. Formal तुम्ही takes its own ending — the verb tells you the relationship.

6. Negation is one word

Put नाही (nahi) after a present-tense verb: मी जात नाही (mi jat nahi) — I don't go. Past tense keeps it after too: गेलो नाही (gelo nahi) — didn't go.

8. Counting words

Marathi counts directly — number + noun, no classifier words:

MarathiHindiNote
एक पुस्तक (ek pustak)एक किताबone book — no classifier word needed
दोन आंबे (don āmbe)दो आमtwo mangoes — number + noun, done
तीन लोक (tīn lok)तीन लोगthree people — people count directly too
