Marathi pronunciation, reading & writing

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. The SAME script as Hindi — if you read Hindi, you can sound out Marathi on day one. Only two surprises wait: ळ (retroflex L, unknown in Hindi) and a few spelling habits of its own.

How to practise
