Marathi basics — the script, sounds and first steps

The starting point: the Devanagari (Balbodh), your first words, and the honest answer to where to begin. The SAME script as Hindi — if you read Hindi, you can sound out Marathi on day one. Only two surprises wait: ळ (retroflex L, unknown in Hindi) and a few spelling habits of its own.

The alphabet at a glance

The Devanagari (Balbodh) script has 13 vowels and 36 consonants. The full tables with sounds are on the pronunciation page — learn to recognise them before you memorise any words.

The order to learn in

  1. Script + sounds — Marathi is phonetic: learn the letters and you can read anything.
  2. Greetings + pronouns — say hello, introduce yourself, ask where.
  3. Numbers 1–20 — prices, time and quantities unlock immediately.
  4. Word order + present tense — one pattern (मी भात खातो) builds a thousand sentences.
  5. Topics (food, travel, shopping) — learn what your life needs first.
Honest note. US Foreign Service Institute Category III (about 1,100 hours to professional proficiency) — grouped with Hindi. The shared script makes reading easy; three genders where Hindi has two make grammar harder.
