Malayalam pronunciation, reading & writing

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. Malayalam has the roundest letters of any Indian script — all curves, no headline bar, written left to right. It is fully phonetic: each letter is one sound, always. There are 13 vowels (അ ആ ഇ ഈ ഉ ഊ ഋ എ ഏ ഐ ഒ ഓ ഔ, plus അം/അഃ) and 34 consonants, including the famous ഴ (ḻa) — a sound between r, l and d that even other Indians practise (മഴ, maḻa, rain). Letters stack into tall conjuncts: സ + ് + ത + ് + ര + ീ = സ്ത്രീ (strī, woman). Read slowly and sound every curve.
