Culture in five words

  • ഓണം (Ōṇam) — Kerala's harvest festival (Aug–Sep) — flower carpets, boat races and the grand സദ്യ feast on a banana leaf.
  • കഥകളി (Kathakaḷi) — The painted-face dance-drama — one eye movement can say a whole sentence.
  • കായൽ (kāyal) — The backwaters — a thousand kilometres of lagoons, houseboats and village life.
  • വള്ളംകളി (vaḷḷamkaḷi) — The snake-boat races of Alappuzha — a hundred oarsmen rowing as one.
  • ആയുർവേദം (āyurvēdam) — The traditional medicine Kerala is famous for — oil massages included.
