Learn Malayalam — the complete course

Everything below is free and trilingual: every word and phrase in English, Hindi and Malayalam (മലയാളം, Malayāḷam) side by side — with pronunciation for each one. over 35 million people speak Malayalam: Kerala and neighbouring regions

How to use this course. Start at Beginner, work down the topics in order, and quiz yourself after each one. Medium — a new script and long agglutinative words, but verbs never conjugate
