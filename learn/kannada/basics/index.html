Kannada basics — the script, sounds and first steps

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The starting point: the Kannada, your first words, and the honest answer to where to begin. Kannada's letters are famously round — the script was written with a stylus on palm leaves, and straight lines would have torn the leaf. It runs left to right with no headline bar, and it is beautifully phonetic: each letter is one sound, always. There are 13 vowels (ಅ ಆ ಇ ಈ ಉ ಊ ಋ ಎ ಏ ಐ ಒ ಓ ಔ, plus ಅಂ/ಅಃ for nasalization) and 34 consonants. Letters join into conjuncts (ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ): ಕ + ್ + ಣ = ಕ್ಷಣ (kshaṇa, moment). Close cousin of the Telugu script — learn one and the other looks familiar.

The alphabet at a glance

13 vowels and 35 consonants. The full tables with sounds are on the pronunciation page — learn to recognise them before you memorise any words.

The order to learn in

  1. Script + sounds — Kannada is phonetic: learn the letters and you can read anything.
  2. Greetings + pronouns — say hello, introduce yourself, ask where.
  3. Numbers 1–20 — prices, time and quantities unlock immediately.
  4. Word order + present tense — one pattern (ನಾನು ಅನ್ನ ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆ.) builds a thousand sentences.
  5. Topics (food, travel, shopping) — learn what your life needs first.
