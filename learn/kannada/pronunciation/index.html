Kannada pronunciation, reading & writing

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. Kannada's letters are famously round — the script was written with a stylus on palm leaves, and straight lines would have torn the leaf. It runs left to right with no headline bar, and it is beautifully phonetic: each letter is one sound, always. There are 13 vowels (ಅ ಆ ಇ ಈ ಉ ಊ ಋ ಎ ಏ ಐ ಒ ಓ ಔ, plus ಅಂ/ಅಃ for nasalization) and 34 consonants. Letters join into conjuncts (ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ): ಕ + ್ + ಣ = ಕ್ಷಣ (kshaṇa, moment). Close cousin of the Telugu script — learn one and the other looks familiar.
