Learn Kannada — the complete course

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Everything below is free and trilingual: every word and phrase in English, Hindi and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ, Kannaḍa) side by side — with pronunciation for each one. over 55 million people speak Kannada: Karnataka and neighbouring states

How to use this course. Start at Beginner, work down the topics in order, and quiz yourself after each one. Medium — a new script and long words, but phonetic spelling and regular grammar

Choose your level

BeginnerFrom zero: the script, first words and simple sentences. ElementaryPast the first plateau: tenses, real conversation, daily topics. IntermediatePoliteness, complex sentences and natural speech — modest and honest.

The same learner, from A1 to C2

Each rung of this course is a person at a different age: a child at the first level, an adult using the language at work in the middle, and by the last level someone who has spoken it for a lifetime. The picture is the level — the same ladder every language here climbs, and here is how the eleven rungs work.
