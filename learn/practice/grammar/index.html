Grammar practice

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

A free EkGuru practice lab · updated 2026-09-11

Gender, plurals, verb agreement and tenses — the four rules that decide whether your sentences are understood as you mean them. Each question explains the rule after you answer.

This is recognition practice: picking the right form from options is easier than producing it in speech. Once the forms feel automatic here, they will come out right under pressure more often.

What you will practise

  • रोटी (bread) is which gender? — feminine
  • घर (house) is which gender? — masculine
  • किताब (book) is which gender? — feminine
  • लड़का (boy) is which gender? — masculine
  • The plural of लड़का (boy) is… — लड़के (ladke)

A few of the items in this lab; the run draws from the full bank and shuffles each time.
