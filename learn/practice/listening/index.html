Listening practice

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

A free EkGuru practice lab · updated 2026-09-11

You hear a Hindi word or phrase spoken aloud, then choose what was said. An honest limitation: this is your browser's computer voice, not a native speaker — useful for training your ear to recognise words, not for learning a natural accent.

If nothing plays, your browser has no speech synthesis; the text is shown so you can still use the drill.

What you will practise

  • You hear a greeting. What was it? — नमस्ते
  • You hear: water. Which word? — पानी
  • You hear: thank you. Which word? — धन्यवाद
  • You hear: how are you? Which phrase? — आप कैसे हैं?
  • You hear: my name is Ravi. Which phrase? — मेरा नाम रवि है

A few of the items in this lab; the run draws from the full bank and shuffles each time.
