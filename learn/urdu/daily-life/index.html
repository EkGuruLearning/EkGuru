Culture in five words

  • لال قلعہ (Lāl Qila) — The Mughal fort-palace in Delhi — Friday prayers, peacocks, and the flag on Independence Day.
  • اودھی کھانے (Avadhī khāne) — Lucknow's slow-cooked dum biryani and kebabs — dinner is an art form in Awadh.
  • مشاعرہ (mushāira) — The poetry gathering — ghazals and nazms recited live, the audience crying واہ واہ.
  • چاند رات (Chānd Rāt) — The night of Eid's moon — henna, sheer khurma, and greetings till late.
  • تہذیب (tehzīb) — Urdu's beloved courtesy language — آپ, فرمائیے, تشریف رکھیے — politeness as poetry.
