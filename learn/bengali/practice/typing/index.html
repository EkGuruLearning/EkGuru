Bengali typing trainer

Type the Bengali word in Bengali script. Small differences in spacing or punctuation count as “minor format”, not wrong.

How the Bengali typing check works

You get a Roman prompt and type the word in Bengali script. The trainer compares what you typed with the stored spelling and accepts spacing and equivalent punctuation as minor format rather than marking them wrong — this is a practice tool, not an exam. Every prompt comes from the Bengali vocabulary on this site, so what you type is what you will actually read later.

Why typing beats re-reading

Recognition is cheap: a word can look familiar long before you can produce it. Typing forces the harder step — recalling the letters and their order — and the mistakes it exposes are exactly the letters you keep mixing up. Ten words a day is a better routine than a hundred once a month.

The score is a recognition score, not a fluency measure — nothing here is a certified test.
