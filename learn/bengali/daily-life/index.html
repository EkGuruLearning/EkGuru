Culture in five words

  • দুর্গাপূজা (Durga Puja) — The biggest Bengali festival — Kolkata's streets fill with pandals for five days in autumn. If you visit Bengal once, come then.
  • পহেলা বৈশাখ (Pohela Boishakh) — Bengali New Year (mid-April) — new clothes, sweets, and শুভ নববর্ষ (shubho noboborsho) greetings.
  • আড্ডা (adda) — The legendary Bengali art of long, wandering conversation over tea — no agenda, no hurry. Join one and your Bengali will grow faster than any app.
  • ২১শে ফেব্রুয়ারি (21st February) — Language Martyrs' Day — Bengalis died for their language in 1952, and UNESCO made the date International Mother Language Day. The language is loved fiercely; learners are welcomed warmly.
  • মিষ্টি দই (mishti doi) — Sweet curd set in clay cups — order it once and you understand half of Bengali food culture.
