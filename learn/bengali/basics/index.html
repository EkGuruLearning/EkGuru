Bengali basics — the script, sounds and first steps

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The starting point: the Bengali script, your first words, and the honest answer to where to begin. A left-to-right Eastern Nagari script, a close cousin of Devanagari. If you read Hindi, many letters will look familiar — but several sounds differ, so learn it fresh rather than guessing from Hindi.

The alphabet at a glance

11 vowels and 39 consonants. The full tables with sounds are on the pronunciation page — learn to recognise them before you memorise any words.

The order to learn in

  1. Script + sounds — Bengali is phonetic: learn the letters and you can read anything.
  2. Greetings + pronouns — say hello, introduce yourself, ask where.
  3. Numbers 1–20 — prices, time and quantities unlock immediately.
  4. Word order + present tense — one pattern (আমি ভাত খাই) builds a thousand sentences.
  5. Topics (food, travel, shopping) — learn what your life needs first.
Honest note. US Foreign Service Institute Category III (about 1,100 hours to professional proficiency) — grouped with Hindi. The script is phonetic, verbs are regular, and there is no grammatical gender, which makes the start gentler than Hindi.
