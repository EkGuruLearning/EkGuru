Bengali pronunciation, reading & writing

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. A left-to-right Eastern Nagari script, a close cousin of Devanagari. If you read Hindi, many letters will look familiar — but several sounds differ, so learn it fresh rather than guessing from Hindi.
