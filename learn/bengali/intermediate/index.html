Bengali Intermediate

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Politeness, complex sentences and natural Bengali speech — a modest, honest level.

Who this is for

  • You handle daily situations but sound blunt or textbook-like.
  • You want formality tiers, negation and joined sentences.

The order to learn in

  1. Re-read grammar: formality tiers, negation, postpositions — then use them in the dialogues.
  2. Study daily life & culture — festivals, register, how people really talk.
  3. Shadow the scenarios: repeat each line aloud until it flows.
  4. Write 5 sentences a day and check them in the quiz explanations.

What you will be able to do

  • Choose the right level of politeness, join sentences naturally, and follow everyday conversation.

Practice

  • Topic quizFilter by topic and level; every answer has an explanation.
  • Review deckThe 20 core words — tick them off as they stick.
