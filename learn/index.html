Want help speaking Hindi?

The self-paced materials are free. Independent Hindi tutoring is optional and separate from the free courses; tutor lessons are not included.

A tutor can help you practise a conversation, ask questions about pronunciation, or work on a specific goal. EkGuru's tutor directory is for Hindi, not a promise of teachers for every language in the library.

  1. Choose a goal: first conversations, travel Hindi, reading, or speaking practice.
  2. Compare profiles: check the tutor's teaching approach, displayed lesson price and current availability. Profile details are tutor-provided, not a guarantee of qualifications or results.
  3. Ask before paying: share your level, goal, timezone and two suitable times. Confirm the lesson price and time directly with the tutor; a request is not a confirmed booking. EkGuru does not collect tutor-lesson payments in this release.

Compare Hindi tutor profiles

If no suitable tutor is listed, you can continue learning Hindi for free. No booking or account is needed to use the self-paced materials.
