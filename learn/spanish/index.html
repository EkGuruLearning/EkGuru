Spanish — practical long-form guides

Written by: AI-assisted draft for owner review. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-05. Editorial policy and corrections.

A first set of readable Spanish guides for English-speaking learners. Each article tackles one real question, pairs Spanish examples with a plain pronunciation cue, and links back to the free authored course. Spanish is spoken across many communities; where usage differs by region, the guides say so instead of pretending there is one universal conversation.

  • 100 Common Spanish Words with PronunciationOne hundred high-use Spanish words grouped by task, with English meanings, approximate pronunciation and short phrases you can use.

Use the full course too

These guides are companions, not a replacement for practice. The Spanish course hub links to lessons, a practice lab, a quiz and review. Start with the guide that answers the question you have today, then use the course to repeat the pattern until it comes out without translating.

About writing and pronunciation

Spanish examples appear in the original Latin alphabet. “Say it” cues in these articles are broad English-friendly approximations, not IPA and not a claim that every Spanish-speaking region sounds identical. Listen to more than one speaker, keep the regional model you are learning consistent, and use the spelling rules on the alphabet and reading pages as your reliable reference.
