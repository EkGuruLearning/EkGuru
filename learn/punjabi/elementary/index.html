Punjabi Elementary

Past the first plateau: tenses, real conversation and daily topics in Punjabi.

Who this is for

  • You can read slowly and hold a 30-second exchange.
  • You know 100+ words and the present tense.

The order to learn in

  1. Full pronouns + past and future — talk about yesterday and tomorrow.
  2. Food + shopping — order, pay, bargain.
  3. Travel + time — directions, days, the clock.
  4. Conversation scenarios — rehearse the dialogues aloud.
  5. Typing trainer — write what you can say.

What you will be able to do

  • Handle a market, a tea stall and directions; talk about past and future; read short real texts.

Practice

  • Topic quizFilter by topic and level; every answer has an explanation.
  • Review deckThe 20 core words — tick them off as they stick.
