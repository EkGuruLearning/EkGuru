Punjabi practice worksheet

Five questions from the free Punjabi course on EkGuru, with the answers at the foot of the sheet. Press Make worksheet for a fresh set, or print this one as it is.

Name   Date

1. What does ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ mean?

2. What does ਸ਼ੁਕਰੀਆ mean?

3. Someone asks 'ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ?' — you reply…

4. The letter ਸ sounds like…

5. ਤਿੰਨ means…

Answers

1. hello! — ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ (Sat Srī Akāl) — 'God is truth' — is the standard Punjabi hello, any time of day.

2. thank you — ਸ਼ੁਕਰੀਆ (shukrīā) is thanks — ਧੰਨਵਾਦ (dhannvād) is the more formal word.

3. ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ … ਹੈ — ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ? = what's your name — answer ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ … ਹੈ (my name is …).

4. s as in sun — ਸ (sassā) is a plain s — ਸ਼ (dotted) is the sh sound.

5. 3 — ਇੱਕ ਦੋ ਤਿੰਨ — 1, 2, 3.

How the Punjabi worksheet is built

Choose a topic and a number of prompts, and the builder lays out a printable Punjabi worksheet with writing space and an answer section at the end. It is designed for paper: one topic per sheet, prompts in a readable size, answers on their own block so they can be folded away. Printing from the browser prints just the sheet, with the watermark and this site's name kept on it.

Marking and reusing a sheet

Write your answers first, then check them against the answer block and note which ones needed a second attempt — those are the words to review tomorrow. Nothing is stored for you, so print a fresh sheet for a fresh attempt; a set of five finished sheets makes an honest revision pack.

The score is a recognition score, not a fluency measure — nothing here is a certified test.
