Culture in five words

  • ਹਰਿਮੰਦਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ (Harimandar Sāhib) — The Golden Temple in Amritsar: Sikhism's holiest shrine, open to everyone, day and night.
  • ਲੰਗਰ (langar) — The free community kitchen at every gurdwara — sit on the floor, eat dal-roti, all are equal.
  • ਵਿਸਾਖੀ (Vaisākhī) — The spring harvest festival (13 April) — and the Khalsa's birthday. Expect bhangra.
  • ਲੋਹੜੀ (Lohrī) — The mid-January bonfire festival — peanuts, rewri and folk songs around the fire.
  • ਭੰਗੜਾ (bhāngṛā) — Punjab's thundering harvest dance — now a global party soundtrack. Women answer with ਗਿੱਧਾ (giddhā).
