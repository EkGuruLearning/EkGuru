Punjabi pronunciation, reading & writing

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. Gurmukhi has 35 core letters — the ਪੈਂਟੀ ਅਖਰ (paintī akhar, 'the 35'). It runs left to right with no headline bar, and its letters look rounder than Devanagari. Six dotted letters (ਸ਼ ਖ਼ ਗ਼ ਜ਼ ਫ਼ ਲ਼) cover Persian sounds, and nasalization is marked with ਬਿੰਦੀ (ਂ) and ਟਿੱਪੀ (ੰ): ਮਾਂ (mān, mother) needs its bindi — drop it and the word breaks. Across the border, Pakistani Punjabi is written in Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic) — same language, different script. Punjabi is tonal: ਘ ਝ ਢ ਧ ਭ and ਹ colour the pitch of nearby vowels — listen more than you read at first.
