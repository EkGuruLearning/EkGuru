Culture in five words

  • નવરાત્રિ (Navrātri) — Nine nights of garba-raas — the whole state dances in circles till late. No experience needed; the circle teaches you.
  • ઉત્તરાયણ (Uttarāyaṇ) — Kite festival (14 January) — the sky over Ahmedabad disappears under kites. Undhiyu and jalebi on every terrace.
  • બેસતું વરસ (Bestu Varas) — Gujarati New Year, right after Diwali — chopda-pujan for the account books, mithaas for everyone.
  • રણ ઉત્સવ (Rann Utsav) — White-desert festival in Kutch — moonlight on salt, folk drums, mirror-work tents.
  • ખમણ (khaman) — Steamed gram-flour sponge tempered with mustard seeds — breakfast, snack, and proof that steamed beats fried.
