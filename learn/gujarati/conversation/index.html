Gujarati conversation

What to actually say to a person in Gujarati: greetings, introductions and the small talk that opens every door.

Greetings

EnglishHindi GujaratiSay it
Helloनमस्तेનમસ્તેnamaste
જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ (JSK) among many Hindu Gujaratis
How are you? (formal)आप कैसे हैं?તમે કેમ છો?tame kem cho?
How are you? (familiar)तुम कैसे हो?તું કેમ છે?tuṁ kem che?
I'm fineमैं ठीक हूँહું મજામાં છુંhuṁ majāmāṁ chuṁ
Literally 'I'm in fun' — Gujaratis are always having fun
Thank youधन्यवादઆભારābhār
Yes / Noहाँ / नहींહા / નાhā / nā
Pleaseकृपयाમહેરબાની કરીનેmaherbānī karīne
Long but polite — streets say પ્લીઝ
Excuse me / Sorryमाफ़ कीजिएમાફ કરશોmāph karśo
Good morningसुप्रभातશુભ સવારśubh savār
Good nightशुभ रात्रिશુભ રાત્રીśubh rātrī
Goodbye (come again!)फिर आनाઆવજોāvjo
Said at parting by either side — literally an invitation to come again
See you againफिर मिलेंगेફરી મળીશુંpharī maḷīśuṁ
Welcomeस्वागत हैસ્વાગત છેsvāgat che
