Gujarati grammar

Gujarati word order, verbs, pronouns and the mistakes everyone makes — explained, not listed.

1. The verb goes last

Gujarati is subject–object–verb: હું ભાત ખાઉં છું (huṁ bhāt khāuṁ chuṁ) — मैं चावल खाता हूँ — “I eat rice (word-for-word: I · rice · eat · am — the helper છું is compulsory)”. Learn this once and every sentence parses.

2. Three genders — hiding in the past tense

Gujarati has masculine, feminine and neuter like Marathi — but the present tense doesn't care (હું ખાઉં છું for men and women alike). Gender appears in adjectives (સારો/સારી/સારું) and the past tense: ગયો (he went), ગઈ (she went), ગયું (it went).

4. Present tense — one full verb

“To eat” (ખાવું) in the present. Gujarati present tense travels with a helper: the main verb plus છું/છે/છીએ (am/is/are). હું ખાઉં છું is literally 'I eat am' — say both parts or it sounds unfinished.

WhoVerbSay it

6. Negation is one word

Put નથી (nathī) after the helper: હું જતો નથી (huṁ jato nathī) — I don't go. Past tense works the same: ગયો નથી (gayo nathī) — didn't go.

The sentence ladder

Do not learn grammar as a list of rules. Learn it as a ladder with seven rungs, where every rung is something you can say out loud today.

  1. Say what you are. Simple I-sentences with no verb change.
  2. Say what you want. The same pattern with a noun from the common words list.
  3. Say what you do. The present-tense table above, first person only.
  4. Say what someone else does. The same table, third person — this is where the endings move.
  5. Say what happened. Past tense, five sentences about yesterday.
  6. Say what will happen. Future tense, five sentences about tomorrow.
  7. Say what does not happen. Negative forms of five sentences you have already built.

The grammar rule that matters most. Gujarati present tense travels with a helper: the main verb plus છું/છે/છીએ (am/is/are). હું ખાઉં છું is literally 'I eat am' — say both parts or it sounds unfinished. Learn the pattern, then change one thing at a time — verb, then person, then tense. Learners who change everything at once produce sentences they cannot debug.
