Gujarati pronunciation, reading & writing

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The Gujarati sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. Devanagari's sister with NO headline bar — the letters hang free. If you read Hindi, you'll decode Gujarati fast; a few letters (ખ ગ જ ટ ણ ફ) look different enough to learn fresh.
