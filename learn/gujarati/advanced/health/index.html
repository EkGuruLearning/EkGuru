Dialogue: At the clinic

Doctor: તમને શું થયું?
Tamne shu thayu? — आपको क्या हुआ? — What happened to you?

Patient: ડૉક્ટર, બે દિવસથી તાવ છે.
Doktar, be divasthi tav chhe. — डॉक्टर, दो दिन से बुख़ार है। — Doctor, fever for two days.

Doctor: ઉધરસ-શરદી છે?
Udharas-shardi chhe? — खाँसी-ज़ुकाम है? — Any cough or cold?

Patient: ફક્ત ઉધરસ છે.
Phakt udharas chhe. — सिर्फ़ ख़ाँसी है। — Only a cough.

Doctor: બીશો નહીં. આ ગોળી લો.
Bisho nahi. Aa goli lo. — घबराइए मत। यह गोली लीजिए। — Don't fear. Take this tablet.

Patient: આભાર ડૉક્ટર. કંઈ પથ્ય છે?
Aabhar doktar. Kai pathya chhe? — शुक्रिया डॉक्टर। कोई परहेज़? — Thanks doctor. Any diet restrictions?
