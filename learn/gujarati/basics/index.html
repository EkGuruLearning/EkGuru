Gujarati basics — the script, sounds and first steps

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The starting point: the Gujarati script, your first words, and the honest answer to where to begin. Devanagari's sister with NO headline bar — the letters hang free. If you read Hindi, you'll decode Gujarati fast; a few letters (ખ ગ જ ટ ણ ફ) look different enough to learn fresh.

The alphabet at a glance

13 vowels and 35 consonants. The full tables with sounds are on the pronunciation page — learn to recognise them before you memorise any words.

The order to learn in

  1. Script + sounds — Gujarati is phonetic: learn the letters and you can read anything.
  2. Greetings + pronouns — say hello, introduce yourself, ask where.
  3. Numbers 1–20 — prices, time and quantities unlock immediately.
  4. Word order + present tense — one pattern (હું ભાત ખાઉં છું) builds a thousand sentences.
  5. Topics (food, travel, shopping) — learn what your life needs first.
Honest note. US Foreign Service Institute Category III (about 1,100 hours to professional proficiency) — grouped with Hindi. The script reads fast for Hindi readers; three genders and the ergative past (મેં … વાંચ્યું) are the real work.
