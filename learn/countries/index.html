Published country guides for learning Hindi

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: legacy editorial date unknown. Editorial policy and corrections.

These are the country pages EkGuru publishes. Each one is a practical note for someone learning Hindi from that country: what a familiar language already gives you, how Indian lesson hours convert, and what a listed price looks like in local money. They are not a promise that a tutor is available in your timezone.

What is listed, and what is not

This directory has 54 guides. A country is here only when its page is indexable. Pages still marked research-only stay out of this list and out of the sitemap. The older hub at learn Hindi by country is a research archive; do not treat it as the public directory.

Prices on those guides start from the lowest tutor-stated profile price, currently $6. Confirm the current price, lesson length and cancellation terms on the tutor profile before you book. EkGuru does not guarantee a trial.

Written and maintained by Prakash, who operates EkGuru from Jaipur. Updated 27 September 2026. This page organises existing guides; it does not add a new claim about a country.
