16. The ri matra — ृ ऋ की मात्रा (ृ)

A small curl under the letter: क + ृ = कृ

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

The thirteenth sign belongs to the vowel ऋ — said 'ri' in modern Hindi. It is a small curl beneath the consonant and it lives in a particular set of words, most of them from Sanskrit: कृपा, ऋषि, मृग, अमृत. Few words, but you meet them often.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • ऋषि वन में रहते हैं। rishi van mein rahte hain.
    EnglishThe sage lives in the forest.
  • कृपया चुप रहो। kripayaa chup raho.
    EnglishPlease be quiet.
  • मृग तृण खाता है। mrig trin khaataa hai.
    EnglishThe deer eats grass.
  • ऋतु बदल गई। ritu badal gaee.
    EnglishThe season changed.
  • कृषक खेत में हल चला रहा है। krishak khet mein hal chalaa rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe farmer is ploughing the field.
  • माँ का हृदय कोमल है। maan kaa hriday komal hai.
    EnglishA mother's heart is tender.
  • अमृत मीठा होता है। amrit meethaa hotaa hai.
    EnglishNectar is sweet.
  • हिंदी मेरी मातृभाषा है। Hindee meree maatribhaashaa hai.
    EnglishHindi is my mother tongue.

ऋषि और मृग

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • वन में एक ऋषि रहते थे। van mein ek rishi rahte the.
    EnglishIn the forest lived a sage.
  • उनका गृह छोटा था, हृदय बड़ा। unkaa grih chhotaa thaa, hriday baraa.
    EnglishHis house was small, his heart big.
  • एक दिन एक मृग आया। ek din ek mrig aayaa.
    EnglishOne day a deer came.
  • ऋषि ने उसे तृण और जल दिया। rishi ne use trin aur jal diyaa.
    EnglishThe sage gave it grass and water.
  • मृग रोज आने लगा। mrig roj aane lagaa.
    EnglishThe deer began to come every day.
  • ऋतु बदली, पर मृग आता रहा। ritu badlee, par mrig aataa rahaa.
    EnglishThe seasons changed, but the deer kept coming.
  • कृपा का फल अमृत जैसा मीठा होता है। kripaa kaa phal amrit jaisaa meethaa hotaa hai.
    EnglishThe fruit of kindness is as sweet as nectar.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write कृ, गृ, तृ, मृ, हृ ten times each — the curl must sit under the letter, not beside it. Then write कृपया on a card and put it on your desk.

Quick check

How is ऋ pronounced in Hindi?

'ri' — ऋषि is rishi, कृपा is kripaa. (Sanskrit scholars say it differently; Hindi does not.)

Where does ृ go?

Underneath the consonant, as a small curl — like ु but curling the other way.

Read मातृभाषा.

मा-तृ-भा-षा — maatribhaashaa, mother tongue.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
