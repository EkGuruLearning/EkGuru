17. Half letters and joined letters आधे अक्षर और संयुक्त अक्षर

हलंत ् · क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र · र के तीन रूप

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Every consonant carries an 'a'. To silence it you add a small slash underneath — the हलंत (्) — and the letter becomes 'half': it loses its vertical stem and leans on the next one. क + ् + ष = क्ष. Once you can see the half letter inside a cluster, the last locked door of Devanagari opens: बच्चा, स्कूल, प्रकाश, कर्म.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • बच्चा स्कूल जा रहा है। bachchaa skool jaa rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe child is going to school.
  • मित्र को पत्र लिख। mitra ko patra likh.
    EnglishWrite a letter to your friend.
  • बिल्ली ने दूध पी लिया। billee ne doodh pee liyaa.
    EnglishThe cat drank the milk.
  • पक्षी वृक्ष पर है। pakshee vriksh par hai.
    EnglishThe bird is on the tree.
  • सूर्य पूर्व में उगता है। soorya poorv mein ugtaa hai.

    EnglishKrishna plays the flute.
  • ज्ञान से प्रकाश मिलता है। gyaan se prakaash miltaa hai.
    EnglishKnowledge brings light.

    EnglishHe sits under the tree and waits for me.

Today's task · आज का काम

Write क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र ten times each as single shapes. Then take any page of Hindi — a newspaper, a shop sign — and circle every half letter you can find. Count them.

Quick check

What does ् do?

Removes the built-in 'a' so the consonant becomes 'half' and joins the next letter: क + ् + ष = क्ष.

What are the three shapes of र?

Tucked under (प्र, क्र), the 'hat' under ट ड (ट्र, ड्र), and the hook on TOP of the following letter when र comes first (कर्म, सूर्य).

Read बच्चा.

ब + च् + चा — bachchaa, child. The first च is half and leans on the second.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
