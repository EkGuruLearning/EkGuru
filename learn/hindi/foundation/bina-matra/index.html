5. Words with no matra बिना मात्रा के शब्द

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-16. Editorial policy and corrections.

Two, three and four letters — just consonants and their built-in 'a'

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Before any vowel sign, a child reads words made of bare consonants: क-म-ल, कमल. Every letter says its own 'a'. Sound each letter out, then blend — slowly at first, and the speed arrives on its own.

The little words — learn their shape now

These appear in almost every Hindi sentence. They carry signs you have not met yet, so children learn them the way English children learn 'the' and 'is': by sight. Their matras will make sense within a few lessons.

हैhai · is हैंhain · are मेंmein · in औरaur · and काkaa · of (m.) कीkee · of (f.) केke · of (pl.) कोko · to सेse · from / with नेne · (past-tense marker) यहyah · this वहvah · that

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • घर चल। ghar chal.
    EnglishGo home.
  • नल पर चल। nal par chal.
    EnglishGo to the tap.
  • फल रख। phal rakh.
    EnglishPut the fruit down.
  • कलम पकड़। kalam pakar.
    EnglishHold the pen.
  • सड़क पर मत चल। sarak par mat chal.
    EnglishDon't walk on the road.
  • नमक कम रख। namak kam rakh.
    EnglishKeep the salt low.
  • शहद चख। shahad chakh.
    EnglishTaste the honey.
  • दशरथ, रथ पर चढ़। Dashrath, rath par charh.
    EnglishDashrath, climb onto the chariot.

    EnglishNow get up, do your exercise.

नटखट अजय

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • अजय नटखट है। Ajay natkhat hai.
    EnglishAjay is naughty.
  • वह घर पर है। vah ghar par hai.
    EnglishHe is at home.
  • अजय, नमक मत चख। Ajay, namak mat chakh.
    EnglishAjay, don't taste the salt.
  • अजय, शहद मत चख। Ajay, shahad mat chakh.
    EnglishAjay, don't taste the honey.
  • अब बस। कलम पकड़। ab bas. kalam pakar.
    EnglishEnough now. Hold the pen.
  • क ख ग घ… अजय सफल है। ka kha ga gha… Ajay saphal hai.
    Englishka kha ga gha… Ajay has done it.
Today's task · आज का काम

Copy all three ladders into a notebook, two letters first. Then close the book and write the words as someone reads them to you — dictation (श्रुतलेख) is how every Indian classroom checks this lesson.

Quick check

How do you read कमल?

Letter by letter — क म ल, each with its own 'a' — then blend: kamal.

What is है?

'Is'. It carries a sign you have not learnt yet, so learn its shape now as a sight word.

Why no है in 'घर चल'?

It is a command — go home! Commands in Hindi need no 'is'.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
