12. The ai matra — ै ऐ की मात्रा (ै)

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-16. Editorial policy and corrections.

Draft: not yet reviewed by a native speaker. Existing availability and indexing are preserved by owner instruction; that is not an editorial approval. Suggest a correction.

Two strokes on top: क + ै = कै

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Double the stroke, change the vowel. Two slanted marks above the headline give the 'ai' sound — in most of north India it is the a of 'cat', held. You already know the most important ै word in Hindi: है.

The sign

कka
+
ैthe sign · ai
=
कैkai

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • बैल मैदान में बैठा है। bail maidaan mein baithaa hai.
    EnglishThe ox is sitting in the field.
  • भैया पैदल सैर पर गए। bhaiyaa paidal sair par gaye.
    EnglishBrother went for a walk on foot.
  • मैना पेड़ पर है। mainaa per par hai.
    EnglishThe myna is on the tree.
  • थैले में पैसे हैं। thaile mein paise hain.
    EnglishThere is money in the bag.
  • सैनिक तैयार हैं। sainik taiyaar hain.
    EnglishThe soldiers are ready.
  • कैसा है, भैया? — ठीक है। kaisaa hai, bhaiyaa? — theek hai.
    EnglishHow are you, brother? — Fine.
  • मैदान में मैच है। maidaan mein maich hai.
    EnglishThere is a match on the ground.
  • बैलगाड़ी धीरे चलती है। bailgaaree dheere chaltee hai.
    EnglishThe bullock cart goes slowly.
  • पैर साफ कर के बैठ। pair saaph kar ke baith.
    EnglishClean your feet and sit.

भैया की सैर

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • सवेरा है। भैया सैर पर निकले। saveraa hai. bhaiyaa sair par nikle.
    EnglishIt is morning. Brother went out for a walk.
  • वे पैदल मैदान तक गए। ve paidal maidaan tak gaye.
    EnglishHe walked as far as the field.
  • मैदान में बैल बैठे थे। maidaan mein bail baithe the.
    EnglishOxen were sitting in the field.
  • पेड़ पर मैना गा रही थी। per par mainaa gaa rahee thee.
    EnglishA myna was singing on the tree.
  • कुछ सैनिक कसरत कर रहे थे। kuchh sainik kasrat kar rahe the.
    EnglishSome soldiers were exercising.
  • भैया हैरान थे — कैसा सवेरा! bhaiyaa hairaan the — kaisaa saveraa!
    EnglishBrother was amazed — what a morning!
  • थैले में पैसे थे। वे जलेबी ले आए। thaile mein paise the. ve jalebee le aaye.
    EnglishThere was money in the bag. He brought back jalebi.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write के / कै, मे / मै, बे / बै side by side — one stroke, two strokes. Say them. Then write है twenty times, the most-used word in the language. Dictation of ten.

Quick check

How is ै different from े?

Two slanted strokes on top instead of one. e → ai.

What does है sound like?

'hai' — the a of 'cat', held. The commonest word in Hindi: 'is'.

Read तैयार.

तै-या-र — taiyaar, ready.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
