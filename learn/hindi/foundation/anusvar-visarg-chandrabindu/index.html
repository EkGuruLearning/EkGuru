15. The dot, the two dots and the moon-dot अं, अः और चंद्रबिंदु (ं ः ँ)

अनुस्वार ं · विसर्ग ः · चंद्रबिंदु ँ

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Three small marks finish the system. The dot on top (अनुस्वार) adds a nasal hum — रंग, अंगूर. The moon-with-a-dot (चंद्रबिंदु) makes the vowel itself nasal — माँ, चाँद, गाँव. The two dots after a letter (विसर्ग) add a breath and live mostly in words from Sanskrit — नमः, दुःख.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • बंदर जंगल में है। bandar jangal mein hai.
    EnglishThe monkey is in the jungle.
  • माँ ने संतरा और अंगूर दिए। maan ne santaraa aur angoor diye.
    EnglishMother gave oranges and grapes.
  • पतंग का रंग लाल है। patang kaa rang laal hai.
    EnglishThe kite's colour is red.
  • गाँव में एक मंदिर है। gaanv mein ek mandir hai.
    EnglishIn the village there is a temple.
  • ऊँट लंबा होता है। oont lambaa hotaa hai.
    EnglishA camel is tall.
  • साँप को देख कर हंस उड़ गया। saanp ko dekh kar hans ur gayaa.
    EnglishSeeing the snake, the swan flew away.
  • पाँच और पाँच दस। paanch aur paanch das.
    EnglishFive and five, ten.
  • चाँद सुंदर है। chaand sundar hai.
    EnglishThe moon is beautiful.

गाँव की रात

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • यह मेरा गाँव है। yah meraa gaanv hai.
    EnglishThis is my village.
  • गाँव में एक मंदिर है और एक कुआँ। gaanv mein ek mandir hai aur ek kuaan.
    EnglishIn the village there is a temple and a well.
  • रात को चाँद निकला। raat ko chaand niklaa.
    EnglishAt night the moon came out.
  • बंदर पेड़ पर सो गए। bandar per par so gaye.
    EnglishThe monkeys slept in the tree.
  • माँ ने ठंडा कंबल दिया। maan ne thandaa kambal diyaa.
    EnglishMother gave a cool blanket.
  • दूर से बाँसुरी की धुन आ रही थी। door se baansuree kee dhun aa rahee thee.

Today's task · आज का काम

Write अं, कं, खं … once across the chart, then माँ चाँद गाँव साँप दाँत हँस ten times. Finally the word मैं — I — twenty times. You will write it more than any other.

Quick check

What does the dot on रंग do?

Adds a nasal hum before the next consonant — 'rang', like the ng in 'sing'.

When is ँ used instead of ं?

When the vowel itself is nasal — माँ, चाँद, गाँव. If a top matra is already there (े ै ो ौ), the plain dot is written instead: मैं, हैं.

Where do you meet ः?

Mostly in Sanskrit-derived words and set phrases: नमः, दुःख, अतः, छः.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
