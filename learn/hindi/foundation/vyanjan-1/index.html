2. Consonants 1 — the throat and palate rows व्यंजन — क वर्ग और च वर्ग

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-28. Editorial policy and corrections.

Draft: not yet reviewed by a native speaker. Existing availability and indexing are preserved by owner instruction; that is not an editorial approval. Suggest a correction.

क ख ग घ ङ · च छ ज झ ञ

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Hindi consonants (व्यंजन, vyanjan) come in rows of five, arranged by where the tongue sits. Row one is made at the back of the throat, row two at the palate. Every letter already contains an 'a': क is 'ka', never a bare 'k'.

The letters

Each letter with its word

This is how the chart hangs on an Indian classroom wall: the letter, and the one word every child learns it by. Say the letter, say the word, tap to hear both.

क और ख — हवा का अंतर (Airflow)

हथेली मुँह के सामने रखिए। क बोलिए, फिर ख। ख में हवा का झोंका अधिक महसूस होता है।

Illustrative airflow, not an anatomical or measured diagram. Close and reopen to replay. Reduced-motion settings keep it still.

What to notice

  • Each row runs plain · puffed · voiced · voiced-puffed · nasal. Hold your palm in front of your mouth: क gives no air, ख gives a puff. That puff is the whole difference between कल (tomorrow) and खल (villain).
  • ङ and ञ rarely begin words in modern Hindi. In रंग, the dot is anusvara, not the written letter ङ; it represents a nasal sound before ग. The conjunct ज्ञ contains ञ, as in ज्ञान.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write the row क ख ग घ ङ five times, saying each letter. Then च छ ज झ ञ. Use the headline — the horizontal bar on top — like washing on a line: letters hang from it.

Quick check

How is ख different from क?

A puff of air. Say both with your palm in front of your mouth — only ख moves it.

What sound is built into every consonant?

A short 'a'. क is 'ka', म is 'ma'. Removing it needs a special sign (lesson 17).

Which two letters here rarely begin modern Hindi words?

ङ and ञ. ज्ञान contains ञ in ज्ञ; रंग uses anusvara, not a written ङ.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
