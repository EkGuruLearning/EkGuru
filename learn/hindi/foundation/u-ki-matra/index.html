A small hook under the letter: क + ु = कु

Now the signs move underneath. The short 'u' of 'put' is a little hook that hangs below the consonant. One letter cheats: र + ु is written रु with the hook tucked into its side — you will see it in गुरु.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • गुरु पाठशाला में हैं। guru paathshaalaa mein hain.
    EnglishThe teacher is in the school.
  • सुमन गुलाब ला। Suman gulaab laa.
    EnglishSuman, bring a rose.
  • कछुआ पुल पर है। kachhuaa pul par hai.
    EnglishThe tortoise is on the bridge.
  • बुलबुल गा रही है। bulbul gaa rahee hai.
    EnglishThe nightingale is singing.
  • अनुज, गुड़ मत खा। Anuj, gur mat khaa.
    EnglishAnuj, don't eat the jaggery.
  • दुकान में साबुन है। dukaan mein saabun hai.
    EnglishThere is soap in the shop.
  • सुबह सुबह बुलबुल गाती है। subah subah bulbul gaatee hai.
    EnglishEarly in the morning the nightingale sings.
  • हनुमान बहुत बलवान हैं। Hanumaan bahut balvaan hain.
    EnglishHanuman is very strong.
  • तुम चुप रहना। tum chup rahnaa.
    EnglishYou stay quiet.

चतुर कछुआ

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • एक पुराना पुल था। ek puraanaa pul thaa.
    EnglishThere was an old bridge.
  • पुल के पास एक कछुआ रहता था। pul ke paas ek kachhuaa rahtaa thaa.
    EnglishNear the bridge lived a tortoise.
  • कछुआ बहुत चतुर था। kachhuaa bahut chatur thaa.
    EnglishThe tortoise was very clever.
  • सुबह सुबह बुलबुल आती थी। subah subah bulbul aatee thee.
    EnglishEarly every morning a nightingale came.
  • बुलबुल मधुर धुन गाती थी। bulbul madhur dhun gaatee thee.
    EnglishThe nightingale sang a sweet tune.
  • कछुआ चुप रह कर सुनता था। kachhuaa chup rah kar suntaa thaa.
    EnglishThe tortoise would listen quietly.
  • कछुआ खुश था। बुलबुल भी खुश थी। kachhuaa khush thaa. bulbul bhee khush thee.
    EnglishThe tortoise was happy. The nightingale was happy too.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write कु खु गु … for the chart, and रु separately ten times — it is the odd one out. Copy the two-letter words; dictation of ten.

Quick check

Where does ु go?

Underneath the consonant, as a small hook: क → कु.

How is र + ु written?

रु — the hook sits in the side of र instead of underneath. As in गुरु.

Short or long: सुन?

Short — the u of 'put'. The long oo (ू) is the next lesson.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
