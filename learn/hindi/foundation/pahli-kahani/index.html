18. Your first reader पहली कहानी

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-16. Editorial policy and corrections.

Draft: not yet reviewed by a native speaker. Existing availability and indexing are preserved by owner instruction; that is not an editorial approval. Suggest a correction.

Two short stories using everything from lessons 1 to 17

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

This is the last page of a class-1 primer: a real story, read start to finish. Everything in it has been taught. Read each line aloud, tap the speaker to check, and only then open the English. If you finish both stories without the English, you can read Hindi.

प्यासा कौआ

pyaasaa kauaa · The thirsty crow

  • गरमी का दिन था। garmee kaa din thaa.
    EnglishIt was a hot day.
  • एक कौआ बहुत प्यासा था। ek kauaa bahut pyaasaa thaa.
    EnglishA crow was very thirsty.
  • वह पानी की खोज में इधर-उधर उड़ा। vah paanee kee khoj mein idhar-udhar uraa.
    EnglishHe flew here and there looking for water.
  • कहीं पानी नहीं मिला। kaheen paanee naheen milaa.
    EnglishHe found water nowhere.
  • तभी उसे एक घड़ा दिखा। tabhee use ek gharaa dikhaa.
    EnglishJust then he saw a pot.
  • घड़े में थोड़ा पानी था — बहुत नीचे। ghare mein thoraa paanee thaa — bahut neeche.
    EnglishThere was a little water in the pot — very low down.
  • कौए की चोंच पानी तक नहीं पहुँची। kaue kee chonch paanee tak naheen pahunchee.
    EnglishThe crow's beak could not reach the water.
  • कौआ चतुर था। उसने पास में कंकड़ देखे। kauaa chatur thaa. usne paas mein kankar dekhe.
    EnglishThe crow was clever. He saw pebbles nearby.
  • वह एक-एक कंकड़ लाया और घड़े में डाला। vah ek-ek kankar laayaa aur ghare mein daalaa.
    EnglishHe brought the pebbles one by one and dropped them in the pot.
  • पानी ऊपर आ गया। paanee oopar aa gayaa.
    EnglishThe water came up.
  • कौए ने पानी पिया और उड़ गया। kaue ne paanee piyaa aur ur gayaa.
    EnglishThe crow drank the water and flew away.
  • सीख: मेहनत और समझ से हर काम बनता है। seekh: mehnat aur samajh se har kaam bantaa hai.
    EnglishLesson: with effort and sense every job gets done.

मीना का एक दिन

Meenaa kaa ek din · A day in Meena's life

  • मीना सुबह पाँच बजे उठती है। Meenaa subah paanch baje uthtee hai.
    EnglishMeena gets up at five in the morning.
  • वह दाँत साफ करती है और नहाती है। vah daant saaph kartee hai aur nahaatee hai.
    EnglishShe brushes her teeth and bathes.
  • माँ ने गरम दूध और रोटी दी। maan ne garam doodh aur rotee dee.
    EnglishMother gave warm milk and roti.
  • मीना बस्ता उठा कर स्कूल गई। Meenaa bastaa uthaa kar skool gaee.
    EnglishMeena picked up her bag and went to school.
  • कक्षा में उसने हिंदी की किताब पढ़ी। kakshaa mein usne Hindee kee kitaab parhee.
    EnglishIn class she read her Hindi book.
  • छुट्टी में सहेलियों के साथ खेली। chhuttee mein saheliyon ke saath khelee.
    EnglishIn the break she played with her friends.
  • दोपहर को घर आई। भोजन किया। dopahar ko ghar aaee. bhojan kiyaa.
    EnglishIn the afternoon she came home. She ate.
  • शाम को दादी ने कहानी सुनाई। shaam ko daadee ne kahaanee sunaaee.
    EnglishIn the evening grandmother told a story.
  • रात को चाँद निकला। मीना सो गई। raat ko chaand niklaa. Meenaa so gaee.
    EnglishAt night the moon came out. Meena fell asleep.
  • कल फिर एक नया दिन। kal phir ek nayaa din.
    EnglishTomorrow, a new day again.

Questions · प्रश्न

Answer in Hindi, aloud, before you open the answer.

  • कौआ क्यों उड़ रहा था? kauaa kyon ur rahaa thaa? · Why was the crow flying about?
    Answerवह प्यासा था और पानी खोज रहा था। — He was thirsty and looking for water.
  • कौए ने घड़े में क्या डाला? kaue ne ghare mein kyaa daalaa? · What did the crow drop into the pot?
    Answerकंकड़। — Pebbles.
  • मीना कितने बजे उठती है? Meenaa kitne baje uthtee hai? · What time does Meena get up?
    Answerसुबह पाँच बजे। — At five in the morning.
  • शाम को कहानी किसने सुनाई? shaam ko kahaanee kisne sunaaee? · Who told the story in the evening?
    Answerदादी ने। — Grandmother.
Today's task · आज का काम

Read both stories aloud twice. Then write five sentences about YOUR day in the style of 'मीना का एक दिन' — simple present, one line each. That is the first Hindi you will have written on your own.

Quick check

What should you do before opening the English?

Read the line aloud and tap the speaker. Meaning first from the Hindi, English only to check.

Which lesson taught चोंच?

Lesson 13 (ो) and lesson 15 (ं) together — चों-च. Every word here is built from signs you already know.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
