14. The au matra — ौ औ की मात्रा (ौ)

The aa stroke plus two strokes on top: क + ौ = कौ

The last of the vowel signs follows the same logic: ा plus two top strokes. The sound is the 'o' of 'hot', held long. It gives you the word you have been using since lesson 5 — और (and) — plus कौन, मौसम and खिलौना.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • कौआ पौधे पर बैठा है। kauaa paudhe par baithaa hai.
    EnglishThe crow is sitting on the plant.
  • मौसी कचौड़ी और पकौड़े बना रही हैं। mausee kachauree aur pakaure banaa rahee hain.
    EnglishAunt is making kachori and pakoras.
  • चौकीदार रात भर जागता है। chaukeedaar raat bhar jaagtaa hai.
    EnglishThe watchman stays awake all night.
  • नौका पानी में दौड़ रही है। naukaa paanee mein daur rahee hai.
    EnglishThe boat is racing on the water.
  • यह खिलौना कौन लाया? yah khilaunaa kaun laayaa?
    EnglishWho brought this toy?
  • सौ में से नौ कम करो। sau mein se nau kam karo.
    EnglishTake nine away from a hundred.
  • मौसम सुहाना है। mausam suhaanaa hai.
    EnglishThe weather is lovely.
  • चौराहे पर मत दौड़ो। chauraahe par mat dauro.
    EnglishDon't run at the crossroads.
  • औरत हथौड़ा और औजार लाई। aurat hathauraa aur aujaar laaee.
    EnglishThe woman brought a hammer and tools.

कौआ और लौकी

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • एक कौआ था। वह बहुत चतुर था। ek kauaa thaa. vah bahut chatur thaa.
    EnglishThere was a crow. He was very clever.
  • चौक के पास एक पौधा था। chauk ke paas ek paudhaa thaa.
    EnglishNear the square there was a plant.
  • पौधे पर एक लौकी थी। paudhe par ek laukee thee.
    EnglishOn the plant was a bottle gourd.
  • कौआ रोज लौकी को देखता था। kauaa roj laukee ko dekhtaa thaa.
    EnglishEvery day the crow looked at the gourd.
  • एक दिन मौसी आई। उसने लौकी तोड़ी। ek din mausee aaee. usne laukee toree.
    EnglishOne day aunt came. She picked the gourd.
  • कौआ दौड़ा, पर देर हो गई। kauaa dauraa, par der ho gaee.
    EnglishThe crow ran, but it was too late.
  • मौसी ने कौए को पकौड़ा दिया। कौआ खुश। mausee ne kaue ko pakauraa diyaa. kauaa khush.
    EnglishAunt gave the crow a pakora. Happy crow.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write को / कौ, मो / मौ, सो / सौ — one top stroke, then two. Now write the whole बारहखड़ी of क: क का कि की कु कू के कै को कौ कं कः — you own all twelve.

Quick check

What is ौ made of?

The ा stroke with two strokes on top — like ो but doubled, as ै is to े.

Read खिलौना.

खि-लौ-ना — khilaunaa, toy. Three signs in one word: ि, ौ, ा.

What does कौन mean?

'Who'. कौन आया? — who came?

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
