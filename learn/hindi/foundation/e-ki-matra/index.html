11. The e matra — े ए की मात्रा (े)

A small stroke on top of the headline: क + े = के

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Now the signs climb on top. The 'e' of 'gate' (without the English glide) is one short slanted stroke above the headline, over the right side of the letter. It is everywhere in Hindi — के, से, ने, मेरे — so your reading speed jumps after this lesson.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • शेर खेत में है। sher khet mein hai.
    EnglishThe lion is in the field.
  • बेटा मेले में खेल रहा है। betaa mele mein khel rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe son is playing at the fair.
  • मेज पर सेब और केला है। mej par seb aur kelaa hai.
    EnglishOn the table are an apple and a banana.
  • रेल देर से आई। rel der se aaee.
    EnglishThe train came late.
  • पेड़ पर बेर हैं। per par ber hain.
    EnglishThere are berries on the tree.
  • सहेली ने पहेली कही। sahelee ne pahelee kahee.
    EnglishThe friend told a riddle.
  • देश की सेना मेहनत करती है। desh kee senaa mehnat kartee hai.
    EnglishThe country's army works hard.
  • मेरा बेटा केवल जलेबी खाता है। meraa betaa keval jalebee khaataa hai.
    EnglishMy son eats only jalebi.
  • सवेरे मेहमान आए। savere mehmaan aaye.
    EnglishGuests came in the morning.

मेले में

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • आज मेले का दिन है। aaj mele kaa din hai.
    EnglishToday is the day of the fair.
  • बेटा और बेटी मेले में गए। betaa aur betee mele mein gaye.
    EnglishThe son and daughter went to the fair.
  • एक ठेला था। ठेले पर केले और सेब थे। ek thelaa thaa. thele par kele aur seb the.
    EnglishThere was a cart. On the cart were bananas and apples.
  • एक सपेरा बीन बजा रहा था। ek saperaa been bajaa rahaa thaa.
    EnglishA snake charmer was playing his pipe.
  • बेटी ने जलेबी खाई। बेटे ने बेर। betee ne jalebee khaaee. bete ne ber.
    EnglishThe daughter ate jalebi. The son, berries.
  • देर हुई। रेल का समय था। der huee. rel kaa samay thaa.
    EnglishIt got late. It was time for the train.
  • बेटा और बेटी पेड़ के पास से घर आए। betaa aur betee per ke paas se ghar aaye.
    EnglishThe son and daughter came home past the tree.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write के खे गे … for the chart. Then write the little words के, से, ने, मेरे, तेरे ten times each — you have earned them now. Dictation of ten words.

Quick check

Where does े sit?

On top, above the headline, as one slanted stroke over the right of the letter.

Is the e in केला like English 'kay'?

Shorter and purer — no glide at the end. Hold your mouth still.

Read मेहनत.

मे-ह-न-त — mehnat, hard work. Only the first letter wears a sign.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
