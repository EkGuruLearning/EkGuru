7. The i matra — ि इ की मात्रा (ि)

The sign that is written BEFORE the letter but read after it: क + ि = कि

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

This is the matra that surprises everyone. The short 'i' sign stands to the LEFT of its consonant — you write it first, but you say it second. कि is 'ki', not 'ik'. Children chant 'छोटी इ की मात्रा पहले' — the small i goes first — until it sticks.

The sign

कka
+
िthe sign · i
=
किki

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • रवि दिन भर किताब पढ़। Ravi din bhar kitaab parh.
    EnglishRavi, read your book all day.
  • किसान हल चला। kisaan hal chalaa.
    EnglishThe farmer drove the plough.
  • पिता घर पर हैं। pitaa ghar par hain.
    EnglishFather is at home.
  • हिरन वन में है। hiran van mein hai.
    EnglishThe deer is in the forest.
  • गिलास में जल है। gilaas mein jal hai.
    EnglishThere is water in the glass.
  • चिड़िया किला पर है। chiriyaa kilaa par hai.
    EnglishThe bird is on the fort.
  • आज शनिवार है, कल रविवार। aaj shanivaar hai, kal ravivaar.
    EnglishToday is Saturday, tomorrow Sunday.
  • डाकिया टिकट लाया। daakiyaa tikat laayaa.
    EnglishThe postman brought the stamp.
  • बारिश में विमान मत चला। baarish mein vimaan mat chalaa.
    EnglishDon't fly the plane in the rain.

किसान और बारिश

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • किसान का नाम किशन है। kisaan kaa naam Kishan hai.
    EnglishThe farmer's name is Kishan.
  • किशन दिन भर काम करता है। Kishan din bhar kaam kartaa hai.
    EnglishKishan works all day.
  • आज बारिश है। aaj baarish hai.
    EnglishToday there is rain.
  • हिरन वन में भाग गया। hiran van mein bhaag gayaa.
    EnglishThe deer ran into the forest.
  • चिड़िया नारियल पर आ गई। chiriyaa naariyal par aa gaee.
    EnglishThe bird came onto the coconut tree.
  • किशन घर आया। पिता चाय लाया। Kishan ghar aayaa. pitaa chaay laayaa.
    EnglishKishan came home. Father brought tea.
  • कल रविवार है — आराम का दिन। kal ravivaar hai — aaraam kaa din.
    EnglishTomorrow is Sunday — a day of rest.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write कि खि गि घि … for the whole chart. Say 'first the sign, then the letter' each time you write, and 'letter, then i' each time you read. Dictation of ten words.

Quick check

Where is ि written?

To the LEFT of the consonant — but it is read AFTER it. कि is 'ki'.

How is दिन read?

द + ि = दि (di), then न: din — 'day'.

What is the trap in किला?

Seeing ि first and reading 'ik'. The sign belongs to the letter on its right: कि-ला.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
