6. The aa matra — ा आ की मात्रा (ा)

One straight stroke after the letter: क + ा = का

The first matra every child learns is the easiest to see: a single vertical stroke to the right of the letter, hanging from the headline. It turns the short 'a' into the long 'aa' of 'father'. क becomes का; म becomes मा; put them together and you can already read माता and काका.

राम का घर

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • राम का घर बड़ा है। Raam kaa ghar baraa hai.
    EnglishRam's house is big.
  • घर में माता, काका और दादा हैं। ghar mein maataa, kaakaa aur daadaa hain.
    EnglishIn the house are mother, uncle and grandfather.
  • आज बाजार जाना है। aaj baajaar jaanaa hai.
    EnglishToday we have to go to the market.
  • काका आम, अनार और गाजर लाया। kaakaa aam, anaar aur gaajar laayaa.
    EnglishUncle brought mangoes, pomegranate and carrots.
  • माता दाल और चावल बना। maataa daal aur chaaval banaa.
    EnglishMother, make dal and rice.
  • दादा चाय के साथ अखबार पढ़। daadaa chaay ke saath akhbaar parh.
    EnglishGrandfather, read the paper with your tea.

    EnglishRam, clean your hands, eat your food.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write का खा गा घा … for the whole consonant chart — every letter wearing ा. Then copy the two-letter words twice and take dictation of ten of them.

Quick check

Where does ा go?

To the right of the consonant, as one vertical stroke hanging from the headline: क → का.

What is the difference between कम and काम?

कम (kam, less) has the built-in short a; काम (kaam, work) wears ा and is held long.

Why does आम start with आ and not ा?

A matra needs a consonant to sit on. At the start of a word, with no consonant before it, the full vowel letter आ is used.

A note on the Hindi spellings
