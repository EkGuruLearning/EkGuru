13. The o matra — ो ओ की मात्रा (ो)

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-16. Editorial policy and corrections.

Draft: not yet reviewed by a native speaker. Existing availability and indexing are preserved by owner instruction; that is not an editorial approval. Suggest a correction.

The aa stroke plus one stroke on top: क + ो = को

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

Look closely: ो is the ा stroke you already know, with the े stroke added on top of it. Two old friends make a new sound — the 'o' of 'go', with no glide. Hindi's little postposition को is written with it, and so are मोर, रोटी and होली.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • मोर नाच रहा है। mor naach rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe peacock is dancing.
  • तोता रोटी खा रहा है। totaa rotee khaa rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe parrot is eating roti.
  • घोड़ा सड़क पर भाग रहा है। ghoraa sarak par bhaag rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe horse is running on the road.
  • कोयल मीठा बोलती है। koyal meethaa boltee hai.
    EnglishThe cuckoo sings sweetly.
  • टोकरी में समोसे और गोभी हैं। tokree mein samose aur gobhee hain.
    EnglishIn the basket are samosas and cauliflower.
  • सोमवार को होली है। somvaar ko Holee hai.
    EnglishHoli is on Monday.
  • मोहन सो रहा है। Mohan so rahaa hai.
    EnglishMohan is sleeping.
  • धोबी कपड़े धो रहा है। dhobee kapre dho rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe washerman is washing clothes.
  • रसोई में शोर मत करो। rasoee mein shor mat karo.
    EnglishDon't make noise in the kitchen.

होली का दिन

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • आज होली है। aaj Holee hai.
    EnglishToday is Holi.
  • भोर से ही ढोल बज रहा है। bhor se hee dhol baj rahaa hai.
    EnglishSince dawn the drum has been beating.
  • मोहन और गोपाल गुलाल ले आए। Mohan aur Gopaal gulaal le aaye.
    EnglishMohan and Gopal brought gulal (coloured powder).
  • लोग गा रहे हैं, नाच रहे हैं। log gaa rahe hain, naach rahe hain.
    EnglishPeople are singing and dancing.
  • रसोई से समोसे की खुशबू आ रही है। rasoee se samose kee khushboo aa rahee hai.
    EnglishThe smell of samosas is coming from the kitchen.
  • तोता भी बोल रहा है — होली है! totaa bhee bol rahaa hai — Holee hai!
    EnglishEven the parrot is saying — it's Holi!
  • दोपहर को सब सो गए। dopahar ko sab so gaye.
    EnglishIn the afternoon everyone slept.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write का / को, मा / मो, सा / सो — add the top stroke and hear the vowel change. Then write को, दो, सो, रो, धो, खो, बो — the one-letter words — and dictation of ten.

Quick check

What is ो made of?

The ा stroke with a े stroke on top: two signs you already know, one new vowel.

Read रोटी.

रो-टी — rotee, flatbread. One sign on top, one on the right.

What is the difference between दो and धो?

दो (do) is 'two'; धो (dho) is 'wash' — the breathy ध, not the plain द.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
