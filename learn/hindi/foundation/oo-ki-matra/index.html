10. The oo matra — ू ऊ की मात्रा (ू)

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-16. Editorial policy and corrections.

Draft: not yet reviewed by a native speaker. Existing availability and indexing are preserved by owner instruction; that is not an editorial approval. Suggest a correction.

A bigger hook under the letter, held long: क + ू = कू

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

The long 'oo' of 'boot' is also written underneath, with a fuller curl than the short one. Again र is the exception: रू with the sign on its side, as in रूप. Compare सुन (listen) and सूँघ (smell) — same place, different length.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • सूरज दूर है। sooraj door hai.
    EnglishThe sun is far away.
  • चूहा दूध पी रहा है। choohaa doodh pee rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe mouse is drinking milk.
  • भालू झूला झूल रहा है। bhaaloo jhoolaa jhool rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe bear is on the swing.
  • माली फूल ला रहा है। maalee phool laa rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe gardener is bringing flowers.
  • दादी आलू और मूली ला। daadee aaloo aur moolee laa.
    EnglishGrandmother, bring potatoes and radish.
  • कबूतर दाना चुग रहा है। kabootar daanaa chug rahaa hai.
    EnglishThe pigeon is pecking at grain.
  • जूता बाहर रख। jootaa baahar rakh.
    EnglishLeave the shoes outside.
  • पूजा का झाड़ू टूट गया। Poojaa kaa jhaaroo toot gayaa.
    EnglishPooja's broom broke.

    EnglishWatermelon tastes sweet in the sunshine.

चूहा और भालू

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • एक चूहा था। एक भालू था। ek choohaa thaa. ek bhaaloo thaa.
    EnglishThere was a mouse. There was a bear.
  • भालू बड़ा था। bhaaloo baraa thaa.
    EnglishThe bear was big.
  • भालू झूला झूलता था। bhaaloo jhoolaa jhoolataa thaa.
    EnglishThe bear used to play on the swing.
  • चूहा दूध पीता था। choohaa doodh peetaa thaa.
    EnglishThe mouse used to drink milk.
  • एक दिन झूला टूट गया। ek din jhoolaa toot gayaa.
    EnglishOne day the swing broke.
  • चूहा दूर से भागा आया। choohaa door se bhaagaa aayaa.
    EnglishThe mouse came running from far away.
  • चूहा आया, भालू उठा। भालू खुश हुआ। choohaa aayaa, bhaaloo uthaa. bhaaloo khush huaa.
    EnglishThe mouse came, the bear got up. The bear was happy.
  • सूरज डूबा। सब घर गए। sooraj doobaa. sab ghar gaye.
    EnglishThe sun set. Everyone went home.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write pairs: सुन / सून, कुल / कूल, पुल / फूल — and feel the length. Then copy the two-letter words and take dictation of ten.

Quick check

How do ु and ू differ on the page?

Both hang under the letter; ू has the fuller, longer curl. Sound: short 'put' vs long 'boot'.

How is र + ू written?

रू, with the sign at the side — as in रूप, रूई.

Read झाड़ू.

झा-ड़ू — jhaaroo, broom. The ड़ has a dot: a flapped r.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
