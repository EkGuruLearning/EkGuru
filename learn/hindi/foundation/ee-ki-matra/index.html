8. The ee matra — ी ई की मात्रा (ी)

The same hook, but after the letter and held long: क + ी = की

🔊 Tap any Hindi word to hear it. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page. This lesson opens one step at a time — press Next to turn the page, or choose Whole lesson to read it straight through.

The long 'ee' sign looks like the short one flipped: it stands to the RIGHT of the letter and the sound is held, like 'see'. So ि before = short, ी after = long. Hindi loves this sound — most feminine words end in it: लड़की, बकरी, रोटी.

Sentences · वाक्य

Read the Hindi first. Open the English only to check.

  • नदी का पानी नीला है। nadee kaa paanee neelaa hai.
    EnglishThe river's water is blue.
  • दीदी खीर बनाती है। deedee kheer banaatee hai.
    EnglishElder sister makes kheer.
  • मछली पानी में रहती है। machhlee paanee mein rahtee hai.
    EnglishThe fish lives in water.
  • सीता गली में खड़ी है। Seetaa galee mein kharee hai.
    EnglishSita is standing in the lane.
  • हाथी बड़ा है। haathee baraa hai.
    EnglishThe elephant is big.
  • नानी कहानी कहती है। naanee kahaanee kahtee hai.
    EnglishGrandmother tells a story.
  • बकरी घास खाती है। bakree ghaas khaatee hai.
    EnglishThe goat eats grass.
  • लड़की साड़ी पहनती है। larkee saaree pahantee hai.
    EnglishThe girl wears a saree.
  • खिड़की से तितली आई। khirkee se titlee aaee.
    EnglishA butterfly came in through the window.

नानी की कहानी

A reading passage — the kind that closes every primer chapter. Read it aloud, start to finish.

  • रात है। दीदी और मीना नानी के पास हैं। raat hai. deedee aur Meenaa naanee ke paas hain.
    EnglishIt is night. Elder sister and Meena are with grandmother.
  • नानी कहानी कहती है। naanee kahaanee kahtee hai.
    EnglishGrandmother tells a story.
  • एक नदी थी। नदी का पानी नीला था। ek nadee thee. nadee kaa paanee neelaa thaa.
    EnglishThere was a river. The river's water was blue.
  • नदी में मछली रहती थी। nadee mein machhlee rahtee thee.
    EnglishA fish lived in the river.
  • पास ही पीपल था। पीपल पर तितली थी। paas hee peepal thaa. peepal par titlee thee.
    EnglishNearby was a peepal tree. On the peepal was a butterfly.
  • मछली और तितली में यारी थी। machhlee aur titlee mein yaaree thee.
    EnglishThe fish and the butterfly were friends.
  • मीना थक गई। कहानी कल। Meenaa thak gaee. kahaanee kal.
    EnglishMeena got tired. The story continues tomorrow.
Today's task · आज का काम

Write दिन / दीन, मिल / मील, सिल / सील — short and long side by side — and say them. Then copy the three-letter words and take dictation of ten.

Quick check

Which side of the letter does ी go on?

The RIGHT side. ि (short) goes on the left, ी (long) on the right.

How long is the sound in नीला?

Held long, like 'see'. नीला is neelaa — blue.

Why do so many words end in ी?

It is the usual feminine ending — लड़की, बकरी, रोटी, साड़ी — and shows up in words for 'she does': खाती, कहती.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.
