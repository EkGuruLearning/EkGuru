4. Consonants 3 — lips, semivowels, sibilants and the three joined letters व्यंजन — प वर्ग, य र ल व, श ष स ह, क्ष त्र ज्ञ

The last row is made with the lips, then come the four 'in-between' letters, the three hissing sounds and ह. Indian primers finish the chart with क्ष, त्र and ज्ञ — three joined letters so common that children learn them as single shapes.

The letters

Each letter with its word

This is how the chart hangs on an Indian classroom wall: the letter, and the one word every child learns it by. Say the letter, say the word, tap to hear both.

What to notice

  • फ is p with a puff, not an English f. The f sound is written फ़ with a dot, in borrowed words like फ़ोन.
  • व sits between v and w; nobody will mind which you say. श and ष both sound like 'sh' in everyday Hindi.
  • क्ष त्र ज्ञ are really two letters joined (क+ष, त+र, ज+ञ). They close every alphabet chart in India, so meet them now and learn how joining works in lesson 17.
Today's task · आज का काम

Copy the full chart now — all 33 consonants plus क्ष त्र ज्ञ — row by row, saying each letter. Keep the page: it is your reference for the next fourteen lessons.

Quick check

Is फ an f sound?

No — it is p with a strong puff of air. The f sound is फ़, with a dot, in words like फ़ोन.

What is ज्ञ made of?

ज + ञ joined. In modern Hindi it is said 'gya', as in ज्ञान (knowledge).

How many consonants in the chart?

33 — five rows of five (25), plus य र ल व and श ष स ह — with क्ष त्र ज्ञ added at the end as joined letters.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Read it to a person

Reading aloud to someone who can correct you is worth five silent repetitions. A single lesson with a tutor at this stage fixes the two or three sounds you cannot hear yourself getting wrong.
