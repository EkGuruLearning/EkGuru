  • Everyday HindiHold a short everyday conversation: how you are, what you did, what you want.
  • Grammar FoundationsUnderstand and use Hindi word order, gender, agreement and tenses reliably.
  • Hindi From ZeroGo from knowing nothing to reading Devanagari and holding a short scripted conversation.
  • Reading HindiRead Devanagari confidently enough to sound out any word and read short real-world texts.

  • Travel HindiHandle the situations a traveller actually meets: food, directions, shopping, prices, and emergencies.
