Dialogue — Grandma's kitchen

Ravi: दादी, खाना तैयार है! (Daadi, khaana taiyaar hai!)
Grandma, the food is ready!

Grandma: आई, बेटा। दाल गरम है? (Aayi, beta. Daal garam hai?)
Coming, child. Is the dal hot?

Ravi: हाँ। दादी, हाथ धो लो। (Haan. Daadi, haath dho lo.)
Yes. Wash your hands, grandma.

Grandma: धो लिए। क्या स्वादिष्ट चावल! (Dho liye. Kya svaadisht chaaval!)
I washed them. What tasty rice!
