Advanced Hindi

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Past conversation into real life: the clinic, the office, school, the weather, home and festivals. Six word lists with phrases, dialogues and self-checks.

Who this is for

You hold conversations, read paragraphs and handle tenses — now you want the vocabulary of real adult life: symptoms at the clinic, files at the office, the playground, the monsoon, the family table and the festival calendar.

What you will be able to do

Use 800+ words across six everyday domains, say the phrases that actually get used, follow short dialogues, and test yourself with ten quick questions per topic.

The six topics

Health & doctorBody, symptoms and the clinic — Hindi for staying well. Office & workStaff, stationery and the daily commute — Hindi for working life. School & learningSchool, exams and the playground — Hindi for learning life. Weather & natureSeasons, sky and the living land — Hindi for the world outside. Home & familyRooms, relations and the family table — Hindi for home. Festivals & worshipFestivals, worship and celebration — Hindi for the days that shine.
