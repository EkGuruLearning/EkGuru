Dialogue — Diwali evening

Mother: शुभ दीवाली, बेटा! (Shubh Deepavali, beta!)
Happy Diwali, son!

Son: शुभ दीवाली, माँ! दीया जलाओ। (Shubh Deepavali, maa! Deeya jalaao.)
Happy Diwali, mother! Light the lamp.

Mother: जला दिया। मिठाई खाओ, बेटा। (Jala diya. Mithaai khaao, beta.)
I lit it. Eat sweets, child.

Son: कितनी स्वादिष्ट! अब पटाखे फोड़ेंगे। (Kitni svaadisht! Ab pataakhe phodenge.)
So tasty! Now let's burst crackers.

Quick check

त्यौहार means…

festival — त्यौहार (tyauhaar) = festival.

Which is 'Diwali'?

दीवाली — दीवाली = Diwali, the festival of lamps.

'त्यौहार मुबारक!' means…

happy festival! — त्यौहार मुबारक! — मुबारक = blessed.

Say 'eat sweets'. You say…

मिठाई खाइए — मिठाई खाइए = eat sweets.

मंदिर means…

temple — मंदिर (mandir) = temple; मस्जिद = mosque.

Which is the nine-night festival?

नवरात्रि — नवरात्रि — nine nights of the goddess.

'दीया जलाइए' means…

light the lamp — दीया जलाइए — जलाना = to light.

Say 'go to the temple'. You say…

मंदिर जाइए — मंदिर जाइए = go to the temple.

भजन means…

devotional song — भजन (bhajan) = devotional song.

Which is 'sweets'?

मिठाई — मिठाई = sweets.
