1. Copy the letters

Check each copy against the printed model. Accept variations in handwriting; focus on recognisable shapes and the headline.

2. First vowel

अनार → अ; आम → आ; इमली → इ.

3. Vowel signs

The vowel is heard after the consonant: कि is ki, even though ि is placed to the left. अ and आ have different shapes and vowel qualities; आ is a longer open vowel.
