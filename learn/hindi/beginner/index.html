Who this is for

You have never studied Hindi, or you tried and stalled. You can read nothing yet, or only a few letters.

What you will be able to do

Read and sound out Devanagari, count to 100, greet people correctly, build simple SOV sentences, and use the right 'you' (aap/tum/tu).
