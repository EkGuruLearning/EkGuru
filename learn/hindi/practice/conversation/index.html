How conversation practice works

Each scenario shows you a line and asks you to pick the best reply. The simulator is rule-based, not AI: it rehearses set phrases for real situations like greetings, shopping, and directions.

Say every line out loud as you go. Quiet reading builds recognition; speaking builds recall. When a scenario feels easy, move to the next one.
