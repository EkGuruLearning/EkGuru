Tamil worksheet — sample

Five questions from the Tamil quiz bank on this page’s topic. Pick a topic and press Make worksheet for a fresh sheet, or print this one as it is.

1. What does வணக்கம் mean?

2. How do you say 'thank you' in Tamil?

3. The letter ழ is…

4. நீங்கள் எப்படி இருக்கிறீர்கள்? means…

5. நாளை means…

Answers

1. hello — வணக்கம் (vaṇakkam) is the respectful universal hello — to elders, officials, anyone.

2. நன்றி — நன்றி (naṉṟi). மன்னிக்கவும் is excuse-me/sorry — don't mix them.

3. a sound no English speaker has (retroflex approximant) — ழ (ḻa) — the letter Tamil is named for (தமிழ்). Curl the tongue back and let sound flow around it.

4. How are you? (formal) — நீங்கள் (nīṅkaḷ) is formal 'you' — safe with every stranger.

5. tomorrow — நாளை (nāḷai) = tomorrow. Yesterday is நேற்று.

How the Tamil worksheet is built

Choose a topic and a number of prompts, and the builder lays out a printable Tamil worksheet with writing space and an answer section at the end. It is designed for paper: one topic per sheet, prompts in a readable size, answers on their own block so they can be folded away. Printing from the browser prints just the sheet, with the watermark and this site's name kept on it.

Marking and reusing a sheet

Write your answers first, then check them against the answer block and note which ones needed a second attempt — those are the words to review tomorrow. Nothing is stored for you, so print a fresh sheet for a fresh attempt; a set of five finished sheets makes an honest revision pack.
