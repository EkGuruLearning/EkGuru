Tamil basics — the script, sounds and first steps

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The starting point: the Tamil script, your first words, and the honest answer to where to begin. Only 31 letters — no aspirates, no separate voiced sounds. க reads ka, ga or kha depending on position: one letter, three jobs. Fewer letters to learn, more listening to do. Spoken Tamil also shortens many written forms — this course teaches the written form first and flags the street version.

The alphabet at a glance

12 vowels and 24 consonants. The full tables with sounds are on the pronunciation page — learn to recognise them before you memorise any words.

The order to learn in

  1. Script + sounds — Tamil is phonetic: learn the letters and you can read anything.
  2. Greetings + pronouns — say hello, introduce yourself, ask where.
  3. Numbers 1–20 — prices, time and quantities unlock immediately.
  4. Word order + present tense — one pattern (நான் சாதம் சாப்பிடுகிறேன்) builds a thousand sentences.
  5. Topics (food, travel, shopping) — learn what your life needs first.
Honest note. US Foreign Service Institute Category III (about 1,100 hours to professional proficiency) — grouped with Hindi. The small alphabet helps; diglossia (written vs spoken differ a lot) and agglutination (long glued-together words) are the real challenges.
