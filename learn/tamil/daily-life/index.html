Culture in five words

  • பொங்கல் (Poṅkal) — The Tamil harvest festival (January) — pongal pots boiling over at sunrise, sugarcane in every hand, and மாட்டுப் பொங்கல் honouring the cattle.
  • தமிழ் புத்தாண்டு (Tamil New Year) — Mid-April new year (புத்தாண்டு) — mango pachadi, new clothes, temple bells, and the year's first banana leaf.
  • தீபாவளி (Dīpāvali) — Tamil Diwali — oil lamps, new dresses at dawn, murukku mountains. Celebrated with full noise, often a day before the north.
  • ஜல்லிக்கட்டு (Jallikattu) — Pongal bull-taming — ancient, fierce, fiercely loved. Part sport, part identity, all Tamil.
  • ஃபில்டர் காபி (filter coffee) — Meter coffee in dabara-tumbler — frothed by pouring arm's length, drunk scalding. The state religion in a steel cup.
