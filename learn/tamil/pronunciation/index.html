Tamil pronunciation, reading & writing

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. Only 31 letters — no aspirates, no separate voiced sounds. க reads ka, ga or kha depending on position: one letter, three jobs. Fewer letters to learn, more listening to do. Spoken Tamil also shortens many written forms — this course teaches the written form first and flags the street version.
