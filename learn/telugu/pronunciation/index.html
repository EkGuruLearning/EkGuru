Telugu pronunciation, reading & writing

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The sounds learners get wrong, and the path from your first letter to reading real words. Round, looping letters — the curves come from writing on palm leaves, where straight cuts would tear the leaf. Left to right and fully phonetic: 16 vowels (short and long e/o matter!) and all the consonant rows Hindi has, plus extras like ళ and a second R (ఱ).
