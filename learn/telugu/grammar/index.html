Telugu grammar

Telugu word order, verbs, pronouns and the mistakes everyone makes — explained, not listed.

1. The verb goes last

Telugu is subject–object–verb: నేను అన్నం తింటాను (nēnu annaṁ tinṭānu) — मैं चावल खाता हूँ — “I eat rice (word-for-word: I · rice · eat)”. Learn this once and every sentence parses.

2. Gender lives in the verb (third person)

Telugu nouns don't force masculine/feminine on you, but third-person verbs agree: వాడు తింటాడు (he eats), ఆమె తింటుంది (she eats). First and second person never split — నేను తింటాను is said by men and women alike.

4. Present tense — one full verb

“To eat” (తినడం) in the present. One verb, seven endings: the ending tells you WHO — and in the third person, their gender. Formal మీరు shares its ending with they.

Iనేనుతింటానుtinṭānu
you (intimate)నువ్వుతింటావుtinṭāvu
you (formal)మీరుతింటారుtinṭāru

6. Negation changes the ending

Telugu negates by swapping the verb ending: నేను తింటాను (I eat) → నేను తినను (tinanu, I don't eat). No extra word — the ending does the work.

The sentence ladder

Do not learn grammar as a list of rules. Learn it as a ladder with seven rungs, where every rung is something you can say out loud today.

  1. Say what you are. Simple I-sentences with no verb change.
  2. Say what you want. The same pattern with a noun from the common words list.
  3. Say what you do. The present-tense table above, first person only.
  4. Say what someone else does. The same table, third person — this is where the endings move.
  5. Say what happened. Past tense, five sentences about yesterday.
  6. Say what will happen. Future tense, five sentences about tomorrow.
  7. Say what does not happen. Negative forms of five sentences you have already built.

The grammar rule that matters most. One verb, seven endings: the ending tells you WHO — and in the third person, their gender. Formal మీరు shares its ending with they. Learn the pattern, then change one thing at a time — verb, then person, then tense. Learners who change everything at once produce sentences they cannot debug.
