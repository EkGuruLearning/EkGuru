Culture in five words

  • సంక్రాంతి (Sankrānti) — The Telugu harvest festival (January) — kites on every terrace, sugarcane in every hand, pongal pots boiling over in the courtyard.
  • ఉగాది (Ugādi) — Telugu New Year — the sweet-bitter ugadi pachadi teaches the year's first lesson: life is a mix, taste it all.
  • బతుకమ్మ (Bathukamma) — Telangana's flower festival — nine nights of stacked marigolds and circle songs. Women build it, everyone sings it.
  • బోనాలు (Bonālu) — Ashada folk festival for Mahankali — pots of offering carried dancing through old Hyderabad's lanes.
  • హైదరాబాదీ బిర్యానీ (Hyderabadi biryani) — The dum-cooked legend — Paradise vs Bawarchi is a friendly war. Pick a side, then eat at both.
