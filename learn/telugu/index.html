Everything below is free and trilingual: every word and phrase in English, Hindi and Telugu (తెలుగు, Telugu) side by side — with pronunciation for each one. 95+ million people speak Telugu: Andhra Pradesh and Telangana — Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam — plus a huge US diaspora, especially in tech.

How to use this course. Start at Beginner, work down the topics in order, and quiz yourself after each one. US Foreign Service Institute Category III (about 1,100 hours to professional proficiency) — grouped with Hindi. The script is phonetic and verbs are regular; the new habits are agglutination (long words glued from small parts) and inclusive/exclusive 'we'.
