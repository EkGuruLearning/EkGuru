Hindi for Family & Heritage Learners

A path for heritage learners: the family words, respectful forms and culture notes that matter at home — without assuming every family speaks the same variety.

Honest scope. This is a curated path built from real published phrases and lessons — a context on top of the shared Hindi course, not a new course. Nothing here is AI-generated; every phrase is copied from a page that already exists on EkGuru.

Reconnect with family and culture — the words and forms that matter at home, without assuming every family speaks the same variety.

Who it is for: NRI and heritage learners who grew up around Hindi but did not learn it formally. Built on the shared Hindi Basics course — the context re-orders real material, it does not replace the course.

Heritage content is built from the family, courtesy and culture material that exists today. It is a starting path, not a claim to cover every diaspora variety of Hindi.

Courtesy & respectful forms
