Learn Hindi for India — the visitor's phrase path

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: legacy editorial date unknown. Editorial policy and corrections.

A visitor's path through Hindi: the phrases that handle a trip — airport, taxi, hotel, food, money and help — organised so you can rehearse each situation before you land.

Honest scope. This is a curated path built from real published phrases and lessons — a context on top of the shared Hindi course, not a new course. Nothing here is AI-generated; every phrase is copied from a page that already exists on EkGuru.

The visitor's path: survive a trip — airport, taxi, food, money, help — with phrases you can rehearse.

Who it is for: Visitors and first-time travellers to India. Built on the shared Hindi Basics course — the context re-orders real material, it does not replace the course.

Greetings & introductions

  • नमस्तेnamasteHello / goodbye
  • आप कैसे हैं?aap kaise hain?How are you? (polite)
  • मैं ठीक हूँmain theek hoonI am fine
  • मेरा नाम ___ हैmera naam ___ haiMy name is ___
  • धन्यवादdhanyavaadThank you
  • How to say hello (and when namaste is wrong)

Taxi & directions

  • मीटर से चलिएmeter se chaliyeGo by the meter
  • यहाँ रोकिएyahaan rokiyeStop here
  • सीधे जाइएseedhe jaaiyeGo straight
  • बाएँ / दाएँbaayen / daayenLeft / right
  • कितनी देर लगेगी?kitni der lagegi?How long will it take?
  • Hindi phrases for travel — the 50 you need

Restaurant & food

  • तीखा नहींteekha nahinNot spicy
  • कम तीखाkam teekhaLess spicy
  • मैं शाकाहारी हूँmain shaakaahaari hoonI am vegetarian
  • पानी की बोतलpaani ki botalA bottle of water
  • बिल दीजिएbil deejiyeThe bill, please
  • Restaurant, shopping & transport phrases

Shopping & money

  • यह कितने का है?yah kitne ka hai?How much is this?
  • बहुत महँगा हैbahut mehenga haiThat's very expensive
  • कम कीजिएkam keejiyePlease reduce it
  • आख़िरी दाम क्या है?aakhiri daam kya hai?What's your final price?
  • नहीं चाहिएnahin chaahiyeI don't want it

Trains & getting around

  • यह ट्रेन कहाँ जाती है?yeh train kahaan jaati hai?Where does this train go?
  • कौन सा प्लेटफॉर्म?kaun sa platform?Which platform?
  • यह सीट खाली है?yeh seat khaali hai?Is this seat free?
  • कितनी देर से है?kitni der se hai?How late is it?

Emergency & help

  • मदद कीजिएmadad keejiyePlease help
  • मुझे मदद चाहिएmujhe madad chaahiyeI need help
  • मेरी तबीयत ठीक नहीं हैmeri tabiyat theek nahin haiI'm not well
  • डॉक्टर कहाँ है?doctor kahaan hai?Where is a doctor?
  • पुलिस को बुलाइएpolice ko bulaaiyeCall the police

Polite requests

  • जीjeeAdd to anything to make it respectful
  • भैया / दीदीbhaiya / didiBrother / sister — how you address people
  • मुझे पानी चाहिएmujhe paani chaahiyeI need water
  • क्या आप अंग्रेज़ी बोलते हैं?kya aap angrezi bolte hain?Do you speak English?
  • Aap, tum or tu — getting politeness right
