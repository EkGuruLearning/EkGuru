Learn Hindi for your context

The same Hindi course, framed for how you will actually use it. A context is a lens — a curated path of real phrases and lessons — on top of the shared Hindi course. It is not a separate course and it does not duplicate the lessons; it re-uses them for your situation.

Honest scope. Today there are two contexts: the India visitor and the family/heritage learner. Every phrase in them is copied from a page that already exists on EkGuru — nothing here is AI-generated or invented for SEO.

Choose your context

  • Learn Hindi for India — the visitor's pathGreetings, taxis, restaurant, shopping, trains and help — the phrases that handle a trip, organised so you can rehearse each situation.
  • Hindi for Family & Heritage LearnersFamily words, respectful forms, culture and reading progression — for reconnecting with family, without assuming every family speaks the same variety.
