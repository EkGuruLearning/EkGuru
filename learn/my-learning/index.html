Family & children

A privacy mode for parents and family learners, saved on this device only. When on, it stops visitor counting and hides the tutor/booking contact points on pages that carry them.

What it never does: no child profile, no messaging, no child contact details, no social features for minors, no hidden recording — the microphone is only ever used after an explicit tap, and there is no account to collect anything anyway.

Honest scope: this is a privacy mode, not child lesson content — no child-specific lessons have been authored yet.
