CHAPTER 34 / LESSON 02

साक्षात्कार और प्रस्तुति

Interviews & presentations · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aकिसी चुनौती का उदाहरण दीजिए।kisee chunautee ka udaaharan deejiyeGive an example of a challenge.
Bहमारे पास समय कम था।hamaare paas samay kam thaWe had little time.
Aआपने क्या किया?aapne kya kiyaWhat did you do?
Bहमने काम बाँटा और समय पर पूरा किया।hamne kaam baanta aur samay par poora kiyaWe divided the work and finished on time.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
What makes the answer concrete?

A situation, an action and a result.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

हमारे पास समय कम था।
