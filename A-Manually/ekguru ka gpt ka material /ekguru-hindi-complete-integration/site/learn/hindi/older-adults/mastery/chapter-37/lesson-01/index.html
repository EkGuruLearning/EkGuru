CHAPTER 37 / LESSON 01

प्रमाण और स्रोत

Research & source evaluation · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Distinguish direct evidence, reported information and inference. Check author, date, purpose and sample. A small observation cannot justify a universal claim.
