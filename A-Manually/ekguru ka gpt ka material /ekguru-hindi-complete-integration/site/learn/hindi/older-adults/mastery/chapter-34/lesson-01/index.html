CHAPTER 34 / LESSON 01

साक्षात्कार और प्रस्तुति

Interviews & presentations · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

A strong spoken answer states a situation, action and result. In a presentation, introduce the purpose, organize points and conclude with the requested action. Do not invent qualifications.
