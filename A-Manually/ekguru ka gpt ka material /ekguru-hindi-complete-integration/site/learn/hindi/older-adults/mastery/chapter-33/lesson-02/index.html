CHAPTER 33 / LESSON 02

सारांश और पुनर्कथन

Summaries & paraphrase · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aलेख का मुख्य विचार क्या है?lekh ka mukhya vichaar kya haiWhat is the article's main idea?
Bपुस्तकालय सबके लिए सुलभ होना चाहिए।pustakaalay sabke liye sulabh hona chaahiyeLibraries should be accessible to everyone.
Aक्या लेखक ने उदाहरण दिया है?kya lekhak ne udaaharan diya haiDid the author give an example?
Bहाँ, बड़े अक्षरों वाली किताबों का।haan, bade aksharon vaalee kitaabon kaYes, large-print books.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Should a summary invent new evidence?

No.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

पुस्तकालय सबके लिए सुलभ होना चाहिए।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Summarize the dialogue in two Hindi sentences and distinguish the argument from its example.
