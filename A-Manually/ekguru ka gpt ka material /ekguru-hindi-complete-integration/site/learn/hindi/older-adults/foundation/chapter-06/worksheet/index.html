समय और दिन

Time & days

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

Today

Yesterday or tomorrow

At three

At half past three

2. Respond to the situation

Arranging tea with a friend.

आप कब आएँगे?

What time is साढ़े तीन?

3. Independent task

Say a suitable meeting time and confirm it with your partner.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

आज — Today

कल — Yesterday or tomorrow

तीन बजे — At three

साढ़े तीन बजे — At half past three

Model reply: आज तीन बजे।

3:30.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
