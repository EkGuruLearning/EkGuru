CHAPTER 06 / LESSON 01

समय और दिन

Time & days · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

बजे is used for clock times. साढ़े means half past; सवा means a quarter past; पौने means a quarter to the stated hour. आज, कल and परसों require context.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
आजaajToday
कलkalYesterday or tomorrow
तीन बजेteen bajeAt three
साढ़े तीन बजेsaadhe teen bajeAt half past three
