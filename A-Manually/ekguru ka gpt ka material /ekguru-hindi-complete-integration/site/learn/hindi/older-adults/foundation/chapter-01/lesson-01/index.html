CHAPTER 01 / LESSON 01

नमस्ते और परिचय

Greetings & introductions · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Start with namaste and the respectful आप. मेरा नाम works regardless of your gender. हूँ goes with मैं, हैं with आप.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
नमस्तेnamasteHello
मेरा नाम एलेक्स है।mera naam Alex haiMy name is Alex.
आप कैसे हैं?aap kaise hainHow are you? (to a man or mixed group)
मैं ठीक हूँ।main theek hoonI am fine.

Which phrase means: Hello

Answer

नमस्ते

Which phrase means: My name is Alex.
