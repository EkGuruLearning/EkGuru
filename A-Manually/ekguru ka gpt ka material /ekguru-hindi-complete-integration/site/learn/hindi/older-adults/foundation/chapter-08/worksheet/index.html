पहला स्वतंत्र संवाद

Foundation review

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

I am learning Hindi (male speaker).

I am learning Hindi (female speaker).

What is your name?

Nice to meet you.

2. Respond to the situation

Joining a language group.

नमस्ते, आपका नाम क्या है?

Which form would a female speaker normally use: रहा or रही?

3. Independent task

Give a one-minute introduction, ask a question, and read five simple signs without help.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मैं हिंदी सीख रहा हूँ। — I am learning Hindi (male speaker).

मैं हिंदी सीख रही हूँ। — I am learning Hindi (female speaker).

आपका नाम क्या है? — What is your name?

आपसे मिलकर खुशी हुई। — Nice to meet you.

Model reply: मेरा नाम सारा है।

रही.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
