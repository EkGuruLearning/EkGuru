STAGE 01 · SELF-CHECK

पहली मुलाकात

Read or listen, then answer before revealing the model.

मीरा आज जयपुर में हैं। वह हिंदी सीख रही हैं।Meera is in Jaipur today. She is learning Hindi.
वह एक दुकान में जाती हैं। उन्हें दो बोतल पानी चाहिए।She goes into a shop. She needs two bottles of water.
दुकानदार कहता है, एक बोतल बीस रुपये की है। मीरा कहती हैं, धीरे बोलिए।The shopkeeper says one bottle costs twenty rupees. Meera says, please speak slowly.

पठन-बोध

Comprehension
Where is Meera?

जयपुर में। In Jaipur.

How much is one bottle?

बीस रुपये। Twenty rupees.

What does she ask the shopkeeper to do?

धीरे बोलिए। Speak slowly.

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Independent task
Introduce yourself, ask a price, understand the answer and request repetition. Read the vowel and consonant charts and write five words.

Self-assessment

Can you understand the main idea, ask for clarification, respond intelligibly and complete the independent task? Ask a fluent speaker for feedback. If you need help, revisit the relevant lessons before progressing.
