CHAPTER 06 / LESSON 02

समय और दिन

Time & days · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aआप कब आएँगे?aap kab aayengeWhen will you come? (to a man)
Bआज तीन बजे।aaj teen bajeToday at three.
Aसाढ़े तीन बजे ठीक रहेगा?saadhe teen baje theek rahegaWould half past three be okay?
Bहाँ, ठीक है।haan, theek haiYes, that's fine.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
What time is साढ़े तीन?

3:30.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

आज तीन बजे।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.
