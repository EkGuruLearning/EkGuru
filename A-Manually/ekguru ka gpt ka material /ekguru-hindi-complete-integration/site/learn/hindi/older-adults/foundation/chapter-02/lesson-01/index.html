स्वर और ध्वनि

Vowels & listening · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Hindi has eleven common independent vowels. Compare short and long sounds; quality also matters. अं and अः contain signs, not additional independent vowels. Listen before copying.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases

आaaLong aa
