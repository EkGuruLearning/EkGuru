CHAPTER 25 / LESSON 01

औपचारिक और अनौपचारिक शैली

Register & politeness · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Hindi register includes pronouns, verb endings, vocabulary and context. आप is not automatically formal in every family; choose forms according to relationship and preferences.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
आप बैठिए।aap baithiyePlease sit.
तुम बैठो।tum baithoSit (familiar).
क्या आप बता सकते हैं?kya aap bata sakte hainCould you tell me?
कृपया सूचित करें।kripaya soochit kareinPlease inform (formal).

Which phrase means: Please sit.

Answer

आप बैठिए।

Which phrase means: Sit (familiar).

Answer

तुम बैठो।

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
आप बैठिए।
तुम बैठो।
