CHAPTER 30 / LESSON 01

विचार-विमर्श और असहमति

Discussion & disagreement · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

A constructive argument contains a claim, reason and relevant evidence. Acknowledge uncertainty and the strongest opposing point. Avoid attacking people.
