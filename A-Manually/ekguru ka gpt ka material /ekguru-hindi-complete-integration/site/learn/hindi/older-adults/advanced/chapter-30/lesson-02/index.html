CHAPTER 30 / LESSON 02

विचार-विमर्श और असहमति

Discussion & disagreement · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aमेरे विचार से शाम को खुलना चाहिए।mere vichaar se shaam ko khulna chaahiyeIn my view it should open in the evening.
Bलेकिन कर्मचारियों का समय भी देखना होगा।lekin karmachaariyon ka samay bhee dekhna hogaBut staff schedules also need consideration.
Aआपकी बात उचित है।aapkee baat uchit haiYour point is reasonable.
Bक्या सप्ताह में दो दिन बढ़ा सकते हैं?kya saptaah mein do din badha sakte hainCould we extend hours on two days a week?

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
What should support a claim?

A relevant reason and evidence.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

लेकिन कर्मचारियों का समय भी देखना होगा।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Argue for a fictional public service, then summarize the opposing position fairly.
