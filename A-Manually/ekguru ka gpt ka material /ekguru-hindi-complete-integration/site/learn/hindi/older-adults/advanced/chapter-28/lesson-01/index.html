CHAPTER 28 / LESSON 01

क्रिया के सूक्ष्म अर्थ

Compound verbs & aspect · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Vector verbs such as देना, लेना and जाना can add completion, direction, benefit or change of state. The nuance depends on context; do not translate every compound literally.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
लिख दिया।likh diyaWrote it (completed)
पढ़ लिया।padh liyaFinished reading
सो गया।so gayaFell asleep (male)
कह दीजिए।kah deejiyePlease tell (someone).

Answer
