समाचार और तथ्य

News & evidence

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

According to the report

It has not been confirmed.

Proposal

Decision

2. Respond to the situation

Discussing a fictional local bus proposal.

खबर में क्या बताया गया?

Is a प्रस्ताव the same as an approved decision?

3. Independent task

Summarize a fictional news story, label confirmed facts and unresolved claims.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

रिपोर्ट के अनुसार — According to the report

पुष्टि नहीं हुई है। — It has not been confirmed.

प्रस्ताव — Proposal

निर्णय — Decision

Model reply: नई बस सेवा का प्रस्ताव है।

No. It is a proposal.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
