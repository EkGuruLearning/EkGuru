CHAPTER 29 / LESSON 01

समाचार और तथ्य

News & evidence · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Separate what a report states, what a witness claims, and what has been independently confirmed. के अनुसार attributes information. कथित or बताया गया can mark an unverified claim.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
रिपोर्ट के अनुसारreport ke anusaarAccording to the report
पुष्टि नहीं हुई है।pushti nahin huee haiIt has not been confirmed.
प्रस्तावprastaavProposal
निर्णयnirnayDecision

Which phrase means: According to the report

Answer

रिपोर्ट के अनुसार

Which phrase means: It has not been confirmed.

Answer

पुष्टि नहीं हुई है।
