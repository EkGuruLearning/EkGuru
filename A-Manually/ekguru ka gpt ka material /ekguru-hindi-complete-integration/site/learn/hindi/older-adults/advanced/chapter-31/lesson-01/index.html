CHAPTER 31 / LESSON 01

कार्यालयी पत्र और ईमेल

Formal correspondence · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Use a concise subject, purpose, relevant dates, requested action and polite closing. Official terminology should be clear rather than unnecessarily ornate.
