CHAPTER 27 / LESSON 01

शर्त और संभावना

Conditions & uncertainty · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

अगर … तो expresses a condition. शायद marks uncertainty. Counterfactual होता/होती can describe an unreal or hypothetical state; distinguish from a confirmed event.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
अगर बारिश हुई तोagar baarish huee toIf it rains, then
शायद देर हो जाए।shaayad der ho jaayePerhaps there will be a delay.
संभव है किsambhav hai kiIt is possible that
अगर समय होताagar samay hotaIf there were time

Which phrase means: If it rains, then

Answer

अगर बारिश हुई तो

Which phrase means: Perhaps there will be a delay.

Answer

शायद देर हो जाए।

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
अगर बारिश हुई तो
शायद देर हो जाए।
