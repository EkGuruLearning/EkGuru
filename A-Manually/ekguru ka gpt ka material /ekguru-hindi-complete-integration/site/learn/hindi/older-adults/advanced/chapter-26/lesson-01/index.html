CHAPTER 26 / LESSON 01

जटिल वाक्य और संबंध

Complex sentences · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

जो … वह links a relative clause with a main clause. क्योंकि explains a reason, जबकि contrasts, हालांकि concedes. The connectors must match the relation.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
जो समय पर आएjo samay par aayeWhoever arrives on time
क्योंकि रास्ता बंद थाkyonki raasta band thaBecause the road was closed
हालांकि समय कम हैhaalaanki samay kam haiAlthough time is short
जिसने कहाjisne kahaWhoever said

Which phrase means: Whoever arrives on time

Answer

जो समय पर आए

Which phrase means: Because the road was closed

Answer

क्योंकि रास्ता बंद था

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
जो समय पर आए
क्योंकि रास्ता बंद था
