औपचारिक और अनौपचारिक शैली

Register & politeness

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

Please sit.

Sit (familiar).

Could you tell me?

Please inform (formal).

2. Respond to the situation

Changing register in two settings.

क्या आप मुझे समय बता सकते हैं?

Which imperative fits आप: बैठो or बैठिए?

3. Independent task

Rewrite a casual message as a respectful office request, preserving meaning.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

आप बैठिए। — Please sit.

तुम बैठो। — Sit (familiar).

क्या आप बता सकते हैं? — Could you tell me?

कृपया सूचित करें। — Please inform (formal).

Model reply: जी, चार बजे हैं।

बैठिए.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
