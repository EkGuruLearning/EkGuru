CHAPTER 18 / LESSON 02

भविष्य और योजना

Future plans · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aरविवार को क्या करेंगे?ravivaar ko kya karengeWhat will you do on Sunday?
Bहम बगीचे में जाएँगे।ham bageeche mein jaayengeWe will go to the park.
Aकितने बजे मिलें?kitne baje mileinAt what time shall we meet?
Bसुबह नौ बजे मिलेंगे।subah nau baje milengeWe will meet at nine in the morning.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Which future form suits मैं for a woman?

जाऊँगी (for जाना).

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

हम बगीचे में जाएँगे।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Plan an imaginary weekend, propose two times and agree on one.
