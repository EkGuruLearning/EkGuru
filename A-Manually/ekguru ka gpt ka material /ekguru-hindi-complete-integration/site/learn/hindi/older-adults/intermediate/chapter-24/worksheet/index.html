पठन और सुनने की रणनीति

Reading & listening strategies

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

Notice

Postponed

The time has changed.

Entry is free.

2. Respond to the situation

Reading a notice about a library event.

सूचना में क्या लिखा है?

Which detail confirms cost?

3. Independent task

Summarize a fictional notice: event, date, time, place and any missing details.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

सूचना — Notice

स्थगित — Postponed

समय बदल गया है। — The time has changed.

प्रवेश निःशुल्क है। — Entry is free.

Model reply: कार्यक्रम शुक्रवार को होगा।

प्रवेश निःशुल्क है।

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
