पठन और सुनने की रणनीति

Reading & listening strategies · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Read for purpose, date, place and required action before translating every word. In listening, use context and ask about only the missing detail.
