CHAPTER 23 / LESSON 02

निमंत्रण और संस्कृति

Invitations & social customs · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aशनिवार को हमारे घर आइएगा।shanivaar ko hamaare ghar aaiyegaPlease come to our home on Saturday.
Bबहुत धन्यवाद, कितने बजे?bahut dhanyavaad, kitne bajeThank you very much, at what time?
Aशाम सात बजे।shaam saat bajeAt seven in the evening.
Bज़रूर, मैं आऊँगी।zaroor, main aaoongeeCertainly, I'll come (female).

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
How do you decline without sounding abrupt?

धन्यवाद, इस बार नहीं आ पाऊँगा/पाऊँगी।

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

बहुत धन्यवाद, कितने बजे?
