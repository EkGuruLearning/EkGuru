CHAPTER 17 · WORKSHEET

बीते अनुभव

Past experiences

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

I went yesterday (male speaker).

I read a book.

We ate food.

She went home.

2. Respond to the situation

Talking about yesterday's trip.

कल आप कहाँ गए थे?

Why पढ़ी in मैंने किताब पढ़ी?

3. Independent task

Tell a partner about three things you did yesterday; include one intransitive and one transitive verb.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मैं कल गया था। — I went yesterday (male speaker).

मैंने किताब पढ़ी। — I read a book.

हमने खाना खाया। — We ate food.

वह घर गई। — She went home.

Model reply: मैं संग्रहालय गया था।

It agrees with the unmarked feminine object किताब.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
