CHAPTER 16 / LESSON 01

फ़ोन और संदेश

Calls & messages · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

In short messages, include the reason, time and expected response. Respectful imperatives remain useful over the phone.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
क्या मैं बाद में फ़ोन करूँ?kya main baad mein phone karoonShall I call later?
आवाज़ साफ़ नहीं आ रही।aawaaz saaf nahin aa raheeI cannot hear clearly.
संदेश भेज दीजिए।sandesh bhej deejiyePlease send a message.
मैं पाँच बजे पहुँचूँगा।main paanch baje pahoonchungaI will arrive at five (male speaker).

Which phrase means: Shall I call later?

Answer

क्या मैं बाद में फ़ोन करूँ?

Which phrase means: I cannot hear clearly.

Answer

आवाज़ साफ़ नहीं आ रही।

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
