CHAPTER 10 / LESSON 01

भोजन और रेस्तराँ

Food & restaurants · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Use मुझे … चाहिए to request something. कम and बिना specify preferences. If you have an allergy, communicate it explicitly and confirm with staff; language practice is not medical assurance.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
मेनू दीजिए।menu deejiyePlease give me the menu.
कम मिर्च डालिए।kam mirch daaliyePlease add less chilli.
मुझे मूँगफली से एलर्जी है।mujhe moongphalee se allergy haiI am allergic to peanuts.
बिल दीजिए।bil deejiyePlease give me the bill.

Which phrase means: Please give me the menu.

Answer

मेनू दीजिए।

Which phrase means: Please add less chilli.

Answer

कम मिर्च डालिए।

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
मेनू दीजिए।
कम मिर्च डालिए।
