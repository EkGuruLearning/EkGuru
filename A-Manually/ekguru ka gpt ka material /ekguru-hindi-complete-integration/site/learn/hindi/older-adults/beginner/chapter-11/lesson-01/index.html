CHAPTER 11 / LESSON 01

बाज़ार और खरीदारी

Shopping & payments · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

ये/वे can refer to plural nouns. कितना/कितनी and adjective forms depend on noun gender. Asking about the price is not the same as accepting it.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
यह कितने का है?yah kitne ka haiHow much is this?
कुछ सस्ता है?kuchh sasta haiIs there something cheaper?
मुझे रसीद चाहिए।mujhe raseed chaahiyeI need a receipt.
क्या कार्ड चलेगा?kya card chalegaWill a card be accepted?

Which phrase means: How much is this?

Answer

यह कितने का है?

Which phrase means: Is there something cheaper?

Answer

कुछ सस्ता है?
