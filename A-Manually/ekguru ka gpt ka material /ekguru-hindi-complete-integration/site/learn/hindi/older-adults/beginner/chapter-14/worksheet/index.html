स्वास्थ्य और सहायता

Health communication

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

I have a fever.

I have a headache.

I need help.

Please call a doctor.

2. Respond to the situation

At a clinic, describing a fictional symptom.

क्या परेशानी है?

How do you say you need help?

3. Independent task

Practise a fictional clinic conversation: symptom, duration, and a request to repeat instructions. Do not self-diagnose.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मुझे बुखार है। — I have a fever.

मेरे सिर में दर्द है। — I have a headache.

मुझे मदद चाहिए। — I need help.

डॉक्टर को बुलाइए। — Please call a doctor.

Model reply: मेरे गले में दर्द है।

मुझे मदद चाहिए।

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
