CHAPTER 09 / LESSON 01

घर और परिवार

Home & family · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Possessive मेरा/मेरी/मेरे agrees with the possessed noun, not the speaker. का/की/के follows the same pattern.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
मेरा घरmera gharMy home
मेरी बहनmeree bahanMy sister
मेरे माता-पिताmere maata-pitaMy parents
आप कहाँ रहते हैं?aap kahaan rahte hainWhere do you live? (to a man)

Which phrase means: My home

Answer

मेरा घर

Which phrase means: My sister

Answer

मेरी बहन

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
मेरा घर
मेरी बहन
Freehand practice, not verified stroke-order instruction.
