CHAPTER 11 / LESSON 02

बाज़ार और खरीदारी

Shopping & payments · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aयह शर्ट कितने की है?yah shirt kitne kee haiHow much is this shirt?
Bपाँच सौ रुपये की।paanch sau rupaye keeFive hundred rupees.
Aक्या दूसरा रंग है?kya doosra rang haiIs there another colour?
Bहाँ, नीला रंग भी है।haan, neela rang bhee haiYes, blue is also available.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
What word means receipt?

रसीद.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

पाँच सौ रुपये की।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Ask price, colour and payment method for a fictional item.
