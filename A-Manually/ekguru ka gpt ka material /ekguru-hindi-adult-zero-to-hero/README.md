# EkGuru Adult Hindi — जीवन की हिंदी (Zero to Hero)
A distinct, practical adult/senior self-study curriculum, including foreign learners, separate from the five academic books. This is an initial substantive draft spanning five stages, not an exhaustive 200-chapter adult program, CEFR certification or verified fluency guarantee. Age does not determine entry level. All learning is free and no account is required. Editorial review is pending.

## Actual contents
5 stages, 40 authored topic chapters, 80 sub-lessons (language patterns and practical conversation), 160 bilingual phrase entries, 40 four-turn bilingual dialogues (160 turns), 40 distinct explanations and independent roleplay tasks, 40 printable worksheets, 120 auto-feedback recognition questions, 40 open-answer language checks, 5 original stage reading assessments with 15 comprehension questions and 5 independent competency tasks, 40 local writing workspaces, 40 freehand practice areas, and 171 generated HTML pages. Script charts include 11 common independent vowels, 33 standard consonants, additional signs, common nukta and conjunct forms, 429 traditional consonant-sign practice combinations, interactive matra builder and numbers 0–100. 32 locally hosted SVG illustrations, including 5 new adult scene artworks and 27 reused from previous EkGuru books. Not every phrase has an individual illustration; art is contextual. Roman transliteration is a simple pronunciation aid, not an IPA system. Some material is explained in English to assist adult foreign beginners; English/Roman assistance can be toggled. Larger-text toggle, reduced-motion CSS, device-dependent Hindi speech synthesis, slow audio, dark mode, search, progressive navigation, local completion and writing drafts are implemented. Audio is not native recordings; no automatic pronunciation or handwriting scoring, and no verified stroke order animations.

## Preview
Extract ZIP, `cd` into extracted directory, and run `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory site`. Open `http://localhost:8000/learn/hindi/older-adults/`. All local course routes are static. Parent site links go to live EkGuru. `preview-lesson.html` is an embedded restaurant conversation lesson; internal course links are disabled in the standalone sample only.

## Rebuild
`python3 source/build_art.py`; `python3 source/build.py`; `python3 source/preview.py`. Curriculum data is in `source/curriculum.txt` (pipe-separated fields; phrase triples separated by semicolons and tildes) and `source/stage-checks.json`. Shared earlier-book artwork is bundled only where referenced. The generator creates HTML, an adult curriculum manifest and correct relative routes. Keep authoring tools private; publish only approved static output.

## Safe integration
`python3 install.py /path/to/EkGuru` (default read-only dry-run). Then, if safe, `python3 install.py /path/to/EkGuru --apply`. New and byte-identical files only; refuses conflicts and symlinks. It connects the five adult registry entries if the Hindi V1 registry is present; verify all routes before exposing them. If any existing adult path conflicts, manually merge through its owning generator. No files are deleted. New pages noindex until owner approval.
