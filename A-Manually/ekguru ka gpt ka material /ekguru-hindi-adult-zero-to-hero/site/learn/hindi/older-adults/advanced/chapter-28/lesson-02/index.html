CHAPTER 28 / LESSON 02

क्रिया के सूक्ष्म अर्थ

Compound verbs & aspect · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aक्या आपने पत्र लिख दिया?kya aapne patr likh diyaHave you written the letter?
Bहाँ, लिख दिया।haan, likh diyaYes, I have written it.
Aक्या उसे पढ़ लिया?kya use padh liyaHave you read it?
Bहाँ, अब भेज सकते हैं।haan, ab bhej sakte hainYes, we can send it now.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
What does लिया contribute in पढ़ लिया?

It commonly indicates completed reading, often for oneself.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

हाँ, लिख दिया।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Contrast पढ़ रहा हूँ, पढ़ा and पढ़ लिया in a fictional situation.
