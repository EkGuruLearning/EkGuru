CHAPTER 29 / LESSON 02

समाचार और तथ्य

News & evidence · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aखबर में क्या बताया गया?khabar mein kya bataaya gayaWhat did the news say?
Bनई बस सेवा का प्रस्ताव है।naee bas seva ka prastaav haiThere is a proposal for a new bus service.
Aक्या निर्णय हो गया है?kya nirnay ho gaya haiHas a decision been made?
Bअभी पुष्टि नहीं हुई है।abhee pushti nahin huee haiIt has not yet been confirmed.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Is a प्रस्ताव the same as an approved decision?

No. It is a proposal.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

नई बस सेवा का प्रस्ताव है।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Summarize a fictional news story, label confirmed facts and unresolved claims.
