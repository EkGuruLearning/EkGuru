CHAPTER 27 / LESSON 02

शर्त और संभावना

Conditions & uncertainty · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aअगर बारिश हुई तो क्या करेंगे?agar baarish huee to kya karengeWhat will we do if it rains?
Bहम संग्रहालय जा सकते हैं।ham sangrahaalay ja sakte hainWe can go to the museum.
Aशायद वहाँ भीड़ हो।shaayad vahaan bheed hoPerhaps it will be crowded.
Bपहले जानकारी ले लेते हैं।pahle jaankaaree le lete hainLet's check the information first.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Does शायद express certainty?

No, possibility or uncertainty.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

हम संग्रहालय जा सकते हैं।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Make a plan with two contingencies and one explicitly uncertain prediction.
