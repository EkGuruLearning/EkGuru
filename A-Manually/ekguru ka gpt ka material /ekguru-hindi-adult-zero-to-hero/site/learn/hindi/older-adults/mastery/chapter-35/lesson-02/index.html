CHAPTER 35 / LESSON 02

अनुवाद और अर्थ

Translation & nuance · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aक्या कल का अर्थ हमेशा tomorrow है?kya kal ka arth hamesha tomorrow haiDoes kal always mean tomorrow?
Bनहीं, बीता या आने वाला दिन हो सकता है।nahin, beeta ya aane vaala din ho sakta haiNo, it can be yesterday or tomorrow.
Aतो संदर्भ देखना होगा।to sandarbh dekhna hogaSo we need the context.
Bसही, तारीख लिखना बेहतर होगा।sahee, taareekh likhna behtar hogaRight, writing the date would be better.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Why confirm कल in an appointment?

It is ambiguous without context.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

नहीं, बीता या आने वाला दिन हो सकता है।
