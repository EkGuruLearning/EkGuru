CHAPTER 39 / LESSON 01

संपादन और शैली सुधार

Editing & clarity · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Edit in passes: factual accuracy, organization, clarity, then spelling and punctuation. Preserve the intended message and voice. Prefer precise verbs and concrete dates over vague expressions.
