CHAPTER 37 / LESSON 02

प्रमाण और स्रोत

Research & source evaluation · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aकितने लोगों से पूछा गया?kitne logon se poochha gayaHow many people were asked?
Bकेवल बीस लोगों से।keval bees logon seOnly twenty people.
Aक्या पूरे शहर के बारे में कह सकते हैं?kya poore shahar ke baare mein kah sakte hainCan we generalize to the whole city?
Bनहीं, नमूने की सीमा बतानी होगी।nahin, namoone kee seema bataanee hogeeNo, we need to state the sample limitation.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Why mention the sample size?

It limits what conclusions are supported.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

केवल बीस लोगों से।
