CHAPTER 38 / LESSON 01

व्यावसायिक बातचीत और समझौता

Negotiation & agreement · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Separate a proposal from agreement; confirm scope, cost and time in writing. Conditional phrases can make tradeoffs explicit. This is language practice, not legal advice.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
शर्तshartCondition
प्रस्ताव स्वीकार है।prastaav sveekaar haiThe proposal is accepted.
इस बिंदु पर सहमति नहीं है।is bindu par sahamati nahin haiThere is no agreement on this point.
