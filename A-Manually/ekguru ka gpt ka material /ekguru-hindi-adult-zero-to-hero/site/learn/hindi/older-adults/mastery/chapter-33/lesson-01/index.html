CHAPTER 33 / LESSON 01

सारांश और पुनर्कथन

Summaries & paraphrase · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

A summary compresses main ideas without adding unsupported facts. A paraphrase preserves meaning in different wording. Attribute another writer's distinctive ideas even when paraphrased.
