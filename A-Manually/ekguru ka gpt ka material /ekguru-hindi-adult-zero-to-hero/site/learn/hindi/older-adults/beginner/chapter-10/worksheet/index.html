भोजन और रेस्तराँ

Food & restaurants

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

Please give me the menu.

Please add less chilli.

I am allergic to peanuts.

Please give me the bill.

2. Respond to the situation

Ordering a meal.

आप क्या लेंगे?

How do you request less chilli?

3. Independent task

Order two dishes, request a change and ask for the bill.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मेनू दीजिए। — Please give me the menu.

कम मिर्च डालिए। — Please add less chilli.

मुझे मूँगफली से एलर्जी है। — I am allergic to peanuts.

बिल दीजिए। — Please give me the bill.

Model reply: दाल और रोटी दीजिए।

कम मिर्च डालिए।

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
