बाज़ार और खरीदारी

Shopping & payments

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

How much is this?

Is there something cheaper?

I need a receipt.

Will a card be accepted?

2. Respond to the situation

Purchasing clothes.

यह शर्ट कितने की है?

What word means receipt?

3. Independent task

Ask price, colour and payment method for a fictional item.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

यह कितने का है? — How much is this?

कुछ सस्ता है? — Is there something cheaper?

मुझे रसीद चाहिए। — I need a receipt.

क्या कार्ड चलेगा? — Will a card be accepted?

Model reply: पाँच सौ रुपये की।

रसीद.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
