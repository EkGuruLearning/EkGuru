CHAPTER 15 · WORKSHEET

रोज़ की दिनचर्या

Daily routine

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

I get up in the morning (male speaker).

I drink tea (female speaker).

I am reading now (male speaker).

She works.

2. Respond to the situation

Chatting about a normal day.

आप सुबह क्या करते हैं?

Which phrase describes action happening now?

3. Independent task

Describe your routine with four actions, then say what you are doing right now.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मैं सुबह उठता हूँ। — I get up in the morning (male speaker).

मैं चाय पीती हूँ। — I drink tea (female speaker).

मैं अभी पढ़ रहा हूँ। — I am reading now (male speaker).

वह काम करती है। — She works.

Model reply: मैं टहलने जाता हूँ।

मैं अभी पढ़ रहा हूँ।

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
