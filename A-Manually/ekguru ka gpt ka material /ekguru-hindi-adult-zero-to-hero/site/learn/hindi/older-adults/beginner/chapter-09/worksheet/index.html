CHAPTER 09 · WORKSHEET

घर और परिवार

Home & family

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

My home

My sister

My parents

Where do you live? (to a man)

2. Respond to the situation

A neighbour asks about your home.

आप कहाँ रहते हैं?

Why मेरी बहन rather than मेरा बहन?

3. Independent task

Describe a fictional family with three possessive phrases.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मेरा घर — My home

मेरी बहन — My sister

मेरे माता-पिता — My parents

आप कहाँ रहते हैं? — Where do you live? (to a man)

Model reply: मैं जयपुर में रहता हूँ।

बहन is feminine.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
