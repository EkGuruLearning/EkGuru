CHAPTER 15 / LESSON 02

रोज़ की दिनचर्या

Daily routine · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aआप सुबह क्या करते हैं?aap subah kya karte hainWhat do you do in the morning?
Bमैं टहलने जाता हूँ।main tahalne jaata hoonI go for a walk (male speaker).
Aफिर आप क्या करते हैं?phir aap kya karte hainWhat do you do afterwards?
Bमैं नाश्ता करता हूँ।main naashta karta hoonI have breakfast.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Which phrase describes action happening now?

मैं अभी पढ़ रहा हूँ।

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

मैं टहलने जाता हूँ।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Describe your routine with four actions, then say what you are doing right now.
