CHAPTER 12 / LESSON 02

रास्ता और यातायात

Directions & transport · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aमाफ़ कीजिए, स्टेशन कहाँ है?maaf keejiye, station kahaan haiExcuse me, where is the station?
Bसीधे जाइए, फिर दाएँ मुड़िए।seedhe jaiye, phir daayen mudiyeGo straight, then turn right.
Aक्या पैदल जा सकते हैं?kya paidal ja sakte hainCan we walk there?
Bहाँ, पाँच मिनट लगेंगे।haan, paanch minute lagengeYes, it will take five minutes.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
What direction does दाएँ mean?

Right.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

सीधे जाइए, फिर दाएँ मुड़िए।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Draw a simple fictional route and give three instructions to reach a station.
