CHAPTER 14 / LESSON 02

स्वास्थ्य और सहायता

Health communication · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aक्या परेशानी है?kya pareshaanee haiWhat is the problem?
Bमेरे गले में दर्द है।mere gale mein dard haiI have a sore throat.
Aकब से?kab seSince when?
Bकल से।kal seSince yesterday.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
How do you say you need help?

मुझे मदद चाहिए।

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

मेरे गले में दर्द है।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Practise a fictional clinic conversation: symptom, duration, and a request to repeat instructions. Do not self-diagnose.
