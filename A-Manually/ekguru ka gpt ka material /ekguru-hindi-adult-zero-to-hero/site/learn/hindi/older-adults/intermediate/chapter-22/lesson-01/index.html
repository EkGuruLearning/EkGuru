CHAPTER 22 / LESSON 01

कार्यस्थल की हिंदी

Workplace conversations · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Use आप with colleagues unless a different convention is agreed. Confirm ownership and deadlines explicitly. Passive language can conceal who must act.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases

मैं रिपोर्ट भेज दूँगा।main report bhej doongaI will send the report (male speaker).
