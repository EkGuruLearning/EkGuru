CHAPTER 24 / LESSON 02

पठन और सुनने की रणनीति

Reading & listening strategies · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aसूचना में क्या लिखा है?soochna mein kya likha haiWhat does the notice say?
Bकार्यक्रम शुक्रवार को होगा।kaaryakram shukravaar ko hogaThe event will take place Friday.
Aक्या प्रवेश शुल्क है?kya pravesh shulk haiIs there an entry fee?
Bनहीं, प्रवेश निःशुल्क है।nahin, pravesh nihshulk haiNo, entry is free.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Which detail confirms cost?

प्रवेश निःशुल्क है।

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

कार्यक्रम शुक्रवार को होगा।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Summarize a fictional notice: event, date, time, place and any missing details.
