CHAPTER 19 · WORKSHEET

भावनाएँ और राय

Feelings & opinions

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

I am happy.

I am worried.

In my opinion

I do not agree.

2. Respond to the situation

Discussing a community event.

आपको कार्यक्रम कैसा लगा?

Does मुझे लगता है कि mark certainty?

3. Independent task

Express a preference, explain why and politely acknowledge a different opinion.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

मैं खुश हूँ। — I am happy.

मुझे चिंता है। — I am worried.

मेरे विचार से — In my opinion

मैं सहमत नहीं हूँ। — I do not agree.

Model reply: मुझे अच्छा लगा, लेकिन आवाज़ तेज़ थी।

No, it introduces the speaker's view or impression.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
