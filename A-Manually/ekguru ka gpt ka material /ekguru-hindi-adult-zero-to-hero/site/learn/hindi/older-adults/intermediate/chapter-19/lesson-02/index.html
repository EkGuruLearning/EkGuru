CHAPTER 19 / LESSON 02

भावनाएँ और राय

Feelings & opinions · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aआपको कार्यक्रम कैसा लगा?aapko kaaryakram kaisa lagaHow did you find the event?
Bमुझे अच्छा लगा, लेकिन आवाज़ तेज़ थी।mujhe achchha laga, lekin aawaaz tez theeI liked it, but the sound was loud.
Aमैं आपकी बात समझता हूँ।main aapkee baat samajhta hoonI understand your point (male speaker).
Bअगली बार हम सुझाव देंगे।aglee baar ham sujhaav dengeNext time we will offer a suggestion.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Does मुझे लगता है कि mark certainty?

No, it introduces the speaker's view or impression.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

मुझे अच्छा लगा, लेकिन आवाज़ तेज़ थी।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Express a preference, explain why and politely acknowledge a different opinion.
