CHAPTER 18 / LESSON 01

भविष्य और योजना

Future plans · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Future endings change by person, gender and number: जाऊँगा/जाऊँगी, जाएँगे/जाएँगी. चाहना expresses intention or desire; the future may also express prediction.

उपयोगी वाक्य

Useful phrases
मैं कल आऊँगी।main kal aaoongeeI will come tomorrow (female speaker).
हम मिलेंगे।ham milengeWe will meet.
आप कब जाएँगे?aap kab jaayengeWhen will you go?
मैं जाना चाहता हूँ।main jaana chaahta hoonI want to go (male speaker).

Which phrase means: I will come tomorrow (female speaker).

Answer

मैं कल आऊँगी।

Which phrase means: We will meet.

Answer

हम मिलेंगे।

लिखें

Copy and read aloud
मैं कल आऊँगी।
हम मिलेंगे।
