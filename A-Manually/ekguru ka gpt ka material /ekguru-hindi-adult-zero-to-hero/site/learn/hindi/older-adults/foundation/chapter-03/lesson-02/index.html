CHAPTER 03 / LESSON 02

व्यंजन और उच्चारण

Consonants & pronunciation · Use it in conversation

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

बातचीत

Listen and roleplay
Aक और ख में क्या अंतर है?ka aur kha mein kya antar haiWhat is the difference between ka and kha?
Bख में हवा ज़्यादा निकलती है।kha mein hawa zyaada nikalti haiMore air comes out with kha.
Aधीरे बोलिए।dheere boliyePlease speak slowly.
Bक, ख।ka, khaKa, kha.

समझ जाँचें

Check understanding
Is त the same sound as ट?

No. त is dental; ट is retroflex.

Which reply follows the first line of this model dialogue?

Answer

ख में हवा ज़्यादा निकलती है।

स्वतंत्र अभ्यास

Transfer to a new situation

Read both roles. Then cover the English, take role B and respond aloud. Change the details for a new conversation.

Compare क/ख and त/ट. Hold your palm near your mouth to feel aspiration, then copy the chart.
