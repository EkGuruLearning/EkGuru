मात्राएँ और अक्षर

Matras & script combinations · Learn the pattern

Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Dependent vowel signs attach to consonants. The sign ि is written to the left of its consonant but pronounced after it. Halant suppresses the inherent vowel; anusvara, chandrabindu and visarga are distinct signs.
