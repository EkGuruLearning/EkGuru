Device Hindi voice required; not a native recording.

भाषा समझें

Understand the pattern

Consonants typically have an inherent vowel in isolation. Dental त and retroflex ट have different tongue positions; ख differs from क in aspiration. Use the chart to compare all 33 standard consonants.
