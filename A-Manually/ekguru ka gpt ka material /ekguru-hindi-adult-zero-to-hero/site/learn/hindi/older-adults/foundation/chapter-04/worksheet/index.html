Matras & script combinations

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________

1. Write the Hindi phrase

Ka with aa

Ka with i

Ka with u

Ka with e

2. Respond to the situation

Read a short sign with a partner.

इसमें कौन-सी मात्रा है?

Is the i vowel in कि pronounced before क?

3. Independent task

Use the vowel-sign builder, then write किताब, पानी, स्कूल and घर.

Model answers (open before printing if needed)

का — Ka with aa

कि — Ka with i

कु — Ka with u

के — Ka with e

Model reply: इसमें इ की मात्रा है।

No. It is pronounced after the consonant.

Independent response varies; review accuracy, clarity and appropriateness.
