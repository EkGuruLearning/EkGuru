Questions about this course

Must I learn the Kannada script before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Kannada in Roman letters, and let the script come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ for respect, ಹೌದು and ಇಲ್ಲ, ಧನ್ಯವಾದ, ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು, ಸರಿ, and ನಾನು — I. With those seven you can open any interaction in Karnataka politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Kannada?

The retroflex family new to most ears — ಳ, ಣ, long-short vowel pairs, rolled ರ. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each one.
