Kannada verbs

ಹೋಗು, ಬಾ, ತಿನ್ನು — twelve verbs that run daily life, and how they bend.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Twelve verbs carry daily life: ಹೋಗು (go), ಬಾ (come), ತಿನ್ನು (eat), ಕುಡಿ (drink), ಓದು (read/study), ಬರಿ (write), ಮಾಡು (do), ಮಾತಾಡು (speak), ಕೊಡು (give), ತೆಗೆದುಕೋ (take), ಇರು (be/stay), ನೋಡು (see). Memorise these stems and you can order food, find toilets, make friends and survive beautifully.

Stems bend by person in the present: ನಾನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (I go), ನೀನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೀಯ (you familiar go), ನೀವು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೀರಿ (you formal go), ಅವನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ (he goes). The -ಉತ್ತ- (utta) infix marks present tense across nearly every verb — one infix, hundreds of verbs, instant sentences.

Polite commands add -ಇ: ಹೋಗಿ (please go), ಬನ್ನಿ (please come), ತಿನ್ನಿ (please eat), ಹೇಳಿ (please say). This one ending turns every order into an invitation — it is the most useful suffix in Kannada, worth more than a hundred nouns.

Negation and existence close the kit: ಇದೆ (ide — it is / there is) anchors locations — ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಮೇಜಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಇದೆ (the book is on the table). ಇಲ್ಲ (illa — there isn't) removes — ಹಣ ಇಲ್ಲ (no money). Twelve verbs plus ಇದೆ/ಇಲ್ಲ: the engine room of Kannada.

A note on ಬನ್ನಿ vs ಬಾ. ಬಾ (baa) orders a friend to come; ಬನ್ನಿ (banni) welcomes a guest like royalty. Same verb, different universe — the -ಇ ending is pure respect. When in doubt, add the respect: Kannada people forgive stiff politeness instantly and casual rudeness never.

Questions learners ask

Are verb stems commands?

Stems are orders to friends — ಹೋಗು! (go!), ಬಾ! (come!), ತಿನ್ನು! (eat!). Polite speech adds endings: ಹೋಗಿ (please go), ಬನ್ನಿ (please come). Learn stems first, politeness second, and never order a stranger.

How do Kannada verbs change?

ನಾನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (I go), ನೀನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೀಯ (you go), ಅವನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ (he goes). Person-markers on every verb — drill one verb through all persons and the pattern owns you.

How do I say no to a verb?

ಇಲ್ಲ (illa — no/not) negates existence — ನನಗೆ ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ uses -ಇಲ್ಲ for verbs (I don't know). Kannada negation splits by word type; learn ಇಲ್ಲ for things, ಬೇಡ (beda — don't want) for refusals.
