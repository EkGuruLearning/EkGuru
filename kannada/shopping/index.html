Shopping and bargaining in Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Prices, bargaining and colours — shop in Kannada from first price to deal.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Prices open every transaction: ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (what's the price?), ಎಷ್ಟು? (how much?), ಇದು ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (what's this one's price?). Listen for the number plus ರೂಪಾಯಿ — repeat it back to confirm — ಐವತ್ತು? (fifty?) — and you are already shopping in Kannada.

Bargaining follows the friendly script: ತುಂಬಾ ದುಬಾರಿ (too costly), ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ (reduce a little), ಡಿಸ್ಕೌಂಟ್ ಇದೆಯಾ? (any discount?). Start walking away slowly — ಧನ್ಯವಾದ, ನೋಡ್ತೀನಿ (thanks, I'll look around) — and the real price often chases you down the street.

Sizes and colours refine the hunt: ದೊಡ್ಡದು (the bigger one), ಚಿಕ್ಕದು (the smaller one), ಹೊಸದು (a new one), ಬೇರೆ ಬಣ್ಣ (a different colour). Point, name the change, nod — Kannada shopkeepers read gestures fluently and reward attempts instantly.

Colours close the sale: ಕೆಂಪು (red), ನೀಲಿ (blue), ಹಸಿರು (green), ಹಳದಿ (yellow), ಬಿಳಿ (white), ಕಪ್ಪು (black). ಕೆಂಪು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ (the red looks good) — then ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತೇನೆ (I'll take it). Deal done, in Kannada, with a smile on both sides.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ. ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ (swalpa kadime maadi — please reduce a little) is the magic sentence of Karnataka markets. Said smiling, it opens every negotiation; said with ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತೇನೆ (I'll take it) right after, it closes most. Bargaining respects the dance — never the demand.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask the price?

ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (what's the price?) — then ಎಷ್ಟು? (how much?) for everything else. Two questions, every shop in Karnataka.

How do I bargain politely?

ತುಂಬಾ ದುಬಾರಿ (too costly) with a smile, then ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ (reduce a little, please). Bargaining is friendly theatre — play your part warmly and the price moves.

Which colours first?

Start with five: ಕೆಂಪು (red), ನೀಲಿ (blue), ಹಸಿರು (green), ಹಳದಿ (yellow), ಬಿಳಿ (white). ಬೇರೆ ಬಣ್ಣ (different colour) shows them all.
