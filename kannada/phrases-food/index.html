Food phrases in Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Dose, idli, bisibelebaat — order like a local, from breakfast to bill.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Breakfast is the glory: ದೋಸೆ (dose — crisp crepe), ಇಡ್ಲಿ (idli — steamed cakes), ಪೂರಿ (poori — puffed bread), ವಡೆ (vade — savoury doughnut), all served with ಸಾಂಬಾರು (saambaaru) and ಚಟ್ನಿ (chutney). ಟಿಫಿನ್ ಮಾಡಿದ್ರಾ? (have you had tiffin?) is asked with love — answer honestly, then accept seconds.

Meals centre on ಊಟ (oota — the rice meal): ಅನ್ನ (anna — rice), ಸಾಂಬಾರು, ಸಾರು (saaru — pepper-tamarind soup), ಮೊಸರು (mosaru — curd), ಹಪ್ಪಳ (happala — crisp papad). Served on banana leaves at weddings, eaten with the right hand — ಊಟ ರೆಡೀನಾ? (is the meal ready?) is the happiest question.

Taste words steer every order: ಖಾರ (khaara — spicy), ಸಿಹಿ (sihi — sweet), ಹುಳಿ (huli — sour), ಉಪ್ಪು (uppu — salt). ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಉಪ್ಪು (a little salt), ಸಕ್ಕರೆ ಬೇಡ (no sugar) — with taste words, your plate obeys you.

Drinks and bills close the meal: ಕಾಫಿ (filter coffee), ಟೀ (tea), ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ (majjige — buttermilk), ಹಣ್ಣಿನ ರಸ (hannina rasa — juice). ಬಿಲ್ ಕೊಡಿ (please give the bill) ends it; ರುಚಿಯಾಗಿತ್ತು (it was delicious) guarantees a smile on your way out.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಬಿಸಿಬೇಳೆಬಾತ್. Bisibelebaat — hot lentil-rice, vegetables, ghee melting on top — is Karnataka on a plate. Order it at any darshini, mix with potato chips and boondi, and understand the state in one meal.

Questions learners ask

How do I order with almost no Kannada?

Point and say ಇದು ಒಂದು (idu ondu — one of these) — it works in every darshini, canteen and bakery in Karnataka. Add ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು (please) and a smile; grammar can wait until dessert.

How do I ask for less spicy food?

ಖಾರ ಕಮ್ಮಿ (khaara kammi — less spicy, please). Kannada food honours chilli deeply; this one sentence saves sensitive stomachs while keeping every flavour.

How do I praise the food?

ರುಚಿಯಾಗಿದೆ (ruchiyaagide — it's delicious). Say it to the cook, the server, the ajji who packed your lunch — it is the highest compliment in Kannada food culture.
