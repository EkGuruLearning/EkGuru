Kannada sentence structure

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Trace a Kannada sentence one role at a time, then notice how its verb completes the thought in the examples.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Simple sentences stack three blocks: ನಾನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (I — to-school — go), ಅವಳು ಹಾಡು ಹಾಡುತ್ತಾಳೆ (she — song — sings), ನಾವು ಕನ್ನಡ ಓದುತ್ತೇವೆ (we — Kannada — study). Subject, object, verb — build a hundred of these and Kannada skeleton is yours.

Animals make perfect practice: ಹುಡುಗ ನಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದಾನೆ (the boy laughs), ನಾಯಿ ಬೊಗಳುತ್ತಿದೆ (the dog barks), ಬೆಕ್ಕು ಮಲಗಿದೆ (the cat sleeps). Short subjects, vivid verbs, zero ambiguity — describe every creature you meet and SOV becomes reflex.

Time flexes the frame: ನಾನು ನಾಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ (I'll come tomorrow), ನಿನ್ನೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಂತು (it rained yesterday). Time words float — front for emphasis, middle for flow — while the verb holds the end. Tense plus time word: past, present, future, mastered.

Identity and questions close the set: ಇದು ನನ್ನ ಮನೆ (this is my house), ಅದು ನಿನ್ನ ಪುಸ್ತಕನಾ? (is that your book?), ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ (all is well). Equate with ಇದು/ಅದು, question with -ಆ, reassure with ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ — sentences for every situation.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಮಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತಿದೆ. Kannada weather does things: ಮಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತಿದೆ (rain comes), ಬಿಸಿಲು ಹೊಡೆಯುತ್ತಿದೆ (sun strikes), ಗಾಳಿ ಬೀಸುತ್ತಿದೆ (wind blows). No empty it — Kannada skies act. Weather sentences teach SOV with zero confusion: subject, then verb, done.

Questions learners ask

What is Kannada word order?

Subject-Object-Verb: ನಾನು ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಓದುತ್ತೇನೆ (I book read). Endings mark roles, so order flexes for emphasis — but verb-last is home.

How do tenses work?

Tense lives on the verb: ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (go-present), ಹೋದೆ (went), ಹೋಗುವೆ. Time words — ನಿನ್ನೆ, ಇಂದು, ನಾಳೆ — frame it; the verb carries it.

How do I ask questions?

Add -ಆ and rise: ಅದು ನಿನ್ನ ಪುಸ್ತಕನಾ? (is that your book?). Question words — ಏನು, ಎಲ್ಲಿ, ಯಾರು — sit where the answer would.
