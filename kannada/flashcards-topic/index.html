Flashcards by topic in Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Use Kannada topic decks for spaced retrieval: a few well-timed reviews beat one long card session each week.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Cards carry memory: ಕಾರ್ಡ್ (card) — one word, one side; meaning, the other. ನೆನಪು (memory) strengthens with every recall; ಮರೆವು (forgetting) is just unreviewed memory. Build decks per topic — greetings, food, verbs — and review each separately.

Spacing schedules success: ಅಂತರ (interval) — review new cards tomorrow, then in three days, seven, thirty. ಮತ್ತೆ ಮತ್ತೆ (again and again), but wider each time. Cramming fills hours; spacing fills years.

Rhythms sustain streaks: ದಿನವೂ (daily) quick decks, ವಾರಕ್ಕೊಮ್ಮೆ (weekly) full reviews, ತಿಂಗಳಿಗೊಮ್ಮೆ (monthly) championships. Ten daily minutes beat Sunday marathons; streaks turn study into ಅಭ್ಯಾಸ (habit).

Scores gamify growth: ಅಂಕಗಳು (marks), ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆ (contest), ಗೆಲುವು (victory) — even ಸೋಲು (defeat) teaches. Quiz yourself, beat yesterday's score, celebrate loudly. Flashcards are games that pay in fluency.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಪುನರಾವರ್ತನೆ. ಪುನರಾವರ್ತನೆ (punaraavartane — revision, repeating-again) is Kannada's own word for spaced review — recalling again, and again, at widening intervals. The language itself teaches the method: revisit, widen, remember.

Questions learners ask

How often should I review?

ಮತ್ತೆ ಮತ್ತೆ (again and again) — but spaced: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 30 days. ಅಂತರ (spacing) beats cramming; ನೆನಪು (memory) loves intervals.

How many cards per topic?

Five to ten per topic — quality over quantity. Master one ಕಾರ್ಡ್ deck fully before opening the next; half-known decks rot.

Daily or weekly review?

ದಿನವೂ (daily) short sessions — ten minutes, every day, no exceptions. Streaks compound; ಮರೆವು (forgetting) loses to routine.
