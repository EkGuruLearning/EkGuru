Kannada

The Kannada alphabet

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Vowels, consonants, ottaksharas and the curly ಳ — the whole alphabet, kindly explained.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Kannada vowels (ಸ್ವರಗಳು) come in thirteen: ಅ ಆ ಇ ಈ ಉ ಊ ಋ ಎ ಏ ಐ ಒ ಓ ಔ ಅಂ ಅಃ. Short and long pairs — ಅ/ಆ, ಇ/ಈ, ಉ/ಊ — change meaning completely: ಕಲ (kala — learn!) versus ಕಾಲ (kaala — time/leg). Length is meaning in Kannada; stretch with care.

Consonants (ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು) run five rows deep: ಕ ಖ ಗ ಘ ಙ, ಚ ಛ ಜ ಝ ಞ, ಟ ಠ ಡ ಢ ಣ, ತ ಥ ದ ಧ ನ, ಪ ಫ ಬ ಭ ಮ — plus ಯ ರ ಲ ವ ಶ ಷ ಸ ಹ ಳ. Round, curly, friendly — the script that looks like Telugu's twin and sounds like music.

Vowels and consonants marry into ಗುಣಿತಾಕ್ಷರಗಳು (gunitaksharas): ಕ್ + ಅ = ಕ (ka), ಕ್ + ಆ = ಕಾ (kaa). Learn the vowel-signs once and every consonant sings the same song — the system is vast but perfectly regular.

Then the crown jewels: ಳ (lla), the retroflex liquid — ಬೆಳಕು (light), ಕಳು (send); and ಣ (nna), the retroflex nasal — ಕಣ್ಣು (eye). Natives will grin when you land them; the grin is worth weeks of practice.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ (ottakshara). Kannada stacks consonants into ottaksharas: ಕ್ + ಷ = ಕ್ಷ, ಜ್ + ಞ = ಜ್ಞ. The second consonant tucks underneath the first — once you see the pattern, stacked letters read themselves. One look down, and the whole conjunct system clicks.

Questions learners ask

How many letters does Kannada have?

Thirteen vowels (ಸ್ವರಗಳು) and thirty-four consonants (ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು) — 47+ letters that combine into ottaksharas. Sounds huge, behaves simply: learn the shapes and the combinations assemble themselves.

What is the hardest Kannada letter?

ಳ (lla) — the retroflex liquid, curled back: ಬೆಳಕು, ಕಳಸ. Then the ottaksharalu stacks — ಕ್ಷ, ಜ್ಞ, ತ್ರ. Listen, curl, practise: they arrive within weeks.

Does Kannada have kh, gh, bh sounds?

Yes — in writing: ಖ ಘ ಛ ಝ ಠ ಢ ಥ ಧ ಫ ಭ appear in Sanskrit loans (ಕ್ಷಮೆ, ಧರ್ಮ). Daily Kannada softens most of them; recognise them in print, relax in speech.
