Kannada

Kannada pronunciation

ಳ, ಶ-ಷ-ಸ, ottaksharas — every tricky sound, one by one, with examples.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The three sibilants sort by tongue: ಶ (palatal — ಶಾಲೆ, school), ಷ (retroflex — ಕಷ್ಟ, difficulty), ಸ (dental — ಸಮಯ, time). Say sha-sha-sa sliding the tongue from high to curled to teeth — three letters, one glide.

The liquid ಳ curls back: ಬೆಳಕು (belaku — light), ಕಳುಹಿಸು (kaluhisu — send). Contrast ಲ (dental, tongue on teeth) with ಳ (retroflex, curled). Glide la-lla daily; the roof-touch becomes reflex.

Dental versus retroflex stops: ತ (teeth — ತಾಯಿ, mother) against ಟ (roof — ಕೋಟೆ, fort). Hold a finger to your lips: dentals puff forward, retroflexes pop upward. Ten minutes of ತ-ಟ alternation retunes the mouth within a week.

The rest behave kindly: ಚ is cha at word start (ಚಂದ) softening inside words, ರ rolls lightly (ಮರ), and long vowels simply hold double — ಆ, ಈ, ಊ. Kannada spelling mirrors speech faithfully: pronounce what you see, and you are right nine times in ten.

Questions learners ask

Why three different s sounds?

ಶ (palatal, tongue high — ಶಾಲೆ), ಷ (retroflex, curled — ಕಷ್ಟ), ಸ (dental, on teeth — ಸಮಯ). Three s-sounds where English has one; Kannada ears hear all three instantly.

How do I make the ಳ sound?

ಳ is retroflex — tongue curled back: ಬೆಳಕು (light), ಕಳುಹಿಸು (send). ಲ is dental — tongue on teeth. Say la-la sliding tongue back and feel the shift.

What are those stacked letters?

Ottaksharas: ಕ್ಷ (ksha — ಕ್ಷಮೆ), ಜ್ಞ (jna — ಜ್ಞಾನ), ತ್ರ (tra). Pronounce each part quickly in sequence — k+sha, j+na. Ottaksharas look fierce and behave kindly.
