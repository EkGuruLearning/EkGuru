Kannada vs Telugu

Twin scripts, sibling sounds — neighbours for 1000 years.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The siblings grew side by side: ಕನ್ನಡ west in ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ, తెలుగు (Telugu) east in Andhra-Telangana. Same Dravidian mother — verb-final grammar, -ಗೆ/-కు wanting, elder-younger siblings — and scripts so alike that learners mix them for weeks.

Shared words delight learners: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ/నమస్కారం (hello), ಅಮ್ಮ/అమ్మ (mother), similar pronouns, matching numbers. Core vocabulary overlaps enormously; learn one list, own two languages' hearts.

Split sounds mark the border: Kannada ನಾನು against Telugu నేను (I), ನೀವು against మీరు, ಹೌದು against అవును. Pronouns and yes-no drifted most — precisely the words learners say most. Drill the pairs; cross the border daily.

Scripts twin but speech parts: ಕನ್ನಡ ಲಿಪಿ and తెలుగు లిపి share letter-shapes and ottakshara logic — yet a Kannada film needs subtitles in Hyderabad. Sisters recognise sisters on paper; ears take longer. Learn either with love and both reward you.

Questions learners ask

Are the scripts really alike?

Scripts read similarly — ಅಮ್ಮ/అమ్మ, similar letter-shapes everywhere. Speech differs more: shared roots, drifted sounds. Learn one script, peek into both.

How many words are shared?

Hundreds: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ/నమస్కారం, ಅಮ್ಮ/అమ్మ, similar numbers, matching grammar. Core vocabulary overlaps enormously; learn one list, own two hearts.

Which should I learn first?

Kannada first for structure, Telugu ears second for music. Each language halves the other's difficulty — learn one, get the second at a discount.
