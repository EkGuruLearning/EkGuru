Kannada

Kannada for absolute beginners

Your first Kannada week: yes, no, I, you, and sentences that survive anything.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Week one needs eleven words: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ (hello), ಹೌದು (yes), ಸರಿ (okay), ಧನ್ಯವಾದ (thanks), ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು (please), ನಾನು (I), ನೀವು (you, formal), ಇದು ಏನು? (what is this?), ಅರ್ಥವಾಯಿತು (I understand), ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ (I don't know), ಹೋಗೋಣ (let's go). Eleven words, and you can greet, agree, thank, ask, confess ignorance and move — the whole beginner game.

Pronouns branch early: ನಾನು (I), ನೀವು (you formal/plural), ನೀನು (you familiar), ಅವನು/ಅವಳು (he/she), ಅವರು (they formal), ನಾವು (we). ನಾವು ಹೋಗೋಣ (let's go, all of us) is Kannada's warmest invitation.

Quantity words multiply everything: ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ (a little), ತುಂಬಾ (a lot), ಸಾಕು (enough), ಮಾತ್ರ (only). ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತಾಡ್ತೀನಿ (I speak a little Kannada) is the perfect learner's confession — humble, charming, instantly rewarded with help.

Action invitations close week one: ಇಲ್ಲಿಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ (come here), ಕೂತ್ಕೊಳ್ಳಿ (please sit), ತಿನ್ನಿ (please eat), ಹೋಗೋಣ (let's go). These four run hospitality, the beating heart of Kannada culture — learn them and you stop being a guest and start being family.

A note on ಹೌದು, ಸರಿ and ಹೂಂ. Kannada yes comes in three flavours: ಹೌದು (haudu — respectful yes), ಸರಿ (sari — okay/agreed), ಹೂಂ (hoom — casual yeah). Learners live on ಹೌದು and ಸರಿ — respectful, warm, never wrong. Save ಹೂಂ for friends.

Questions learners ask

What are the first five words?

ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ಹೌದು, ಸರಿ, ಧನ್ಯವಾದ, ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು — five words, infinite mileage. Add ನಾನು and ನೀವು and you can introduce yourself to anyone in Karnataka.

How much should I study daily?

Fifteen focused minutes beat two distracted hours. One topic page, every speaker tapped, five words reviewed — daily beats heroic.

Must I learn the script first?

No — speak first, script second. Roman letters carry you through months of conversation; the alphabet page waits patiently until curiosity pulls you in.
