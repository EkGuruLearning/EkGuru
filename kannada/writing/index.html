Writing Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Letters to lists to letters — writing Kannada from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with strokes: Kannada letters curve and loop — ಬರೆಯಿರಿ (please write) circles, hooks and waves before full letters. Trace ಅ ಆ ಇ ಈ ಉ ಊ big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: ಲೇಖನಿ (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Kannada children trace in rice, sand and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: ಪಟ್ಟಿ (list) — ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ (notes), ವಿಳಾಸ (addresses), ದಿನಾಂಕ (dates). Copy your own ಹೆಸರು (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: ಪತ್ರ (letters), ಅರ್ಜಿ (applications), ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ (exams), ಮನೆಕೆಲಸ (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Kannada — short, joyful, posted. First posted Kannada is never forgotten.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಸಹಿ. Your ಸಹಿ (signature) in Kannada script is a milestone — practise it until it flows in one confident motion. Signing Kannada-style on forms, cards and letters declares belonging more than any certificate.

Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Kannada?

Round strokes before all: Kannada letters flow circular — ಅ, ಆ, ಉ, ಎ — so practise circles and hooks first. Trace big, shrink slowly; ಕೈಬರಹ (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

ಪಟ್ಟಿ (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

ಪತ್ರ (letters) to friends, ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ (notes) to self, ಮನೆಕೆಲಸ (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
