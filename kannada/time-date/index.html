Time and dates in Kannada

Seven days, four day-parts, weeks and months — Kannada time fully told.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The week runs Sunday-first: ಭಾನುವಾರ (Sunday), ಸೋಮವಾರ (Monday), ಮಂಗಳವಾರ (Tuesday), ಬುಧವಾರ (Wednesday), ಗುರುವಾರ (Thursday), ಶುಕ್ರವಾರ (Friday), ಶನಿವಾರ (Saturday). Planet-named like most of Asia — the sky keeps Kannada's calendar, and every day ends in ವಾರ.

Day-parts steer greetings and meals: ಬೆಳಿಗ್ಗೆ (morning — ಶುಭೋದಯ), ಮಧ್ಯಾಹ್ನ (afternoon — lunch/oota o'clock), ಸಂಜೆ (evening — ಶುಭಸಂಜೆ, coffee round two), ರಾತ್ರಿ (night — goodnight is usually just ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ with a smile).

Bigger units plan life: ವಾರ (week), ತಿಂಗಳು (month), ವರ್ಷ (year) — ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾರ (next week), ಕಳೆದ ತಿಂಗಳು (last month), ಈ ವರ್ಷ (this year). Kannada plans in weeks for errands, months for visits, years for weddings.

Dates assemble simply: ದಿನಾಂಕ (dinaanka — date), ಮೊದಲನೇ ತಾರೀಕು (first), ಇಂದು ದಿನಾಂಕ ಎಷ್ಟು? (what's today's date?). Numbers from the numbers page slot straight in — ಹದಿನೈದನೇ ತಾರೀಕು (the 15th). Time, dates, plans: one page, total coverage.

A note on ಶುಕ್ರವಾರ and ಮಂಗಳವಾರ. Friday (ಶುಕ್ರವಾರ) and Tuesday (ಮಂಗಳವಾರ) are auspicious days — temples fill, new ventures launch, ದೇವಿ worship peaks. Knowing the days means knowing the rhythm: never schedule a sceptical meeting on a festival Friday.

Questions learners ask

How do I memorise the days?

Start the week Sunday like Kannada calendars: ಭಾನುವಾರ, ಸೋಮವಾರ, ಮಂಗಳವಾರ, ಬುಧವಾರ, ಗುರುವಾರ, ಶುಕ್ರವಾರ, ಶನಿವಾರ. Chant them in order — rhythm makes them permanent.

How does Kannada split the day?

ಬೆಳಿಗ್ಗೆ (morning), ಮಧ್ಯಾಹ್ನ (afternoon), ಸಂಜೆ (evening), ರಾತ್ರಿ (night). Greetings, meals and plans all key off these four — ಶುಭಸಂಜೆ (good evening) after four-ish.

Week, month, year?

ವಾರ (vaara — week), ತಿಂಗಳು (tingalu — month), ವರ್ಷ (varsha — year). ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾರ (next week), ಈ ತಿಂಗಳು (this month) — plans assemble instantly.
