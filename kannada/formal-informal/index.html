Formal and informal Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

ನೀವು or ನೀನು? ಬನ್ನಿ or ಬಾ? Respect levels, decoded.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Kannada has two yous: ನೀವು (formal/plural — strangers, elders, officials) and ನೀನು (familiar — friends, children, family). English erased this distinction centuries ago; Kannada keeps it razor-sharp. Every conversation opens with an invisible choice — make it consciously, and make it respectful.

Verbs mirror the split: ಬನ್ನಿ (please come) versus ಬಾ (come!), ಹೇಳಿ (please say) versus ಹೇಳು (say!), ಹೋಗಿ (please go) versus ಹೋಗು (go!). The -ಇ ending is pure respect — same verb, different universe. Stem-alone orders bark; -ಇ invites.

Giving and forgiving show the range: ಕೊಡಿ (please give) versus ಕೊಡು (give!), ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ (please forgive) versus ಕ್ಷಮಿಸು (forgive, familiar). Beggars and kings both hear ಕೊಡಿ — politeness in Kannada costs nothing and buys everything.

Strangers get particles and titles: ರೀ (respectful hey), ಅಮ್ಮ (madam), ಅಣ್ಣ (brother), ಅಕ್ಕ (sister). ರೀ, ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡಿ (excuse me, please help) opens any door; barking orders at strangers closes them. Kannada warmth is earned with two syllables of respect.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ರೀ. ರೀ (ree) is Kannada's pocket-sized respect — attach it anywhere and warmth ignites: ಬನ್ನಿ ರೀ (do come), ನೋಡಿ ರೀ (please see), ಹೇಳಿ ರೀ (please tell). Two sounds that upgrade every request instantly.

Questions learners ask

When do I use ನೀವು?

ನೀವು for every adult you don't know well — shopkeepers, officials, elders, strangers. ನೀನು waits for close friends, children and family. Wrong-side errors embarrass; learners are forgiven, but respect first is always safest.

How do I make a command polite?

Add -ಇ: ಬಾ becomes ಬನ್ನಿ, ಮಾಡು becomes ಮಾಡಿ, ಹೇಳು becomes ಹೇಳಿ. One ending, instant politeness — the highest-value suffix in Kannada.

How do I address strangers?

ರೀ (ree — respectful hey) and ಅಮ್ಮ (amma — madam) — or ಸಾರ್ (saar) borrowed and beloved. ರೀ, ಟೈಮ್ ಎಷ್ಟು? (excuse me, what time is it?). Better than pointing and shouting in any language.
