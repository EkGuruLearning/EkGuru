Kannada

Kannada slang

ಸಖತ್, ಮಚ್ಚಾ, ಬೇರೆ ಲೆವೆಲ್ — street Kannada that fun runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Praise slang opens doors: ಸೂಪರ್ (super), ಸಖತ್ (awesome), ಬೇರೆ ಲೆವೆಲ್ (next level), ಮಜಾ (fun/great), ಅಬ್ಬಾ (wow). Food, films, friends' outfits — praise everything loudly. Kannada people praise generously; learners should too.

Friendship words bond crews: ಮಚ್ಚಾ (dude), ಗುರು (guru/dude), ಭಾಯ್ (bro) — friends-only, always warm. ಮಚ್ಚಾ ಬಾ (come on dude) starts a thousand conversations daily. Never aim them upward at elders; sideways at friends, fire freely.

Reaction words colour stories: ಅಯ್ಯೋ (oh no!), ಅಬ್ಬಾ (wow!), ಕತೆ ಹೇಳ್ಬೇಡ (stop making things up!). Kannada storytelling runs on reactions — gasp, laugh, disbelieve theatrically. Flat faces kill stories; big reactions feed them.

Playful edges season everything: ಹೋಗೋ (get lost — playful among friends, rude otherwise), ಛೇ (mild disgust), ಸುಮ್ನೆ (just because). Learn the edges, respect the edges: slang's power is knowing exactly where fun ends.

A note on ಸುಮ್ನೆ. ಸುಮ್ನೆ (sumne) defies translation — just because, for fun, no reason, chill. ಏಕೆ? — ಸುಮ್ನೆ (why? — just because). Whole philosophies hide in two syllables; use it freely, smile always.

Questions learners ask

What slang is safe everywhere?

ಸೂಪರ್ (super) and ಸಖತ್ (awesome) — positive, universal, safe. Sprinkle them on food, films and friends; instant insider status.

Can I call people machchaa?

ಮಚ್ಚಾ (dude) and ಗುರು (guru/dude) — for friends only, never elders or strangers. Friendship slang misused offends; rightly used, it bonds instantly.

What does sumne mean?

ಸುಮ್ನೆ (just like that / for fun) — ಸುಮ್ನೆ ಹೇಳ್ದೆ (just kidding), ಸುಮ್ನೆ ಬಂದೆ (just dropped by). The most Kannada word in existence: purposeless joy, declared proudly.
