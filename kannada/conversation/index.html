Kannada

Real Kannada conversation

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Start a Kannada exchange with a name, a familiar place, and one easy follow-up; the model lines show each turn.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Every first conversation follows the same dance: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು? (what is your name?), ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು... (my name is...), ನೀವು ಎಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಬರುತ್ತೀರಿ? (where are you from?). Four lines, and you have had a real conversation. Practise them in order until they flow without thinking.

Small talk runs on three questions: ನೀವು ಏನು ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀರಿ? (what do you do?), ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತಾಡ್ತೀರಾ? (do you speak Kannada?), ಊಟ ಮಾಡಿದ್ರಾ? (have you eaten? — asked with genuine care). Answer briefly, ask back immediately — ನೀವು? — because conversations are volleys, not speeches.

When the torrent comes — and Kannada hospitality pours words — deploy the brakes: ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಮಾತಾಡಿ (please speak slowly), ನನಗೆ ಅರ್ಥವಾಗಲಿಲ್ಲ (I didn't understand), ಮತ್ತೆ ಹೇಳಿ (please say it again). Every Kannada speaker honours these instantly; they are the learner's seatbelt.

Close warmly and promise return: ನಿಮ್ಮನ್ನು ಭೇಟಿಯಾಗಿದ್ದು ಸಂತೋಷ (pleased to meet you), ಮತ್ತೆ ಸಿಗೋಣ (we'll meet again). Leave with a ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ and the door stays open — Kannada conversations end the way they begin: with folded hands.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ನೀವು, ನೀನು and ರೀ. Kannada has two yous where English has one: ನೀವು (neevu — formal/plural) and ನೀನು (neenu — familiar) — plus ರೀ (ree), the friendly respect-particle. Start with ನೀವು for every adult; let friendship invite you to ನೀನು.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask someone's name?

ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು? (nimma hesaru enu — what is your name?) for respect; ನಿನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು? (ninna hesaru enu) for friends and children. Answer with ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು... (nanna hesaru — my name is...). Names first, always.

How do I say where I'm from?

ನೀವು ಎಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಬರುತ್ತೀರಿ? (where are you from?) — then answer for yourself: ನಾನು ... ಇಂದ ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ. Origin questions are the universal second question; prepare your answer before you need it.

What do I say when people speak too fast?

ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಮಾತಾಡಿ (please speak slowly) and ಮತ್ತೆ ಹೇಳಿ (please say it again). Said with a smile, these two sentences make every Kannada speaker slow down and help.
