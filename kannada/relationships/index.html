Talking about relationships in Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Friends, lovers, weddings — the Kannada of hearts, spoken truly.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Friendship opens hearts: ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ (male friend), ಸ್ನೇಹಿತೆ (female friend) — ನನ್ನ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ (my friend) introduces proudly. Kannada friendships run lifelong and deep; ಸ್ನೇಹ (friendship) is celebrated in poetry, films and daily tea. Make one Kannada friend and gain a hundred phrases.

Romance speaks softly: ಪ್ರಿಯಕರ (boyfriend), ಪ್ರಿಯತಮೆ (girlfriend), ಪ್ರೀತಿ (love). Kannada romance favours poetry over bluntness — film songs teach the register better than textbooks. Learn gently; speak truly.

Weddings unite families: ಮದುವೆ (wedding spoken), ವಿವಾಹ (wedding formal), ಮದುಮಗ (groom), ಮದುಮಗಳು (bride). Kannada weddings run for days — music, food, rituals, blessings. ಮದುವೆ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (wedding congratulations) is your ticket to the feast.

Bonds deepen and mend: ಅನುರಾಗ (love), ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ (affection), ಜೀವನ (life) — ನನ್ನ ಜೀವನ (my life) means my beloved. And when bonds strain: ಕ್ಷಮೆ (forgiveness), ಸಂಧಾನ (peace). Kannada hearts forgive spaciously; ask with folded hands.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಪ್ರೀತಿ vs ಅನುರಾಗ. ಪ್ರೀತಿ (preeti) is romance specifically; ಅನುರಾಗ (anuraaga) is broad love — family, friends, humanity. ನಿಮ್ಮ ಅನುರಾಗಕ್ಕೆ ಧನ್ಯವಾದ (thanks for your love) suits everyone; ಪ್ರೀತಿ belongs to one. Kannada loves precisely.

Questions learners ask

Friendship words first?

ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ (male friend), ಸ್ನೇಹಿತೆ (female friend) — then ಅನುರಾಗ (love) and ವಾತ್ಸಲ್ಯ (affection) as bonds deepen. Friendship words first; romance follows naturally.

How do I talk about weddings?

ಮದುವೆ (spoken wedding), ವಿವಾಹ (formal wedding) — ಮದುಮಗ (groom), ಮದುಮಗಳು (bride). ಮದುವೆ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (wedding greetings) blesses every couple.

How do I apologise deeply?

ಕ್ಷಮೆ (forgiveness) — ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ (please forgive me), then ಸಂಧಾನ (peace). Kannada reconciliations run deep and warm; ask sincerely, receive fully.
