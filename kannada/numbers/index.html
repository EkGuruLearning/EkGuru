Counting in Kannada: 1 to 100

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

ಒಂದು ಎರಡು ಮೂರು — numbers, clock time and market-ready counting.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

One to ten: ಒಂದು (ondu), ಎರಡು (eradu), ಮೂರು (mooru), ನಾಲ್ಕು (naalku), ಐದು (aidu), ಆರು (aaru), ಏಳು (elu), ಎಂಟು (entu), ಒಂಬತ್ತು (ombattu), ಹತ್ತು (hattu). Chant them in rhythm — Kannada numbers love rhythm — and they stick in days, not weeks.

Eleven to nineteen build on ಹತ್ತು: ಹನ್ನೊಂದು (11), ಹನ್ನೆರಡು (12), ಹದಿಮೂರು (13)... then the tens march: ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು (20), ಮೂವತ್ತು (30), ನಲವತ್ತು (40), ಐವತ್ತು (50), ಅರವತ್ತು (60), ಎಪ್ಪತ್ತು (70), ಎಂಬತ್ತು (80), ತೊಂಬತ್ತು (90), ನೂರು (100). Past twenty, glue tens to units — ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತೊಂದು (21) — and count to a thousand (ಸಾವಿರ) without fear.

Money runs on numbers: the currency is the ರೂಪಾಯಿ (roopaayi — rupee), and ಎಷ್ಟು? (eshtu — how much?) is the most useful sentence in Karnataka. Listen for the number, repeat it back — ಐವತ್ತು? (fifty?) — and your first Kannada transaction completes itself.

Time reuses numbers with ಗಂಟೆ (gante — hour): ಎರಡು ಗಂಟೆ (two o'clock), ಐದು ಗಂಟೆ (five o'clock). ಈಗ ಟೈಮ್ ಎಷ್ಟು? (what time is it now?) is the question; half hours add -ವರೆ. Numbers, money, clocks: one skill, three daily victories.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಒಂದೂವರೆ, ಎರಡೂವರೆ. Half hours glue -ವರೆ to the number: ಒಂದೂವರೆ (1:30), ಮೂರುವರೆ (3:30), ಐದೂವರೆ (5:30). Every language hides one clock trick; Kannada's is ವರೆ. Learn it and sound native instantly.

Questions learners ask

Kannada digits or international digits?

International digits for daily life — prices, phones, buses all use them. Kannada has traditional number words in full use (ಒಂದು, ಹತ್ತು, ನೂರು) but modern digits in print.

How do I say the time in Kannada?

Take the number, add ಗಂಟೆ (gante — hour): ಮೂರು ಗಂಟೆ (three o'clock). Half past adds -ವರೆ: ಮೂರುವರೆ is half past three. One rule, every clock in Karnataka.

How do I ask the price?

ಎಷ್ಟು? (eshtu — how much?). Then listen for the number plus ರೂಪಾಯಿ (roopaayi — rupees). Market Kannada is the fastest teacher there is.
