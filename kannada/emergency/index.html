Emergency Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Help, doctor, hospital — emergency Kannada you hope to never need.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The core three save lives: ಸಹಾಯ (help), ಅಪಾಯ (danger), ತುರ್ತು (emergency). ನನಗೆ ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡಿ (please help me) — say it to anyone, anywhere. Kannada crowds respond to distress calls faster than any app; your job is only to ask.

Medical words route the rescue: ಡಾಕ್ಟರ್ (doctor), ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ (hospital), ಔಷಧಿ (medicine), ಮೆಡಿಕಲ್ ಶಾಪ್ (pharmacy). ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where is the hospital?) plus a taxi wave — in cities, help arrives in minutes.

Symptoms speak simply: ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನೋವಾಗಿದೆ (it hurts here — point!), ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಸರಿಯಿಲ್ಲ (I feel unwell), ತಲೆನೋವು (headache), ಜ್ವರ (fever). Pain words plus pointing hands diagnose across every language barrier on earth.

Danger and loss close the kit: ಬೆಂಕಿ (fire), ಕಳ್ಳ (thief), ಪೋಲೀಸರು (police), ಕಳೆದುಹೋಗಿದೆ (it's lost), ಎಂಬಸಿ (embassy). Memorise these cold — emergencies never wait for phrasebooks, and ten minutes of drilling buys total peace of mind.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಸಹಾಯ. ಸಹಾಯ (sahaaya — help) is Kannada's shortest lifeline — shout it and crowds come running. Karnataka's bystander culture is famously kind: strangers will escort you to hospitals, translate for doctors, and refuse thanks. Ask loudly; receive gratefully.

Questions learners ask

What is the one emergency sentence?

ನನಗೆ ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡಿ (please help me) — loud, clear, repeated. Add ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where is the hospital?) and Kannada crowds mobilise instantly.

How do I explain pain?

Point and say ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನೋವಾಗಿದೆ (it hurts here). Then ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಸರಿಯಿಲ್ಲ (I feel unwell) and ಡಾಕ್ಟರ್ ಬೇಕು (I need a doctor). Pain plus pointing crosses every language gap.

How do I report something lost?

ಕಳೆದುಹೋಗಿದೆ (it is lost) — ಹಣ ಕಳೆದುಹೋಗಿದೆ (money lost), ಬ್ಯಾಗ್ ಕಳೆದುಹೋಗಿದೆ (bag lost). Then ಪೋಲೀಸ್ ಸ್ಟೇಷನ್ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where is the police station?).
