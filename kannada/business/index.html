Kannada for work and business

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Meetings, customers, contracts — professional Kannada for offices.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Workplaces run on roles: ಕೆಲಸ (work/job), ಆಫೀಸು (office), ಬಾಸ್ (boss), ಗ್ರಾಹಕ (customer), ಸಹೋದ್ಯೋಗಿ (colleague). ನನ್ನ ಕೆಲಸ... (my work is...) introduces your profession; ಆಫೀಸು ಎಲ್ಲಿ? finds any office. Roles first, tasks second.

Meetings move business: ಸಭೆ (meeting), ಒಪ್ಪಂದ (contract/deal), ಸಹಿ (signature). ಸಭೆ ನಾಳೆ ಬೆಳಿಗ್ಗೆ (meeting tomorrow morning) — confirm with ಸರಿ, ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ (okay, I'll come). Kannada meetings open with ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ and close with ಧನ್ಯವಾದ.

Money talks plainly: ಲಾಭ (profit), ನಷ್ಟ (loss), ಸಂಬಳ (salary), ರಜೆ (leave). ಸಂಬಳ ಯಾವಾಗ? (when is payday?) — asked smiling, everywhere. Business Kannada respects directness wrapped in politeness.

Digital tools connect all: ಈಮೇಲ್ (email), ಫೋನ್ (phone), ಮೆಸೇಜ್ (message). ಈಮೇಲ್ ನೋಡಿ (please check email) — then ಯಶಸ್ಸು (success) follows effort. Professional Kannada: formal words, warm heart.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ಗ್ರಾಹಕ. ಗ್ರಾಹಕ (graahaka — customer) rules Kannada business — pure, proud, universal. It takes practice to say smoothly (graa-ha-ka); clients notice the effort and reward it. Master it syllable by syllable.

Questions learners ask

How do I introduce myself at work?

ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ನಾನು... (hello, I'm...) — then ನಿಮ್ಮನ್ನು ಭೇಟಿಯಾಗಿದ್ದು ಸಂತೋಷ (pleased to meet you). Business Kannada opens formal and warms slowly.

How do I ask about meetings?

ಸಭೆ ಯಾವಾಗ? (when is the meeting?) and ಸಭೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where?). Meetings run Kannada offices — never miss one for language reasons.

Email and phone in Kannada?

ಈಮೇಲ್ ಕಳಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ (I'll send an email) and ಫೋನಲ್ಲಿ ಮಾತಾಡೋಣ (let's talk on the phone). Digital Kannada borrows freely — clarity beats purity in business.
