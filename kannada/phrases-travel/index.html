Travel phrases in Kannada

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Stations, directions, tickets and toilets — travel Kannada that gets you there.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Destinations unlock with one ending: ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (bus stand), ರೈಲ್ವೆ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (railway station), ವಿಮಾನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (airport), ಹೋಟೆಲ್ (hotel). Add ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where?) and you can find every transport hub in the state — ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? is the traveller's master key.

Directions come in threes: ಎಡ (left), ಬಲ (right), ನೇರವಾಗಿ (straight ahead) — plus ಹತ್ತಿರ (nearby) and ದೂರ (far). ಎಡಗಡೆ (on the left side), ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (go straight) — follow the words, watch the inevitable hand gestures, arrive smiling.

Tickets and seats run the journey: ಟಿಕೆಟ್ (ticket), ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ (one ticket), ಎಷ್ಟು? (how much?), ಸೀಟು (seatu — seat). ಬಸ್ ಯಾವಾಗ ಬರುತ್ತೆ? (when does the bus come?) — asked at every stand, answered with cheerful vagueness. Patience is also vocabulary.

Emergencies and essentials close the kit: ಸಹಾಯ (help), ಶೌಚಾಲಯ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where is the toilet?), ನೀರು (water), ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ (hospital). Hope never to need ನನಗೆ ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡಿ (please help) — but carry it always, like travel insurance in a single word.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ. ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (nildaana — station/stand) anchors every journey: ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (bus stand), ರೈಲ್ವೆ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (railway station), ವಿಮಾನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (airport). Learn the pattern once — vehicle + ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ — and every signboard in Karnataka reads itself.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask where things are?

...ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (...elli — where is...?) plus a pointing finger. ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? ರೈಲ್ವೆ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? One pattern, every destination in Karnataka.

Left, right, straight?

ಎಡ (left), ಬಲ (right), ನೇರವಾಗಿ (straight) — then ಹತ್ತಿರ (near) and ದೂರ (far). Kannada directions come with generous hand-waving; watch the hands as well as the words.

How do I find a toilet?

ಶೌಚಾಲಯ ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (where is the toilet?) — say it clearly, without embarrassment. Every bus stand, station and restaurant understands instantly.
