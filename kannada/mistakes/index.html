Kannada

Mistakes every learner makes in Kannada

Verb-first sentences, ನಾನು ಬೇಕು, ಇಲ್ಲ vs ಅಲ್ಲ — fix them all.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Kannada spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Mistake one: English word order. ನಾನು ಹೋಗು (I go — bare stem, wrong) instead of ನಾನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (I go — conjugated, right); verbs wandering mid-sentence instead of anchoring the end. Kannada is verb-final, always — SOV is not a suggestion, it is the law.

Mistake two: ನಾನು ಬೇಕು (I want — wrong) instead of ನನಗೆ ಬೇಕು (to-me wanted — right). Kannada wanting flows toward the wanter; English wanting fires from the wanter. Flip the arrow and dozens of sentences — ನನಗೆ ಅರ್ಥವಾಯಿತು, ನನಗೆ ಗೊತ್ತು — unlock at once.

Mistake three: one-size no. ಇಲ್ಲ denies existence — ಹಣ ಇಲ್ಲ (there's no money); ಅಲ್ಲ denies identity — ನಾನು ಟೀಚರ್ ಅಲ್ಲ (I am not a teacher). Beginners spray ಇಲ್ಲ everywhere; natives smile and correct. Learn both no's, use each precisely.

Mistake four: rushing sounds — flat long vowels, merged sibilants, skipped -ಇ. ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ (slowly), ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ (correctly), ಮತ್ತೆ (again): the holy trinity of repair. ಪ್ರಯತ್ನ (effort) plus ಅಭ್ಯಾಸ (practice) plus ತಾಳ್ಮೆ (patience) — and ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು, mistakes become fluency.

A note on ತಪ್ಪು. ತಪ್ಪು (tappu — mistake) is the kindest word in this lesson — ನನ್ನ ತಪ್ಪು (my mistake) disarms every situation. Kannada people adore learners who err boldly and laugh warmly; the only true ತಪ್ಪು is silence.

Questions learners ask

What is the most common mistake?

English order with Kannada words: ನಾನು ಹೋಗು instead of ನಾನು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ, verb first, question words misplaced. Cure: every sentence ends with the verb — practise SOV until English order feels foreign.

Why is ನಾನು ಬೇಕು wrong?

Wanting flows toward people: ನನಗೆ ಬೇಕು (to-me it-is-wanted), never ನಾನು ಬೇಕು. One of Kannada's deepest patterns — experiences come TO you — and beginners fight it for weeks.

ಇಲ್ಲ or ಅಲ್ಲ?

ಇಲ್ಲ denies existence and possession — ಹಣ ಇಲ್ಲ (no money); ಅಲ್ಲ denies identity — ನಾನು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ ಅಲ್ಲ (I am not a student). Two no's, two jobs, zero overlap.
