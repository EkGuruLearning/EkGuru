The learning material

The guides, tools and lessons here are for general education. They are written carefully and corrected when errors are reported, but Hindi is a living language with real regional variation, and no free page replaces a teacher who can hear you. Devanagari typed by hand goes wrong in small ways — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. Where a page's spelling disagrees with a native speaker, trust the speaker, and please tell us.

Romanised spellings throughout are a guide to the sound, not a standard. Hindi has no single agreed romanisation, which is why you will see both shukriya and shukriyaa.
