The whole Haitian Creole A1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

Who this level is for. A1 is one of the six CEFR levels and the A1 rung on this site’s ladder: the learner in the picture is a child, because on this site age is the rung — the same learner grows up the ladder as the language grows. By the end of it you can greet, count to twenty, read a shop sign.

Bonjou ak prezantasyon

Haitian Creole combines postposed determiners, plural yo, preverbal tense-mood-aspect markers, invariant verb stems, SVO/topic-focus order, serial verbs, official sound-oriented spelling, and prosody. Choices encode reference, time, evidence, focus, and register.

topic + TMA/negation + predicate + postposed determiner + stance
