Evidence, Stance, and Responsible Claims

Learn to separate observation, inference, and attributed claim. Hindi academic and professional prose uses frames such as उपलब्ध साक्ष्य से…प्रतीत होता है to calibrate commitment rather than present every interpretation as fact.

Evidential stance

प्रतीत होना and संकेत देना leave room for revision; साबित करना requires stronger evidence. दावा reports a proposition without automatically endorsing it. संभवतः hedges probability, whereas निस्संदेह signals strong commitment and should not be used merely to sound formal.

Coherence Across Complex Arguments

Build paragraphs whose concession, contrast, cause, and consequence are recoverable. Formal pairs such as यद्यपि…तथापि are useful, but a connector cannot repair a missing logical relation.

Concession and rhetorical sequence

यद्यपि introduces a point that could support an opposite expectation; तथापि marks the maintained conclusion. एक ओर…दूसरी ओर balances considerations. इसके विपरीत contrasts evidence; फलस्वरूप should mark an actual consequence, not a new unrelated fact.

Honorific agreement and genre recasting

आप normally takes plural/honorific agreement: आप आएँगे/आएँगी. तुम is familiar; तू can express intimacy in some relationships but can insult elsewhere and is never the beginner default. पधारिए elevates स्वागत contexts. Formal writing may use अवगत कराना, while conversation prefers बताना.
