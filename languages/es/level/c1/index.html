Mood after stance expressions

Indicative mood presents information as asserted; the subjunctive after no parece que, es posible que, and evaluative frames suspends or evaluates commitment. The choice is semantic, not decorative.

stance frame + que + indicative/subjunctive
WordSay itMeaning
Los datos indican una tendencia, pero no permiten constatar su causa.los datos indikan una tendensia pero no permiten konstatar su kausaThe data indicate a trend but do not establish its cause.
No parece que la medida haya reducido la desigualdad.no parese ke la medida aya redusido la desigualdadThe measure does not appear to have reduced inequality.
Cabe señalar que el resultado depende del período analizado.kabe senyalar ke el resultado depende del periodo analisadoIt should be noted that the result depends on the period analysed.
Watch out:
  • Preserve mood, stance, cohesion, and register; a grammatically possible form may still be pragmatically inappropriate.

Cohesive reference and sequence

C1 discourse uses lexical reference, demonstratives, and reported speech to avoid both mechanical repetition and vague pronouns. Connectors must encode a real relation between propositions.

claim → source attribution → concession → consequence

Conditional mitigation

The conditional and imperfect subjunctive can soften proposals and requests. Mitigation protects rapport, but an effective professional message still names the action, actor, and deadline.

¿Sería posible que + imperfect subjunctive…?

Qualification and nominalisation

Academic Spanish often packages processes as nouns, but nominalisation must not hide the agent or inflate certainty. A comparison states population, criterion, and limitation.

scope + finding + limitation + faithful reformulation
WordSay itMeaning
La conclusión se limita a la muestra urbana estudiada.la konklusion se limita a la muestra urbana estudyadaThe conclusion is limited to the urban sample studied.
La correlación observada no demuestra por sí sola causalidad.la korrelasion observada no demuestra por si sola kausalidadThe observed correlation does not by itself demonstrate causality.
En otras palabras, aumentó el promedio, no cada uno de los valores.en otras palabras aumento el promedyo no kada uno de los valoresIn other words, the average increased, not every value.
