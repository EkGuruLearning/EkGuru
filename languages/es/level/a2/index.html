Getting around

Around the city (ESTAR)

SER is for WHO you are; ESTAR is for WHERE you are. ESTOY = I am (located), ESTÁS = you are, ESTÁ = it is. La estación ESTÁ allí = The station is over there. Add IZQUIERDA (left) and DERECHA (right) and you can follow any directions.

WordSay itMeaning
la callelah KAH-yehstreet
la plazalah PLAH-sahsquare
la estaciónlah ehs-tah-SYOHNstation
el bancoehl BAHN-kohbank
el hospitalehl ohs-pee-TAHLhospital
la tiendalah TYEHN-dahshop
la izquierdalah ees-KYEHR-dahleft
la derechalah deh-REH-chahright
ESTAR for location: estoy, estás, está

ESTAR answers WHERE. ESTOY EN CASA = I am at home. Never use SER for location! Also learn CERCA (near) and LEJOS (far): Está cerca = It is near.

estoy/estás/está + en + place
WordSay itMeaning
Estoy en casa.ehs-TOY ehn KAH-sah.I am at home.
¿Dónde estás?DOHN-deh ehs-TAHS?Where are you?
La tienda está cerca.lah TYEHN-dah ehs-TAH SEHR-kah.The shop is near.
Watch out:
  • Saying SOY en casa — location is always ESTAR.
  • Saying ESTÁ for yourself — I am = ESTOY.

Travel and the past

Talk about finished trips with the PRETÉRITO (simple past). For -AR verbs: HABLÉ (I spoke), HABLASTE (you spoke), HABLÓ (he/she spoke). VIAJÉ A ESPAÑA = I travelled to Spain. One accent mark changes everything!

WordSay itMeaning
el viajeehl VYAH-hehtrip
el aviónehl ah-VYOHNplane
el hotelehl oh-TEHLhotel
la maletalah mah-LEH-tahsuitcase
el billeteehl bee-YEH-tehticket
la playalah PLAH-yahbeach
la montañalah mohn-TAHN-yahmountain
la fotolah FOH-tohphoto
Past -AR: hablé, hablaste, habló

Take off -AR, add -É (I), -ASTE (you), -Ó (he/she). Viajé = I travelled. These are finished, done actions: AYER (yesterday) loves the pretérito.

stem + é/aste/ó
WordSay itMeaning
Viajé a España.vyah-HEH ah ehs-PAHN-yah.I travelled to Spain.
¿Compraste billetes?kohm-PRAHS-teh bee-YEH-tehs?Did you buy tickets?
Ella llegó ayer.EH-yah yeh-GOH ah-YEHR.She arrived yesterday.
Watch out:
  • Saying HABLO for the past — past I = HABLÉ (with accent!).
  • Forgetting AYER goes with finished past, not present.

Life and plans

Daily routines (reflexive verbs)

Routines use REFLEXIVE verbs: the action goes back to you. ME LEVANTO = I get (myself) up. ME = myself, TE = yourself, SE = himself/herself. Every morning runs on ME/TE/SE!

WordSay itMeaning
la mañanalah mahn-YAH-nahmorning
la tardelah TAHR-dehafternoon
la nochelah NOH-chehnight
el trabajoehl trah-BAH-hohwork / job
la escuelalah ehs-KWEH-lahschool
la duchalah DOO-chahshower
el desayunoehl deh-sah-YOO-nohbreakfast
la cenalah SEH-nahdinner
Reflexive ME/TE/SE: me levanto

LEVANTARSE = to get up. ME LEVANTO (I get up), TE LEVANTAS (you get up), SE LEVANTA (he/she gets up). The little word before the verb points back at the person.

me/te/se + verb
WordSay itMeaning
Me levanto a las siete.meh leh-VAHN-toh ah lahs SYEH-teh.I get up at seven.
¿Te duchas por la mañana?teh DOO-chahs pohr lah mahn-YAH-nah?Do you shower in the morning?
Se acuesta tarde.seh ah-KWEHS-tah TAHR-deh.He/She goes to bed late.
Watch out:
  • Saying LEVANTO without ME — it means 'I lift', not 'I get up'!
  • Mixing TE and SE: TE = you, SE = he/she.

Health and future plans

At the doctor: ME DUELE LA CABEZA = my head hurts (to me it pains the head). For plans, Spanish loves IR A + verb: VOY A VIAJAR = I am going to travel. Easy future, no new endings!

WordSay itMeaning
la cabezalah kah-BEH-sahhead
la manolah MAH-nohhand
el ojoehl OH-hoheye
el médicoehl MEH-dee-kohdoctor
la farmacialah fahr-MAH-syahpharmacy
el dolorehl doh-LOHRpain
la fiebrelah FYEH-brehfever
la saludlah sah-LOODhealth
Future with IR A: voy a viajar

VOY = I go, VAS = you go, VA = he/she goes. VOY A + verb = I am going to…: VOY A COMER = I am going to eat. The second verb never changes!

voy/vas/va + a + verb
WordSay itMeaning
Voy a viajar.voy ah vyah-HAHR.I am going to travel.
¿Vas a estudiar?vahs ah ehs-too-DYAHR?Are you going to study?
Va a llover.vah ah yoh-VEHR.It is going to rain.
Watch out:
  • Changing the second verb: VOY A VIAJAR, never VOY A VIAJO.
  • Forgetting A: VOY A comer, not VOY comer.

ESTE vs ESE: this vs that

ESTE/ESTA = this (near me). ESE/ESA = that (near you). They match gender: ESTE libro, ESTA camisa, ESE vestido, ESA tienda.

Weather and -ER/-IR past

Weather uses HACER (to make): HACE SOL (it is sunny), HACE FRÍO (it is cold). Past of -ER/-IR verbs: COMÍ (I ate), VIVÍ (I lived), COMISTE, COMIÓ. Same endings for -ER and -IR!

WordSay itMeaning
el solehl sohlsun
la lluvialah YOO-vyahrain
el fríoehl FREE-ohcold
el calorehl kah-LOHRheat
el veranoehl veh-RAH-nohsummer
el inviernoehl een-VYEHR-nohwinter
la nubelah NOO-behcloud
el vientoehl VYEHN-tohwind
Past -ER/-IR: comí, comiste, comió

Drop -ER/-IR, add -Í (I), -ISTE (you), -IÓ (he/she). COMER → comí, comiste, comió. VIVIR → viví, viviste, vivió. Accents matter: COMIO (wrong) vs COMIÓ (he ate)!

Watch out:
  • Forgetting the accent: COMIÓ, not COMIO.
  • Mixing -AR endings: COMÍ (not COMÉ) — -ER past I is -Í.
