B1, unit by unit

Stories and opinions

Stories: pretérito vs imperfecto

Two pasts! PRETÉRITO = finished actions (LLEGUÉ = I arrived). IMPERFECTO = ongoing scenes (HACÍA SOL = the sun was shining). Tell stories: set the scene with imperfecto, fire events with pretérito.

Pretérito vs imperfecto: hablé vs hablaba

HABLÉ = I spoke (done). HABLABA = I was speaking / used to speak (ongoing). -AR imperfecto: HABLABA, HABLABAS, HABLABA. Weather, feelings, habits → imperfecto. Single events → pretérito.

pretérito (done) · imperfecto (was doing)
WordSay itMeaning
Llegué a las ocho.yeh-GEH ah lahs OH-choh.I arrived at eight. (done)
Hacía sol.ah-SYAH sohl.It was sunny. (scene)
Hablaba cuando llegaste.ah-BLAH-bah KWAHN-doh yeh-GAHS-teh.I was speaking when you arrived.
Watch out:
  • Using pretérito for weather: HACÍA sol, not HIZO sol.
  • Using imperfecto for single arrivals: LLEGUÉ, not LLEGABA.

Opinions and comparisons

Compare with MÁS…QUE (more…than) and MENOS…QUE (less…than). Specials: MEJOR (better), PEOR (worse). Give opinions: CREO QUE (I think that), EN MI OPINIÓN (in my opinion).

WordSay itMeaning

peorpeh-OHRworse / worst
mayormah-YOHRolder / bigger
menormeh-NOHRyounger / smaller
mismoMEES-mohsame
diferentedee-feh-REHN-tehdifferent
la opiniónlah oh-pee-NYOHNopinion
la razónlah rah-SOHNreason
Comparisons: más… que, mejor, peor

MÁS + adjective + QUE = more…than: MÁS GRANDE QUE = bigger than. EL MÁS… = the most: EL MÁS GRANDE = the biggest. MEJOR and PEOR never take MÁS — MEJOR already means better!

más/menos + adj + que · mejor/peor
WordSay itMeaning
Es más grande que mi casa.ehs mahs GRAHN-deh keh mee KAH-sah.It is bigger than my house.
Este libro es mejor.EHS-teh LEE-broh ehs meh-HOHR.This book is better.
Creo que es peor.KREH-oh keh ehs peh-OHR.I think it is worse.
Watch out:
  • Saying MÁS MEJOR — just MEJOR (better already!).
  • Forgetting QUE: MÁS GRANDE QUE, with QUE!

Real world

Work and polite YOU (usted)

At work, use USTED (polite you) with strangers and bosses. Usted uses HE/SHE verb forms: ¿HABLA inglés? = Do you (formal) speak English? TÚ is for friends; USTED is for respect.

WordSay itMeaning
el trabajoehl trah-BAH-hohwork / job
la oficinalah oh-fee-SEE-nahoffice
el jefeehl HEH-fehboss
la reuniónlah reh-oo-NYOHNmeeting
el correoehl koh-RREH-ohmail / email
el proyectoehl proh-YEHK-tohproject
el clienteehl klee-EHN-tehclient
el horarioehl oh-RAH-ryohschedule
USTED: polite you uses he/she forms

¿HABLAS? (tú, friend) vs ¿HABLA? (usted, formal). Same -A ending as HE/SHE. Use usted with bosses, elders, officials. Dropping it too fast can sound rude!

usted + he/she verb form
WordSay itMeaning

Usted tiene razón.oos-TEHD TYEH-neh rah-SOHN.You (formal) are right.
Watch out:
  • Using TÚ with a new boss — start with USTED.
  • Using HABLAS with usted — usted takes HABLA.

Plans and wishes

Dream big! QUISIERA (I would like) + verb makes soft wishes: QUISIERA VIAJAR = I would like to travel. QUIZÁ (maybe) + future guesses: QUIZÁ VIAJARÉ = maybe I will travel. OJALÁ (hopefully!) opens every dream.

Soft wishes: quisiera + verb

QUISIERA = I would like. Add any verb whole: QUISIERA APRENDER = I would like to learn. QUIZÁ (maybe) softens guesses. These two words make you sound kind and natural.

Future tense: viajaré

The real future adds endings to the WHOLE verb: VIAJAR → VIAJARÉ (I will travel), VIAJARÁS (you will), VIAJARÁ (he/she will). Never changes the stem — just add -É/-ÁS/-Á!

Future: whole verb + é/ás/á

Keep the infinitive, add -É, -ÁS, -Á: HABLARÉ, COMERÉ, VIVIRÉ. Works for -AR, -ER, -IR — all the same! A few rebels (TENDRÉ, HARÉ) come later.

Watch out:
  • Cutting the verb: VIAJARÉ keeps -AR + É — never VIAJÉ for future!
