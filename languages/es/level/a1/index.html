A1, unit by unit

First words

Hello! Greetings and politeness

Start with the magic words. Say HOLA to anyone, anytime. Spanish greets the day in parts: morning (buenos días), afternoon/evening (buenas tardes), night (buenas noches). POR FAVOR (please) and GRACIAS (thanks) open every door.

Every noun is EL or LA

Spanish nouns are masculine (EL) or feminine (LA). Words ending in -o are usually EL; words ending in -a are usually LA. Learn each noun WITH its el/la — never alone.

el + masculine noun · la + feminine noun
WordSay itMeaning
el libroehl LEE-brohthe book
la casalah KAH-sahthe house
el aguaehl AH-gwahthe water (looks odd, still feminine!)
Watch out:
  • Saying LA libro — libro is masculine: EL libro.
  • Forgetting el/la and saying just Casa — say LA casa.

Numbers and introductions

Numbers 1–10 unlock prices, ages, and phone numbers. To introduce yourself use SER (to be): SOY = I am. Soy Ana = I am Ana. To ask a name: ¿CÓMO TE LLAMAS? (What are you called?).

SER: soy, eres, es (I am, you are, he/she is)

SER describes who you are: name, origin, job. SOY = I am, ERES = you are, ES = he/she is. Drop the pronoun: SOY Ana is enough — no YO needed.

soy + name · eres + … · es + …
WordSay itMeaning
Soy Ana.soy AH-nah.I am Ana.
¿Eres Pedro?EH-rehs PEH-droh?Are you Pedro?
Es español.ehs ehs-pahn-YOHL.He/She is Spanish.
Watch out:
  • Saying YO SOY Ana every time — SOY Ana is natural.
  • Using ES for yourself — I am is SOY, never ES.

Daily life

Family and TENER (age)

Meet the family: MADRE (mother), PADRE (father), HERMANO/A (brother/sister). Age uses TENER (to have), not 'to be': TENGO 10 AÑOS = I have 10 years = I am 10 years old.

WordSay itMeaning
la madrelah MAH-drehmother
el padreehl PAH-drehfather
el hermanoehl ehr-MAH-nohbrother
la hermanalah ehr-MAH-nahsister
el hijoehl EE-hohson
la hijalah EE-hahdaughter
la familialah fah-MEE-lyahfamily
el amigo / la amigaehl ah-MEE-gohfriend (m/f)
TENER for age: tengo… años

TENGO = I have, TIENES = you have, TIENE = he/she has. For age: TENGO + number + AÑOS. Ask age: ¿CUÁNTOS AÑOS TIENES? (How many years do you have?).

tengo/tienes/tiene + number + años
WordSay itMeaning
Tengo diez años.TEHN-goh dyehs AHN-yohs.I am 10 years old.
¿Cuántos años tienes?KWAHN-tohs AHN-yohs TYEH-nehs?How old are you?
Mi hermana tiene siete años.mee ehr-MAH-nah TYEH-neh SYEH-teh AHN-yohs.My sister is 7.
Watch out:
  • Saying SOY diez años — age always uses TENER.
  • Saying AÑOS without T: ANOS is a rude word — say AHN-yohs with Ñ!

Adjectives match their noun: ROJO (red, masculine) but ROJA casa? No — LA casa ROJA. Ending -O goes with EL, -A goes with LA. Position: AFTER the noun — LA CASA BLANCA (the white house).

Days, months, and telling time

Days and months are lowercase in Spanish: LUNES, ENERO. Time: ES LA UNA (it is one) but SON LAS DOS (two). Ask: ¿QUÉ HORA ES? Answer: SON LAS + number.

WordSay itMeaning

Telling time: es la… / son las…

ES LA UNA (1:00 — singular), SON LAS DOS/TRES… (plural). Half past: Y MEDIA — SON LAS TRES Y MEDIA (3:30). Quarter: Y CUARTO.
