Focus and semantic scope

Focus markers restrict alternatives; their position changes what is excluded. A C2 speaker can state which proposition was asserted and which conclusion remains inferential.

focused constituent + precisamente/meramente/ni siquiera
WordSay itMeaning
El problema no es meramente presupuestario, sino también institucional.el problema no es meramente presupuestaryo sino tambyen institusionalThe problem is not merely budgetary but also institutional.
Fue precisamente la falta de consulta lo que generó rechazo.fue presisamente la falta de konsulta lo ke henero re hasoIt was precisely the lack of consultation that generated opposition.
Que haya vuelto a protestar presupone que ya lo hizo antes.ke aya vuelto a protestar presupone ke ya lo iso antesThat she protested again presupposes that she did so before.
Watch out:
  • Preserve mood, stance, cohesion, and register; a grammatically possible form may still be pragmatically inappropriate.

Compound conditionals and viewpoint

The pluperfect subjunctive plus conditional perfect constructs an unreal past. Evidential futures and conditionals can mark probability or reported distance rather than time alone.

si + hubiera + participle, habría + participle
WordSay itMeaning
Si se hubiera consultado antes, se habría evitado parte del conflicto.si se ubyera konsultado antes se abria evitado parte del konfliktoIf consultation had happened earlier, part of the conflict would have been avoided.
De haber sabido el coste real, no habríamos aprobado el proyecto.de aber sabido el koste real no abriamos aprobado el proyektoHad we known the real cost, we would not have approved the project.
El ministro habría recibido el informe, según fuentes no confirmadas.el ministro abria resibido el informe segun fuentes no konfirmadasThe minister reportedly received the report, according to unconfirmed sources.

Rhetorical stance

Irony depends on contextual mismatch, while concession can strengthen credibility. Ethical persuasion distinguishes evidence, warrant, and value judgment.

fair objection → concession → rebuttal → proportionate proposal

Deontic modality and agency

Deber, haber de, poder, quedar obligado and impersonal constructions differ in force. C2 editing exposes the responsible actor and avoids turning recommendations into obligations.

actor + modal force + action + condition
