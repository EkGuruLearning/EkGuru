Spanish Numbers 1 to 20

The first twenty numbers with pronunciation, plus how to ask and say prices.

Full course. Authored Spanish content, part of the free EkGuru Spanish course. Audio is your browser’s computer voice, not a native recording.

1 to 10

The first ten numbers are the foundation: uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez. Notice uno shortens to un before a masculine noun: un libro (one book).

11 to 15

once, doce, trece, catorce, quince. These five are single words you simply memorise — there is no pattern to lean on.

16 to 20

From 16 the pattern appears: dieciséis (16, literally "ten and six"), diecisiete (17), dieciocho (18), diecinueve (19), veinte (20).

Asking and saying prices

¿Cuánto cuesta? means "how much does it cost?" (one thing); ¿Cuánto cuestan? for several. The answer is just the number: Diez euros (ten euros). In most of Latin America you will also hear ¿Cuánto es?.
