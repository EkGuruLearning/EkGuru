Conditionals and plans

If: -(으)면

The all-round IF! 먹으면 (if I eat), 가면 (if I go), 비가 오면 (if it rains). Vowel-stems: -면, consonants: -으면! 와도 (even if): 와도 가요 = Even if it rains, I go!

WordSay itMeaning

만약man-yakif / in case
-(으)면 (if) · -아/어도 (even if)

먹으면 (if eat), 가면 (if go). 비가 오면 안 가요 (if it rains, I won't go). 와도 가요 (even if rains, go)! 공부하면 알아요 (if you study, you know)!

Honorifics and debate

Full honorifics: -시-

Honor in every verb! -시- infix: 가세요 (honorific go), 드세요 (honorific eat), 주무세요 (honorific sleep), 계세요 (honorific be). Honorific nouns: 말씀 (words), 성함 (name), 댁 (home)!

WordSay itMeaning

드세요deuseyohonorific eat
주무세요jumuseyohonorific sleep
계세요gyeseyohonorific be
말씀malsseumhonorific words
성함seonghamhonorific name
댁daekhonorific home
드리다deu-ridahumble give
-시- infix + honorific nouns

선생님이 계세요? (Is the teacher there?). 잡수세요 = honorific eat (alt). 말씀하세요 (please speak, honored). Humble ME: 드리다 (I humbly give)!

The B2 test — 10 items

The level test closes the level. Answers are shown; in the course player the same test decides whether the next level unlocks.

  1. translate into the languageSay: If it rains, I won't go

    Show the answer

    비가 오면 안 가요

  2. translate into the languageSay: Let's eat together!

    Show the answer

    같이 먹읍시다

  3. choose'Is the teacher there? (honorific)'

    1. 선생님이 있어요?
    2. 선생님이 계세요?
    3. 선생님이 하세요?
    4. 선생님이 와요?
    Show the answer

    선생님이 계세요?

  4. translate into English즉, 지하철이 좋아요!

    Show the answer

    In short, the subway is good!

  5. fill the gap와___ 가요! (even if)

    Show the answer

    도

  6. choose'Have you tried kimchi?'

    1. 김치를 먹어요?
    2. 김치를 먹어 봤어요?
    3. 김치를 먹고 싶어요?
    4. 김치를 먹었어요 어제?
    Show the answer

    김치를 먹어 봤어요?

  7. put it in orderOrder: 왔어요 / 눈이 / 갑자기

    Show the answer

    갑자기 눈이 왔어요

  8. say it out loudSay aloud: 즉, 지하철이 좋아요!

    Show the answer

    즉, 지하철이 좋아요!

  9. fill the gap한국에 가___ 했어요. (decided to)

    Show the answer

    기로

  10. translate into the languageSay: Long ago, I went to Seoul

    Show the answer

    옛날 옛적에 서울에 갔어요

After B2: the B2+ checkpoint

B2+ is not a certificate — it is the half-step between this level and the next one, and it is on this site’s ladder because language schools use it. You are at B2+ when you can run a meeting, read a contract, disagree well. Get there by working the recall drills above and the review deck until nothing on this page surprises you, then open the B3 pages.
