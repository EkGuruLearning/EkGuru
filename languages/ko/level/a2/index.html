A2, unit by unit

Past and desires

Past: 먹었어요/였어요

Past power! 먹어요→먹었어요 (ate), 가요→갔어요 (went). 입니다→이었어요/였어요: 학생이었어요 (was a student). Time words: 어제 (yesterday), 오늘 (today), 내일 (tomorrow)!

었/았어요 · 이었어요

먹→먹었어요, 가→갔어요, 하→했어요. 학생이었어요 (was a student), 아니었어요 (wasn't). 이미 + past = already: 이미 먹었어요!

었/았어요 · 이었어요/였어요
WordSay itMeaning
어제 책을 읽었어요.eoje chaeg-eul ilg-eosseoyo.Yesterday I read a book.
학생이었어요.haksaeng-i-eoss-eoyo.I was a student.
이미 먹었어요.imi meog-eoss-eoyo.I already ate.
Watch out:
  • Saying 먹었어요 for habits — 었어요 = finished past!
  • Saying 입니다었어요 — 입니다 becomes 이었어요/였어요!

Want: -고 싶어요

Desire grammar! -고 싶어요 (I want to…): 먹고 싶어요 (want to eat), 가고 싶어요 (want to go). Requests: -아/어 주세요 (please do): 봐 주세요! Want + please = unstoppable!

WordSay itMeaning

마시고 싶어요masigo sip-eoyowant to drink
가고 싶어요gago sip-eoyowant to go
보고 싶어요bogo sip-eoyowant to see / miss
자고 싶어요jago sip-eoyowant to sleep
쉬고 싶어요swigo sip-eoyowant to rest
놀고 싶어요nolgo sip-eoyowant to play

-고 싶어요 · -아/어 주세요

먹고 싶어요 (want to eat), 자고 싶어요 (want to sleep). 보고 싶어요 ALSO means I MISS you! Requests: 도와주세요 (please help), 보여 주세요 (please show)!

verb-고 싶어요 · verb-아/어 주세요
WordSay itMeaning

보고 싶어요!bogo sip-eoyo!I miss you! / I want to see (you)!

Watch out:
  • Saying 먹고 싶다 to elders — polite: 싶어요!
  • Saying 보고 싶어 for missing — needs 보고 싶어요!

Places and movement

Particles: 에/에서/으로

Place particles! 에 (to/at-point), 에서 (action-at/from), 으로 (toward/by). 학교에 가요 (go to school), 학교에서 공부해요 (study AT school), 버스로 가요 (go BY bus)! 여기/거기/저기 = here/there/over there!

에 (to) · 에서 (at) · 으로 (by)

학교에 가요 (go to school), 집에 있어요 (am at home), 학교에서 공부해요 (study at school), 버스로 가요 (go by bus)!

The A2 test — 10 items

The level test closes the level. Answers are shown; in the course player the same test decides whether the next level unlocks.

  1. translate into the languageSay: Yesterday I read a book

    Show the answer

    어제 책을 읽었어요

  2. translate into the languageSay: I want to eat kimchi

    Show the answer

    김치를 먹고 싶어요

  3. choose'I go to school'

    1. 학교에서 가요
    2. 학교에 가요
    3. 학교를 가요
    4. 학교에 공부해요
    Show the answer

    학교에 가요

  4. translate into English생선을 좋아해요.

    Show the answer

    I like fish.

  5. fill the gap학생___. (was)

    Show the answer

    이었어요

  6. choose'I miss you!'

    1. 보고 있어요
    2. 보고 싶어요
    3. 보고 싶었어요
    4. 볼 거예요
    Show the answer

    보고 싶어요

  7. put it in orderOrder: 가요 / 여섯 시에

    Show the answer

    여섯 시에 가요

  8. say it out loudSay aloud: 생선을 좋아해요.

    Show the answer

    생선을 좋아해요.

  9. fill the gap이거 ___? (how much)

    Show the answer

    얼마예요

  10. choose다녀오겠습니다!

    1. Welcome home
    2. I'm off (and back)
    3. Goodbye forever
    4. Good night

    I'm off (and back)

After A2: the A2+ checkpoint

A2+ is not a certificate — it is the half-step between this level and the next one, and it is on this site’s ladder because language schools use it. You are at A2+ when you can keep a conversation going, tell a short story. Get there by working the recall drills above and the review deck until nothing on this page surprises you, then open the A3 pages.
