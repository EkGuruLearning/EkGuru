How the levels work

Every course here is written in the same order, so you always know what you are getting. A1 is survival: greetings, numbers, asking for things, reading your first words. A2 is sentences you can use in a shop, a classroom or an office. B1 and B2 are where you stop translating in your head — longer texts, ordinary conversation, opinions. C1 and C2 are for people who need the language for work or study: nuance, registers, idioms, and the mistakes that sound foreign even when the grammar is correct.

A level is only listed as finished when its lessons, its quiz bank and its review cards all exist. Nothing here is a placeholder: if a language shows a starter pack, that is what it is, and it says so on the card.
