Polish A1 — Polish A1

The whole Polish A1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

Who this level is for. A1 is one of the six CEFR levels and the A1 rung on this site’s ladder: the learner in the picture is a child, because on this site age is the rung — the same learner grows up the ladder as the language grows. By the end of it you can greet, count to twenty, read a shop sign.

What you can do when A1 is finished

  • Read core Polish spelling and pronounce key contrasts
  • Introduce yourself and manage basic daily exchanges
  • Use present-tense verbs and introductory nominative, instrumental, accusative, and locative patterns

Powitania i pan/pani

Polish marks grammatical roles with endings. At A1, learn each noun together with its sentence pattern: nominative for naming, instrumental after jestem, accusative for a direct object, and locative after selected prepositions. Verbs agree with person; pan/pani requires third-person grammar.

subject or polite address + agreeing verb + case-marked complement
WordSay itMeaning
Dzień dobry, mam na imię Anna.Dzień dobry, mam na imię Anna.Good morning, my name is Anna.
Jestem studentką, a pan jest nauczycielem.Jestem studentką, a pan jest nauczycielem.I am a student, and you are a teacher.
Codziennie rano uczę się polskiego.Codziennie rano uczę się polskiego.I study Polish every morning.
Watch out:
  • {'wrong': 'Jestem studentka.', 'right': 'Jestem studentką.', 'why': 'The complement after jestem uses the instrumental case.'}
