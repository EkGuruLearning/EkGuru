Dystans epistemiczny

Polish combines seven-case government, gender and number agreement, lexical aspect, derivational prefixes, relatively flexible word order, and prosody. The selected form encodes participant roles, event boundaries, topic, focus, and social register.

topic + aspectually marked predicate + case-governed arguments + stance
