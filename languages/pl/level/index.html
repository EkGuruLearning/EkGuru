Polish levels — all six, A1 to C2

Polish runs the same six levels as every other course here, and the same eleven rungs as the rest of the site. Below is the whole ladder for Polish: what each level holds, how many questions are in it, and who the learner in the picture is — a child at the first rung, an elder by the last.

The whole course, counted. 36 lessons, 288 words with romanisation and 1248 questions with answers — every one of them readable on the level pages below.

The eleven rungs, in pictures

CEFR has six levels. This site also names the half-step after each of the first five — A1+, A2+, B1+, B2+, C1+ — because that is the honest label for the learner who has finished the lessons but is not yet ready for the next level. There is no A3 and no C3–C5; a page that prints them is a page an examiner stops trusting. The full explanation is here.
