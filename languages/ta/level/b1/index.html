Tamil B1 — B1 Intermediate Tamil

The whole Tamil B1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

What this level is. B1 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the B1 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can explain a problem, write a message, follow a podcast.
