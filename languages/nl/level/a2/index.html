The whole Dutch A2 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 47 words, 206 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

What this level is. A2 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the A2 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can talk about your day, shop, order food.
