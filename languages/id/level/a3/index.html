akan / mau + verba

Rencana yang sudah diputuskan.

saya akan mengulang malam ini
WordSay itMeaning
Saya akan mengulang malam ini.I'm going to revise tonight.
Dia akan bolos kuliah.She's going to miss the lecture.
Kamu mau menyelesaikan PR?Are you going to finish the homework?
Watch out:
  • Jangan ‘saya akan untuk mengulang’.
  • akan + verba mentah.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
AnaBesok ada ujian?Have you got an exam tomorrow?
LuisYa, sejarah. Habis kelas saya akan mengulang.Yes, history. I'll revise after class.
AnaBelajar di perpustakaan?Shall we study in the library?
LuisYa. Tenggat catatan hari ini.Yes. The notes deadline is today.

suka + verba

Suka diikuti verba atau nomina.

saya suka main gitar
WordSay itMeaning
Saya suka main gitar.I like playing guitar.
Dia tidak suka menunggu.She doesn't like waiting.
Kamu suka menggambar di akhir pekan?Do you like drawing at weekends?
Watch out:
  • Bukan ‘saya suka untuk main’.
  • tidak suka, bukan tidak menyukai selalu.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
AshaAkhir pekan kamu ngapain?What do you do at the weekend?
BenMain gitar. Sabtu ada konser.I play guitar. There's a concert on Saturday.
AshaSaya suka menggambar. Ikut?I like drawing. Are you coming?
BenHabis latihan.Yes, after practice.

harus + verba

Kewajiban luar.

kamu harus tunjukkan paspor
WordSay itMeaning
Kamu harus tunjukkan paspor.You have to show your passport.
Kita harus transit di Bandung.We have to change in Bandung.
Dia tidak harus memesan hari ini.He doesn't have to book today.
Watch out:
  • harus + verba, tanpa ‘untuk’ wajib.
  • tidak harus ≠ dilarang.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
LoketSekali jalan atau pulang-pergi?Single or return?
NinaPulang-pergi. Peron berapa?Return. Which platform?
LoketEmpat. Ada keterlambatan singkat.Platform 4. There's a short delay.
NinaHarus transit?Do I have to change?

WordSay itMeaning
Saya mau sop.I'd like the soup.
Mau pedas?Would you like it spicy?
Boleh lihat menu?I'd like to see the menu.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

PelayanSedikit. Ada alergi?A little. Are you allergic?

Saran, bukan perintah.

bagaimana kalau ketemu jam tujuh?
WordSay itMeaning
Bagaimana kalau ketemu jam tujuh?Why don't we meet at seven?
Minggu depan saja?What if we catch up next week?
Kita bawa makanan.Let's bring some food.
Watch out:
  • ‘Tidak’ polos terasa keras: beri alasan.
  • bagaimana kalau + klausa.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
RiyaJumat ada pesta. Pergi?There's a party Friday. Shall we go?
OmarJumat sibuk. Minggu depan?I'm busy Friday. Next week?
RiyaMinggu? Setelah jam enam saya luang.Sunday? I'm free after six.
OmarSip. Saya bawa pencuci mulut.Perfect. I'll bring dessert.

Syarat nyata.

kalau hujan, kita batalkan
WordSay itMeaning
Kalau hujan, kita batalkan piknik.If it rains, we'll cancel the picnic.
Kalau prakiraan bagus, kita jalan.If the forecast is good, we'll walk.
Kalau tidak cerah, kita di dalam.If it doesn't clear up, we'll stay inside.
Watch out:
  • Jangan tumpuk akan di klausa kalau.
  • kalau = syarat.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
JenPrakiraan bilang gerimis.The forecast says showers.
KaiKalau hujan, ke museum.If it rains, we'll go to the museum.
JenTetap bawa payung.Take an umbrella anyway.
KaiKalau cerah, jalan di sungai.If it clears up, we'll walk by the river.
