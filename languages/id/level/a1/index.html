Indonesian A1 — Indonesian A1

The whole Indonesian A1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 47 words, 206 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

What this level is. A1 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the A1 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can greet, count to twenty, read a shop sign.

Salam dan perkenalan

Indonesian combines productive meN-, ber-, di-, ter-, peN-, -kan and -i morphology with aspect particles and flexible topic structure. Active-passive choice tracks discourse perspective; formal and colloquial forms serve different settings without being interchangeable.
