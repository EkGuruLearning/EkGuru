Motion and aspects

Motion: идти vs ходить

Russian splits GOING in two! ИДТИ = going right now (one way); ХОДИТЬ = go regularly/there-and-back. Same for riding: ЕХАТЬ (now) vs ЕЗДИТЬ (usually). Я ИДУ = I'm going; Я ХОЖУ = I go (habitually)!

One-way vs habitual motion

ИДТИ/ЕХАТЬ = right now, one direction: Я ИДУ В ШКОЛУ (I'm going). ХОДИТЬ/ЕЗДИТЬ = habits and round trips: Я ХОЖУ В ШКОЛУ (I go/attend). КУДА ТЫ ИДЁШЬ? = Where are you going (now)?

Aspect: читал vs прочитал

Two verbs for one idea! ЧИТАТЬ = to read (process); ПРОЧИТАТЬ = to read (finish it!). Past: Я ЧИТАЛ = I was reading; Я ПРОЧИТАЛ = I read (done!). Prefixes finish actions: ДЕЛАТЬ→СДЕЛАТЬ, ПИСАТЬ→НАПИСÁТЬ!

WordSay itMeaning
делатьDYEH-lahtyto do (process)
сделатьZDYEH-lahtyto do (complete)
читатьchee-TAHTYto read (process)
прочитатьprah-chee-TAHTYto read (finish)
писатьpee-SAHTYto write (process)
написатьnah-pee-SAHTYto write (finish)
естьyehstyto eat
съестьsyehstyto eat up
Aspect pairs: process vs result

Imperfective = process (Я ЧИТАЛ = I was reading). Perfective = result (Я ПРОЧИТАЛ = I finished it). Past perfective looks the same: ПРОЧИТАЛ/ПРОЧИТАЛА. Ask: finished? → perfective!

читал (was -ing) · прочитал (finished)
WordSay itMeaning
Я читал книгу.yah chee-TAHL KNEE-goo.I was reading a book.
Я прочитал книгу.yah prah-chee-TAHL KNEE-goo.I read (finished) the book.
Ты сделал уроки?tih ZDYEH-lah-oo-ROH-kee?Did you finish your homework?
Watch out:
  • Using ЧИТАЛ for finished books — finished = ПРОЧИТАЛ!
  • Using perfective in present — perfective has no present!

Dative and people

Dative: мне нравится

LIKES flip too! МНЕ НРАВИТСЯ = To-me it-pleases = I like it. Dative pronouns: МНЕ, ТЕБЕ, ЕМУ, ЕЙ, НАМ, ВАМ, ИМ. ДАЙ МНЕ = Give me. Russians give everything TO people!

Мне нравится = I like

МНЕ НРАВИТСЯ КНИГА = I like the book (to-me pleases the-book). Plural: МНЕ НРАВЯТСЯ КНИГИ. The THING is the subject — it pleases you!
