Negation scope and focus

Russian uses six-case government, verbal aspect, participial and adverbial-participial compression, impersonal constructions, evidential particles, information-structure word order, negation scope and formal–colloquial register. The model coordinates grammar with commitment, information structure, social distance, and cohesion; mediation must preserve those relations.
