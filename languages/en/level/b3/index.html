Could you + verb? / Would you mind + -ing?
WordSay itMeaning

WordSay itMeaning

reported speech (say/tell)

Backshift the verb when the reporting verb is past.

They said (that) + past
WordSay itMeaning
The paper said the talks had failed.backshift
She told me the source was unnamed.tell + object
According to witnesses, nobody was hurt.attribution
Watch out:
  • Tell needs a person: tell me, not tell that.
  • Don't present a claim as a fact.

Formal versus informal register

Rewrite a casual message as a formal one without changing the facts.

WordSay itMeaning
registerlevel of formality
slangvery informal words
requestpolite ask
apologisesay sorry
enquireask formally
yours sincerelyformal sign-off
cheersinformal thanks
toneattitude in wording
register recasting

Same meaning, different social distance.

Can you…? → I would be grateful if you could…
WordSay itMeaning
Can you send it? → I would be grateful if you could send it.up-shift
Sorry I'm late. → Please accept my apologies for the delay.apology
Cheers. → Yours sincerely.sign-off
Watch out:
  • Don't mix slang with a formal greeting.
  • Yours sincerely follows a named Dear X.

Opinions and debate

Agree, disagree and concede without shutting the other person down.

WordSay itMeaning
pointargument item

Name the other view before you disagree.

While X is true, Y…
WordSay itMeaning
While the cost is real, the evidence is thin.concede+contrast
I see your point. On the other hand…soft disagree
Fair enough — let's look for a compromise.close
Watch out:
  • Don't start with 'You're wrong'.
  • Don't overstate a small sample as proof.

used to vs be used to

Used to + verb is a past habit. Be used to + -ing is familiarity.

used to + verb / be used to + -ing
WordSay itMeaning
We used to remove shoes at the door.past habit
I'm used to eating late.familiar now
She isn't used to such direct questions.not familiar
Watch out:
  • Don't say 'I am used to go'.
  • Don't mix the two frames.

Advice with different force.

You should… / You'd better… (stronger)
WordSay itMeaning
You should rest until you recover.advice
You'd better book an appointment today.stronger

Watch out:
  • Had better is a warning, not a casual idea.
  • Don't self-prescribe from a headline.
