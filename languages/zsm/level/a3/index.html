Keputusan yang sudah dibuat.

saya akan ulangkaji malam ini
WordSay itMeaning
Saya akan ulangkaji malam ini.I'm going to revise tonight.
Dia akan tertinggal kuliah.She's going to miss the lecture.
Awak nak habiskan kerja rumah?Are you going to finish the homework?
Watch out:
  • Jangan ‘saya akan untuk ulangkaji’.
  • akan + kata kerja.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
AnaEsok ada peperiksaan?Have you got an exam tomorrow?
LuisAda, sejarah. Lepas kelas saya akan ulangkaji.Yes, history. I'll revise after class.
AnaBelajar di perpustakaan?Shall we study in the library?
LuisYa. Tarikh akhir nota hari ini.Yes. The notes deadline is today.

Suka diikuti kata kerja atau nama.

saya suka main gitar
WordSay itMeaning
Saya suka main gitar.I like playing guitar.
Dia tak suka menunggu.She doesn't like waiting.
Awak suka melukis hujung minggu?Do you like drawing at weekends?
Watch out:
  • Bukan ‘saya suka untuk main’.
  • tak suka, bukan haram.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
AshaHujung minggu awak buat apa?What do you do at the weekend?
BenMain gitar. Sabtu ada konsert.I play guitar. There's a concert on Saturday.
AshaSaya suka melukis. Nak ikut?I like drawing. Are you coming?
BenLepas latihan.Yes, after practice.

Kewajipan luar.

awak kena tunjuk pasport
WordSay itMeaning
Awak kena tunjuk pasport.You have to show your passport.
Kita perlu bertukar di Ipoh.We have to change in Ipoh.
Dia tak perlu tempah hari ini.He doesn't have to book today.
Watch out:
  • kena + kata kerja.
  • tak perlu ≠ dilarang.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
KaunterSehala atau pergi balik?Single or return?
NinaPergi balik. Platform mana?Return. Which platform?
KaunterEmpat. Ada kelewatan sikit.Platform 4. There's a short delay.
NinaPerlu bertukar ke?Do I have to change?

Pesanan sopan.

saya nak sup
WordSay itMeaning
Saya nak sup.I'd like the soup.
Nak pedas?Would you like it spicy?
Boleh tengok menu?I'd like to see the menu.
Watch out:
  • Pada pelayan, ‘bagi ni’ kasar.
  • nak + nama.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
PelayanBoleh order?Ready to order?
SamSaya nak pasta. Pedas ke?I'd like the pasta. Is it spicy?
PelayanSikit. Ada alahan?A little. Are you allergic?
SamTak. Bil kat hujung, terima kasih.No. The bill at the end, please.

Cadangan.

WordSay itMeaning
Jom jumpa jam tujuh?Why don't we meet at seven?
Minggu depan?What if we catch up next week?

  • ‘Tidak’ kosong keras: beri sebab.

WhoLineSay itEnglish

OmarOkay. Saya bawa pencuci mulut.Perfect. I'll bring dessert.

Syarat nyata.

kalau hujan, kita batal
WordSay itMeaning
Kalau hujan, kita batal piknik.If it rains, we'll cancel the picnic.
Kalau ramalan elok, kita jalan.If the forecast is good, we'll walk.
Kalau tak cerah, kita duduk dalam.If it doesn't clear up, we'll stay inside.
Watch out:
  • Jangan tindan akan dalam klausa kalau.
  • kalau = syarat.

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

WhoLineSay itEnglish
JenRamalan kata hujan renyai.The forecast says showers.
KaiKalau hujan, ke muzium.If it rains, we'll go to the museum.
JenBawa payung juga.Take an umbrella anyway.
KaiKalau cerah, jalan tepi sungai.If it clears up, we'll walk by the river.
