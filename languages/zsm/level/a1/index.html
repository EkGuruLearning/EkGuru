Standard Malay A1 — Standard Malay A1

The whole Standard Malay A1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

Who this level is for. A1 is one of the six CEFR levels and the A1 rung on this site’s ladder: the learner in the picture is a child, because on this site age is the rung — the same learner grows up the ladder as the language grows. By the end of it you can greet, count to twenty, read a shop sign.

Salam dan sapaan

Standard Malay combines analytic time marking, classifiers, reduplication, derivational and voice affixes, SVO/topic-comment order, relative yang, social address, and prosody. Choices encode event structure, participant prominence, evidence, focus, and register.

topic + voice/affix-marked predicate + arguments + stance
