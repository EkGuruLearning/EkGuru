A2, unit by unit

Past and future

Past tense: درستُ

The past wears SUFFIXES! درستُ (I studied), درستَ (you-m studied), درستِ (you-f studied), درسَ (he studied), درسَتْ (she studied). One stem درسـ + five tails. Your tail tells who!

WordSay itMeaning
أمسamsyesterday
اليومal-yawmtoday
غداghadantomorrow
صباحاṣabāḥanin the morning
مساءmasāʾanin the evening
ثمthummathen
قبلqablabefore
بعدbaʿdaafter
Past suffixes: -تُ/-تَ/-تِ/-َ/-َتْ

درستُ (I), درستَ (you-m), درستِ (you-f), درسَ (he), درسَتْ (she). She adds -تْ to his form! Negate past with ما: ما درستُ = I didn't study.

stem + تُ/تَ/تِ/َ/َتْ
WordSay itMeaning
درستُ العربية أمس.darastu al-ʿarabiyya ams.I studied Arabic yesterday.
هي درستْ في المساء.hiya darasat fī al-masāʾ.She studied in the evening.
ما درستُ.mā darastu.I didn't study.
Watch out:
  • Saying درستَ for 'I studied' — I takes درستُ!
  • Saying هو درستْ — HE takes درسَ (no ت)!

Future: سأدرس

Two futures! سـ + present (سأدرس = I will study) for quick plans, سوف + present (سوف أدرس) for bigger ones. Just glue سـ onto the present verb — done!

WordSay itMeaning
قريباqarībansoon
بعد قليلbaʿda qalīlin a little while
بعد ساعةbaʿda sāʿain an hour
الأسبوع القادمal-usbūʿ al-qādimnext week
يوم الاثنينyawm al-ithnaynon Monday
دائماdāʾimanalways
أحياناaḥyānansometimes
أبداabadannever
سـ / سوف + present

سأدرس (I will study), ستدرس (you will), سيدرس (he will). سوف أدرس = same, grander. Negate future with لن: لن أذهب = I won't go.

سـ/سوف + present · لن negates
WordSay itMeaning
سأدرس العربية.sa-adrus al-ʿarabiyya.I will study Arabic.
سوف نتكلم!sawfa natakallam!We will speak!
لن أذهب.lan adhhab.I won't go.
Watch out:
  • Gluing سـ to the PAST: سـ loves PRESENT verbs!
  • Negating future with ما — future needs لن!

Descriptions

Adjectives: الكتاب الكبير

Describe everything! Adjectives FOLLOW nouns and MATCH: الكتاب الكبير (the big book), السيارة الكبيرة (the big car-f). ال on BOTH words! Without ال: كتاب كبير (a big book) — match but no ال!

WordSay itMeaning
مدينةmadīnacity
شارعshāriʿstreet
محلmaḥallshop
عملʿamalwork
حديقةḥadīqagarden / park
متحفmatḥafmuseum
محطةmaḥaṭṭastation
جديدjadīdnew
Noun + matching adjective

كتاب كبير (a big book), الكتاب الكبير (THE big book). Feminine: سيارة كبيرة. Sun letters (ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن) swallow the L: الشارع = ash-shāriʿ!

noun + adj (both ال or both bare)
WordSay itMeaning
الكتاب الكبير.al-kitāb al-kabīr.The big book.
سيارة جديدة.sayyāra jadīda.A new car.
الشارع الجديد.ash-shāriʿ al-jadīd.The new street.
Watch out:
  • Saying الكتاب كبير for 'the big book' — needs الكتاب الكبير!
  • Saying سيارة كبير — feminine needs كبيرة!

Places: في، إلى، من

Move around! في (in/at), إلى (to), من (from): في البيت (at home), إلى المدرسة (to school), من مصر (from Egypt). أين؟ (where?) vs إلى أين؟ (where to?) — tiny إلى, big difference!

في / إلى / من

أنا في البيت (I'm at home), أذهب إلى المدرسة (I go to school), أنا من مصر (I'm from Egypt). Verbs of going demand إلى!

بكم هذا؟ = How much?

بكم هذا؟ (How much is this?), بكم الخبز؟ (How much is the bread?). Answer: بعشرة جنيه (for ten pounds). هذا غالٍ! = This is expensive!

The A2 test — 10 items

The level test closes the level. Answers are shown; in the course player the same test decides whether the next level unlocks.

  1. translate into the languageSay: I studied Arabic yesterday

    Show the answer

    درستُ العربية أمس

  2. translate into the languageSay: I will study Arabic

    Show the answer

    سأدرس العربية

  3. choose'The big book'

    1. كتاب كبير
    2. الكتاب الكبير
    3. الكتاب كبير
    4. كتاب الكبير
    Show the answer

    الكتاب الكبير

  4. translate into Englishأسكن في القاهرة.

    Show the answer

    I live in Cairo.

  5. fill the gapهي ___ في المساء. (studied, f)

    Show the answer

    درستْ

  6. choose'I'm from Egypt'

    1. أنا في مصر
    2. أنا إلى مصر
    3. أنا من مصر
    4. أنا مصر
    Show the answer

    أنا من مصر

  7. put it in orderOrder: القاهرة / في / أسكن

    Show the answer

    أسكن في القاهرة

  8. say it out loudSay aloud: أحب الجبن والخبز.

    Show the answer

After A2: the A2+ checkpoint

A2+ is not a certificate — it is the half-step between this level and the next one, and it is on this site’s ladder because language schools use it. You are at A2+ when you can keep a conversation going, tell a short story. Get there by working the recall drills above and the review deck until nothing on this page surprises you, then open the A3 pages.
