Past and future

Past tense: درستُ

The past wears SUFFIXES! درستُ (I studied), درستَ (you-m studied), درستِ (you-f studied), درسَ (he studied), درسَتْ (she studied). One stem درسـ + five tails. Your tail tells who!

WordSay itMeaning
أمسamsyesterday
اليومal-yawmtoday
غداghadantomorrow
صباحاṣabāḥanin the morning
مساءmasāʾanin the evening
ثمthummathen
قبلqablabefore
بعدbaʿdaafter
Past suffixes: -تُ/-تَ/-تِ/-َ/-َتْ

درستُ (I), درستَ (you-m), درستِ (you-f), درسَ (he), درسَتْ (she). She adds -تْ to his form! Negate past with ما: ما درستُ = I didn't study.

stem + تُ/تَ/تِ/َ/َتْ
WordSay itMeaning
درستُ العربية أمس.darastu al-ʿarabiyya ams.I studied Arabic yesterday.
هي درستْ في المساء.hiya darasat fī al-masāʾ.She studied in the evening.
ما درستُ.mā darastu.I didn't study.
Watch out:
  • Saying درستَ for 'I studied' — I takes درستُ!
  • Saying هو درستْ — HE takes درسَ (no ت)!

Future: سأدرس

Two futures! سـ + present (سأدرس = I will study) for quick plans, سوف + present (سوف أدرس) for bigger ones. Just glue سـ onto the present verb — done!

WordSay itMeaning
قريباqarībansoon
بعد قليلbaʿda qalīlin a little while
بعد ساعةbaʿda sāʿain an hour
الأسبوع القادمal-usbūʿ al-qādimnext week
يوم الاثنينyawm al-ithnaynon Monday
دائماdāʾimanalways
أحياناaḥyānansometimes
أبداabadannever
سـ / سوف + present

سأدرس (I will study), ستدرس (you will), سيدرس (he will). سوف أدرس = same, grander. Negate future with لن: لن أذهب = I won't go.

سـ/سوف + present · لن negates
WordSay itMeaning

سوف نتكلم!sawfa natakallam!We will speak!

Watch out:
  • Gluing سـ to the PAST: سـ loves PRESENT verbs!
  • Negating future with ما — future needs لن!

Pinning time: أمس، الآن، غداً

Arabic often lets the time word carry what English does with tense: أمس with a perfect verb, غداً with سـ, الآن with the present. Put the time word first and it frames everything after it.

WordSay itMeaning
أمسamsyesterday
غداًghadantomorrow
الآنal-ānnow
دائماًdāʾimanalways
بعد قليلbaʿda qalīlin a little while
Time word + tense

غداً سأدرس (tomorrow I will study). أمس درستُ (yesterday I studied). الآن أدرس (now I am studying). The time word does not change the verb — but it must agree with it.

time word first + verb in the matching form
WordSay itMeaning
غداً سأسافر إلى دبي.ghadan sa-usāfir ilā Dubayy.Tomorrow I will travel to Dubai.
الآن ندرس العربية.al-ān nadrus al-ʿarabiyya.Now we are studying Arabic.
دائماً نشرب القهوة صباحاً.dāʾiman nashrab al-qahwa ṣabāḥan.We always drink coffee in the morning.
Watch out:
  • غداً درستُ الدرس. → غداً سأدرس الدرس. غداً cannot sit with a past verb.
  • دائماً أشرب القهوة أمس. → أمس شربتُ القهوة. A frequency word and a one-time past word contradict each other.

Describe everything! Adjectives FOLLOW nouns and MATCH: الكتاب الكبير (the big book), السيارة الكبيرة (the big car-f). ال on BOTH words! Without ال: كتاب كبير (a big book) — match but no ال!

Culture note

The Arabic past tense is not only 'past': with قد and the perfect form it also reports news the listener has not heard yet, which is why the same sentence can mean 'he wrote' and 'he has written'. Learners who translate word-for-word miss that the verb form is doing the work of an English tense-and-aspect pair, and Arabic instead leans on time words — أمس، الآن، غداً — to place the action.

Source (named, not linked): en.wikipedia.org, the article “Arabic verbs”.

أمس ذهبتُ إلى السوق مع أخي. اشتريتُ خبزاً وفاكهة، ثمّ شربنا القهوة في مقهى صغير. قال لي: «سأسافر غداً إلى بيروت». سألتُ: «لماذا؟» فأجاب: «لأنّ عملي هناك». في المساء درسنا معاً، ونحن الآن متعبان قليلاً.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • أنا ذهبت أمس إلى السوق. → ذهبتُ أمس إلى السوق. The perfect ending -تُ already means 'I'; keeping أنا is not wrong but sounds heavy in a plain past narrative.
  • سأذهب أمس. → ذهبتُ أمس. سـ marks the future only; it cannot sit with أمس.
  • هذا الكتاب كبير والكتاب هذا كبير أيضاً. → هذا الكتاب كبير وهذا الكتاب كبير أيضاً. Two full noun phrases need a joining و and one demonstrative each; repeating the whole phrase without a connector is a run-on.

You are at A2+ when you can keep a conversation going, tell a short story. Get there by working the recall drills above and the review deck until nothing on this page surprises you, then open the B1 pages.
