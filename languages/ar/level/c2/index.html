Scope, focus, and negation

Arabic uses case-sensitive formal phrasing, verbal and nominal sentences, إنّ and أنّ complement structures, كان وأخواتها, passive and impersonal framing, discourse particles, diglossic register and politeness. Here these resources control commitment, information structure, social distance, and cohesion. C2 production must preserve scope when audience or genre changes.
