First words

Greetings: السلام عليكم

The famous greeting! السلام عليكم (peace upon you) → answer وعليكم السلام (and upon you peace). Morning: صباح الخير → answer صباح النور! Greetings always come in PAIRS.

WordSay itMeaning
السلام عليكمas-salāmu ʿalaykumpeace upon you (hello)
وعليكم السلامwa ʿalaykum as-salāmand upon you peace (reply)
مرحباmarḥabanhello
صباح الخيرṣabāḥ al-khayrgood morning
مساء الخيرmasāʾ al-khayrgood evening
شكراshukranthanks
عفواʿafwanyou're welcome / excuse me
مع السلامةmaʿa as-salāmagoodbye
Greetings come in pairs

السلام عليكم → وعليكم السلام. صباح الخير → صباح النور (morning of light!). مساء الخير → مساء النور. Never answer a greeting with the same greeting — use its PAIR!

greeting → pair-answer
WordSay itMeaning
السلام عليكم!as-salāmu ʿalaykum!Peace upon you!
وعليكم السلام!wa ʿalaykum as-salām!And upon you peace!

Watch out:
  • Answering السلام عليكم with السلام عليكم — reply وعليكم السلام!
  • Saying صباح الخير at night — evenings need مساء الخير!

Names and how are you

اسمي ليلى = My name is Layla. Ask: ما اسمك؟ (m) / ما اسمكِ؟ (f) — -KA for men, -KI for women! How are you: كيف حالك؟ → بخير، الحمد لله (fine, praise God)!

WordSay itMeaning
ما اسمكmā ismuk / ismukīwhat is your name
اسميismīmy name is
كيف حالكkayfa ḥāluk / ḥālukihow are you
بخيرbikhayrfine / well
الحمد للهal-ḥamdu lillāhpraise God
وأنتwa anta / antiand you
من فضلكmin faḍlikplease
نعم / لاnaʿam / lāyes / no
-ka (m) / -ki (f): your

اسمكَ (ismuka) = your name (to a man), اسمكِ (ismukī) = your name (to a woman). حالكَ/حالكِ the same. اسمي = MY name (works for everyone). Check who you talk to — pick -KA or -KI!

-ka (to men) · -ki (to women)
WordSay itMeaning

ما اسمكِ؟ (to Layla)mā ismukī?What is your name? (f)
اسمي ليلى.ismī laylā.My name is Layla.
Watch out:
  • Saying اسمك to a woman — women get اسمكِ (-KI)!
  • Forgetting الحمد لله after بخير — it always follows!

Gender and plurals

Gender: هذا / هذه

Two genders! هذا = this (m), هذه = this (f). Feminine usually ends in ة (ta marbuta): مدرسة (school-f), سيارة (car-f). No ة? Usually masculine: كتاب (book-m), بيت (house-m)!

WordSay itMeaning
هذاhādhāthis (m)
هذهhādhihīthis (f)
رجلrajulman
امرأةimraʾawoman
ولدwaladboy
بنتbintgirl
كبيرkabīrbig (m)
صغيرṣaghīrsmall (m)
هذا (m) / هذه (f) + ة

هذا كتاب (this is a book-m), هذه مدرسة (this is a school-f). Adjectives match: رجل كبير (big man), امرأة كبيرة (big woman) — feminine adds ة!

هذا + m · هذه + f (ة)
WordSay itMeaning
هذا كتاب.hādhā kitāb.This is a book.
هذه مدرسة.hādhihī madrasa.This is a school.
امرأة كبيرة.imraʾa kabīra.A big woman.
Watch out:
  • Saying هذا مدرسة — ة-words need هذه!
  • Saying امرأة كبير — feminine needs كبيرة!

Plurals: كتاب → كتب

Plurals transform! Sound feminine: سيارة → سيارات (add -ات). People masculine: مدرس → مدرسون (-ون). And BROKEN plurals reshape the word: كتاب → كتب, قلم → أقلام. Learn each word WITH its plural!

The A1 test — 10 items

The level test closes the level. Answers are shown; in the course player the same test decides whether the next level unlocks.

  1. translate into the languageSay: Peace upon you!

    Show the answer

    السلام عليكم!

  2. fill the gapصباح ___! (morning reply)

    Show the answer

    النور

  3. chooseReply to السلام عليكم

    1. السلام عليكم
    2. وعليكم السلام
    3. صباح الخير
    4. مع السلامة
    Show the answer

    وعليكم السلام

  4. translate into the languageSay: I study Arabic

    Show the answer

    أنا أدرس العربية

  5. translate into Englishعندي ماء.

    Show the answer

    I have water.

  6. fill the gap___ مدرسة. (This, f)

    Show the answer

    هذه

  7. put it in orderOrder: ليلى / اسمي

    Show the answer

    اسمي ليلى

  8. say it out loudSay aloud: أنا أدرس العربية.

    Show the answer

    أنا أدرس العربية.

  9. fill the gapما عندي ___. (time)

    Show the answer

    وقت

    1. I speak English

    4. You speak English
    Show the answer

After A1: the A1+ checkpoint

A1+ is not a certificate — it is the half-step between this level and the next one, and it is on this site’s ladder because language schools use it. You are at A1+ when you can ask for things, give a phone number, name the days. Get there by working the recall drills above and the review deck until nothing on this page surprises you, then open the A2 pages.
