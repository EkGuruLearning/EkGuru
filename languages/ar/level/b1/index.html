Past stories and dual

كان: was and were

كان = was/were! كان كبيرا (he was big), كانت جميلة (she was beautiful). Stories: كان يدرس = he WAS studying (كان + present = was doing!). Unlock the storytelling past!

WordSay itMeaning
كانkānahe was
كانتkānatshe was
عندماʿindamāwhen
بينماbaynamāwhile
فجأةfajʾatansuddenly
مرةmarraonce / a time
قصةqiṣṣastory
حفلةḥaflaparty
كان + word / كان + present

كان مدرسا (he was a teacher), كانت جميلة (she was beautiful). كان يدرس = he was studying. كنتُ (I was), كنتَ (you-m were), كنتِ (you-f were)!

كان/كانت + noun/adj · كان + present
WordSay itMeaning

كانت في الحفلة.kānat fī al-ḥafla.She was at the party.
Watch out:
  • Saying أنا كان — I-was is كنتُ!
  • Saying كانت جميلةٌ with ة-damma — كان's خبر takes -اً/-ةً!

Dual: كتابان

Arabic has a DUAL — a special TWO form! كتابان (two books), يومان (two days), مدرستان (two schools-f). Just add -ان! 1–2 come AFTER nouns: كتاب واحد, يومان اثنان!

WordSay itMeaning
اثنانithnāntwo
كتابانkitābāntwo books
يومانyawmāntwo days
مرتانmarratāntwice
أولawwalfirst
ثانthānsecond
بعدbaʿdaafter
قبلqablabefore
Dual with -ان

كتاب→كتابان, يوم→يومان, مدرسة→مدرستان. Verbs too: يدرسان (they-two study)! Dual is everywhere — two eyes (عينان), two hands (يدان)!

noun + ان = two
WordSay itMeaning
عندي كتابان.ʿindī kitābān.I have two books.
بعد يومين.baʿda yawmayn.After two days.
هما يدرسان.humā yadrusān.They (two) study.
Watch out:
  • Saying اثنين كتاب — duals: كتابان!
  • Forgetting feminine ة→ت: مدرستان, not مدرسةان!

Commands and negation

Imperative: ادرس!

Give orders! ادرس! (study! -m), ادرسي! (study! -f), اجلس! (sit!), تعال! (come!), اذهب! (go!). Soften with من فضلك: اجلس من فضلك = Please sit. Command + please = polite power!

WordSay itMeaning
اجلسijlissit! (m)
قفqifstand! (m)
تعالtaʿālcome! (m)
اذهبidhhabgo! (m)
افتحiftaḥopen! (m)
اغلقighliqclose! (m)
اسمعismaʿlisten! (m)
انظرunẓurlook! (m)
Imperative: ادرس/ادرسي

To men: ادرس، اجلس، تعال. To women add -ي: ادرسي، اجلسي، تعالي. To groups: ادرسوا! Negate with لا: لا تذهب! = Don't go!

ادرسي (f) · ادرسوا (pl) · لا + تـ- (don't)
WordSay itMeaning

Watch out:
  • Saying ادرس to a woman — women get ادرسي!
  • Saying ما تذهب for don't — commands negate with لا!

Negation: لا، ما، لم، لن

Four NEVERS! لا + present (لا أدرس = I don't study), ما + past (ما درستُ = I didn't study), لم + bare present (لم أدرس = I didn't study), لن + present (لن أدرس = I won't study). Pick by TENSE!

WordSay itMeaning
لاlāno / don't (present)
ماmānot (past)
لمlamdidn't (past)
لنlanwon't (future)
أبداabadannever
أيayyany
شيءshayʾthing
أحدaḥadanyone
لا/لم/لن/ما by tense

لا أدرس (I don't study), لم أدرس / ما درستُ (I didn't study), لن أدرس (I won't study). لم eats endings: لم يدرسْ (he didn't study)!

لا+present · لم+bare · لن+future · ما+past
WordSay itMeaning
لا أدرس اليوم.lā adrus al-yawm.I don't study today.
لم أدرس أمس.lam adrus ams.I didn't study yesterday.
لن أدرس غدا.lan adrus ghadan.I won't study tomorrow.
Watch out:
  • Saying لا درستُ — past needs ما or لم!
  • Saying لن درستُ — لن loves PRESENT verbs!

Opinions and أفضل

Argue in Arabic! أعتقد أن… (I think that…), أفضل (better), أسوأ (worse), أكبر (bigger). أفعل pattern = comparatives! الأفضل = THE best. معك حق! = You're right!

Weather and plans

Arabic weather! الجو بارد (it's cold), الجو حار (it's hot), تمطر (it's raining), تثلج (it's snowing). الجو = the air — weather lives in الجو! Plus future plans with سـ!

الجو + adjective

الجو بارد (it's cold), الجو جميل (it's beautiful). Verbs: تمطر (it rains), تثلج (it snows). Future: سيكون الجو دافئا (it will be warm)!

The B1 test — 10 items

The level test closes the level. Answers are shown; in the course player the same test decides whether the next level unlocks.

  1. translate into the languageSay: I was studying

    Show the answer

    كنتُ أدرس

  2. translate into the languageSay: I have two books

    Show the answer

    عندي كتابان

  3. choose'Don't go!'

    1. ما تذهب
    2. لا تذهب
    3. لم تذهب
    4. لن تذهب
    Show the answer

    لا تذهب

  4. translate into Englishالجو بارد! إنها تمطر.

    Show the answer

    It's cold! It's raining.

  5. fill the gap___ في الحفلة. (She was)

    Show the answer

    كانت

  6. choose'I didn't study yesterday'

    1. لا أدرس أمس
    2. لم أدرس أمس
    3. لن أدرس أمس
    4. ما أدرس أمس
    Show the answer

    لم أدرس أمس

  7. put it in orderOrder: أدرس / كنتُ

    Show the answer

    كنتُ أدرس

  8. say it out loudSay aloud: أعتقد أن المترو أفضل.

    Show the answer

    أعتقد أن المترو أفضل.

  9. fill the gapمعك ___! (right)

    Show the answer

    حق

  10. translate into the languageSay: I think this is better

    Show the answer

    أعتقد أن هذا أفضل

After B1: the B1+ checkpoint

B1+ is not a certificate — it is the half-step between this level and the next one, and it is on this site’s ladder because language schools use it. You are at B1+ when you can work in the language, argue a point politely. Get there by working the recall drills above and the review deck until nothing on this page surprises you, then open the B2 pages.
