Evidential stance with అని

In Telugu, this lesson uses quotative అని, converbs, participial relatives, case suffixes, inclusive/exclusive person reference, honorific plural and diglossic literary–colloquial choices. At C1, form and word order encode stance, information structure, social distance, and textual cohesion rather than merely tense.
