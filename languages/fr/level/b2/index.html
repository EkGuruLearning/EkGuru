B2, unit by unit

Opinion and debate

Wishes need subjunctive (je veux que…)

After wishes, French switches mood: JE VEUX QUE TU VIENNES (I want you to come). For -ER verbs the subjunctive looks the same (que tu PARLES) — the magic shows in rebels: que tu SOIS (be), que tu FASSES (do), que tu AILLES (go).

Subjunctive after JE VEUX QUE

JE VEUX QUE + subjunctive: TU VIENS (fact) → QUE TU VIENNES (wish). Stars to memorise: QUE JE SOIS (that I be), QUE J'AIE (that I have), QUE TU FASSES, QUE TU AILLES, QUE TU VIENNES.

je veux que + subjunctive (sois/aie/fasses/ailles/viennes)

Je veux que vous fassiez attention.zhuh vuh kuh voo fah-SYEH ah-tah(n)-SYOH(n).I want you to pay attention.
J'espère que tu es prêt.zhes-PAIR kuh tew eh preh.I hope you are ready. (espérer keeps normal!)
Watch out:
  • Saying JE VEUX QUE TU VIENS — wishes need VIENNES.
  • Using subjunctive after J'ESPÈRE — hope keeps the normal form.

Reported speech: il dit / il a dit que…

IL DIT QUE + sentence (he says that…). Past: IL A DIT QUE + past (he said that…). The intro verb moves in time; QUE always stays.

il dit que + present · il a dit que + past

Work and formal writing

Polite professional French (conditionnel)

Sound professional: J'AIMERAIS (I would like), POURRIEZ-VOUS (could you…?). Conditionnel adds imparfait endings to the future stem: PARLER → PARLERAIS. POURRIEZ-VOUS M'AIDER? = Could you help me?

l'e-mailee-mehlemail
la pièce jointelah pyes zhwa(n)tattachment
la signaturelah see-nyah-TEWRsignature
l'objetlohb-ZHEHsubject (line)
le délailuh deh-LEHdeadline
le budgetluh bew-ZHEHbudget
la facturelah fahk-TEWRinvoice
cordialementkohr-dyahl-MAH(n)kind regards
Conditionnel: future stem + ais/iez

J'AIMERAIS (I would like), VOUS POURRIEZ (you could), CE SERAIT (it would be). Same stem as future + soft -AIS/-IEZ endings. J'AIMERAIS + verb = I would like to…

WordSay itMeaning

  • Saying J'AIME for formal wishes — J'AIMERAIS is softer.
  • Cutting the stem: PARLERAIS keeps -ER + AIS.

Y and EN (little giants)

Two tiny words replace whole phrases! Y = there / à + thing: J'Y VAIS (I go there). EN = some / of it: J'EN VEUX (I want some). They jump BEFORE the verb, just like LE/LA.

WordSay itMeaning
le cadeauluh kah-DOHgift
la raisonlah reh-ZOH(n)reason
la causelah kohzcause
la destinationlah des-tee-nah-SYOH(n)destination

le moyenluh mwah-YA(n)means
le serviceluh sair-VEESservice
le changementluh shah(n)zh-MAH(n)change
Y = there · EN = some/of it

J'Y VAIS = I go (there). J'Y PENSE = I think about it. J'EN VEUX = I want some (of them). J'EN AI DEUX = I have two (of them). Before the verb — always!

The level test closes the level. Answers are shown; in the course player the same test decides whether the next level unlocks.

    1. Tu m'aides?
