Mood and evidential stance

The indicative asserts within the discourse frame; the subjunctive after doubt, necessity, and evaluation presents content as non-asserted or evaluated. Lexical hedges further calibrate commitment.

stance expression + que + indicative/subjunctive
WordSay itMeaning
Les données laissent penser que la mesure a eu un effet limité.le do-ne les pɑ̃-se kə la mə-zyr a y œ̃ e-fe li-mi-teThe data suggest that the measure had a limited effect.
Il ne semble pas que cette conclusion soit suffisamment étayée.il nə sombl pa kə set kon-kly-zjɔ̃ swa sy-fi-za-mɑ̃ e-te-yeThis conclusion does not appear sufficiently substantiated.
Encore faut-il distinguer un indice d’une preuve.on-kor fo-til dis-tɛ̃-ge œ̃ ɛ̃-dis dyn prœvIt remains necessary to distinguish an indication from proof.
Watch out:
  • Preserve mood, stance, cohesion, and register; a grammatically possible form may still be pragmatically inappropriate.

Conditional mitigation

The conditional softens requests and proposals; imperfect and pluperfect forms construct distance or unreal alternatives. Mitigation must not hide who should do what by when.

Serait-il possible de + infinitive / que + subjunctive
