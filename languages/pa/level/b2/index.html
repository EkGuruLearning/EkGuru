Reported Speech and Viewpoints

Report what another person said, asked, or believed without losing the original viewpoint. Punjabi commonly uses ਕਿ (ki) to introduce reported content and adjusts pronouns or time expressions to the reporting context.

Reporting with ਕਿ

Place a reporting clause before ਕਿ (ki), then give the reported message. Punjabi often keeps a natural tense rather than mechanically shifting every verb as English sometimes does.

reporter + reporting verb + ਕਿ + reported clause

Hypotheses and Unreal Conditions

Discuss imagined present and past outcomes. These patterns let advanced learners consider alternatives, regrets, and consequences using ਜੇ (je), ਤਾਂ (tan), and conditional verb forms.

WordSay itMeaning
ਸ਼ਰਤsharatcondition
ਨਤੀਜਾnatijaresult
ਸੰਭਾਵਨਾsambhavnapossibility
ਕਲਪਨਾkalpanaimagination
ਅਫ਼ਸੋਸafsosregret
ਨਹੀਂ ਤਾਂnahin tanotherwise
ਬਚ ਸਕਣਾbach saknato be able to avoid
ਚੋਣchonchoice
Unreal ਜੇ…ਤਾਂ conditions

Use ਜੇ plus a past habitual or pluperfect form for an unreal condition; the result often uses a conditional ending such as -ਦਾ/-ਦੀ. Context shows whether the speaker imagines the present or regrets the past.

ਜੇ + unreal condition, ਤਾਂ + conditional result
WordSay itMeaning
ਜੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਸਮਾਂ ਹੁੰਦਾ, ਤਾਂ ਮੈਂ ਆਉਂਦਾ।je mere kol sama hunda, tan main aunda.If I had time, I would come.
ਜੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਨਿਕਲੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ, ਤਾਂ ਰੇਲ ਮਿਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ।je asin pahilan nikle hunde, tan rel mil jandi.If we had left earlier, we would have caught the train.
ਜੇ ਤੂੰ ਦੱਸਦਾ, ਤਾਂ ਮੈਂ ਮਦਦ ਕਰਦੀ।je tun dassda, tan main madad kardi.If you had told me, I would have helped.
Watch out:
  • Do not use a simple future in both clauses when describing an unreal past result.

Formal Public Communication

Passive and Impersonal Style

Recognise and produce passive constructions used in notices, news, and formal explanations. Punjabi forms many passives with a perfective participle plus ਜਾਣਾ (jana), while impersonal wording keeps attention on the action.

Passive with ਜਾਣਾ

Make a passive from the perfective verb plus a form of ਜਾਣਾ (jana). The participle agrees with the grammatical subject, and the agent may be omitted when unknown or unimportant.

subject + perfective participle + ਜਾਣਾ form
WordSay itMeaning
ਨਵਾਂ ਨਿਯਮ ਲਾਗੂ ਕੀਤਾ ਗਿਆ ਹੈ।navan niyam lagu kita gia hai.A new rule has been implemented.
ਅਰਜ਼ੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਜਾਂਚ ਕੀਤੀ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ।arzian di janch kiti javegi.The applications will be examined.
ਸਾਰੇ ਮੈਂਬਰਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸੂਚਿਤ ਕੀਤਾ ਗਿਆ।sare memberan nun suchit kita gia.All members were informed.
Watch out:
  • Do not translate every English passive literally; use an active Punjabi sentence when the agent is central and natural.
