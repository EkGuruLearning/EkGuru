Sequencing the perfective

Use ਪਹਿਲਾਂ (pahilan), ਫਿਰ (phir), and ਆਖ਼ਰਕਾਰ (akhirkar) to order events. Completed transitive verbs commonly mark the doer with ਨੇ (ne), while the verb may agree with an unmarked object.

time connector + subject + ਨੇ + object + perfective verb

Watch out:
  • Do not place ਨੇ (ne) after a pronoun in every past clause; check whether the verb is completed and transitive.

Duties, Needs, and Possibility

Express what must happen, what is needed, and what may be possible. Compare obligation with ਪੈਣਾ (paina), need with ਲੋੜ (lor), and possibility with ਸਕਣਾ (sakna).

WordSay itMeaning
ਜ਼ਿੰਮੇਵਾਰੀzimmevariresponsibility
ਲੋੜlorneed
ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀzarurinecessary
ਸੰਭਵsambhavpossible
ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼koshishattempt

Obligation with ਪੈਣਾ

Use an infinitive plus ਪੈਣਾ (paina) when circumstances require an action. The form of ਪੈਣਾ changes for tense and agreement: ਪੈਂਦਾ ਹੈ, ਪਵੇਗਾ, or ਪਿਆ.

person + ਨੂੰ + infinitive + ਪੈਣਾ form
WordSay itMeaning
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਅੱਜ ਕੰਮ ਪੂਰਾ ਕਰਨਾ ਪਵੇਗਾ।mainun ajj kam pura karna pavega.I will have to finish the work today.
ਸਾਨੂੰ ਨਿਯਮ ਮੰਨਣੇ ਪੈਂਦੇ ਹਨ।sanun niyam manne painde han.We have to follow the rules.
ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਜਲਦੀ ਜਾਣਾ ਪਿਆ।us nun jaldi jana pia.He or she had to leave early.
Watch out:
  • Do not use ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ (chahida hai) for every obligation; ਪੈਣਾ (paina) signals external necessity.

Softening commands

Use respectful imperative forms with ਤੁਸੀਂ (tusin). Add ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਕੇ (kirpa karke), ਜ਼ਰਾ (zara), or ਸਕਦੇ ਹੋ (sakde ho) to soften a request.
