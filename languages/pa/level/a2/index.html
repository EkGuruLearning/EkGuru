Giving route instructions

Punjabi commonly uses an imperative verb after the direction. Use ਜਾਓ (jao) for a polite instruction to one person or a group. Location words often come before ਹੈ (hai).

direction + ਜਾਓ (jao); place + location + ਹੈ (hai)
WordSay itMeaning
ਸਿੱਧੇ ਜਾਓ।siddhe jao.Go straight.
ਬੈਂਕ ਦੇ ਕੋਲ ਖੱਬੇ ਮੁੜੋ।bank de kol khabbe muro.Turn left near the bank.
ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਇੱਥੋਂ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ।station itthon dur hai.The station is far from here.
Watch out:
  • Do not use the intimate command ਜਾ (ja) with a stranger; choose polite ਜਾਓ (jao).

Tickets and Transport

Practise buying a ticket, asking about departure time, and stating a destination. Punjabi often marks the destination with ਨੂੰ (nun) and uses polite request forms in service encounters.

WordSay itMeaning
ਟਿਕਟtikatticket
ਕਿਰਾਇਆkiraiafare
ਸਵਾਰੀsavaripassenger; ride
ਚੱਲਣਾchallnato leave; to move
ਪਹੁੰਚਣਾpahunchnato arrive
ਸਮਾਂsamatime
ਸੀਟsitseat
ਵਾਪਸੀvapasireturn
Polite requests with ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ

To request a countable feminine item such as ਟਿਕਟ (tikat), use ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ (chahidi hai). Add destination + ਨੂੰ (nun) to say where you need to go.

person + ਨੂੰ (nun) + item + ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ (chahidi hai)
WordSay itMeaning
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਸਰ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਟਿਕਟ ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ।mainun amritsar di ikk tikat chahidi hai.I need one ticket to Amritsar.
ਸਾਨੂੰ ਦੋ ਸੀਟਾਂ ਚਾਹੀਦੀਆਂ ਹਨ।sanun do sitan chahidiyan han.We need two seats.
ਬੱਸ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਚੱਲੇਗੀ।bass naon vajje challegi.The bus will leave at nine o’clock.
Watch out:
  • Match ਚਾਹੀਦਾ/ਚਾਹੀਦੀ/ਚਾਹੀਦੇ (chahida/chahidi/chahide) with the requested noun, not with the speaker.

Comparing with ਨਾਲੋਂ

Place the item used as the comparison before ਨਾਲੋਂ (nalon), meaning “than.” The adjective agrees with the thing being described.

comparison + ਨਾਲੋਂ (nalon) + description

Giving advice with ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ

Use a verb stem plus ਣਾ and ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ (na chahida hai) to say what someone should do. The experiencer can be marked with ਨੂੰ (nun).

Future plans

Future forms attach to the verb stem and agree with the subject. Common forms include ਮੈਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ/ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ (main javanga/javangi) and ਅਸੀਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ (asin javange).
