Reported discourse and Konjunktiv I

German deploys verb-second and verb-final clause architecture, Konjunktiv I/II evidential distance, modal particles, nominalisation, information-structure fronting, register-sensitive compounds and pronominal reference. The model coordinates grammatical form with evidential commitment, information structure, social distance, and cohesion; mediation must preserve these relations.
