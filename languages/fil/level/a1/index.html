The whole Filipino A1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 0 words, 112 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

What this level is. A1 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the A1 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can greet, count to twenty, read a shop sign.

Pagbati at po

Gamitin nang wasto ang pagbati at po sa makabuluhang usapan at pagsulat.

Pagbati at po

Filipino combines predicate structure, ang/ng/sa marking, verbal voice and aspect, linkers, enclitic particles, stress, and intonation. The chosen form identifies the prominent participant and calibrates time, evidence, politeness, and discourse stance.

panaguri + minarkahang kalahok + panandang diskurso
WordSay itMeaning
Magandang araw po. Ako si Ana.Magandang araw po. Ako si Ana.I am Asha, and I am a student.
