What this level is. A2 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the A2 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can talk about your day, shop, order food.

Naganap na kilos

Gamitin nang wasto ang naganap na kilos sa makabuluhang usapan at pagsulat.

Naganap na kilos

Filipino combines predicate structure, ang/ng/sa marking, verbal voice and aspect, linkers, enclitic particles, stress, and intonation. The chosen form identifies the prominent participant and calibrates time, evidence, politeness, and discourse stance.

panaguri + minarkahang kalahok + panandang diskurso
WordSay itMeaning
Bumisita kami sa museo noong Sabado.Bumisita kami sa museo noong Sabado.We visited the museum last Saturday.
Naghahanda sila ng hapunan ngayon.Naghahanda sila ng hapunan ngayon.They are preparing dinner at the moment.
Mas mabilis ang tren ngunit mas mura ang bus.Mas mabilis ang tren ngunit mas mura ang bus.The train is faster, but the bus is cheaper.
