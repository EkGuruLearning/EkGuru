Filipino levels — A1 to C5, in order

Filipino runs the same eleven levels as every other course here, in the same order: A1 A2 A3 B1 B2 B3 C1 C2 C3 C4 C5. The six official CEFR levels carry the full authored content below; A3, B3, C3, C4 and C5 are EkGuru Extended Mastery levels — EkGuru's own extension, never official CEFR. A level is a description of what you can do, not an age group: a learner of any age can study any level.
