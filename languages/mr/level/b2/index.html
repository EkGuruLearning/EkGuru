अंदाजपत्रक आणि मर्यादा

Planning speaks in conditions and obligations: खर्च वाढल्यास …, प्रत्येक टप्प्याला मर्यादा ठरवावी लागेल. The formal conditional is -ल्यास, the spoken one is तर, and obligation uses the -ावी/-ावे form with लागेल, agreeing with its noun. Marathi keeps the obligation impersonal, so the person is named only when it matters.

WordSay itMeaning
अंदाजपत्रकandaajpatrakbudget
खर्चkharchexpenditure, cost
मर्यादाmaryadalimit
तरतूदtartudprovision, arrangement
ठरवणेtharvneto decide, to fix
Condition and obligation

-ल्यास is the formal if (वाढल्यास, झाल्यास) and closes its own clause; तर is the spoken one and follows it. Obligation takes -ावी/-ावे with लागेल, and it agrees with its noun: मर्यादा ठरवावी लागेल, खर्च ठरवावा लागेल, काम करावे लागेल.

खर्च वाढल्यास … · मर्यादा ठरवावी लागेल · तरतूद ठेवली आहे
WordSay itMeaning
खर्च वाढल्यास मुदत वाढवावी लागेल.Kharch vaadlyas mudat vaadhavi lagel.If the cost rises, the deadline will have to be extended.
प्रत्येक टप्प्याला मर्यादा ठरवावी लागेल.Pratyek tappyala maryada tharvavi lagel.A limit will have to be fixed for each stage.
यासाठी तरतूद ठेवली आहे.Yasathi tartud thevli ahe.A provision has been kept for this.
Watch out:
  • टप्प्याला मर्यादा ठरवली लागेल. → टप्प्याला मर्यादा ठरवावी लागेल. Obligation takes the -ावी form: ठरवावी लागेल.
  • खर्च वाढला तर मुदत वाढेल लागेल. → खर्च वाढल्यास मुदत वाढवावी लागेल. One obligation frame, not two futures stacked.

वाटाघाटी आणि तडजोड

A negotiation leaves room inside the sentence: या दरात पुरवठा करता येईल. The potential -ता येईल makes an offer without promising it, a refusal names what is impossible rather than the person, and तडजोडीची तयारी states readiness. A counter-offer usually opens with थोडे or पण, so that the door stays open.

अटatcondition, term

तयारीtayarireadiness, preparation

Softening and refusing

The potential -ता येईल (करता, देता, घेता) offers without committing. A refusal names the impossible: इतक्यात शक्य नाही. Readiness takes the -ची possessive of what one is ready for — तडजोडीची तयारी, बोलणीची तयारी.

WordSay itMeaning
या दरात पुरवठा करता येईल.Ya daarat purvatha karta yeil.Supply can be made at this rate.
इतक्यात शक्य नाही, पण विचार करता येईल.Itkyat shakya nahi, pan vichaar karta yeil.It is not possible at that rate, but it can be considered.
आमची तडजोडीची तयारी आहे.Aamchi tadjodichi tayari ahe.We are ready to compromise.
Watch out:
  • देणे करता येईल. → देणे देता येईल. The potential takes the -ता stem: देता येईल, करता येईल.
  • आमची तयारी आहे तडजोड. → आमची तडजोडीची तयारी आहे. तयारी takes -ची of what one is ready for.

अहवाल आणि आढावा

A report names its finding impersonally: अहवालात नमूद केले आहे की …, and it keeps what is pending visible with बाकी आहेत. आढावा घेणे is the verb for holding a review, and the passive जाईल announces the next step without naming who takes it. A reported clause always takes की.

Reporting a finding

A reported clause takes की. नमूद केले आहे and म्हटले आहे keep the writer out of the sentence, and बाकी agrees with what is pending (मुद्दे बाकी आहेत). The passive जाईल announces the next step, and तपासले आहे reports what is already done.

अहवालात नमूद केले आहे की … · पडताळणीच्या बाकी आहेत · आढावा घेतला जाईल

Marathi B2 beyond the drills — culture, reading, listening, idioms

These sections are written for Marathi B2 specifically. The reading text is EkGuru's own; the culture note cites where its facts come from; the idioms and the mistakes list are the ones this level actually trips over.

Culture note

Marathi is the official language of Maharashtra and one of India's 22 scheduled languages, with roughly 83 million speakers. The state was redrawn on language lines in 1960, and the Marathi-speaking belt runs past its border — Belagavi and the Goa coast included — which is why a Marathi speaker may be in Karnataka or Goa as naturally as in Pune. In the courts, the collector's office and the municipal corporation, Marathi and English share the page, and the negotiating vocabulary is mostly Marathi words doing English work.

Source (named, not linked): en.wikipedia.org, the article “Marathi people”.

Reading — read this aloud, slowly

दोन्ही बाजूंनी कराराच्या अटी वाचल्या. पुरवठादाराने मुदत तीन महिन्यांची मागितली, पण आमच्या बाजूने खर्च वाढणार होता. बोलणी दोन तास चालली. शेवटी एक तडजोड झाली: पहिला टप्पा दोन महिन्यांत, दुसरा टप्पा चार महिन्यांत. दोन्ही बाजूंनी अटी लिखित स्वरूपात मान्य केल्या आणि करारावर स्वाक्षरी केली. कोंडी फुटली कारण दोन्ही बाजूंनी अंतिम मुदत वाढवायला तयारी दाखवली. पुढील बैठकीत दररोजच्या पुरवठ्याचा आढावा घ्यायचे ठरले.

What it says: Both sides read the terms of the contract. The supplier asked for a three-month deadline, but on our side the cost was going to rise. The talks went on for two hours. In the end there was a compromise: the first stage in two months, the second in four. Both sides accepted the conditions in writing and signed the contract. The deadlock broke because both sides showed readiness to extend the final deadline. At the next meeting it was decided to review the daily supply.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • आम्ही अट मान्य केला. → आम्ही अट मान्य केली. अट is feminine, so the verb follows it: अट मान्य केली, अटी मान्य केल्या.
  • वाटाघाटी सुरू आहे. → वाटाघाटी सुरू आहेत. वाटाघाटी is plural: वाटाघाटी सुरू आहेत, वाटाघाटी तडकल्या.
  • मी हा करार मान्य आहे. → मी हा करार मान्य करतो. मान्य आहे describes the contract; accepting it needs the verb मान्य करतो / करते.
