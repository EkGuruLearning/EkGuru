Evidential reporting and conditional

Portuguese uses personal infinitive, future subjunctive, mesoclisis/formal clitics, concessive subjunctive, information-structure fronting, evidential conditional and European/Brazilian register variation. The model coordinates grammar with commitment, information structure, social distance, and cohesion; mediation must preserve those relations.
