A2, unit by unit

Getting around

Around the city (estou no/na)

Location uses ESTAR: ESTOU EM CASA (I am at home). EM melts: EM+O=NO, EM+A=NA: ESTOU NO BANCO (at the bank), NA PRAÇA (at the square). Esquerda (left), direita (right), em frente (ahead)!

WordSay itMeaning
a ruaah HOO-uhstreet
a praçaah PRAH-sahsquare
a estaçãoah shtah-SOW(n)station
o bancooo BAH(n)-koobank
o hospitaloo ohsh-pee-TAH-oohospital
a lojaah LOH-zhahshop
a esquerdaah shkehr-DAHleft
a direitaah dee-RAY-tahright
ESTAR + em/no/na

ESTOU = I am, ESTÁ = you/he/she is. EM + O = NO (no banco), EM + A = NA (na praça). ESTOU NO HOTEL = I am at the hotel.

estou/está + em/no/na + place
WordSay itMeaning

Watch out:
  • Saying SOU em casa — location is always ESTAR.
  • Saying EM O banco — melt it: NO banco.

Travel and pretérito (simple past)

Finished trips use PRETÉRITO PERFEITO: VIAJEI (I travelled), VIAJOU (you/he/she travelled). -AR verbs: -EI (eu), -OU (você/ele/ela). FALEI, COMPROU, CHEGOU!

WordSay itMeaning
a viagemah vee-AH-zhay(n)trip
o aviãooo ah-VYOW(n)plane
o hoteloo oh-TEH-oohotel
a malaah MAH-lahsuitcase
a passagemah pah-SAH-zhay(n)ticket
a praiaah PRAH-yahbeach
a montanhaah moh(n)-TAH-nyahmountain
a fotoah FOH-toophoto
Past -AR: viajei, viajou

Drop -AR, add -EI (I), -OU (você/ele/ela). VIAJEI = I travelled. COMPROU = you/he/she bought. ONTEM (yesterday) loves this past!

stem + ei/ou
WordSay itMeaning
Viajei para a Espanha.vee-ah-ZHAY pah-rah ah sh-PAH-nyah.I travelled to Spain.
Você comprou passagens?voh-SEH koh(m)-PROH ahsh pah-SAH-zhaysh?Did you buy tickets?
Ela chegou ontem.EH-lah sheh-GOH OH(n)-tay(n).She arrived yesterday.
Watch out:
  • Saying VIAJO for the past — past I = VIAJEI.
  • Forgetting ONTEM goes with finished past, not present.

Life and plans

Daily routines (reflexive verbs)

Routines bounce back: ME LEVANTO = I get (myself) up. ME = myself, SE = yourself/himself/herself (você!). Mornings run on ME/SE: levantar-se, banhar-se, vestir-se!

WordSay itMeaning
a manhãah mah-NYAH(n)morning
a tardeah TAHR-deeafternoon
a noiteah NOY-cheenight
o trabalhooo trah-BAH-lyoowork / job
a escolaah sh-KOH-lahschool
o banhooo BAH-nyoobath / shower
o café da manhãoo kah-FEH dah mah-NYAH(n)breakfast
o jantaroo zhahn-TAHRdinner
Reflexive ME/SE: me levanto

LEVANTAR-SE = to get up. ME LEVANTO (I get up), SE LEVANTA (você/he/she gets up). The little word points back — don't drop it!

me/se + verb
WordSay itMeaning
Me levanto às sete.mee leh-VAHN-too ahsh SEH-chee.I get up at seven.
Você se banha de manhã?voh-SEH see BAH-nyah dee mah-NYAH(n)?Do you bathe in the morning?
Ela se veste tarde.EH-lah see VEHSH-chee TAHR-dee.She gets dressed late.
Watch out:
  • Saying LEVANTO without ME — it means 'I lift', not 'I get up'!
  • Saying TE (Portugal) in Brazil — Brazil uses SE for você.

Future: vou/vai + verb

VOU = I go, VAI = you/he/she goes. VOU + verb = I am going to…: VOU COMER = I am going to eat. The second verb never changes!

vou/vai + verb
WordSay itMeaning

Você vai estudar?voh-SEH vey shtoo-DAHR?Are you going to study?
Vai chover.vey shoo-VEHR.It is going to rain.
Watch out:
  • Changing the second verb: VOU VIAJAR, never VOU VIAJO.
  • Saying EU VAI — I go is VOU.

ESTE vs ESSE: this vs that

ESTE/ESTA = this (near me). ESSE/ESSA = that (near you). They match gender: ESTA camisa, ESSE vestido, ESSA loja. (AQUELE = far from both!)

Weather stories use IMPERFEITO: FAZIA SOL (it was sunny), HAVIA VENTO (there was wind). Just two phrases paint any past sky!

First imperfeito: fazia, havia

FAZIA SOL/FRIO/CALOR = it was sunny/cold/hot. HAVIA + noun = there was…: HAVIA SOL (there was sun). Weather past = imperfeito, always!
