Advanced past and hypotheticals

Advanced past: tinha feito

Going deeper back: TINHA FEITO = I had done (before something else). TINHA + past participle: TINHA COMIDO, TINHA VIAJADO. Tell layered stories!

Pluperfect: tinha + participle

TINHA + participle = HAD done: TINHA FEITO, TINHA COMIDO, TINHA VIAJADO. Use when one past happened before another: Quando cheguei, TINHA COMIDO = When I arrived, I had (already) eaten.

tinha + participle
WordSay itMeaning
Quando cheguei, ele tinha saído.KWAHN-doo sheh-GAY, EH-lee CHEE-nyah seh-EE-doo.When I arrived, he had left.
Eu já tinha comido.eh-oo zhah CHEE-nyah koh-MEE-doo.I had already eaten.
Nunca tinha visto isso.NOON-kah CHEE-nyah VEESH-too EE-soo.I had never seen that.
Watch out:
  • Using pretérito for the earlier event: TINHA SAÍDO for had-left.
  • Forgetting JÁ: já + tinha = already had — natural pair!

Dream-grammar! FALARIA = I would speak. Infinitive + -IA: VIAJARIA, COMERIA, SERIA. With SE (if): SE PUDESSE, VIAJARIA = If I could, I would travel.

Conditional: falaria (would speak)

Add -IA to the infinitive: FALAR + IA = FALARIA. EU/VOCÊ/ELE all FALARIA. Perfect for dreams, wishes, polite requests: EU ADORARIA = I would love to!

Subjunctive basics: que eu fale

Feelings and doubts unlock a new mood! ESPERO QUE VOCÊ FALE = I hope you speak — FALE not FALA! Doubt, hope, wishing: QUE + subjunctive. -AR verbs flip A→E!

WordSay itMeaning
espero quesh-PEH-roo keeI hope that
duvido quedoo-VEE-doo keeI doubt that
é importante queeh ee(m)-pohr-TAHN-chee keeit is important that
tomaratoo-MAH-rahlet's hope

QUE + subjunctive: que eu fale

After hope/doubt/wishing (ESPERO QUE, DUVIDO QUE), -AR verbs flip A→E: FALA→FALE, VIAJA→VIAJE. Small flip, huge fluency leap!

espero/duvido que + verb-flip (A→E)
WordSay itMeaning

Watch out:
  • Using FALA after ESPERO QUE — flip to FALE!
  • Using subjunctive after SEI QUE (I know) — facts stay normal!

Debating and disagreeing

Debate connectors: glue for arguments

Connectors chain ideas: PORQUE (because) explains, MAS (but) contrasts, ALÉM DISSO adds, NO ENTANTO concedes. Chain 3+ for real B2 fluency: CONCORDO, MAS… ALÉM DISSO…

opinion + porque/mas + além disso/no entanto
WordSay itMeaning

Watch out:
  • Stopping at CONCORDO — add PORQUE + a reason!
  • Using MAS twice in one sentence — vary with NO ENTANTO.
