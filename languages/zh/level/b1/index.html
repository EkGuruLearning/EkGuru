把 and 被

把: handle things!

把 GRABS objects! 把书放在桌子上 = Take the book, put it on the table. 把门关上 (close the door!), 把茶喝了 (drink up the tea!). Object JUMPS before the verb — with a result attached!

WordSay itMeaning
把bǎtake (disposal marker)
放fàngto put
拿náto take / hold
带dàito bring
送sòngto give / send
借jièto borrow / lend
还huánto return (thing)
用yòngto use
把 + object + verb + result

把门关上 (close the door), 把作业写完 (finish homework), 把茶喝了 (drink the tea up). Verb needs a RESULT: 上/完/了/给…!

Watch out:
  • Saying 把书放 — verbs need RESULTS: 放在…上!
  • Using 把 for new info — 把 handles KNOWN things!

被: passive + 是…的

Bad things HAPPEN with 被! 我的杯子被打破了 (my cup got broken). 是…的 stresses HOW/WHEN: 我是昨天来的 (it was YESTERDAY I came). Passive pain + emphasis power!

WordSay itMeaning
被bèiby (passive)
打破dǎ pòto break
偷tōuto steal
淋línto drench
批评pī píngto criticize
表扬biǎo yángto praise
让ràngto let / by
是…的shì…deit is…that
被 + doer + verb · 是…的

杯子被打破了 (cup got broken), 我被雨淋了 (I got rained on). 是…的: 我是坐车来的 (I came BY CAR — stress!).

X 被 (doer) verb了 · 是 + detail + 的
WordSay itMeaning
我的杯子被打破了。wǒ de bēi zi bèi dǎ pò le.My cup got broken.
我被雨淋了!wǒ bèi yǔ lín le!I got soaked by rain!
我是昨天来的。wǒ shì zuó tiān lái de.It was yesterday I came.
Watch out:
  • Saying 被 for happy things — 被 loves BAD news!
  • Forgetting 的: 是…的 needs BOTH!

Describe a family routine

Describe a routine and compare it with a past habit.

WordSay itMeaning
以前yǐ qiánformerly; in the past
现在xiàn zàinow
习惯xí guànhabit
一起yì qǐtogether
后来hòu láilater
past experience with 过

Use 过 to describe an experience or former habit, then contrast it with the current routine.

以前 ...；后来 ...；现在 ...
WordSay itMeaning
以前我们周末常常一起做饭。yǐ qián wǒ men zhōu mò cháng cháng yì qǐ zuò fàn。We often cooked together on weekends before.
后来大家的时间不一样了。hòu lái dà jiā de shí jiān bù yí yàng le。Later everyone's schedules changed.
现在我们每个月见一次。xiàn zài wǒ men měi gè yuè jiàn yí cì。Now we meet once a month.
Watch out:
  • 以前我每周末去过公园。 → 以前我每周末都去公园。 Use a habitual marker such as 都; 过 is not needed for a repeated routine.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • 我把书在桌子。 → 我把书放在桌子上。 The 把 construction needs a disposal verb and a complete location.
