B1, unit by unit

把 and 被

把: handle things!

把 GRABS objects! 把书放在桌子上 = Take the book, put it on the table. 把门关上 (close the door!), 把茶喝了 (drink up the tea!). Object JUMPS before the verb — with a result attached!

WordSay itMeaning
把bǎtake (disposal marker)
放fàngto put
拿náto take / hold
带dàito bring
送sòngto give / send
借jièto borrow / lend
还huánto return (thing)
用yòngto use
把 + object + verb + result

把门关上 (close the door), 把作业写完 (finish homework), 把茶喝了 (drink the tea up). Verb needs a RESULT: 上/完/了/给…!

把 + obj + verb + result
WordSay itMeaning
把书放在桌子上。bǎ shū fàng zài zhuō zi shang.Put the book on the table.
把门关上!bǎ mén guān shang!Close the door!
把茶喝了。bǎ chá hē le.Drink up the tea.
Watch out:
  • Saying 把书放 — verbs need RESULTS: 放在…上!
  • Using 把 for new info — 把 handles KNOWN things!

被: passive + 是…的

Bad things HAPPEN with 被! 我的杯子被打破了 (my cup got broken). 是…的 stresses HOW/WHEN: 我是昨天来的 (it was YESTERDAY I came). Passive pain + emphasis power!

WordSay itMeaning
被bèiby (passive)
打破dǎ pòto break
偷tōuto steal
淋línto drench
批评pī píngto criticize
表扬biǎo yángto praise
让ràngto let / by
是…的shì…deit is…that
被 + doer + verb · 是…的

杯子被打破了 (cup got broken), 我被雨淋了 (I got rained on). 是…的: 我是坐车来的 (I came BY CAR — stress!).

X 被 (doer) verb了 · 是 + detail + 的
WordSay itMeaning
我的杯子被打破了。wǒ de bēi zi bèi dǎ pò le.My cup got broken.
我被雨淋了!wǒ bèi yǔ lín le!I got soaked by rain!
我是昨天来的。wǒ shì zuó tiān lái de.It was yesterday I came.
Watch out:
  • Saying 被 for happy things — 被 loves BAD news!
  • Forgetting 的: 是…的 needs BOTH!

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

Family and age

Family: 家

Family! 爸爸 (dad), 妈妈 (mum), 哥哥 (older brother), 姐姐 (older sister), 弟弟/妹妹 (younger). Age-ranked! 的 = 's: 我的妈妈 (my mum), 他的爸爸 (his dad)!

的 possession + sibling ranks

我的书 (my book), 他的妈妈 (his mum). Siblings: 哥哥/弟弟 (older/younger brother), 姐姐/妹妹 (older/younger sister) — age matters!

X + 的 + thing · 哥哥/弟弟

  • Saying 我的爸爸 always — close family drops 的: 我爸爸!
  • Calling older brother 弟弟 — older: 哥哥!
