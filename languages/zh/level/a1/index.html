First words

Greetings: 你好

你好 (hello), 您好 (polite hello). Meeting: 初次见面 (first meeting) → 我是 + name → 很高兴认识你 (happy to know you)! Nod warmly!

Names and 你叫什么

Ask names: 你叫什么名字? (what are you called?) → 我叫… How are you: 你好吗? → 我很好! 吗 makes questions, 呢 bounces back: 我很好，你呢? (I'm fine, and you?)!

WordSay itMeaning
我wǒI / me
你nǐyou
名字míng ziname
你叫什么名字nǐ jiào shén me míng ziwhat is your name
我叫wǒ jiàoI'm called
你好吗nǐ hǎo mahow are you
请qǐngplease
好hǎogood / fine
吗 asks, 呢 bounces

你好吗? (how are you?), 你叫什么? (what are you called?). 呢 = and…?: 你呢? = And you? 我叫小红，你呢?!

…吗? → …。你呢?
WordSay itMeaning
你好吗?nǐ hǎo ma?How are you?
我很好!wǒ hěn hǎo!I'm very fine!
你叫什么名字?nǐ jiào shén me míng zi?What is your name?
Watch out:
  • Saying 我好吗 — ask OTHERS: 你好吗!
  • Forgetting 很: 我很好 (very-fine), not 我好!

是 and measure words

是 and 这/那

Point! 这 (this), 那 (that). 这是书 = This is a book. Negative: 不是 (is not). Adjectives need 很: 很好 (very-good = good), 很大 (big)! 没有很 = sounds wrong!

WordSay itMeaning
这zhèthis
那nàthat
书shūbook
学校xué xiàoschool
车chēcar
大dàbig
小xiǎosmall
人rénperson
这是X · 不是 · 很 + adj

这是书 (this is a book), 那是学校 (that is a school). 不是书 (is not a book). 很大 (big), 很小 (small) — 很 required before adjectives!

这/那 + 是 + X · 很 + adj
WordSay itMeaning
这是书。zhè shì shū.This is a book.
那是学校。nà shì xué xiào.That is a school.
这不是书。zhè bú shì shū.This is not a book.
Watch out:
  • Saying 这书 — 是 required: 这是书!
  • Saying 大书 for 'big' — pair adjectives: 大书 ok, but predicate needs 很大!

Culture note

Chinese characters are not an alphabet: many characters represent a syllable and a meaning-bearing unit, and words may use one or several characters. Measure words such as 个 and 本 are part of ordinary counting patterns. The note describes writing, not a single cultural identity.

Source (named, not linked): unicode.org.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • 我叫是小林。 → 我叫小林。 叫 already introduces a name; do not add 是.
  • 你几岁吗？ → 你几岁？ A question word already marks the question; do not add 吗.
  • 一个书。 → 一本书。 Use 本 as the measure word for a book.
