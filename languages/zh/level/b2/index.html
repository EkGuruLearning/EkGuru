Conditionals and plans

如果…就

The IF-frame! 如果下雨，我就不去 (if it rains, I won't go). 即使…也 (even if): 即使下雨，我也去! 虽然…但是 (although): 虽然贵，但是好! 只要 (as long as), 只有 (only if)!

Culture note

Chinese New Year and other festivals are celebrated in varied ways by families and communities. Food, visits and public events differ by region, religion, migration history and personal choice. A description should name its source or community rather than turn one family's practice into a rule for everyone.

Source (named, not linked): ich.unesco.org.

社区准备了一场节日活动。组织者公布了时间和地点，也说明参加活动完全自愿。居民提出了几个建议，团队把建议分类后再决定哪些可以实行。

What it says: The community prepared a festival event. Organisers announced the time and place and clarified that attendance was voluntary. Residents offered several suggestions; the team grouped them before deciding which could be implemented.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • 如果下雨，但是我们在家。 → 如果下雨，我们就在家。 Use 就 for the result clause; do not stack 如果 with 但是.
  • 虽然方案便宜，所以大家同意。 → 虽然方案便宜，但是还要讨论。 Pair 虽然 with 但是, or use 所以 in a separate cause-result sentence.
  • 所有人都一样喜欢这个节日。 → 不同家庭庆祝节日的方式可能不同。 Avoid presenting varied cultural practice as universal.
