B2, unit by unit

Conditionals and plans

如果…就

The IF-frame! 如果下雨，我就不去 (if it rains, I won't go). 即使…也 (even if): 即使下雨，我也去! 虽然…但是 (although): 虽然贵，但是好! 只要 (as long as), 只有 (only if)!

如果…就 · 即使…也 · 虽然…但是

如果下雨，我就不去 (if rains, won't go). 即使下雨，我也去 (even if rains, go)! 虽然贵，但是好 (although expensive, good)!

Politeness and debate

Politeness: 请/麻烦/您

Polite power! 请 (please), 麻烦你 (trouble-you = please…): 麻烦你开门! 不好意思 (excuse me), 劳驾 (excuse me, passing). 您 = honored you. 贵姓? = Your honorable surname?

麻烦你… + 您/贵

麻烦你开门 (trouble-you open the door = please open it). 您好 (honored hello). 贵姓? → 免贵姓王 (no-honor, surname Wang — humble reply)!

Debate connectors

Argue in Chinese! 但是 (but), 所以 (so), 也就是说 (in other words), 比如 (for example), 而且 (moreover), 总之 (in short), 结果 (in the end). Chain: 比如…而且…所以…!

而且ér qiěmoreover

Connector chains

地铁很方便。但是很挤。也就是说，很好! (The subway is handy. But crowded. In short, good!). Start sentences with 但是/所以/比如!

…。但是…。所以…。
WordSay itMeaning
地铁很方便。但是很挤。dì tiě hěn fāng biàn. dàn shì hěn jǐ.The subway is handy. But it's crowded.
比如，北京很大。bǐ rú, Běi jīng hěn dà.For example, Beijing is big.
也就是说，地铁很好!yě jiù shì shuō, dì tiě hěn hǎo!In short, the subway is good!
Watch out:
  • Ending sentences with 但是 — 但是 starts sentences!
  • Stacking 但是…但是 — vary with 而且/也就是说!

Story spine: 从前…最后

从前…有一天…突然…终于…最后 — the five-beat Chinese story spine. Mix 了/过/会: 去了北京，吃过烤鸭，会再去!

从前 → 有一天 → 突然 → 最后
