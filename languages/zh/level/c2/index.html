Culture note

In expert communication, a conclusion should match the strength and scope of its evidence. A pause may have several interpretations, and an inference is not an observation. Careful Chinese prose can be concise while marking uncertainty with words such as 可能, 尚 and 就现有证据而言.

Source (named, not linked): britannica.com.

就现有证据而言，这是一种可能的解释，还不是最终结论。作者同时说明样本有限，因此读者可以判断这一解释适用到什么范围。

What it says: On the evidence currently available, this is a possible interpretation, not yet a final conclusion. The author also notes that the sample is limited, allowing readers to judge the scope to which the interpretation applies.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • 沉默一定表示同意。 → 沉默可能有多种解释，不能单独证明同意。 Do not claim to know intent from silence alone.
  • 速度就是进步。 → 没有问责的速度未必构成进步。 Qualify a broad claim and state the relevant condition.
  • 现在的证据已经是最终结论。 → 现有证据支持一种解释，但还不是最终结论。 Distinguish a plausible interpretation from a final verdict.
