Chinese C1 beyond the drills — culture, reading, listening, idioms

These sections are written for Chinese C1 specifically. The reading text is EkGuru's own; the culture note cites where its facts come from; the idioms and the mistakes list are the ones this level actually trips over.

Culture note

A formal Chinese report can distinguish a source's statement, a directly observed result and the writer's interpretation. Phrases such as 据报告 and 由此可见 signal different relationships to evidence. Naming those relationships helps readers assess the conclusion without overstating what the source establishes.

Source (named, not linked): britannica.com.

Reading — read this aloud, slowly

两项研究都发现参与人数增加，但研究地点和统计时间不同。把结果放在一起看，可以看到一个共同趋势；语境差异仍然会影响解释。

What it says: Both studies found an increase in participation, but their locations and measurement periods differed. Read together, they show a shared trend; contextual differences still affect the interpretation.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • 报告证明所有地区都改善了。 → 报告显示部分地区有所改善。 Attribute the finding and keep its geographic scope.
  • 有限证据应该被忽略。 → 有限证据不应被忽略，也不应被当作定论。 Do not turn limited evidence into either dismissal or certainty.
