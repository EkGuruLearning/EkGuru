Past and ability

了 and 过

Past helpers! Verb + 了 (completed): 我吃了 (I ate). Didn't: 没吃 (NOT 不吃 for past!). Experience: 过 (have done): 我吃过 (I've eaten before). 已经 = already!

明天míng tiāntomorrow
早上zǎo shangmorning
晚上wǎn shangevening
夜里yè lǐat night
然后rán hòuthen
已经yǐ jīngalready
Verb了 · 没verb · Verb过

我吃了 (I ate), 我没吃 (I didn't eat), 我吃过 (I've eaten before). 没 = past-negative! 过 = ever-experience!

了 (done) · 没 (didn't) · 过 (ever)
WordSay itMeaning
昨天我读了书。zuó tiān wǒ dú le shū.Yesterday I read a book.
我没吃。wǒ méi chī.I didn't eat.
我吃过北京烤鸭!wǒ chī guo Běi jīng kǎo yā!I've eaten Peking duck!
Watch out:
  • Saying 不吃 for past — past-negative: 没吃!
  • Saying 吃了过 — pick ONE: 了 or 过!

会/能/可以 + 想/要

Three CANS! 会 (learned skill: 我会说中文), 能 (able: 我能去), 可以 (may/allowed: 可以进来吗?). Wants: 想 (would like), 要 (want/will). 想喝茶 (I'd like tea), 我要走了 (I'm leaving)!

会/能/可以 + 想/要

我会游泳 (I can swim — learned), 我能来 (I can come — able), 可以坐吗? (may I sit?). 想吃 (would like to eat), 我要茶 (I want tea / I'll have tea)!

Explain a travel delay

Describe a delay, state what you have already done and ask about the next step.

WordSay itMeaning
晚点wǎn diǎndelayed
已经yǐ jīngalready
改签gǎi qiānchange a ticket
车站chē zhànstation
下一班xià yì bānnext service
completed action with 了

Use 了 for a completed action. Keep a later plan separate from what has already happened.

subject + verb + 了 + object
WordSay itMeaning
火车晚点了二十分钟。huǒ chē wǎn diǎn le èr shí fēn zhōng。The train was delayed by twenty minutes.
我已经改签了车票。wǒ yǐ jīng gǎi qiān le chē piào。I have already changed the ticket.
请问下一班几点出发？qǐng wèn xià yì bān jǐ diǎn chū fā？May I ask what time the next service leaves?
Watch out:
  • 我已经了改签车票。 → 我已经改签了车票。 Place 了 after the verb and before its object here.

Chinese A2 beyond the drills — culture, reading, listening, idioms

Culture note

您 is a respectful form of 你, but address choices depend on relationship, setting and local habit. A friendly conversation may use 你, while a service interaction may choose 您. Learners should listen to how people address one another instead of treating one form as universally required.

Source (named, not linked): britannica.com.

小周昨天去过新图书馆。她说那里很安静，书也很多。朋友问她怎么去，她解释了公交路线，还提醒朋友周末要先查开放时间。

What it says: Xiao Zhou has been to the new library. She said it was quiet and had many books. A friend asked how to get there; she explained the bus route and reminded the friend to check opening hours first on the weekend.

The 3 mistakes this level makes

  • 我昨天去过了医院。 → 我昨天去了医院。 了 and 过 express different aspect meanings; avoid stacking them here.
  • 我可以会游泳。 → 我会游泳。 会 marks a learned ability; do not add 可以 without a permission meaning.
  • 这个多少钱？ → 这个多少钱？ Ask price with 多少钱; do not add a second price marker.
