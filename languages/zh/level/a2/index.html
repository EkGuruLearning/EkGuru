Chinese A2 — Elementary: 了/过, ability, places, food

The whole Chinese A2 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

What this level is. A2 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the A2 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can talk about your day, shop, order food.

A2, unit by unit

Past and ability

了 and 过

Past helpers! Verb + 了 (completed): 我吃了 (I ate). Didn't: 没吃 (NOT 不吃 for past!). Experience: 过 (have done): 我吃过 (I've eaten before). 已经 = already!

WordSay itMeaning
昨天zuó tiānyesterday
今天jīn tiāntoday
明天míng tiāntomorrow
早上zǎo shangmorning
晚上wǎn shangevening
夜里yè lǐat night
然后rán hòuthen
已经yǐ jīngalready
Verb了 · 没verb · Verb过

我吃了 (I ate), 我没吃 (I didn't eat), 我吃过 (I've eaten before). 没 = past-negative! 过 = ever-experience!

了 (done) · 没 (didn't) · 过 (ever)
WordSay itMeaning
昨天我读了书。zuó tiān wǒ dú le shū.Yesterday I read a book.
我没吃。wǒ méi chī.I didn't eat.
我吃过北京烤鸭!wǒ chī guo Běi jīng kǎo yā!I've eaten Peking duck!
Watch out:
  • Saying 不吃 for past — past-negative: 没吃!
  • Saying 吃了过 — pick ONE: 了 or 过!

Dialogue. Read it aloud twice — once for each speaker.

会/能/可以 + 想/要

Three CANS! 会 (learned skill: 我会说中文), 能 (able: 我能去), 可以 (may/allowed: 可以进来吗?). Wants: 想 (would like), 要 (want/will). 想喝茶 (I'd like tea), 我要走了 (I'm leaving)!

WordSay itMeaning
会huìcan (learned skill)
能néngcan (able)
可以kě yǐmay / can
想xiǎngwould like / miss
要yàowant / will
喜欢xǐ huanto like
希望xī wàngto hope
懂dǒngto understand
会/能/可以 + 想/要

我会游泳 (I can swim — learned), 我能来 (I can come — able), 可以坐吗? (may I sit?). 想吃 (would like to eat), 我要茶 (I want tea / I'll have tea)!

会 (skill) · 能 (able) · 可以 (may)
WordSay itMeaning
我会说中文。wǒ huì shuō Zhōng wén.I can speak Chinese.
我能去。wǒ néng qù.I can go.
我想喝茶!wǒ xiǎng hē chá!I'd like tea!
Watch out:
  • Saying 我能说中文 for skills — skills: 会!
  • Saying 我要想 — pick ONE: 想 or 要!

Places and movement

在/去/从…到

Move! 在 (at), 去 (go to), 从…到 (from…to): 从家到学校 (from home to school). 这里/那里/哪里 = here/there/where! 你去哪里? = Where are you going?

公园gōng yuánpark
博物馆bó wù guǎnmuseum

在 · 去 · 从…到

我在家 (I'm at home), 我去学校 (I go to school), 从北京到上海 (from Beijing to Shanghai). Verbs of going demand 去!

在 (at) · 去 (to) · 从…到
