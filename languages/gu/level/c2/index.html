Precision and Discourse Architecture

Meaning Between the Lines

Distinguish propositions from implications and select near-synonyms by degree, evidence and speaker intention. At C2, ‘what was said’ and ‘what the wording invites us to infer’ must not be confused.

Evidence strength and invited inference

સૂચવવું presents a conclusion as supported but not settled; દર્શાવવું can present something as made evident. દાવો attributes a proposition without endorsing it. માત્ર narrows the relevant alternative: ‘માત્ર ખર્ચ’ corrects an inference that other factors matter. Context decides whether an utterance is cautious, corrective or sceptical.

Control given and new information, emphatic જ, contrastive તો, and paragraph sequencing. Standard Gujarati permits strategic rearrangement, but marked order must serve a clear contrast; findings about Surati Gujarati should not be generalized to every variety.

Focus particles and information flow

જ commonly marks an exclusive or emphatic focus: તેણે જ કહ્યું can answer ‘who said it?’ તો can establish a contrastive topic: ખર્ચ તો ઘટ્યો, પણ સમય વધ્યો. Place familiar context before the key update when it aids processing. A coherent sequence frames the issue, states the pivot, supports it, then draws the warranted consequence.
