What this level is. A2 is one of the six official CEFR levels, and the A2 rung on this site’s ladder. A learner of any age can study it — the level describes what you can do, not who you are. By the end of it you can talk about your day, shop, order food.

  • Handle routine health, school, workplace, and home interactions.

  • Participate respectfully in everyday community conversations.

Experiencer with مجھے

Urdu often expresses physical states with مجھے (mujhe, “to me”) plus a noun or sensation and ہے (hai).

مجھے + condition + ہے
WordSay itMeaning
مجھے بخار ہے۔mujhe bukhār hai.I have a fever.
میرے سر میں درد ہے۔mere sar men dard hai.My head hurts.
آپ کو کیا ہوا؟āp ko kyā huā?What happened to you?
Watch out:
  • Do not copy English “I have” word for word; health states commonly use مجھے.

Use a respectful imperative ending such as کیجیے, بھیجیے, or آئیے with colleagues, customers, and elders.

Respectful classroom imperatives

Many respectful commands use ـیے. Learn the whole spoken form because spelling changes with different verb stems.

learning object + respectful command
WordSay itMeaning
اپنی کتاب کھولیے۔apnī kitāb kholiye.Open your book.

Watch out:
  • Do not attach one ending mechanically without checking the conventional verb form.

Progressive weather statements

Use a noun plus ہو رہا/رہی ہے for an event in progress. The participle agrees with the grammatical gender of the event noun.

weather noun + ہو رہا/رہی ہے
WordSay itMeaning
بارش ہو رہی ہے۔bārish ho rahī hai.It is raining.
ہوا چل رہی ہے۔havā chal rahī hai.The wind is blowing.
سورج نکل رہا ہے۔sūraj nikal rahā hai.The sun is coming out.
Watch out:
  • Use رہی with feminine بارش and ہوا, not رہا.
