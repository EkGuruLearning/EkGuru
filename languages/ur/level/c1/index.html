Urdu C1 — Proficient Urdu

The whole Urdu C1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

Who this level is for. C1 is one of the six CEFR levels and the C1 rung on this site’s ladder: the learner in the picture is an older adult, because on this site age is the rung — the same learner grows up the ladder as the language grows. By the end of it you can idiom, register, tone — and hear when it is wrong.

What you can do when C1 is finished

  • Communicate connected ideas accurately for the level.
  • Adapt register and structure to audience and purpose.
  • Interpret and produce Urdu with script, romanisation, and cultural awareness.
