A1, unit by unit

Script and Social Basics

Greetings and Courtesy

Begin with respectful Urdu greetings and survival courtesy. Read from right to left, use the romanisation as a bridge, and listen before repeating. آپ (āp) is the safe respectful form for a new person or an elder.

WordSay itMeaning
السلام علیکم!Assalām Alaikum!Hello! (peace greeting)
آداب!Ādāb!Hello! (Lucknow style)
آپ کیسے ہیں؟Āp kaise hain?How are you? (formal)
میں ٹھیک ہوںMain ṭhīk hūnI'm fine

Respectful introductions

Use آپ (āp) for respectful “you.” A name is linked with ہے (hai), while a self-description with میں (main) commonly ends in ہوں (hūn).

person + name/description + ہے/ہوں
WordSay itMeaning
میرا نام علی ہے۔merā nām Alī hai.My name is Ali.
میں ٹھیک ہوں۔main ṭhīk hūn.I am fine.
آپ کیسے ہیں؟āp kaise hain?How are you?
Watch out:
  • Do not use informal تم (tum) for an elder or a stranger.

People, Names, and Pronouns

Identify speakers and talk about names with basic pronouns. Urdu does not mark he and she with different pronouns: وہ (vo) can mean either, so context supplies gender.

WordSay itMeaning
میںmainI
تمtumyou (informal)
آپāpyou (formal)
یہyethis
وہvohe / she / that
ہمhamwe
وہvothey
مجھےmujheme / to me
Pronouns and politeness

Urdu's آپ mirrors Hindi's aap; use it for respect.

میں · تم/آپ · وہ · ہم

تم بولتے ہوtum BOHL-teh hoyou speak (informal)

Urdu word order

Urdu is nearly identical to Hindi in structure: 'I water drink'.

subject — object — verb
