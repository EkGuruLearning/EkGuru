Evidential stance and attribution

Bengali uses case markers and postpositions, non-finite conjunctive and conditional forms, relative-correlative constructions, evidential নাকি/মনে হয়, classifiers, honorific agreement, topic/focus particles and সাধু–চলিত/formal register choices. Here these resources control commitment, information structure, social distance, and cohesion. C1 production must preserve scope when audience or genre changes.
