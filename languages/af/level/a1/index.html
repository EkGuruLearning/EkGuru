Afrikaans A1 — Afrikaans A1

The whole Afrikaans A1 level as one readable page: 3 units, 6 lessons, 48 words, 208 questions with every answer shown, a worksheet and a 10-item level test. Nothing here is behind JavaScript, and nothing needs an account.

Who this level is for. A1 is one of the six CEFR levels and the A1 rung on this site’s ladder: the learner in the picture is a child, because on this site age is the rung — the same learner grows up the ladder as the language grows. By the end of it you can greet, count to twenty, read a shop sign.

Groete en kennismaking

Afrikaans combines verb-second main clauses with verb-final subordinate clauses, largely invariant present verbs, het plus participle past, modal chains, separable verbs and clause-bounding double negation. Spelling, register, information structure and community variation shape accurate use.
