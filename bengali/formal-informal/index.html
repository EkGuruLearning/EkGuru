Formal and informal Bengali

আপনি, তুমি, তুই — three levels of you, and exactly when to use each.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Bengali has three yous where English has one: আপনি (apni — formal), তুমি (tumi — familiar), তুই (tui — intimate). আপনি for elders, strangers and officials; তুমি for friends, peers and younger family; তুই for childhood friends, small children and lovers. The beginner rule is simple: start at আপনি with every adult, and accept every invitation down.

Each you drags its own verb endings with it. Sit down: বসুন (boshun — formal), বসো (bosho — familiar), বস (bosh — intimate). To do: করেন, করো, কর. To go: যান, যাও, যা. The endings rhyme in threes across every verb in the language — learn the pattern once on করা and বলা, and you own it everywhere.

Moving down a level is a social ceremony. The elder says তুমি করে বলো (speak to me as তুমি) — sometimes with the words, sometimes with just a smile and a তুমি aimed at you. Mirror what you receive: if they তুমি you, তুমি them back. Asymmetry (they তুমি, you আপনি) is the natural shape of respect toward elders — don't rush to close it.

Above the yous floats one more register: সাধু ভাষা (shadhu bhasha), the old literary style of Tagore's essays, versus চলিত ভাষা (cholito bhasha), the living language of this page. তাহারা খাইতেছেন (they eat, literary) versus তারা খাচ্ছে (they eat, living). Admire the old style in books; speak the living style everywhere — nobody orders tea in সাধু ভাষা.

A note on করি, করো, করেন. Every verb wears its politeness on its ending: আমি করি (I do), তুমি করো (you do, familiar), আপনি করেন (you do, formal). Learn one verb in all three forms — করা (to do) is the classic — and the whole politeness system clicks into place at once.

Questions learners ask

Which you should a beginner use?

Start with আপনি for every adult you don't know well — it is never wrong, only occasionally too formal, and Bengalis simply smile and invite you down to তুমি. Starting too casual is the only real mistake.

When is তুই okay?

For close friends, children, and affection — তুই with a stranger is an insult, তুই with your best friend is love. Learners should understand it, rarely say it, and let friends invite them into it.

What is সাধু ভাষা versus চলিত ভাষা?

It's the old literary style (সাধু ভাষা) versus today's living language (চলিত ভাষা) — তাহারা versus তারা for they. Read the old style in Tagore; speak the new style everywhere. This page teaches the living one.
