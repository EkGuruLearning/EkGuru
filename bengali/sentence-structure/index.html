Bengali sentence structure

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Verb last, না last, questions in place — the simple skeleton under every Bengali sentence.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The whole skeleton is three letters: SOV. আমি (I) ভাত (rice) খাই (eat). সে (she) স্কুলে (to school) যায় (goes). The verb anchors the end of every statement, and once you stop fighting it, Bengali sentences feel calm and inevitable — everything introduced before anything happens.

Negation and questions refuse to disturb the order. আমি জানি না (I know not) parks না at the very end. তুমি কোথায় যাও? (you where go?) leaves কোথায় exactly where the answer will sit — ঢাকায় যাই (to Dhaka go). Question words are placeholders for answers, sitting in the answer's own seat.

Location words come after their nouns. টেবিলে (table-on), বাড়িতে (home-at), বাজারে (market-in): noun first, little -এ or -তে tail after. Direction works the same — ঢাকায় যাই, স্কুলে যাই. English speakers must flip every in, on and to backwards; do it deliberately for a week and it becomes reflex.

Emphasis moves to the front. ভাত আমি খাই (rice, I eat — as for rice, I eat it) topicalises ভাত without changing a single ending. Politeness softens the edges — দয়া করে বসুন (please sit) — but the skeleton never moves. SOV today, SOV tomorrow: learn it once, use it forever.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on টা, টি and খানা. The definite article hides inside the noun: বই (a book) becomes বইটা (the book) with one small টা. টি is the polite sibling (বইটি), খানা counts flat things (দুখানা রুটি — two breads). No separate word for the — just a tail on the noun, elegant once seen.

Questions learners ask

What is the basic Bengali word order?

I rice eat — আমি ভাত খাই. The verb goes last, always: subject, object, verb. English speakers feel the verb itch early for weeks, then one day the verb-last order clicks and English starts sounding impatient.

Where do না and question words go?

Same trick, new address: আমি জানি না puts না dead last. Questions keep their question words in place too — তুমি কোথায় যাও? (you where go?) — so Bengali word order barely moves whatever the sentence does.

What are postpositions?

Postpositions — little location words that come AFTER the noun: টেবিলে (on the table), স্কুলে (to school), বাড়িতে (at home). English prepositions stand before; Bengali postpositions sit after. Same job, opposite side.
