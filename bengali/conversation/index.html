Real Bengali conversation: market, meetings, small talk

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Introductions, bargaining and small talk — the dialogues you will actually have, with rescue lines for when you get lost.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Every Bengali conversation you will ever have starts the same way: a name, a place, a little warmth. আপনার নাম কী? (apnar nam ki — what is your name?), আমি … থেকে এসেছি (ami … theke esechi — I have come from …). Ask where someone is from before you ask what they do — roots interest Bengalis more than jobs, and the question always lands well.

The market is your second classroom. কটা? or দাম কত? (how much?), দামটা একটু কমান (damta ektu koman — lower the price a little), ঠিক আছে, নিলাম (thik achhe, nilam — okay, I'll take it). Bargain with a smile and accept the final price gracefully; the performance is the point, and both sides know it.

Small talk runs on three rails: food (খেয়েছেন? — have you eaten?), family, and the weather during monsoon. কী খবর? (ki khobor — what's new?) is not really a question — answer with ভালো, আপনি বলুন (bhalo, apni bolun — good, you tell me) and the conversation rolls.

And when you get lost — you will, daily — three rescue lines save you: বুঝতে পারছি না (I don't understand), আস্তে বলুন (speak slowly, please), আবার বলুন (say it again, please). Said with a smile, they make Bengalis slow down and lean in. Getting lost is not failure; it is the lesson.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on code-switching. Real Bengali conversation mixes in English constantly — time, market, bill, okay. This is not broken Bengali; it is how the language is actually spoken in cities. Use the English word without shame when the Bengali one escapes you.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask someone's name?

আপনার নাম কী? (apnar nam ki). Answer with আমার নাম … (amar nam …). Then ask back: আর আপনার? It is the friendliest three seconds in the language.

What do I say when I don't understand?

বুঝতে পারছি না (bujhte parchi na — I don't understand), আস্তে বলুন (aste bolun — please speak slowly) and আবার বলুন (abar bolun — please say it again). These three sentences rescue every conversation.

Is there one all-purpose word?

Yes — ঠিক আছে (thik achhe) is the universal softener: okay, agreed, got it, fine. Nod while saying it and half of daily life runs itself.
