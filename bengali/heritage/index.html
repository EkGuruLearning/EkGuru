Bengali for heritage learners

You understand everything and say nothing — the road back to your family's Bengali.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Heritage learners share one sentence: বুঝি কিন্তু বলতে পারি না (I understand but can't speak). You followed every family argument, laughed at every joke, and answered in English. That comprehension is not nothing — it is thousands of buried hours. Your job is not learning Bengali; it is excavating it.

Start where the language lives: home. Narrate your day to your mother in Bengali — আজ অফিসে ব্যস্ত ছিলাম (today I was busy at the office). Call your grandmother weekly: ঠাকুমা, কেমন আছেন? Family corrects with love and repeats without limit; no classroom matches a kitchen table.

Festivals are immersion you can schedule. দুর্গাপূজা brings the family home and the language with it — প্যান্ডেলে যাব (we'll go to the pandal). পহেলা বৈশাখ opens the new year with শুভ নববর্ষ (happy new year) and sweet shops. Show up, listen hard, speak badly, eat well: that is the heritage curriculum.

Fill the gaps deliberately. Kitchen Bengali needs office words, school words, street words — the registers your childhood never required. Read one children's book aloud weekly, keep the diary, and within months the buried language surfaces: first sentences, then stories, then dreams. Heritage learners don't finish last; they finish fast.

Questions learners ask

Why do I understand but can't speak?

Completely normal — you grew up hearing Bengali (understanding) without pressure to speak it (producing). The comprehension is buried treasure: heritage learners typically start speaking within weeks once they begin, far faster than true beginners.

How do I practise with family?

Call home weekly and hold one topic in Bengali — what you ate, the weather, your week. Grandparents are the world's most patient teachers, and ঠাকুমার সঙ্গে কথা বলা (talking with grandmother) beats any app ever made.

Which festivals should I know?

দুর্গাপূজা (autumn, the great homecoming), পহেলা বৈশাখ (mid-April, the new year — শুভ নববর্ষ!), সরস্বতী পূজা (spring, goddess of learning). Festivals are language baths: go, listen, eat, repeat.
