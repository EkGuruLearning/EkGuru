Speaking practice that actually works in Bengali

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Shadow, record, brave the tea stall — speaking drills for Bengali that survive contact with real people.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Start with shadowing: play any dialogue on this site and mumble along half a beat behind the speaker, copying rhythm before words. Ten minutes of shadowing rewires your mouth faster than an hour of silent study, because Bengali lives in its lilt — the rise and fall that makes বসুন sound like an invitation instead of an order.

Then record yourself. One sentence a day — আজকের বাক্য (today's sentence) — spoken into your phone and compared against the site's speaker button. Your live ears flatter you; the recording tells the truth about your ধ, your ণ, your swallowed endings. Keep a week's recordings and the progress becomes audible, which is pure motivation fuel.

Take it outside with three brave sentences: আমি বাংলা শিখছি (I'm learning Bengali), ধীরে বলুন (please speak slowly), ভুল হলে শুধরে দিন (correct me if I'm wrong). The tea stall is the world's kindest classroom — order একটা চা দিন, answer কোথা থেকে এসেছেন? with your country, and you have had a real conversation in Bengali.

Finally, think small thoughts in Bengali. এখন চা খাব (now I'll drink tea). বাইরে বৃষ্টি (rain outside). আজ ভালো দিন (today is a good day). Nobody hears these rehearsals, but they build the reflex that matters — reaching for Bengali first instead of translating from English at the speed of panic.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ঠিক করে বলেছি? Four words that buy you infinite free teaching: ঠিক করে বলেছি? (thik kore bolechhi — did I say it right?). Ask it after every brave sentence and Bengalis will fix your tones, your endings and your word order with visible joy. The learners who improve fastest are not the talented ones — they are the asking ones.

Questions learners ask

What do I say when people speak too fast?

ধীরে বলুন (dhire bolun — please speak slowly) and আবার বলুন (please say it again). Said with a smile, these two sentences make every Bengali speaker slow down and help — they are the learner's seatbelt and airbag.

Should I record my own voice?

Yes, and it is the fastest mirror you own. Record one sentence a day — আমি বাংলা শিখছি is a fine start — and compare it weekly against the speaker buttons on this site. Ears lie to you live; recordings don't.

How do I get people to correct me?

ভুল হলে শুধরে দিন (correct me if I'm wrong) said upfront turns every conversation into a free lesson. Bengalis correct kindly and love being asked — the invitation matters more than the grammar.
