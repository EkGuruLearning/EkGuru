Bengali food phrases: eat well from day one

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Order, season and praise your food — the phrases that turn every meal into a lesson.

Bengali food runs on rice and rhythm: ভাত (bhat — rice), ডাল (dal — lentils), ভাজা (bhaja — fried), মাছ (machh — fish), মাংস (mangsho — meat), চাটনি (chatni — chutney), মিষ্টি (mishti — sweets). Order by pointing and naming: ভাত-ডাল দিন (bhat-dal din — give rice-lentils). Meals arrive in waves; pace yourself.

Season your order like a local. ঝাল কম (jhal kom — less spicy), মিষ্টি কম (mishti kom — less sweet, for tea and desserts), গরম গরম (gorom gorom — piping hot, the highest praise for fried food). These three adjustments mark you as someone who eats with attention, and kitchens respond to attention.

Praise is a social duty. খুব মজা (khub moja — very tasty), দারুণ হয়েছে (darun hoyechhe — it's turned out superb), আরেকটা দিন (arekta din — give one more). The cook — professional or grandmother — runs on this fuel. Eat everything offered seconds of; refusal needs the shield of পেট ভরে গেছে (pet bhore gechhe — my stomach is full).

End with ceremony: বিল দিন (bill din — the bill, please), pay with a smile, and finish with খুব ভালো ছিল (khub bhalo chhilo — it was very good). Then walk — ভাতঘুম (bhatghum — the rice-sleep) is real, and the evening adda (chat session) over চা is where the day's Bengali gets reviewed.

A note on মাছ. মাছ (machh — fish) is not a side dish in Bengal; it is the centre of the cuisine and, many would say, of civilisation. Even the phrase মাছে-ভাতে বাঙালি (fish-and-rice Bengali) defines the people. Respect the fish and Bengal respects you.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask for less spicy food?

ঝাল কম দিন (jhal kom din — make it less spicy). Say it with a smile when ordering and every kitchen understands. Bengali heat is flavourful rather than fierce, but restaurant spice assumes local stomachs.

I am vegetarian — what do I say?

আমি নিরামিষ খাই (ami niramish khai — I eat vegetarian). Bengal loves its fish and meat, but pure-veg cooking is deep and respected — ডাল, শাক, সবজি done properly need no apology.

How do I ask for the bill?

বিল দিন (bill din — give the bill). In small eateries the bill comes on memory and a scrap of paper; check it with good humour, pay, and say খুব ভালো ছিল (it was very good).
