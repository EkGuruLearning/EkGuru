Bengali slang, honestly explained

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

ফাটাফাটি, ব্যাপক, দারুণ — the slang Bengalis actually say, with the warnings textbooks skip.

Every learner's first slang word should be ফাটাফাটি (fatafati — awesome, fantastic). It is joyful, completely clean, and delights Bengalis coming from a foreign mouth — খাবারটা ফাটাফাটি (the food is awesome) has never failed anyone. Its cousins ব্যাপক (byapok — great, huge) and দারুণ (darun — wonderful) complete the safe superlative trio.

Reactions come next: ধুর (dhur — meh, nonsense) dismisses with affection, যাঃ (jah — no way!) disbelieves with delight, বাহ! (bah — wow!) applauds everything. These tiny explosions carry whole conversations — যাঃ, সত্যি? (no way, really?) — and using them right marks you as someone who listens to how Bengalis actually talk.

Now the warnings. কেটে পড় (kete poro — scram) is genuinely rude outside horseplay. ঐ (oi — hey you) summons waiters but insults peers. And everything in তুই-form — খাবি, যাবি, শোন — belongs only to close friends, children and film dialogues. Slang without the relationship is costume without the party.

Learn slang like a naturalist: collect it, label where you heard it and who said it, and deploy each word first with the friend who taught it. Slang learned from films stays in films until a living Bengali clears it for use. One cleared word a week beats fifty risky ones — and friends LOVE being your slang customs officers.

A note on মামা and বস. On the street, মামা (mama — mother's brother) means dude and বস (bosh — boss) means mate — মামা, চা খাবি? (dude, having tea?). Lovely between friends, absurd from strangers. The rule of all slang: relationship first, words second. Earn the মামা before you spend it.

Questions learners ask

Should beginners use slang?

Understand all of it, say little of it — until friends invite you in. Slang without friendship sounds like mockery; the same words with friendship sound like belonging. Let a Bengali friend be your slang licence.

What is the most dangerous slang mistake?

তুই and its verbs (খাবি, যাবি, করবি) with anyone you wouldn't hug. তুই with a stranger is an insult; তুই with your best friend is love. Film heroes break this rule constantly — you are not a film hero yet.

What is the safest Bengali slang word?

ফাটাফাটি (fatafati — awesome) is the safest superlative in Bengal: joyful, clean, and welcome from any mouth including yours. Start there; graduate to ব্যাপক when friends start laughing WITH you.
