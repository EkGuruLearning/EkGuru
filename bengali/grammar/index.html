Bengali grammar, plainly explained

Verb last, no gender, little helper-words after nouns — the whole skeleton on one page.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Bengali sentences end with the verb — always. আমি ভাত খাই (ami bhat khai — I rice eat). ইংরেজি শিখছি (English am-learning). Whatever the sentence carries, the verb waits at the gate. English speakers must learn to hold their verb back; once that reflex forms, Bengali order feels inevitable rather than backwards.

English has prepositions (before nouns); Bengali has postpositions (after them). বাড়িতে (barite — in the house), টেবিলের উপর (on the table), আমার জন্য (for me), তোমার সাথে (with you). The helper-word leans on its noun from behind — same logic as the verb waiting at the end. Bengali is a language that finishes its thoughts late.

Then the great gift: no grammatical gender. Adjectives never change — বড় ছেলে, বড় মেয়ে, বড় বাড়ি: same বড় everywhere. Verbs care about person (I/you/he) but never about masculine or feminine. Every hour Hindi or French learners spend on gender, you spend on words.

Questions and negatives are small. What-questions use কী (এটা কী? — what is this?); yes/no questions use কি (যাবে কি? — will you go?). Negation sits at the verb: খাই না (don't eat), খাইনি (didn't eat), খাবো না (won't eat). Verb last, helpers behind, negate late — that is ninety percent of Bengali grammar.

Questions learners ask

What is Bengali word order?

Subject-Object-Verb: আমি ভাত খাই (I rice eat). The verb always comes last — train yourself to hold the verb until the end and Bengali word order clicks within days.

কী vs কি — what is the difference?

কী (ki — with long ী) asks <i>what</i>: এটা কী? (what is this?). কি (ki — short ি) asks yes/no: তুমি যাবে কি? (will you go?). Same sound, different spelling, different job — written Bengali cares, spoken Bengali doesn't.

Does Bengali have grammatical gender?

There is none — adjectives and verbs never change for gender. বড় ছেলে, বড় মেয়ে: same বড়. After Hindi or French, this feels like a holiday.
