Emergency Bengali

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

সাহায্য করুন! — the sentences you hope never to need, learned before you need them.

Learn this page backwards: start with সাহায্য করুন! (shahajjo korun — please help!), ডাক্তার (daktar — doctor), হাসপাতাল (hashpatal — hospital), পুলিশ (pulish — police). Four words, and any crowd in Bengal will organise itself around you — Bengalis help first and ask questions later, especially when a foreigner speaks their language in trouble.

The body has its own vocabulary: ব্যথা (byatha — pain), জ্বর (jwor — fever), ওষুধ (oshudh — medicine), রক্ত (rokto — blood). এখানে ব্যথা (pain here), pointing, is a complete medical consultation for starters. আমার ডাক্তার লাগবে (I need a doctor) is never misunderstood and never ignored.

Being lost is the friendliest emergency: আমি হারিয়ে গেছি (ami hariye gechhi — I am lost) turns every stranger into a guide. Add your hotel's name plus কোথায়? and follow the pointing armada — in Bengal, lost foreigners get escorted, not just directed. Keep your hotel card in your pocket and your phone charged.

Two practical sentences close the kit: ফোন করতে পারি? (phone korte pari — may I make a call?) and ঠিকানা লিখে দিন (thikana likhe din — please write the address). Paper, pen, pointing and politeness solve what vocabulary cannot — and দয়া করে (please) said with eye contact has rescued more travellers than any phrasebook.

A note on করুন, দিন, ডাকুন. Emergency Bengali runs on three polite commands: করুন (korun — please do), দিন (din — please give), ডাকুন (dakun — please call). ডাক্তার ডাকুন (call a doctor), জল দিন (give water), সাহায্য করুন (please help). Three verbs, infinite emergencies — and the politeness costs nothing even when seconds count.

Questions learners ask

What is the single most important emergency word?

সাহায্য করুন! (shahajjo korun — please help!), loud and clear, then point at the problem. In a real emergency nobody grades your grammar — volume, pointing and the word ডাক্তার (doctor) communicate everything.

জল or পানি for water?

জল (jol) in West Bengal, পানি (pani) in Bangladesh — same water, different word, and a famous shibboleth between the two Bengals. জল দিন (jol din — give water) works everywhere on the Indian side.

How do I explain I need medicine?

আমার ওষুধ লাগবে (amar oshudh lagbe — I need my medicine) and keep the prescription on your phone. Pharmacy staff in Bengal cities usually read English prescriptions without trouble.
