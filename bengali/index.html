Questions about this course

Is this Bengali course really free?

Yes — every lesson, every speaker button, every quiz. No account, no trial, no card. The course is supported by voluntary contributions on the support page.

Do I need to know Hindi first?

No. Everything is explained in plain English from zero. If you do know Hindi, the scripts are cousins and you will move faster — but nothing assumes it.

New topics are published every week. The coming-soon cards on this page turn into full lessons one by one — bookmark the hub and check back.
