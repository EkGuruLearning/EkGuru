The Bengali alphabet: less scary than it looks

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Fifty letters, one honest script — and the single sound that separates learners from real speakers.

The Bengali script has eleven vowels (স্বরবর্ণ) and thirty-nine consonants (ব্যঞ্জনবর্ণ). It runs left to right, and like its cousin Devanagari it hangs words from a horizontal headline. Every consonant carries a built-in vowel — ক is not just k, it is kaw. That built-in vowel is the whole game, so let us say it plainly.

In Bengali the built-in vowel অ sounds like the o in English hot — an aw sound — not the flat a of Hindi. So কলকাতা is Kawlkata, never Kalkata. Learners coming from Hindi get this wrong for months; learners who fix it on day one sound strikingly better for almost no effort. This single habit is the cheapest upgrade in the language.

A few letters deserve a personal introduction. য় sounds like y in yes. ড় and ঢ় are flaps — put your tongue where it goes for the tt in American butter and you are nearly there. ং, ঃ and ঁ are nasal marks that colour the vowel beside them rather than sounding on their own. And ৎ (khanda-ta) is a t with its vowel chopped off, ending words like হঠাৎ (hothat — suddenly).

Then there are the joint letters, যুক্তাক্ষর — two or three consonants sharing one body, like ক্ষ or জ্ঞ. They look frightening in a chart and feel ordinary in real words, because only a handful are common. Do not memorise the chart; meet each conjunct inside a word you actually want, the way children do.

Start here: the eleven vowels, then the first row ক খ গ ঘ ঙ, then your own name. Write a little every day — the script was designed by hand and it only surrenders to the hand.

Questions learners ask

Is the Bengali script the same as Hindi's?

Cousins, not twins. Both hang words from a headline and both descend from the same ancient script, but the letter shapes differ and Bengali pronounces its built-in vowel as aw where Hindi says a. One does not read the other.

How long does the alphabet take?

A focused learner reads slowly within two weeks and comfortably within two months. Writing your own name on day one is the traditional — and correct — way to start.

Are the joint letters (conjuncts) hard?

No. Conjuncts are just two letters sharing one body — ক্ষ is ক plus ষ. Learn them inside real words (which this course does) and they stop looking scary within days.
