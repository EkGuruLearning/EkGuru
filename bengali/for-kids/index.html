Bengali for kids

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Animals, colours, rhymes and games — Bengali for little learners, built for play.

Kids learn Bengali through ছড়া (chhora — rhymes) before anything else. Rhythm teaches sounds, repetition teaches words, and actions teach meaning — clap the beats, mime the animals, march the numbers. One rhyme recited together daily (ছড়া শোনাও!) builds more Bengali than any worksheet.

Animals are the first vocabulary: বিড়াল (biral — cat), কুকুর (kukur — dog), পাখি (pakhi — bird), মাছ (machh — fish), হাতি (hati — elephant), বাঘ (bagh — tiger). Ask বিড়াল কী বলে? (what does the cat say?) and let them roar the answers — মিউ (miu), ঘেউ (gheu). Sound effects are not distractions; they are the lesson.

Colours paint the world next: লাল (lal — red), নীল (nil — blue), সবুজ (shobuj — green), হলুদ (holud — yellow), সাদা (shada — white), কালো (kalo — black). Play colour hunts — লাল কোথায়? (where is red?) — and count everything in Bengali: একটা লাল বল (one red ball). Colours plus counting is a full curriculum disguised as a game.

Keep sessions short and physical: fifteen minutes of naming games, then খেলি? (shall we play?) and ঘুমাও (sleep) at bedtime with a Bengali lullaby. Praise effort over accuracy — বাহ! for every brave word — and stop while it's still fun. The goal is a child who begs for more Bengali tomorrow, not one who survived it today.

Questions learners ask

Where do kids start with Bengali?

ছড়া (chhora — rhymes) first: rhythm and repetition teach sounds before sense, exactly how Bengali children learn. Recite one rhyme together daily — ছড়া শোনাও (recite a rhyme) — and the language arrives disguised as play.

Which words do children learn first?

Animals first (বিড়াল, কুকুর, পাখি — they move and make noises), then colours (লাল, নীল, সবুজ — the world is painted with them), then counting games. Nouns children can point at beat every abstract word.

How long should kids practise?

Fifteen minutes of play beats an hour of lessons: naming games, colour hunts, animal sounds (বিড়াল কী বলে? — what does the cat say?). Stop while it's still fun and they'll beg to continue tomorrow.
