Time and dates in Bengali

আজ, কাল, পরশু — weekdays, clock time, and the two-sided কাল.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Learn Bengali spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Anchor yourself with five words: আজ (aaj — today), কাল (kal — yesterday/tomorrow), পরশু (porshu — day after tomorrow), এখন (ekhon — now), পরে (pore — later). Five words cover ninety percent of daily planning — কাল আসুন (come tomorrow) and পরে কথা হবে (we'll talk later) will carry you through weeks of real life.

The week runs রবিবার (robibar — Sunday), সোমবার (shombar — Monday), মঙ্গলবার (mongolbar — Tuesday), বুধবার (budhbar — Wednesday), বৃহস্পতিবার (brihoshpotibar — Thursday), শুক্রবার (shukrobar — Friday), শনিবার (shonibar — Saturday). Thursday's name is the week's heavyweight — practise brihoshpotibar slowly and the rest of the week feels easy.

Clock time is beautifully simple: number plus টা. একটা (one o'clock), দুটো (two o'clock — note the change!), তিনটে (three o'clock), চারটে, পাঁচটা. এখন কটা বাজে? (ekhon kota baje — what time is it now?) is the question; the answer is just the number with its টা. Two and three bend the rule (দুটো, তিনটে) — learn those two exceptions and the clock is yours.

Parts of the day paint every plan: সকাল (shokal — morning), দুপুর (dupur — midday), বিকেল (bikel — afternoon), সন্ধ্যা (shondha — evening), রাত (raat — night). সকালে (in the morning) and রাতে (at night) add one small -এ sound — সকালে যাব (I'll go in the morning) — and suddenly your sentences have schedules.

A note on সাড়ে, সোয়া and পৌনে. Bengali slices the hour three ways: সোয়া (shoa — quarter past), সাড়ে (sare — half past), পৌনে (poune — quarter to). সোয়া চারটে is quarter past four; পৌনে পাঁচটা is quarter to five. Three little words, and you can read every clock and catch every train in Bengal.

Questions learners ask

Does কাল really mean both yesterday and tomorrow?

Yes — কাল means yesterday looking back and tomorrow looking forward; tense and context choose. Learners panic, natives never notice: গতকাল (gotokal — yesterday) and আগামীকাল (agamikal — tomorrow) exist for the rare moments when it truly matters.

How do I say the time in Bengali?

Take the number, add টা: তিনটে (three o'clock), পাঁচটা (five o'clock). Half past adds সাড়ে (sare) — সাড়ে তিনটে is half past three. Bengali clock time is one small rule with an enormous payoff.

What order do the Bengali weekdays go in?

রবিবার (Sunday) first, then সোমবার through শনিবার. Six of the seven end in -বার (bar — day), so learn রবি, সোম, মঙ্গল, বুধ, বৃহস্পতি, শুক্র, শনি and the week assembles itself.
