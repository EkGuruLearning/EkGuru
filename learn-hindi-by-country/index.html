Learn Hindi, from wherever you are

Lessons are online, so your country does not decide which tutor you can work with. It does decide three real things: the hours that suit you both, what an hour costs in your own money, and — most usefully — what your first language already gives you. Somebody learning Hindi from Japan and somebody learning it from Spain are solving different problems, and it is worth knowing which one is yours.
