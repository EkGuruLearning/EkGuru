Questions about supporting

Where does my contribution go?

Directly to the owner, Prakash. It pays server and domain bills and funds new free lessons, tools and worksheets — payment providers take only their usual fee.

Which methods can I use?

UPI for India; PayPal, Razorpay, Stripe and Revolut for cards; Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDT for crypto supporters worldwide. Cards marked “coming soon” are being connected.

Will EkGuru stay free?

Yes — every lesson is free and always will be. Contributions keep it that way instead of a paywall.
