Questions about supporting

Where does my contribution go?

Directly to the owner, Prakash. It pays server and domain bills and funds new free lessons, tools and worksheets — payment providers take only their usual fee.

Which methods can I use?

The payment page is hosted by Razorpay, so the exact methods — UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets — are shown to you at checkout. What appears can vary by location and by the payment page's configuration. More methods may be added over time.

Will EkGuru stay free?

Yes — every lesson is free and always will be. Contributions keep it that way instead of a paywall.

Is my payment secure?

No card details ever touch EkGuru. Payment happens on Razorpay's own secure hosted page, and EkGuru stores no payment information at all.

Do I get a receipt?

Razorpay issues the receipt for the transaction — check the confirmation email or your bank/UPI app history. EkGuru is not a registered charity and does not claim tax-deductible status.
