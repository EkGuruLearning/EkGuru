How to read this page

Each entry gives the price per lesson, the lesson length, the levels the tutor teaches and where they are based. Click a name for the full profile: the subjects they cover, how they teach, their weekly availability converted to your timezone, and any reviews from real students.

Choosing between them

If you are a complete beginner, look for "Beginner" in the levels and for a tutor who mentions the Devanagari script — starting with the alphabet is the part people skip and then regret. If you already speak some Hindi and want to stop hesitating, look for conversational practice instead.

Price is worth the least attention of anything here. The difference between the cheapest and the dearest tutor on this page is a few dollars a lesson; the difference between a tutor you look forward to and one you do not is whether you are still learning in three months.
