# Nigeria Language Inventory — Deep Audit (Phase 6)

Batch: western-africa (pass 1) · Status: PRELIMINARY · Date: 2026-09-14

Nigeria is Africa's hardest inventory problem (≈520 living languages, no
census language data EVER collected at usable granularity) and its biggest
learner market south of the Sahara. This file is the Phase 6 deep audit:
the 3 national languages + English against the 1999 Constitution, the Pidgin
question (the country's true lingua franca has zero official status), and
the code-boundary disputes that pass 2 must rule on.

## 1. Coverage

- Entries: **13** (English + 3 nationals + Pidgin + Fulfulde + 6 regionals + NSL).
- Living languages (Ethnologue 27, medium confidence): **≈520**.
- Census language data: **none** — the 2006 census asked no language question
  (and 1991's was never published at language granularity). EVERY speaker
  figure below is an estimate; treat all bands as ±30% until pass 2.

## 3. Gap analysis (EkGuru coverage)

- P1 plans exist for hau/yor/ibo/pcm — the correct four: they cover >90% of
  Nigerians as L1-or-L2.
- fuv (15M, northern lingua franca alongside Hausa) is the largest
  inventoried language with status `none` — first promotion candidate if
  northern-NG demand materialises.
- Middle Belt cluster (Berom, Ngas, Eggon, Mwaghavul, Goemai — each 100K–1M)
  sits entirely in notesLongTail: individually below threshold, jointly
  ≈5M speakers. Pass 2 ruling needed on a "Middle Belt pack" concept.
- Arabic (Quranic schooling, millions of readers, no L1 community) is
  deliberately NOT an entry — liturgical-only, same treatment as BD/PK.

## 4. Constitutional backbone (what the entries cite)

- 1999 Constitution s.55: National Assembly business in English; Hausa, Igbo
  and Yoruba "when adequate arrangements have been made" — the legal basis
  for the 3 nationals' status (de facto, arrangements were never completed).
- National Policy on Education: mother-tongue/3-major medium in early
  primary, English from upper primary — widely cited, patchily implemented.
- No constitutional role for Pidgin (see §5) or for any regional language;
  state assemblies may use local languages ad hoc (Hausa in northern
  assemblies, Yoruba/Igbo in south-west/south-east).

## 5. The Pidgin question (status artefact — do not take roles at face value)

Nigerian Pidgin (pcm) is spoken by more Nigerians than any other language
(≈120M L2) yet holds NO constitutional status, while English — mastered by
a minority — is the sole official language. Consequences for the inventory:

- `pcm` roles carry `national` on the strength of de-facto national use
  (media, music, NTA, legislature debate), NOT on statute; the notes field
  says so explicitly.
- Learner Pidgin = Lagos/Motor-park standard emerging via the Naijá Langwej
  Akademi + BBC News Pidgin orthography (variation note, Phase 10).
- The pcm–English continuum (basilect→acrolect) means L1/L2 splits are
  judgement calls; band uses the 120M total with a confidence penalty.

## 6. Code treatments specific to Nigeria

- **Fulfulde boundary**: NG `fuv` (Nigerian Fulfulde) vs NE/BF `fuh`
  (Western Niger Fulfulde) — adjacent codes, same continuum; Sokoto-area
  varieties could be cited under either. Boundary TBD pass 2.
- **Kanuri cluster**: `knc` (Central/Yerwa Kanuri) is the NG entry; Manga
  Kanuri (`kby`) and Tumari are cross-border (NE) with NG spillover —
  currently folded into the knc note, split TBD pass 2.
- **Efik/Ibibio pair**: `efi` (historic missionary/literary standard,
  Calabar) vs `ibb` (larger L1 base, Uyo axis) — distinct codes, high mutual
  intelligibility; separate entries, cross-noted, never merged.
- **Tiv**: `tiv` (Tivoid; NOT Bantu) — Benue/Nasarawa/Plateau; single-code
  treatment holds (dialects mutually intelligible).
- **Edo**: `bin` (Edoid; Benin City) — distinct from Esan (`ish`, long-tail).
- **Nupe**: `nup` (Nupoid; Niger/Kwara/Kogi) — Nupe-Nupe Tako (`nup` covers;
  Bassa-Nge `bqx` long-tail).
- **NSL**: `nsi` (Nigerian Sign Language) — ASL-influenced school system;
  Hausa/Yoruba/Igbo sign varieties vs one-code treatment TBD pass 2.
