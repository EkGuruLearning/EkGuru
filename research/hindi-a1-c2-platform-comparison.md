## Feature coverage decision matrix
| Capability | Core launch | Later | Do not claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| native audio | every core dialogue + pronunciation contrasts | broad regional/age library | TTS as native |
| TTS | transparent fallback and generated practice | expressive synthesis after evaluation | certification evidence |
| ASR | intelligibility/transcription confidence and retry | phoneme diagnostics validated for Hindi | accent elimination or sole pass/fail |
| AI roleplay | bounded scenarios, transcript, safety and rubric | open goal simulation | human-equivalent cultural judgment |
| certificates | completion credential with score profile | externally moderated assessment | official recognition without accreditation |
| personalization | weak skills, mistakes, goals and pace | learned recommendation model | hidden level inflation |
| print/offline | per-unit worksheet/audio/transcript bundle | offline speech capture/sync | online-only essential learning |

## Hindi-specific competitive moat
1. Script recognition, handwriting, typing, dictation and reading-speed benchmarks remain connected after A1.
2. Relationship-aware आप/तुम/तू, imperatives, titles and indirectness are metadata, not footnotes.
3. Aspect/agreement engine diagnoses the *reason* for an error (gender, ergativity, object marking, auxiliary).
4. Every advanced text is tagged colloquial, neutral formal, administrative, academic, journalistic or literary; transformations are assessed.
5. B2–C2 use unseen source synthesis, mediation, inference and performance—not recycled sentence taps.
