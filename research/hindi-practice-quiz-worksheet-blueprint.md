## Assessment architecture
- **Lesson quiz:** 6–10 items; objective coverage, misconception distractors, one transfer item; explanations and targeted retry. Never a certification claim.
- **Unit quiz:** interleaves prior units, includes unseen scenario plus one short productive task; first attempt has no hints.
- **Level test:** parallel forms; reading/listening/writing/speaking/interaction/mediation; unseen input; separate skill scores and confidence interval.
- **Diagnostic:** fine-grained prerequisite gaps after difficulty or lapse; remediation without punitive demotion.
- **Placement:** adaptive receptive screen followed by Devanagari, spoken and written verification; provisional placement until two units confirm.
- **Review test:** sample at 7/30/90 days; weigh communicative importance, weakness and prior uncertainty.

### Scoring by band
A1/A2 emphasizes task completion, intelligibility and core form; B1 adds connectedness/repair; B2 adds evidence, interaction and genre; C1 adds synthesis/implicit stance/register; C2 adds semantic precision, rhetorical effect and effortless reformulation. Analytic rubrics: task fulfillment 25%, comprehensibility/coherence 20%, range 15%, accuracy 15%, interaction/mediation 15%, sociolinguistic/genre control 10%; at C1/C2 shift 5 points from basic accuracy to precision/style. Critical meaning reversal caps task score.

Every packet: objective, estimated time, three difficulty bands, grayscale-safe A4/Letter layout, mobile-fillable fields, answer key with rationale/acceptable alternatives, self-check and QR/deep link to audio. Teacher version adds rubric and likely Hindi-specific errors. Keep prompts with answers on separate pages.

## Audio/voice specification
- Commission native recordings for core dialogues and minimal pairs; preserve raw master, speaker consent, region/age/gender metadata and transcript alignment.
- Record normal and genuinely pedagogical slow versions where useful; do not time-stretch into distorted “slow native” audio.
- TTS is labeled **synthetic** and is a fallback/variable-generation tool, never “native audio.”
- Listening ladder: isolated contrast→phrase→turn→dialogue→announcement/story→multi-speaker argument→implicit/idiomatic authentic-style discourse.
- ASR provides transcript match, segment confidence and retry target. It does not judge cultural appropriateness or certify pronunciation alone.
- Support line repeat, A/B role isolation, shadow pause, speed controls in learning, transcript toggle, one-play assessment and offline bundles.

## SRS specification
Use a validated FSRS-style scheduler where feasible. New learning steps occur same day; later intervals adapt to recall, latency and card history. Cap load; allow pause/reschedule. Cards have one retrieval target, source lesson, register, audio provenance, accepted variants and prerequisite. Recognition graduates to production. Sentence cloze is preferred for gender/case/aspect; isolated cards suit script and concrete high-frequency lexicon. Never auto-card every word, whole explanations, long passages, ambiguous sentences or unverified translations. Repeated lapse triggers contrast/explanation/transfer task, not endless identical repetition.

## Feedback loop
Wrong answer → error tag (script/sound/gender/case/aspect/lexis/register/comprehension) → brief explanation → minimal contrast → delayed transfer item → weak-topic card only if retrieval is the issue. Dashboard separates exposure, lesson accuracy, delayed retention and verified performance.
