# Hindi A1–C2 Platform Study

**Scope/date:** desk research synthesized 17 September 2026. Product features and Hindi availability change; catalogue claims must be rechecked before implementation. This is feature analysis, not an overall ranking. No competitor lesson content is reproduced.

## Method and evidence discipline
We triangulated official product documentation, independent hands-on analyses, CEFR descriptors, university/academic Hindi resources, dictionaries and government/standards resources. “Native audio,” TTS and ASR are distinct. A platform's global feature is not assumed available in Hindi. Marketing level labels are not accepted as proof of balanced performance.

## Market design patterns
1. **Habit loop:** 5–15 minute path lessons, streaks, progress, immediate correction (Duolingo/Memrise/Ling). Benefit: return frequency. Risk: optimizing taps rather than transfer. EkGuru: optional short route, but promotion requires open tasks.
2. **Audio anticipation:** learner hears a situation and must respond before model (Pimsleur/Rocket). Benefit: retrieval under time. Risk: literacy and free interaction remain thin. EkGuru: hands-free track linked to Devanagari and roleplay.
3. **Dialogue decomposition:** full dialogue, line audio, transcript, literal/natural gloss, notes (HindiPod101/Mango/Rocket). Benefit: form–meaning–use integration. EkGuru: native-speed first/last, line controls in learning only.
4. **SRS:** word or sentence reviews selected by memory model (Anki/Memrise/Glossika/Clozemaster). Benefit: efficient retention. Risk: memorizing ambiguous/low-value cards. EkGuru: strict eligibility and mistake repair.
5. **Human feedback:** tutor or community correction (Preply/italki/Busuu/HindiPod101 tiers). Benefit: open production and pragmatic correction. Risk: inconsistency, delay and cost. EkGuru: common rubrics, reviewer calibration and AI triage—not AI pretense.
6. **CEFR visibility:** can-do levels, section checks and certificates in selected products/languages. Benefit: goal clarity. Risk: content completion ≠ proficiency. EkGuru: per-skill evidence and delayed retention.

## Product dimensions EkGuru must capture
- **Structure:** level→unit→lesson→objective→guided learning→transfer→review; 8 recommended units × 4 core lessons per level, plus labs.
- **Lesson length:** 12–18 minute core path; 3–8 minute review; 20–35 minute audio/worksheet/deep-reading extensions. Preserve position across modes.
- **Feedback:** answer/rationale/error tag/repair item; confidence-aware pronunciation; rubric on open work.
- **Personalization:** prerequisite graph, weak-skill queue, goal scenarios, pace and script remediation; never silently skip an entire skill.
- **Motivation:** can-do milestones, streak repair and portfolios; no punitive mechanism that discourages productive risk.
- **Offline:** downloadable audio/transcripts/worksheets/cards with later sync.
- **Children/adults:** shared linguistic objective and assessment construct; age-appropriate context, safeguarding, explanation and reward skin.

## Hindi linguistic findings that shape product design
- Devanagari is an abugida: independent/dependent vowels, consonants, matras, virama/conjuncts and nasal signs require recognition **and production**. Unicode is the encoding authority [UNICODE].
- Hindi contrasts dental/retroflex and aspirated/unaspirated stops; perception must precede increasingly spontaneous production [KOUL].
- Gender, number, person/honorificity, case/postpositions and aspect interact in agreement. Perfective transitive **ने** constructions and differential object marking require staged contrasts [KOUL].
- Hindi verbal competence is aspect-centered (habitual, progressive, perfective) with tense/modal auxiliaries, not a simple English tense substitution [UT; CU].
- Compound/light verbs add completion, affectedness, direction or speaker construal; causatives include productive morphological patterns with lexical/phonological constraints [UT; ACL].
- Relative-correlative constructions, particles ही/भी/तो, flexible order and honorific choices connect grammar to discourse/pragmatics.
- “Hindi” spans colloquial Hindustani, Sanskritic official/academic choices, Perso-Arabic resources, regional speech and contextual Hindi-English mixing. Teach labeled choice, not one purity ideology.

## Child, adult and universal principles
| Universal | Child-friendly adaptation | Adult-friendly adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| same sound/script target | tracing, image story, short recording, no public profile | keyboard + handwriting choice, explicit articulatory diagram |
| same grammar function | pattern discovery, concrete characters | concise rule, contrastive explanation, error analysis |
| same pragmatic relation | safe school/family/community scene | work, travel, services, family and study |
| same performance rubric | shorter turns and age-appropriate cognition | authentic genre and longer output |
| spaced retrieval | lower daily load, guardian controls | goal/deadline settings and professional deck |
