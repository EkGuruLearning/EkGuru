## 3. Gap analysis (EkGuru coverage)

- P1 plan for fas is correct (official 75M + AF/TJ macro partners).
- azb (16M) is the largest Turkic `none` in Western Asia — no official
  status anywhere on the southern side; promotion watch only.
- **Bakhtiari gap**: Bakhtiari (`bqi`, Chahar Mahal ~1M) is NOT entered —
  distinct code between lrc/luz, entry-class by threshold. Pass-2 entry.
- **Gorani gap**: Gorani (`hac`, Hawraman ~500K) is NOT entered — distinct
  code (NOT Southern Kurdish despite geography). Pass-2 entry.
- Zoroastrian Dari (`gbz` ~10K), Mandaic (`mid`, Ahwaz remnant), Khalaj
  (`kjf` ~50K), Semnani cluster correctly sit in long-tail.

## 4. Constitutional backbone (what the entries cite)

- Art.15: Persian is official; regional/tribal languages may be used in
  press/media, and their literature is taught in schools alongside Persian
  (implementation: media yes, schools patchy-to-absent).
- Art.16: Arabic is compulsory through secondary school (Quranic/Islamic
  sciences) — the legal basis for Arabic-literacy WITHOUT an Arabic-L1
  entry outside Khuzestan.
- Art.13: Zoroastrian/Jewish/Christian recognised minorities with school
  rights — the legal basis for the hye/aii entries (Isfahan Jolfa,
  Tehran, Urmia schools).

## 5. The no-data artefact (read every figure as an estimate)

Iran publishes no ethnic or mother-tongue census tables — the only major
multilingual state with a TOTAL statistical blackout (Turkey at least has
1965). Consequences:

- Azeri 16M spans published 12–20M; Kurdish-triple splits (ckb/kmr/sdh)
  rest on dialectology, not headcounts; Khuzestan Arabic 1.5M spans 1–2M.
- All 18 bands carry low-medium confidence; pass 2 triangulates CIA
  Factbook/L Atlases/academic surveys WITHOUT laundering any single
  partisan source.
- Shift is the one measured trend: Caspian (glk/mzn) youth Persian-dominance
  is documented in sociolinguistic literature — the decline notes are the
  most-cited claims in the entries.

- **Luri pair + Bakhtiari**: `lrc` + `luz` entered; `bqi` missing (§3) —

- **Script splits (all Perso-Arabic IR-side)**: azb Arabic vs AZ-side
  `azj` Latin; tuk Arabic vs TM-side Latin; Kurdish Arabic vs TR-side
  Kurmanji Latin — script notes mandatory on every cross-border pack.
- **Qashqai/Khorasani**: `qxq` + `kmz` (Oghuz individuals, NOT Azeri
  dialects, NOT Turkmen) — separate entries.
