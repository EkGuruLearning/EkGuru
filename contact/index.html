Questions about contacting us

How quickly will I get a reply?

Usually within a day. Messages are read by a person, not a system, and that person is in India (UTC+5:30) — so if you write from Europe or the Americas the reply often lands overnight for you. If two days pass with nothing, check your spam folder before writing again: an automated acknowledgement from a small site is exactly the sort of mail that gets filtered.

Who actually reads this?

Prakash, who runs EkGuru, reads every message. There is no support team, no ticket queue and no chatbot. That is the honest state of a site this size, and it is why the answer usually comes from someone who can actually change the thing you are writing about.

Do I need an account to write to you?

No. There is no account system on EkGuru at all. Fill in three fields and press send. Nothing is stored on this website — there is no database here — your message is simply emailed to us and a copy is emailed to you.

I found a mistake in a Hindi word on one of your pages.

Please do send it. Devanagari typed by hand goes wrong in small ways — a missing matra, the wrong nukta, a halant in the wrong place — and a native speaker spotting it is worth more than any amount of proofreading on our side. Pick “A Hindi spelling looks wrong” in the topic list and say which page. Corrections are made on the next build, usually the same week.

Can I book a lesson through this form?

You can ask about one, but the booking form on a tutor's own page is faster: it goes straight to that tutor with your level, your timezone and the slot you want already attached. Use this form if you are not sure which tutor suits you, and say what you want to be able to do in Hindi — that is more useful to us than a level.

I want to teach Hindi on EkGuru.

Choose “I want to teach on EkGuru” in the topic list, or use the Become a Tutor page which asks the specific things we need to know. Tutors here are native Hindi speakers who teach one to one. We are small and we read every application properly, which also means we are slower than a marketplace.

What do you do with my email address?

Reply to you. That is the whole list. It is not added to a mailing list, there is no newsletter to be added to, and it is not passed to anyone. The message is delivered by a third-party form relay because this site has no server of its own — the same way the booking form works.

Is there a phone number or a WhatsApp?

No, and that is deliberate. Everything is handled in writing so there is a record both sides can refer back to, and so that a tutor's personal number is never published anywhere on this site.

Before you write

Two things answer themselves faster than we can:

  • Prices and lesson lengths are on each tutor's page and are always current — they are read live from our own records, so a page can never show a stale price. See the tutors.
  • How to start learning is covered free: the guides, the tools and the thirty-day path need no account and cost nothing. If your question is “where do I begin”, start there — then write to us about the part that is not working.
Want a person to practise with rather than an inbox?

Our tutors are native Hindi speakers in India teaching one to one over video. You can send a booking request without writing to us first.
