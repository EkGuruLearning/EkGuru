Malayalam

The Malayalam alphabet

Vowels, consonants, conjuncts and the famous ഴ — the whole alphabet, kindly explained.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Malayalam spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Malayalam vowels (സ്വരങ്ങൾ) come in fifteen: അ ആ ഇ ഈ ഉ ഊ ഋ എ ഏ ഐ ഒ ഓ ഔ അം അഃ. Short and long pairs — അ/ആ, ഇ/ഈ, ഉ/ഊ — change meaning completely: കല (kala — art) versus കാലം (kaalam — time). Length is meaning in Malayalam; stretch with care.

Consonants (വ്യഞ്ജനങ്ങൾ) run five rows deep: ക ഖ ഗ ഘ ങ, ച ഛ ജ ഝ ഞ, ട ഠ ഡ ഢ ണ, ത ഥ ദ ധ ന, പ ഫ ബ ഭ മ — plus യ ര ല വ ശ ഷ സ ഹ ള ഴ റ. Round, curly, generous — the script that writes Tamil's sister with Sanskrit's wardrobe.

Vowels and consonants marry into കൂട്ടക്ഷരങ്ങൾ (koottaksharams): ക് + അ = ക (ka), ക് + ആ = കാ (kaa). Learn the vowel-signs once and every consonant sings the same song — the system is vast but perfectly regular.

Then the crown jewel: ഴ (zha), the retroflex approximant — പഴം (fruit), വാഴ (plantain). Curl the tongue back without touching the roof, voice it, and feel the most Malayali sound in existence. Natives will grin when you land it; the grin is worth weeks of practice.

A note on ചന്ദ്രക്കല (chandrakkala). The little crescent ് kills a consonant's hidden vowel: ക is ka, ക് is just k. Malayalam writes pure consonants with the chandrakkala and syllables without — ക് + അ = ക. One crescent, and the whole reading system clicks.

Questions learners ask

How many letters does Malayalam have?

Fifteen vowels (സ്വരങ്ങൾ) and thirty-six consonants (വ്യഞ്ജനങ്ങൾ) — 51+ letters that combine into conjuncts. Sounds huge, behaves simply: learn the shapes and the combinations assemble themselves.

What is the hardest Malayalam letter?

ഴ (zha) — the retroflex approximant Malayalam shares with Tamil: പഴം, വാഴ. Curl the tongue back without touching, voice it. Listen, curl, practise: it arrives within weeks.

Does Malayalam have kh, gh, bh sounds?

Yes — in writing: ഖ ഘ ഛ ഝ ഠ ഢ ഥ ധ ഫ ഭ appear in Sanskrit loans (ക്ഷമ, ധർമ്മം). Daily Malayalam softens most of them; recognise them in print, relax in speech.
