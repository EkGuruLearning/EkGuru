Malayalam

Writing Malayalam

Letters to lists to letters — writing Malayalam from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Malayalam spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with strokes: Malayalam letters curve and loop — എഴുതൂ (please write) circles, hooks and waves before full letters. Trace അ ആ ഇ ഈ ഉ ഊ big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: പേന (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Malayali children trace in rice, sand and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: പട്ടിക (list) — കുറിപ്പ് (notes), വിലാസം (addresses), തീയതി (dates). Copy your own പേര് (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: കത്ത് (letters), അപേക്ഷ (applications), പരീക്ഷ (exams), വീട്ടുപാഠം (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Malayalam — short, joyful, posted. First posted Malayalam is never forgotten.

Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Malayalam?

Round strokes before all: Malayalam letters flow circular — അ, ആ, ഉ, എ — so practise circles and hooks first. Trace big, shrink slowly; കൈയെഴുത്ത് (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

പട്ടിക (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

കത്ത് (letters) to friends, കുറിപ്പ് (notes) to self, വീട്ടുപാഠം (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
