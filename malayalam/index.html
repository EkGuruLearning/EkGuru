Questions about this course

Must I learn the Malayalam script before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Malayalam in Roman letters, and let the script come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

നമസ്കാരം for respect, അതെ and ഇല്ല, നന്ദി, ദയവായി, ശരി, and ഞാൻ — I. With those seven you can open any interaction in Kerala politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Malayalam?

The famous ഴ plus retroflexes — ള, ണ, long-short vowel pairs. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each one.
