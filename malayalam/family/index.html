Family words in Malayalam

അമ്മ, അച്ഛൻ, ചേട്ടൻ, ചേച്ചി — family, including the elder-younger split.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Malayalam spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Parents anchor everything: അമ്മ (amma — mother) and അച്ഛൻ (achchhan — father) — the first words of every Malayali child and the warmest words in the language. അമ്മ calls across market crowds; അച്ഛൻ advises over evening chaya. Learn them with love, not just memory.

Siblings split by age, never blend: ചേട്ടൻ (elder brother), അനിയൻ (younger brother), ചേച്ചി (elder sister), അനിയത്തി (younger sister). There is no plain brother — every brother is elder or younger, and the word you choose declares the hierarchy. Ask ages early; address correctly always.

Grandparents rule the roost: മുത്തച്ഛൻ (muttachchhan — grandfather) and മുത്തശ്ശി (muttashshi — grandmother) — storytellers, sweet-sneakers, family courts of final appeal. മുത്തശ്ശി കഥ (grandmother's tale) is a whole genre; മുത്തച്ഛൻ advice ends arguments. Respect flows upward; treats flow down.

The wider net holds daily life: അമ്മാവൻ (uncle), അമ്മായി (auntie), ഭർത്താവ് (husband), ഭാര്യ (wife), കുടുംബം (family itself). എന്റെ കുടുംബം (my family) introduces them all — practise the sentence with your own names filled in, because you will say it at every Malayali gathering forever.

A note on അമ്മാവൻ, അമ്മായി. Malayali kinship runs precise: അമ്മാവൻ (mother's brother / respected uncle), അമ്മായി (auntie), ചേട്ടത്തി (elder brother's wife). Learners need the big six first; the full map unfolds at weddings.

Questions learners ask

Why two words for brother?

Yes — Malayalam splits siblings by age: ചേട്ടൻ (elder brother) versus അനിയൻ (younger brother), ചേച്ചി (elder sister) versus അനിയത്തി (younger sister). Age order is grammar in Malayali families.

Can I call strangers chettan and chechi?

ചേട്ടൻ (chettan) and ചേച്ചി (chechi) address any slightly-older man or woman — shopkeepers, colleagues, strangers. It borrows family warmth for the whole world.

How do I ask about family?

നിങ്ങളുടെ കുടുംബത്തിൽ എത്ര പേർ? (how many people in your family?) — then listen proudly to the answer. Family questions are love in Malayali culture.
