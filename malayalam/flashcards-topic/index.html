Flashcards by topic in Malayalam

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Revisit Malayalam topic cards just before they fade, keeping spaced practice short enough to fit an ordinary day.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Malayalam spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Cards carry memory: കാർഡ് (card) — one word, one side; meaning, the other. ഓർമ്മ (memory) strengthens with every recall; മറവി (forgetting) is just unreviewed memory. Build decks per topic — greetings, food, verbs — and review each separately.

Spacing schedules success: ഇടവേള (interval) — review new cards tomorrow, then in three days, seven, thirty. വീണ്ടും വീണ്ടും (again and again), but wider each time. Cramming fills hours; spacing fills years.

Rhythms sustain streaks: ദിവസേന (daily) quick decks, ആഴ്ചതോറും (weekly) full reviews, മാസത്തിലൊരിക്കൽ (monthly) championships. Ten daily minutes beat Sunday marathons; streaks turn study into ശീലം (habit).

Scores gamify growth: മാർക്ക് (marks), മത്സരം (contest), നേട്ടം (achievement) — even പരാജയം (defeat) teaches. Quiz yourself, beat yesterday's score, celebrate loudly. Flashcards are games that pay in fluency.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ആവർത്തനം. ആവർത്തനം (aavarthanam — revision, repeating) is Malayalam's own word for spaced review — recalling again, and again, at widening intervals. The language itself teaches the method: revisit, widen, remember.

Questions learners ask

How often should I review?

വീണ്ടും വീണ്ടും (again and again) — but spaced: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 30 days. ഇടവേള (spacing) beats cramming; ഓർമ്മ (memory) loves intervals.

How many cards per topic?

Five to ten per topic — quality over quantity. Master one കാർഡ് deck fully before opening the next; half-known decks rot.

Daily or weekly review?

ദിവസേന (daily) short sessions — ten minutes, every day, no exceptions. Streaks compound; മറവി (forgetting) loses to routine.
