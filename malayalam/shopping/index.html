Shopping and bargaining in Malayalam

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Prices, bargaining and colours — shop in Malayalam from first price to deal.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Malayalam spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Prices open every transaction: വില എത്ര? (what's the price?), എത്ര? (how much?), ഇതിന് എത്ര? (what's this one's price?). Listen for the number plus രൂപ — repeat it back to confirm — അമ്പത്? (fifty?) — and you are already shopping in Malayalam.

Bargaining follows the friendly script: വളരെ കൂടുതലാണ് (too costly), കുറച്ച് കുറയ്ക്കൂ (reduce a little), ഡിസ്കൗണ്ട് ഉണ്ടോ? (any discount?). Start walking away slowly — നന്ദി, നോക്കാം (thanks, I'll look around) — and the real price often chases you down the street.

Sizes and colours refine the hunt: വലിയത് (the bigger one), ചെറിയത് (the smaller one), പുതിയത് (a new one), വേറെ നിറം (a different colour). Point, name the change, nod — Malayali shopkeepers read gestures fluently and reward attempts instantly.

Colours close the sale: ചുവപ്പ് (red), നീല (blue), പച്ച (green), മഞ്ഞ (yellow), വെള്ള (white), കറുപ്പ് (black). ചുവപ്പ് നന്നായിരിക്കുന്നു (the red looks good) — then വാങ്ങാം (I'll take it). Deal done, in Malayalam, with a smile on both sides.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on കുറയ്ക്കൂ. കുറച്ച് കുറയ്ക്കൂ (kurach kuraykkoo — please reduce a little) is the magic sentence of Kerala markets. Said smiling, it opens every negotiation; said with വാങ്ങാം (I'll take it) right after, it closes most. Bargaining respects the dance — never the demand.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask the price?

വില എത്ര? (what's the price?) — then എത്ര? (how much?) for everything else. Two questions, every shop in Kerala.

How do I bargain politely?

വളരെ കൂടുതലാണ് (too costly) with a smile, then കുറച്ച് കുറയ്ക്കൂ (reduce a little, please). Bargaining is friendly theatre — play your part warmly and the price moves.

Which colours first?

Start with five: ചുവപ്പ് (red), നീല (blue), പച്ച (green), മഞ്ഞ (yellow), വെള്ള (white). വേറെ നിറം (different colour) shows them all.
