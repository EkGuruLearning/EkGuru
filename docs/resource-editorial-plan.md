# Resource editorial plan

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: no parallel blog created

## Decision

EkGuru already publishes a public learning library under `/learn/`, `/hindi/`, `/ask/`, `/answers/`, `/daily-hindi/` and `/toolbox/`. Those pages answer real learner questions (script, pronunciation, first phrases, grammar, practice). Creating a second `/blog/` or twenty new articles to hit a count would be padding. This audit did not do that.

Google does not require a fixed article count or a fixed word count. Readiness is about useful, original, crawlable public information, not a quota.

## What was added instead

| Item | Intent | Audience | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/learn/countries/` | One index of the country guides that are actually published | Learner comparing timezone and first-language notes | PUBLISHED |
| Navigation: Home, Learn, Tutors, Courses, About, Contact | Make the public site understandable without opening the app | Every visitor and reviewer | PUBLISHED |

`/learn/countries/` is a directory of existing reviewed guides. It does not invent country facts. Research-only country pages stay noindex and are not listed.

## Not published

- No 20-article batch.
- No thin tag archives.
- No competitor comparison table with unverified fees. The old italki/Preply answer that stated commission rates and a broken arithmetic example was rewritten to refuse unverified marketplace claims.
- Draft tutor profiles stay out of the public roster.

## Review rule

AI-assisted drafting may propose a guide. It may not invent tutors, reviews, ratings, learner counts, qualifications, or marketplace terms. A new guide is PUBLISHED only when it has a unique title, one H1, a useful example, internal links, and no placeholder. Otherwise it stays DRAFT and noindex.
