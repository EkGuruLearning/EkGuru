## Quality & Brand Accuracy Audit
- **Official Logo:** Uses the exact, unmodified `images/logo.svg` vector geometry and gradients.
- **Brand Accuracy:** Zero invented student/tutor counts, zero fabricated reviews/testimonials, zero payment-processing claims, zero fake website screenshots, zero discount/offer claims.
- **Typography & Devanagari Shaping:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (display headlines), `Inter` (editorial body), and OpenType HarfBuzz-shaped `Noto Sans Devanagari` for 100% accurate Hindi conjuncts, matras, and nuktas (`सिर्फ`, `संस्कृति`, `जुड़ने`, `जोड़ने`, `जुड़िए`, `अंदाज़`, `दुनिया।`).
