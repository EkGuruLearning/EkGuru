Search EkGuru

Everything on the site — 651 pages of lessons, answers, practice labs, materials, country pages, tools and tutors. Pick the country you are in and the language you think in, and the search keeps only what is written for you. Searching happens in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Country pages carry lesson times in local time and prices in local currency; language pages are the ones written for speakers of your first language.

How to get a good result

🕉️

Devanagari works

Type पानी, किताब or नमस्ते — the index is Unicode-normalised, so accents and matras do not have to be perfect.

🔤

Roman works too

“paani”, “kitab” or “namaste” find the same pages: the fuzzy helper maps Roman spellings onto Devanagari and back.

🌍

Search by place

Japan, Brazil, the UAE — every country funnel is indexed, with the languages that are documented there. The Country list above does the same thing in one click.

🧰

Search by tool

flashcards, alphabet chart, transliteration, numbers, verb drill — the interactive labs are in the same index.

🎚️

Narrow by type, country, language

The filter chips keep only one kind of page — lessons, tools, tutors, materials, questions — and show how many exist. Country keeps the funnels written for where you are; Language keeps the pages written for speakers of your first language. All three combine, and the line above the results always says which are switched on.

🔒

Nothing is logged

There is no search server. Your query is matched against a JSON file in your browser and disappears when you close the tab.
