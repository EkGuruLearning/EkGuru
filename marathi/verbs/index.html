Marathi verbs

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

असणे, जाणे, करणे — the verbs that carry every Marathi sentence.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Being grounds all: असणे (to be) — मी आहे (I am), आम्ही आहोत (we are), तुम्ही आहात (you are), ते आहेत (they are). आहे anchors identity, place, state — मी इथे आहे (I'm here), मी ठीक आहे (I'm fine). Be first.

Going and coming move life: जाणे (to go), येणे (to come) — मी जातो/जाते (I go), मी येतो/येते (I come). Direction verbs plus कुठे (where) navigate Maharashtra — मी मुंबईला जातो (I'm going to Mumbai).

Doing and eating fill days: करणे (to do), खाणे (to eat), पिणे (to drink) — मी काम करतो (I work), मी जेवतो (I eat a meal). Daily verbs, daily use; conjugate them in your sleep.

Speaking and knowing crown all: बोलणे (to speak), वाचणे (to read), लिहिणे (to write) — मी मराठी बोलतो (I speak Marathi). Say it now, imperfectly; say it daily until the mirror agrees.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on जातो/जाते/जातं. Marathi verbs wear gender openly — मी जातो (I go, male speaker), मी जाते (female), ते जातं (neuter it). Three endings, one verb. Learn your own gender's forms first; the others come through listening.

Questions learners ask

How do I say 'I am'?

असणे (to be): मी आहे (I am), तू आहेस, तो/ती आहे, आम्ही आहोत, तुम्ही आहात, ते आहेत. Being first, everything else after.

Why do verbs change by gender?

Three genders show on verbs: जातो (m), जाते (f), जातं (n). Say your gender's ending confidently — मी जातो / मी जाते.

Which five verbs first?

जाणे (to go), येणे (to come), करणे (to do), खाणे (to eat), बोलणे (to speak). Five verbs, half of daily speech.
