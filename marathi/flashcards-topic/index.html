Marathi

Flashcards by topic

Spaced repetition, topic decks, daily streaks — flashcards that work.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Cards carry memory: कार्ड (card) — one word, one side; meaning, the other. आठवण (memory) strengthens with every recall; विस्मरण (forgetting) is just unreviewed memory. Build decks per topic — greetings, food, verbs — and review each separately.

Spacing schedules success: अंतर (interval) — review new cards tomorrow, then in three days, seven, thirty. पुन्हा पुन्हा (again and again), but wider each time. Cramming fills hours; spacing fills years.

Rhythms sustain streaks: रोज (daily) quick decks, आठवड्यातून (weekly) full reviews, महिन्यातून (monthly) championships. Ten daily minutes beat Sunday marathons; streaks turn study into सवय (habit).

Scores gamify growth: गुण (marks), स्पर्धा (contest), यश (achievement) — even अपयश (defeat) teaches. Quiz yourself, beat yesterday's score, celebrate loudly. Flashcards are games that pay in fluency.

A note on उजळणी. उजळणी (ujalani — revision, brushing up) is Marathi's own word for spaced review — recalling again, and again, at widening intervals. The language itself teaches the method: revisit, widen, remember.

Questions learners ask

How often should I review?

पुन्हा पुन्हा (again and again) — but spaced: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 30 days. अंतर (spacing) beats cramming; आठवण (memory) loves intervals.

How many cards per topic?

Five to ten per topic — quality over quantity. Master one कार्ड deck fully before opening the next; half-known decks rot.

Daily or weekly review?

रोज (daily) short sessions — ten minutes, every day, no exceptions. Streaks compound; विस्मरण (forgetting) loses to routine.
