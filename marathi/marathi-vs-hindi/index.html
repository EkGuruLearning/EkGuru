Marathi vs Hindi

Cousin languages, shared script — shared words, split sounds.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The cousins grew side by side: मराठी bloomed in Maharashtra, हिंदी spread across the north. Same Indo-Aryan mother, same Devanagari dress — Marathi kept three genders and ळ while Hindi streamlined to two. History in every sentence.

Shared words delight learners: घर/घर (house), Marathi आई and Hindi माँ differ, but पाणी/पानी (water), घर/घर (house), नाव/नाम (name) overlap enormously. Core vocabulary crosses the border daily.

Split sounds mark the border: Marathi ळ (lla) against Hindi ल, मला (mala — to-me) against मुझे, हो (ho — yes) against हाँ. Pronouns and yes-no drifted most — precisely the words learners say most. Drill the pairs; cross the border daily.

Greetings show both faces: नमस्कार serves both formal while नमस्ते stands Hindi-classic — yet each understands the other's instantly. Cousins recognise cousins; learn either with love and both reward you.

Questions learners ask

Can Marathis understand Hindi?

Largely yes for simple speech — घर, पाणी, आई, नाही/नहीं are near-identical. Speed and slang divide them; patience reunites them.

Why do they sound similar yet different?

Marathi kept the ळ and three genders; Hindi simplified to two and lost the flap. Cousins with different habits — precisely the words learners say most.

Which should I learn first?

Marathi first for Maharashtra, Hindi structure second for the nation. Each language halves the other's difficulty — learn one, get the second at a discount.
