Write your name in Marathi

Rahul becomes राहुल — spell, write and own your name in Devanagari.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Names transliterate by ear: break yours into sounds, match each to Devanagari letters — रवी (Ravi), लक्ष्मी (Lakshmi), किरण (Kiran) show the pattern. Say it slow, write it true — स्पेलिंग (spelling) by sound, not by English letters.

Foreign sounds borrow dotted letters: ज for azure-sounds (ॲझर), फ for phone (फोन), ऑ for coffee (कॉफी). These spotted letters are Marathi citizens now — use them proudly for every imported sound.

Introductions pair speech and script: ओळख (introduction) — माझं नाव (my name is), spoken, then written. नावाचे बोर्ड (name boards) on doors and desks teach daily — read every board, collect every name.

Nicknames warm everything: टोपणनाव (nickname) — बाळ (dear child), सोन्या (golden one), पिल्लू (little one). Marathi shortens with love: Ravi becomes रवी, Lakshmi becomes लचू. Earn a Marathi nickname and you have arrived.

Questions learners ask

How do I write my name in Marathi?

Sound it out in Devanagari letters: Rahul → राहुल, Priya → प्रिया, John → जॉन. Marathi spells by ear — say your name slowly, catch each sound, write each letter.

What about sounds Marathi lacks?

Nukta letters — ज (za), फ (fa) — handle foreign sounds. जॉन (John), फोन (phone) — spotted letters for imported sounds, fully Marathi now.

How do I introduce my name?

पहिलं अक्षर (first letter) starts it, शेवटचं अक्षर (last letter) ends it. Introduce with माझं नाव (my name is), then write it — introductions stick twice as well written.
