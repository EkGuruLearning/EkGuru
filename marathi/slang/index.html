Marathi slang

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

भारी, राडा, एक नंबर — street Marathi that fun runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Praise slang opens doors: भारी (awesome), एक नंबर (number one), लय भारी (very awesome), राडा (rocking), कडक (solid). Food, films, friends' outfits — praise everything loudly. Marathis praise generously; learners should too.

Friendship words bond crews: भाऊ (bro), दादा (big bro), मित्रा (friend) — friends-only, always warm. भाऊ, ये ना (come on bro) starts a thousand conversations daily. Never aim them upward at elders; sideways at friends, fire freely.

Reaction words colour stories: अय्यो (oh no!), अर्रर्र (wow!), गप्प बस (stop making things up, sit quiet!). Marathi storytelling runs on reactions — gasp, laugh, disbelieve theatrically. Flat faces kill stories; big reactions feed them.

Playful edges season everything: चल निघ (get lost — playful among friends, rude otherwise), शी (mild disgust), टाइमपास (chilling). Learn the edges, respect the edges: slang's power is knowing exactly where fun ends.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on टाइमपास. टाइमपास (timepass) defies translation — chilling, hanging out, passing time joyfully. काय करतोयस? — टाइमपास (what are you doing? — timepass). Whole philosophies hide in two syllables; use it freely, smile always.

Transfer practice. Before repeating a slang expression, identify who said it, to whom, and in what mood. Practise recognising the phrase before trying it yourself. Slang can sound friendly in one group and rude in another; avoid using it with elders, customers, or strangers until a trusted speaker confirms the setting. Start from भारी (bhaari) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Context check. For Marathi slang, “Sit quiet / stop it” belongs to a particular setting. A common mistake is to treat an English gloss as universal; read the language-specific note above and ask a fluent speaker when local usage differs.

Questions learners ask

What slang is safe everywhere?

भारी (awesome) and एक नंबर (number one) — positive, universal, safe. Sprinkle them on food, films and friends; instant insider status.

Can I call people bhau?

भाऊ (bro) and दादा (big bro) — for friends only, never elders or strangers. Friendship slang misused offends; rightly used, it bonds instantly.

What does timepass mean?

Nothing much, just chilling — काय चाललंय? — काई नाई, टाइमपास (what's up? — nothing, timepass). The most Marathi answer in existence: relaxed joy, declared proudly.
