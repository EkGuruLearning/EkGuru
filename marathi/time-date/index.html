Time and dates in Marathi

किती वाजले? सोमवार to रविवार — time, days and dates.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Clock time strikes: किती वाजले? (what time?), एक वाजला (one o'clock), पाच वाजले (five o'clock). साडेपाच (5:30), पावणेसहा (5:45), सव्वासहा (6:15) — halves and quarters sing. Ask time daily; numbers stick.

Day parts flow: सकाळ (morning), दुपार (afternoon), संध्याकाळ (evening), रात्र (night). सकाळी (in the morning), रात्री (at night) — suffixes time the day. Parts first, precision after.

Weeks cycle planet-wise: सोमवार (Monday) through रविवार (Sunday) — Moon-day to Sun-day. आज सोमवार आहे (today is Monday) — date yourself daily. Weekdays rhythm life.

Dates frame plans: आज (today), उद्या (tomorrow), काल (yesterday), तारीख (date). उद्या भेटूया? (shall we meet tomorrow?) — propose, confirm, arrive. Plans in Marathi keep in Marathi.

A note on वाजले. वाजले (vaajale — has struck) makes time musical — किती वाजले? (how much has struck?), पाच वाजले (five has struck). Clocks strike, bells ring, Marathis ask poetically. Time doesn't pass; it chimes.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask the time?

किती वाजले? (how much has struck?) — answer पाच वाजले (five o'clock). Time strikes in Marathi; ask it everywhere.

Days of the week?

सोमवार मंगळवार बुधवार गुरुवार शुक्रवार शनिवार रविवार — chant the week; planet-days roll musically.

Today, tomorrow, yesterday?

आज (today), उद्या (tomorrow), काल (yesterday), परवा (day after/before). Four words frame every plan.
