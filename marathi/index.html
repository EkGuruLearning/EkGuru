Questions about this course

Must I learn Devanagari before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Marathi in Roman letters, and let Devanagari come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

नमस्कार for respect, हो and नाही, धन्यवाद, माफ करा, ठीक आहे, and मी — I. With those seven you can open any interaction in Maharashtra politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Marathi?

The rolled ळ, nasal vowels, and three-gender verbs — जातो, जाते, जातं. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each sound.
