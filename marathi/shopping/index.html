Shopping and bargaining

किंमत, सूट, रंग — shop in Marathi from first price to final deal.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Prices open every transaction: याची किंमत काय? (what's this one's price?), किती रुपये? (how many rupees?). Listen for the number, repeat it back — पन्नास? (fifty?) — and you are already shopping in Marathi.

Bargaining follows the friendly script: खूप महाग आहे (too costly), सूट द्या (give a discount), थोडं कमी करा (reduce a little). Start walking away slowly — बरं, बघतो (okay, I'll look around) — and the real price often chases you down the street.

Sizes and colours refine the hunt: मोठं द्या (give the big one), लहान द्या (give the small one), नवीन दाखवा (show a new one), दुसरा रंग (another colour). Point, name the change, nod — Marathi shopkeepers read gestures fluently and reward attempts instantly.

Colours close the sale: लाल (red), निळा (blue), हिरवा (green), पिवळा (yellow), पांढरा (white), काळा (black). लाल छान दिसतं (the red looks nice) — then घेतो (I'll take it). Deal done, in Marathi, with a smile on both sides.

A note on थोडं कमी करा. थोडं कमी करा (thodan kami karaa — please reduce a little) is the magic sentence of Maharashtra's markets. Said smiling, it opens every negotiation; said with घेतो (I'll take it) right after, it closes most. Bargaining respects the dance, never the demand.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask the price?

याची किंमत काय? (what's the price of this?) — then फक्त किती? (just how much?) for everything else. Two questions, every shop in Maharashtra.

How do I bargain politely?

खूप महाग आहे (too costly) with a smile, then थोडं कमी करा (reduce a little). Bargaining is friendly theatre — play your part warmly and the price moves.

Which colours first?

Start with five: लाल (red), निळा (blue), हिरवा (green), पिवळा (yellow), पांढरा (white). दुसरा रंग दाखवा (show another colour) reveals the rest.
