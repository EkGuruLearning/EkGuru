Marathi

Writing Marathi

Letters to lists to letters — writing from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with the headline: Devanagari letters hang from a top bar — लिहा (please write) the line first, then hang अ आ इ ई क ख below it. Trace big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: पेन (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Marathi children trace in notebooks, slates and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: यादी (list) — टीप (notes), पत्ता (addresses), तारीख (dates). Copy your own नाव (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: पत्र (letters), अर्ज (applications), परीक्षा (exams), गृहपाठ (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Marathi — short, joyful, posted. First posted Marathi is never forgotten.

Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Marathi?

Straight headline first: Devanagari letters hang from a top bar — अ, आ, क, म. Rule the line, hang the letters; अक्षर (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

यादी (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

पत्र (letters) to friends, टीप (notes) to self, गृहपाठ (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
