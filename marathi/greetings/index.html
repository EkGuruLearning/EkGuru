Saying hello the Marathi way

नमस्कार and beyond — greet Maharashtra with warmth from your first word.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Greetings open every door: नमस्कार (respectful hello), राम राम (informal hello), हाय (casual hi). Fold your hands, say नमस्कार, and Maharashtra opens — buses, bazaars, boardrooms. One word, infinite welcome.

Health-questions show you care: तुम्ही कसे आहात? (how are you, formal), मी ठीक आहे (I'm fine), आणि तुम्ही? (and you?). Marathi small talk runs on genuine enquiry — ask properly, listen properly, belong quickly.

Time splits the day: शुभ सकाळ (good morning), शुभ दुपार (good afternoon), शुभ संध्याकाळ (good evening), शुभ रात्री (good night). Morning greetings before noon, रात्री only at parting — timing your शुभ shows cultural fluency.

Partings promise return: निरोप (farewell), पुन्हा भेटू (we'll meet again), काळजी घ्या (take care). Marathis rarely say goodbye without a next meeting — पुन्हा भेटू assumes friendship continues. Say it warmly; mean it always.

A note on नमस्कार. नमस्कार (namaskar — I bow to you) carries folded hands and genuine warmth — Maharashtra's universal key. Say it to auto drivers, CEOs and grandmothers alike; the returned smile is guaranteed. Respect first, everything else follows.

Questions learners ask

How do I say hello respectfully?

नमस्कार with folded hands — respectful everywhere, from Mumbai locals to village homes. Informal राम राम works with friends; नमस्कार never fails.

How do I ask how someone is?

तुम्ही कसे आहात? (formal) or तू कसा आहेस? (informal) — and answer मी ठीक आहे, धन्यवाद (I'm fine, thanks). Health-questions open Marathi hearts.

What are the time greetings?

शुभ सकाळ (good morning), शुभ दुपार (good afternoon), शुभ संध्याकाळ (good evening), शुभ रात्री (good night). Time-greetings show care; use them generously.
