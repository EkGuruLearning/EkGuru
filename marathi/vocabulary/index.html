Core Marathi vocabulary

पाणी, घर, काम, प्रेम — the words Marathi daily life runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Survival nouns anchor reality: पाणी (water), अन्न (food), घर (house), रस्ता (road). Point at each, say it aloud, use it today — पाणी हवं आहे (I want water). Concrete first, abstract later.

People words warm speech: माणूस (person), मित्र (friend), आई (mother), मूल (child). माझा मित्र (my friend) introduces proudly; आई calls home. People before things, always.

Describing words add colour: मोठं (big), लहान (small), चांगलं (good), नवीन (new). Adjectives dance with gender — मोठा भाऊ (big brother), मोठी बहीण (big sister). Triple-learn each one.

Time and feeling complete the core: आज (today), प्रेम (love), आनंद (joy), काम (work). आज खूप काम आहे (there's lots of work today) — then प्रेम आणि आनंद balance it. Words for living, not just talking.

A note on छान. छान (chhaan — nice/great) is Maharashtra's universal praise — छान आहे (it's nice), खूप छान (very nice). Food, films, friends' work — praise everything. Generous छान earns generous friendship.

Questions learners ask

Which words give fastest fluency?

Body, home, food, people: पाणी (water), घर (house), आई (mother), मित्र (friend). Concrete nouns first — point, name, own.

How do adjectives work?

Adjectives agree with gender: चांगला (good-m), चांगली (f), चांगलं (n). Learn triples together — agreement becomes music.

Essential time words?

आज (today), उद्या (tomorrow), काल (yesterday), आता (now). Time words frame every plan — learn them this week.
