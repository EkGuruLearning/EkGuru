Marathi for work and business

Meetings, customers, contracts — professional Marathi for offices.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Workplaces run on roles: नोकरी (job), ऑफिस (office), बॉस (boss), ग्राहक (customer), सहकारी (colleague). माझी नोकरी (my job is) introduces your profession; ऑफिस कुठे आहे? finds any office. Roles first, tasks second.

Meetings move business: मीटिंग (meeting), करार (contract/deal), सही (signature). मीटिंग उद्या सकाळी (meeting tomorrow morning) — confirm with ठीक आहे, येतो (okay, I'll come). Marathi meetings open with नमस्कार and close with धन्यवाद.

Money talks plainly: नफा (profit), तोटा (loss), पगार (salary), रजा (leave). पगार कधी होतो? (when is payday?) — asked smiling, everywhere. Business Marathi respects directness wrapped in politeness.

Digital tools connect all: ईमेल (email), फोन (phone), मेसेज (message). ईमेल बघा (please check email) — then यश (success) follows effort. Professional Marathi: formal words, warm heart.

A note on ग्राहक. ग्राहक (graahak — customer) rules Marathi business — ग्राहक हाच राजा (the customer is king). Master it syllable by syllable; clients notice the effort and reward it.

Questions learners ask

How do I introduce myself at work?

नमस्कार, मी (hello, I'm) — then तुम्हाला भेटून आनंद झाला (pleased to meet you). Business Marathi opens formal and warms slowly.

How do I ask about meetings?

मीटिंग कधी आहे? (when is the meeting?) and मीटिंग कुठे आहे? (where?). Meetings run Marathi offices — never miss one for language reasons.

Email and phone in Marathi?

ईमेल पाठवतो (I'll send an email) and फोनवर बोलूया (let's talk on the phone). Digital Marathi borrows freely — clarity beats purity in business.
