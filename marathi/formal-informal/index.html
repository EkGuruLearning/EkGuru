Formal and informal Marathi

तुम्ही or तू? या or ये? Respect levels, decoded.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Marathi has two yous: तुम्ही (formal and plural — strangers, elders, officials) and तू (familiar — friends, children, family). English erased this distinction centuries ago; Marathi keeps it razor-sharp. Every conversation opens with an invisible choice — make it consciously, and make it respectful.

Verbs mirror the split: या (please come) versus ये (come!), सांगा (please say) versus सांग (say!), जा (jaa, please go) versus जा (ja, go — same letters, shorter vowel!). The -ा ending is pure respect — same verb, different universe. Stems bark; -ा invites.

Giving and forgiving show the range: द्या (please give) versus दे (give, familiar), माफ करा (please forgive) versus माफ कर (forgive, familiar). Beggars and kings both hear द्या — politeness in Marathi costs nothing and buys everything.

Strangers get titles: साहेब (sir), दादा (brother), ताई (sister). साहेब, मदत करा (sir, please help) opens any door; barking orders at strangers closes them. Marathi warmth is earned with two syllables of respect.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on -ा. The -ा ending (aa) is Marathi's respect rocket — या (do come), बघा (please see), सांगा (please tell). One sound that upgrades every request instantly. Stem-alone orders bark; -ा invites.

Questions learners ask

When do I use तुम्ही?

तुम्ही for every adult you don't know well — shopkeepers, officials, elders, strangers. तू waits for close friends, children and family. Wrong-side errors embarrass; learners are forgiven, but respect first is always safest.

How do I make a command polite?

Use the -ा form: ये becomes या, जा becomes जा, सांग becomes सांगा. One ending, instant politeness — the highest-value sound in Marathi.

How do I address strangers?

साहेब (sir) and ताई for slightly-older strangers. साहेब, वेळ काय? (sir, what time is it?). Better than pointing and shouting in any language.
