Talking about relationships in Marathi

Friends, lovers, weddings — the Marathi of hearts, spoken truly.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Friendship opens hearts: मित्र (male friend), मैत्रीण (female friend) — माझा मित्र (my friend) introduces proudly. Marathi friendships run lifelong and deep; मैत्री (friendship) is celebrated in poetry, films and daily chai. Make one Marathi friend and gain a hundred phrases.

Romance speaks softly: प्रियकर (boyfriend), प्रेयसी (girlfriend), प्रेम (love). Marathi romance favours poetry over bluntness — film songs teach the register better than textbooks. Learn gently; speak truly.

Weddings unite families: लग्न (wedding spoken), विवाह (wedding formal), नवरदेव (groom), नवरी (bride). Marathi weddings run for days — music, food, rituals, blessings. लग्नाच्या शुभेच्छा (wedding congratulations) is your ticket to the feast.

Bonds deepen and mend: प्रेम (love), आपुलकी (affection), आयुष्य (life) — माझं आयुष्य (my life) means my beloved. And when bonds strain: माफी (forgiveness), शांती (peace). Marathi hearts forgive spaciously; ask with folded hands.

A note on प्रेम vs प्रीती. प्रेम (prem) is broad love — family, friends, humanity; प्रीती (preeti) leans romantic. तुमच्या प्रेमासाठी धन्यवाद (thanks for your love) suits everyone; प्रीती belongs to one. Marathi loves precisely.

Questions learners ask

Friendship words first?

मित्र (male friend), मैत्रीण (female friend) — then प्रेम (love) and आपुलकी (affection) as bonds deepen. Friendship words first; romance follows naturally.

How do I talk about weddings?

लग्न (spoken wedding), विवाह (formal wedding) — नवरदेव (groom), नवरी (bride). लग्नाच्या शुभेच्छा (wedding greetings) blesses every couple.

How do I apologise deeply?

माफी (forgiveness) — मला माफ करा (please forgive me), then शांती (peace). Marathi reconciliations run deep and warm; ask sincerely, receive fully.
