Family words in Marathi

आई, बाबा, आजी, आजोबा — family words that open Marathi homes.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Parents anchor all: आई (mother), बाबा (father) — माझी आई (my mother), माझे बाबा (my father). Marathi children chant आई first; learners should too. Home begins here.

Siblings share childhood: भाऊ (brother), बहीण (sister), दादा (elder brother), ताई (elder sister). दादा and ताई mark respect for age — birth order lives in address. Call elders rightly; love follows.

Grandparents crown memory: आजी (grandmother), आजोबा (grandfather) — story-keepers, sweet-givers, wisdom-wells. माझी आजी छान गोष्ट सांगते (my grandmother tells lovely stories) — every Marathi smiles. Honour them in speech.

The household gathers: कुटुंब (family), घर (home), लग्न (wedding), मूल (child). माझं कुटुंब मोठं आहे (my family is big) — then listen; Marathis narrate families with epic joy. Share yours; hear theirs.

A note on आजी. आजी (aaji — grandmother) rules Marathi hearts — आजीच्या गोष्टी (grandmother's stories) raised generations. Learn आजी and आजोबा (grandfather); elders glow when learners speak family. Respect the roots; the tree welcomes you.

Questions learners ask

Which family words first?

आई (mother), बाबा (father), भाऊ (brother), बहीण (sister). Family first — Marathis ask about yours within minutes.

Extended family?

काका (uncle), काकू (aunt), आजी (grandmother), आजोबा (grandfather). Extended family, extended vocabulary.

How do I describe my family?

माझं कुटुंब मोठं आहे (my family is big) — माझी आई शिक्षिका आहे (my mother is a teacher). Describe yours; hearts open.
