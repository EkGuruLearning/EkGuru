Marathi for kids

Toys, moons, stars and gold stars — Marathi for little learners, big joy.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Little learners start with love: मुलं (children), बाळ (baby), सोन्या (golden one), चांगलं मूल (good child). Marathi childhood floats on affection-words — call every child बाळ and watch faces light like दिवाळी.

Play teaches fastest: खेळ (play), खेळणं (toy), चेंडू (ball) — खेळूया (let's play)! Count throws, name colours, chase and giggle. Play first; Marathi hitchhikes on joy.

School words structure days: शाळा (school), शिक्षक (teacher), धडा (lesson), गृहपाठ (homework). नमस्कार सर (hello sir) opens every school day — respect and learning, hand in hand.

Sky words spark wonder: सूर्य (sun), चंद्र (moon), तारा (star) — चंद्र बघ (look at the moon)! Night-sky Marathi, whispered on terraces, becomes lifelong memory. Teach the sky; the child keeps the language.

A note on बाळ. बाळ (baal — baby/darling) melts every Marathi heart — माझं बाळ (my darling) for children, pets and beloveds. Terms of endearment are Marathi's mother tongue; every child swims in them.

Questions learners ask

How do kids learn Marathi best?

Songs and गोष्ट (stories) — गाणं with actions, tales with voices. Children absorb Marathi through music and play long before grammar.

How do I praise a child?

चांगलं मूल (good child) — praise every attempt loudly. Marathi parenting runs on encouragement; learners of all ages bloom under it.

Games or lessons?

खेळ (games): name objects — चेंडू! खेळणं! — count steps, sing counts. Play first, words hitchhike.
