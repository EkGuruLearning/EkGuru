Marathi pronunciation

ळ, nasal vowels, rhythm — speak Marathi like a native.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The signature ळ rules: बाळ (child), शाळा (school), पोळी (bread) — tongue back, flap forward. Minimal pairs drill it: काळ (time) vs काल (yesterday). Master ळ and Marathis beam; skip it and they still understand, smiling.

Nasals hum through speech: हवं (want) — the चंद्रबिंदु moon-dot nasalises vowels — गावं (villages), पोरं (kids). Hum the vowels; let the nose join the mouth. Music, not mechanics.

Length carries meaning: इ vs ई, उ vs ऊ — short and long are different letters, different words. Double the long ones fully; clip the short ones crisp. Ears measure; mouths must comply.

Rhythm ties it together: हळू (slowly) first, then natural speed; मोठ्याने (loudly) for clarity, शांत (quiet) for calm. उच्चार (pronunciation) improves with echoing — shadow natives daily, record yourself weekly.

Questions learners ask

How do I pronounce ळ?

The retroflex flap: tongue curls back, flaps forward — बाळ (child), शाळा (school). Hindi speakers must learn it fresh; practise flapping daily.

What are nasal vowels?

Vowels nasalise before न/म or with चंद्रबिंदु — हवं (want-neuter) hums in speech. Listen for the hum; imitate the music.

Do vowel lengths matter?

Long-short pairs: इ/ई, उ/ऊ change words. Hold long vowels fully double — Marathi ears measure length precisely.
