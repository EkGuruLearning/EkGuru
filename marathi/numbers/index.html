Counting in Marathi

एक दोन तीन to शंभर — count, pay and bargain in Marathi.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

One to ten sings: एक दोन तीन चार पाच सहा सात आठ नऊ दहा — chant it walking, cooking, waiting. Marathi numbers love rhythm; your tongue learns faster than your brain. Ten numbers, total freedom in every shop.

Teens climb steadily: अकरा बारा तेरा चौदा पंधरा सोळा सतरा अठरा एकोणवीस वीस — eleven to twenty. एकोणवीस (19) and वीस (20) open the tens; climb daily and teens become trivial.

Tens multiply power: वीस तीस चाळीस पन्नास साठ सत्तर ऐंशी नव्वद शंभर — 20 to 100. Combine freely: एकवीस (21), पंचवीस (25), त्र्याऐंशी... practise सत्तावीस (27) and अडतीस (38) — the tongue-twisters that prove fluency.

Hundreds rule markets: शंभर (100), दोनशे (200), हजार (1000), लाख (100,000). दीडशे (150) and अडीचशे (250) charm shopkeepers — halves and quarters in pure Marathi. Money talks; count it in Marathi.

A note on किती? किती? (kiti — how much / how many?) is Maharashtra's most useful question — किती रुपये? (how many rupees?), किती वाजले? (what time is it?). One word, every transaction. Ask किती fearlessly; numbers come to you.

Questions learners ask

How do I start counting?

एक दोन तीन चार पाच सहा सात आठ नऊ दहा — chant to ten, then अकरा बारा तेरा... to twenty. Rhythm first, precision follows.

How do tens work?

वीस (20), तीस (30), चाळीस (40), पन्नास (50) — then combine: एकवीस (21), बत्तीस (32). Tens plus units, endlessly.

Big numbers?

शंभर (100), हजार (1000), लाख (100,000). किती? (how many?) asks; numbers answer. Markets run on these three.
