Travel phrases in Marathi

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

तिकीट, स्टेशन, कुठे? — travel Maharashtra by train, bus and word.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Stations start journeys: रेल्वे स्टेशन (railway station), बस स्टॉप (bus stop), विमानतळ (airport). स्टेशन कुठे आहे? (where is the station?) — ask anyone; Marathis direct travellers with pride.

Tickets buy passage: तिकीट (ticket) — एक तिकीट द्या (give one ticket), तिकीट किती? (how much?). गाडी कधी येईल? (when will the train come?) — गाडी means both train and car; context steers.

Directions decode cities: उजवीकडे (right), डावीकडे (left), सरळ (straight), जवळ (near), लांब (far). सरळ जा, मग उजवीकडे (go straight, then right) — follow, arrive, thank.

Stays shelter nights: हॉटेल (hotel), खोली (room), पत्ता (address). खोली कितीला? (how much for a room?) — bargain smiling, sleep soundly. प्रवास ends in rest; rest remakes travellers.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on प्रवास. प्रवास (pravaas — journey) is Maharashtra's romance — रेल्वे प्रवास (train journey) through the Ghats, monsoon mist on windows. सुखाचा प्रवास (happy journey) blesses every traveller. Journey well; arrive storied.

Transfer practice. Picture one journey and ask for a destination, a direction, or a stop. Practise the request slowly enough to be understood, then repeat the place name back to check it. If the route is important, combine the spoken phrase with a map or written address rather than relying on one phrase alone. Start from प्रवास (pravaas) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Context check. For Travel phrases, “Address” belongs to a particular setting. A common mistake is to treat an English gloss as universal; read the language-specific note above and ask a fluent speaker when local usage differs.

Questions learners ask

How do I find stations?

रेल्वे स्टेशन कुठे आहे? (where is the railway station?) — swap in बस स्टॉप, विमानतळ. Where-questions navigate nations.

How do I buy tickets?

तिकीट किती? (how much is the ticket?) and एक तिकीट द्या (give one ticket). Tickets first, journeys after.

Left, right, straight?

उजवीकडे (right), डावीकडे (left), सरळ (straight). उजवीकडे वळा (turn right) — direct and be directed.
