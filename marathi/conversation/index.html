Marathi

Real Marathi conversation

मी, तुम्ही, हो, नाही — hold your first real Marathi conversations.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Marathi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

People anchor talk: मी (I), तुम्ही (you formal), तो (he), ती (she), ते (they). मी मराठी शिकतो/शिकते (I'm learning Marathi) — gender shows on verbs, proudly. Introduce yourself; conversations ignite.

Questions drive dialogue: काय? (what?), कुठे? (where?), कधी? (when?), का? (why?), कसं? (how?). तुम्ही कुठे राहता? (where do you live?) — one question word plus any verb equals conversation.

Politeness oils everything: Marathi says प्लीज (please) and माफ करा (sorry/excuse me). माफ करा, हळू बोला (sorry, speak slowly) — apologies plus requests, the learner's armour.

Repair keeps talk alive: मला समजलं नाही (I didn't understand), पुन्हा सांगा (please say again), याचा अर्थ काय? (what does this mean?). Broken Marathi beats silent perfection — stumble forward daily.

A note on मला. मला (mala — to-me) is Marathi's magic word — मला हवं आहे (I want), मला समजलं (I understood), मला माहीत आहे (I know). Experiences flow TOWARD you in Marathi; master मला and fifty sentences unlock.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask someone's name?

तुमचं नाव काय? (what's your name?) — answer माझं नाव... आहे (my name is...). Names first, friendship follows.

Yes and no in Marathi?

हो (yes), नाही (no) — short, clear, universal. होय adds formality; नको means don't-want. Yes-no fluency first.

When I don't understand?

मला समजलं नाही (I didn't understand) and हळू बोला (please speak slowly). Honesty tunes every speaker to your level.
