Why people in Bangalore learn Hindi

Bangalore is Kannada-speaking, but its tech industry is not. Teams pull people from Delhi, Lucknow and Patna, and the shared language in the corridor is often Hindi. Many learners here are from Tamil Nadu or Kerala and want workplace Hindi, not tourist phrases.
