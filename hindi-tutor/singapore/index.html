Hindi is offered as a mother-tongue subject in Singapore schools, so a good number of learners here are students working towards an exam with a real syllabus and a real deadline. That needs structured teaching, not conversation practice.

Common questions

Do you have Hindi tutors in Singapore?

No, and we would rather say so plainly. Our tutors live in India and teach online over video. In practice that is an advantage: you get a native speaker who uses the language every day, rather than whoever is available locally.

How much does a Hindi lesson cost in SGD?

Lessons start at $6, roughly 8 SGD at today's rate. Every price on this site converts to your own currency automatically, using live exchange rates. Most tutors offer a trial lesson.

Do I need to know the Devanagari script first?

Not to start. Plenty of students learn to speak first and add reading later. If you do want the script, our free alphabet guide covers every letter, and most learners read simple words within six weeks.
