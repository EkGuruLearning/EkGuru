Do I need to know the Devanagari script first?

Not to start. Plenty of students learn to speak first and add reading later. Gujarati script is close to Devanagari with the top line removed: many letters share their shapes and the vowel signs work the same way, so a Gujarati reader usually reads Hindi within days.
