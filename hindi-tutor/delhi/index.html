Why people in Delhi learn Hindi

Delhi runs on Hindi. If you have moved here for work or study from the south, the north-east or abroad, you will find that English gets you through the office and almost nothing else — not the auto driver, not the sabziwala, not your neighbours. Most people who take lessons in Delhi are not beginners in theory but in practice: they understand more than they can say.

Common questions

Do you have Hindi tutors in Delhi?

No, and we would rather say so plainly. Our tutors live in India and teach online over video. In practice that is an advantage: you get a native speaker who uses the language every day, rather than whoever is available locally.

How much does a Hindi lesson cost in INR?

Lessons start at $6, roughly 567 INR at today's rate. Every price on this site converts to your own currency automatically, using live exchange rates. Most tutors offer a trial lesson.

Do I need to know the Devanagari script first?

Not to start. Plenty of students learn to speak first and add reading later. If you do want the script, our free alphabet guide covers every letter, and most learners read simple words within six weeks.
