How to choose the right Hindi class

Pick the class that matches the goal you can describe. The questions below are the ones people actually ask before starting, and answering them here is the difference between a class you keep and one you drop after a week.

Decide: self-study or live class

If you are disciplined and curious, the free course is enough for the early stages — letters, sounds, first words and grammar. If you want to actually speak Hindi, a live class is the step that matters: a person corrects you, answers your questions and pushes you past the point where a course stalls. Many learners do both: the course for structure, a tutor for conversation.

Check what the class actually covers

A class is only right if it covers what you need. For reading and writing, it must teach the Devanagari script, not romanisation only. For speaking, it must give you chances to talk, not just listen. For school or heritage learning, check the level and topics. The Hindi course lists every topic and level so you can confirm coverage before you start.

Check the time against your day

For a live class, the lesson time is what "near me" really decides. Each tutor location page works out the lesson hours in your own timezone, so you can see whether a slot lands in your morning, evening or late night. See Hindi tutors by location →

Confirm cost and terms before committing

The self-study course is free. A live class is booked per lesson at a price the tutor states on their profile; EkGuru takes no commission on tutor lessons and adds no platform fee to the displayed price in this release, and EkGuru does not collect tutor-lesson payments. Do not assume every tutor offers the same trial, duration or cancellation terms — check the profile and confirm directly before you commit. A lesson request is an enquiry until current availability and terms are confirmed in a reply.

Common questions

Are there Hindi classes physically near me?

EkGuru runs online Hindi classes only — there is no physical classroom or local branch to visit. Every class is taken over the internet, so "near me" means the lesson time and timezone fit your day rather than a location you travel to.

Do I need to pay to start a Hindi class?

No. The self-study Hindi course, the practice tools and the questions section are free and need no sign-up. You only pay for a live tutor class, and only at the price the tutor states on their profile.

Can I learn Hindi online without the script?

You can begin with speech and romanisation, but romanisation is inconsistent and cannot show every sound clearly. If reading is part of your goal, the free alphabet guide is the right place to start.

How long until I can hold a conversation?

There is no reliable universal timetable. It depends on your language background, how often you practice, whether you take live classes, and the kind of conversation you mean. Ask a prospective tutor for a plan tied to your goal rather than a guaranteed date.

Can a child take a Hindi class here?

Use a tutor profile that explicitly states experience with children, and have a parent or guardian handle booking, supervision, privacy and any payment. The free course is also suitable for a child working with a grown-up.
