What EkGuru is

EkGuru introduces students to independent Hindi tutors and publishes free learning material. We are an introduction service, not the teacher. The lesson itself is an arrangement between you and the tutor. We are not a party to it, we do not supervise it, and we do not take payment for it.

Booking requests

  • Pressing "send booking request" sends a request. It is not a confirmed lesson until the tutor replies and agrees a time with you.
  • Tutors set their own prices and their own availability, and may decline.
  • EkGuru does not collect tutor-lesson payments. The separate Support page may link to a Razorpay-hosted page for an optional one-time contribution; that contribution does not book a lesson or buy course access.

Prices and currency

Prices are set by each tutor in US dollars. Where a price is shown in your own currency it has been converted using a public exchange rate for your convenience, and it is an estimate. The dollar figure is the real one. Rates move; your bank's rate will differ.

The free material

The guides, tools, question pages and daily lessons are free to read and use. You may link to them and quote a reasonable extract with attribution. You may not republish them wholesale, or feed them into a scraper to rebuild them elsewhere. Published course levels are drawn from EkGuru's own course data; generated drills are labelled and only re-ask authored vocabulary.

Accuracy

Hindi is written here by people, and Devanagari typed by hand goes wrong in small ways. We correct what is reported. Please do report it — the contact form takes a moment. See also the disclaimer.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use the contact or booking forms to send bulk or commercial mail.
  • Do not attempt to harvest tutor or student contact details.
  • Do not misrepresent who you are when contacting a tutor.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled in the current release. If third-party advertising is enabled later, ads will not be recommendations and will remain excluded from legal, search, booking, payment, form and interactive-learning pages. See the privacy policy and monetization disclosure.
