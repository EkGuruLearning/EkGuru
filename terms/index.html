Who we are

EkGuru is operated by Prakash, based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, trading as EkGuru (“we”, “us”, “EkGuru”). Write to us at EkGuruLearning@gmail.com or use the contact form.

Who can use EkGuru

  • You must be 18 or older to book a lesson, become a tutor, or make a contribution.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms and make the booking or the contribution for you.
  • You may read the free material at any age, with no account.
  • Tutors must be able to lawfully work as a tutor in the country where they teach. That is their responsibility, not ours.

What EkGuru is

EkGuru introduces students to independent Hindi tutors and publishes free learning material. We are an introduction service, not the teacher. The lesson itself is an arrangement between you and the tutor. We are not a party to it, we do not supervise it, we do not set the tutor’s teaching methods, and we do not take payment for it.

Booking requests

  • Pressing “send booking request” sends a request. It is not a confirmed lesson until the tutor replies and agrees a time with you. How a live class works sets out the steps.
  • Tutors set their own prices and their own availability, and may decline any request.
  • Booking requests are delivered by email to the tutor or to EkGuru for onward delivery. Delivery is not guaranteed, so keep a copy of anything important.
  • EkGuru does not collect tutor-lesson payments. The separate Support page may link to a Razorpay-hosted page for an optional one-time contribution; that contribution does not book a lesson or buy course access.

  • The tutor sets the lesson length, the price in US dollars, the schedule, and the cancellation and rescheduling rules. Ask the tutor before you book.

For tutors

Applying and approval

  • An application does not make you a public tutor. We publish a profile only after we have checked your identity, your teaching details and your consent, and the profile carries an approval mark.

  • Tell us promptly when your details change, your availability changes, or you can no longer teach.
  • Your contact details stay private. We send booking requests to your private inbox and do not publish your email address or phone number on the site.
