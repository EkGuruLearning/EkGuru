# 📋 EkGuru — Tutor Application Google Form
## Complete copy-paste guide

Everything below is ready to paste straight into Google Forms.
**Time needed: about 15 minutes.**

---

# STEP 1 — Create the form

1. Go to **https://forms.google.com**
2. Sign in with **EkGuruLearning@gmail.com** *(important — replies must land in that inbox)*
3. Click **Blank form** (the `+` tile)

---

# STEP 2 — Form title and description

Click the title at the top and paste:

### 📌 FORM TITLE
```
Become an EkGuru Tutor — Application
```

### 📌 FORM DESCRIPTION
```
Thank you for your interest in teaching with EkGuru.

EkGuru is a Hindi-only tutoring platform. Every tutor listed here teaches Hindi, and that focus is exactly why our students trust us.

Listing is completely free. We take no commission — students contact you directly and you agree payment between yourselves.

This form takes about 10 minutes. Please have your profile photo and intro video link ready before you start.

We review every application within 2–3 days and reply to the email address you give below.
```

# STEP 3 — Form settings ⚙️

Click the **gear icon ⚙️** at the top right.

### Tab: "Responses"
| Setting | Turn it |
|---|---|
| Collect email addresses | **ON** → choose **Responder input** |
| Send responders a copy of their response | **Always** |
| Allow response editing | **ON** |
| Limit to 1 response | **OFF** |

### Tab: "Presentation"
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Show progress bar | **ON** |
| Shuffle question order | **OFF** |

**Confirmation message** — paste this:
```
Thank you for applying to EkGuru.

We have received your application and will review it within 2–3 days. We will reply to the email address you provided.

If your application is approved, we may ask for a short video call to say hello before your profile goes live.

— The EkGuru Team
One Student. One Guru. One Goal.
```

### Tab: "Defaults"
* Make questions required by default: **ON** *(saves you clicking on every question)*

# STEP 7 — Connect the form to your website

Open **`js/site-config.js`** and find this line:

```js
applyFormUrl: "",
```

Paste your form link between the quotes:

```js
applyFormUrl: "https://forms.gle/AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOp",
```

Save and commit. That is all.

**What changes on the site automatically:**

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Become a Tutor page — main button | Opens an email | **Opens your Google Form** |
| Become a Tutor page — sidebar button | Opens an email | **Opens your Google Form** |
| Below the button | — | A small "or email us instead" link appears as a backup |

Leave `applyFormUrl: ""` empty and the buttons keep using email, exactly as they do now.
Nothing breaks either way.

# STEP 8 — Manage the responses

### See applications as a spreadsheet
**Responses** tab → the green **Sheets icon** → **Create a new spreadsheet**

Every application becomes one row. Photos appear as Drive links you can click.

### Where the uploaded photos go
Google Drive → a folder named **"Become an EkGuru Tutor — Application (File responses)"**

### Your workflow when an application arrives
1. Email notification arrives
2. Open the spreadsheet row and read it
3. Download their photo from Drive, rename it `firstname.jpg`, all lowercase
4. Upload it to your repository's `images/` folder
5. Open `js/tutors-data.js`, paste the template from README section 8, fill in their answers
6. Commit → their profile is live in about a minute

### Optional: turn off the form when you have enough tutors
**Responses** tab → switch **"Accepting responses"** to off.
Add a closing message such as:
```
Thank you for your interest. We are not accepting new tutor applications at the moment. Please check back soon.
```
