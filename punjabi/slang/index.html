Punjabi slang

ਬੱਲੇ ਬੱਲੇ, ਚੱਕ ਦੇ, ਇੱਕ ਨੰਬਰ — street Punjabi that fun runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Praise slang opens doors: ਬੱਲੇ ਬੱਲੇ (hooray), ਇੱਕ ਨੰਬਰ (number one), ਚੱਕ ਦੇ (go for it), ਫ਼ਸਟ ਕਲਾਸ (first class), ਕੈਂਟ (solid). Food, films, friends' outfits — praise everything loudly. Punjabis praise generously; learners should too.

Friendship words bond crews: ਵੀਰ (bro), ਭਰਾਵਾ (brother mine), ਯਾਰ (buddy) — friends-only, always warm. ਵੀਰੇ, ਆ ਨਾ (come on bro) starts a thousand conversations daily. Never aim them upward at elders; sideways at friends, fire freely.

Reaction words colour stories: ਹਾਏ ਓ ਰੱਬਾ (oh my God!), ਵਾਹ ਜੀ ਵਾਹ (wow!), ਗੱਪਾਂ ਨਾ ਮਾਰ (stop making things up!). Punjabi storytelling runs on reactions — gasp, laugh, disbelieve theatrically. Flat faces kill stories; big reactions feed them.

Playful edges season everything: ਚੱਲ ਨਿਕਲ (get lost — playful among friends, rude otherwise), ਛੀ (mild disgust), ਟਾਈਮਪਾਸ (chilling). Learn the edges, respect the edges: slang's power is knowing exactly where fun ends.

A note on ਚੱਕ ਦੇ ਫੱਟੇ. ਚੱਕ ਦੇ ਫੱਟੇ (chak de fatte — lift the planks, go for it!) is Punjab's battle-cry — sports, exams, life. Shout it with friends; charge at everything. Three words, total fearlessness.

Questions learners ask

What slang is safe everywhere?

ਬੱਲੇ ਬੱਲੇ (hooray) and ਇੱਕ ਨੰਬਰ (number one) — positive, universal, safe. Sprinkle them on food, films and friends; instant insider status.

Can I call people veer?

ਵੀਰ (bro) and ਭਰਾਵਾ (brother mine) — for friends only, never elders or strangers. Friendship slang misused offends; rightly used, it bonds instantly.

What does timepass mean?

Hanging out, passing time joyfully — ਕੀ ਚੱਲਦਾ ਹੈ? — ਕੁਝ ਨਹੀਂ, ਟਾਈਮਪਾਸ (what's up? — nothing, timepass). The most Punjabi answer in existence: relaxed joy, declared proudly.
