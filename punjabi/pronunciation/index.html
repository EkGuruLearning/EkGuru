Punjabi pronunciation

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Three tones, retroflex ੜ, bold rhythm — speak Punjabi like a native.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The signature ੜ rules: ਪੜ੍ਹਨਾ (to read), ਲੜਕਾ (boy), ਕੁੜੀ (girl) — tongue back, flap forward. Minimal pairs drill it: ਪੜ੍ਹ (read!) vs ਪਰ (but). Master ੜ and Punjabis beam; skip it and they still understand, smiling.

Tones sing through speech: ਘਰ (house, falling), ਘੋੜਾ (horse, rising) — pitch shapes meaning. Don't fear tones; children absorb them from bhangra alone. Sing along; tones arrive.

Length carries meaning: ਇ vs ਈ, ਉ vs ਊ — short and long are different letters, different words. Double the long ones fully; clip the short ones crisp. Ears measure; mouths must comply.

Rhythm ties it together: ਹੌਲੀ (slowly) first, then natural speed; ਉੱਚੀ (loudly) for clarity, ਚੁੱਪ (quiet) for calm. ਉਚਾਰਨ (pronunciation) improves with echoing — shadow natives daily, record yourself weekly.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ਹੌਲੀ. ਹੌਲੀ (hauli — slowly) is the learner's best friend — ਹੌਲੀ ਬੋਲੋ (please speak slowly). Say it with a smile and every Punjabi speaker shifts to teacher-mode. Slow speech is kind speech; ask for it freely.

Questions learners ask

How do I pronounce ੜ?

The retroflex flap: tongue curls back, flaps forward — ਪੜ੍ਹਨਾ (to read), ਲੜਕਾ (boy). Practise flapping daily; Punjabi ੜ is bolder than Hindi ड़.

What are Punjabi tones?

High, low and level pitch on words with ਘ ਝ ਢ ਧ ਭ — ਘਰ (house) falls, ਘੋੜਾ (horse) rises. Tones change meaning; ears learn them through songs.

Do vowel lengths matter?

Long-short pairs: ਇ/ਈ, ਉ/ਊ change words. Hold long vowels fully double — Punjabi ears measure length precisely.
