Punjabi sentence structure

Who does what, verb last — sentences decoded with living examples.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Simple sentences stack three blocks: ਮੈਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ (I — to-school — go), ਉਹ ਗੀਤ ਗਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ (she — song — sings), ਅਸੀਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਸਿੱਖਦੇ ਹਾਂ (we — Punjabi — study). Subject, object, verb — build a hundred of these and Punjabi skeleton is yours.

Animals make perfect practice: ਮੁੰਡਾ ਹੱਸਦਾ ਹੈ (the boy laughs), ਕੁੱਤਾ ਭੌਂਕਦਾ ਹੈ (the dog barks), ਬਿੱਲੀ ਸੌਂਦੀ ਹੈ (the cat sleeps). Short subjects, vivid verbs, zero ambiguity — describe every creature you meet and SOV becomes reflex.

Time flexes the frame: ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵਾਂਗਾ (I'll come tomorrow), ਬੀਤਿਆ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੀਂਹ ਪਿਆ (it rained yesterday). Time words float — front for emphasis, middle for flow — while the verb holds the end. Tense plus time word: past, present, future, mastered.

Identity and questions close the set: ਇਹ ਮੇਰਾ ਘਰ ਹੈ (this is my house), ਇਹ ਤੇਰੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ? (is this your book?), ਸਭ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ (all is well). Equate with ਇਹ/ਉਹ, question with rising tone, reassure with ਸਭ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ — sentences for every situation.

Questions learners ask

What is Punjabi word order?

Subject-Object-Verb: ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਪੜ੍ਹਦਾ ਹਾਂ (I book read). Endings mark roles, so order flexes for emphasis — but verb-last is home.

How do tenses work?

Tense lives on the verb, with gender: ਪੜ੍ਹਦਾ/ਪੜ੍ਹਦੀ (read-present), ਪੜ੍ਹਿਆ (read-past), ਪੜ੍ਹਾਂਗਾ (will read). Time words — ਬੀਤਿਆ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ, ਅੱਜ, ਕੱਲ੍ਹ — frame it; the verb carries it.

How do I ask questions?

Rise at the end: ਇਹ ਤੇਰੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ? (is this your book?). Question words — ਕੀ, ਕਿੱਥੇ, ਕੌਣ — sit where the answer would.
