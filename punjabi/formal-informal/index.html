Formal and informal Punjabi

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

ਤੁਸੀਂ or ਤੂੰ? ਆਓ or ਆ? Respect levels, decoded.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Punjabi has two yous: ਤੁਸੀਂ (formal and plural — strangers, elders, officials) and ਤੂੰ (familiar — friends, children, family). English erased this distinction centuries ago; Punjabi keeps it razor-sharp. Every conversation opens with an invisible choice — make it consciously, and make it respectful.

Verbs mirror the split: ਆਓ (please come) versus ਆ (come!), ਦੱਸੋ (please say) versus ਦੱਸ (say!), ਜਾਓ (please go) versus ਜਾ (go!). The -ਓ ending is pure respect — same verb, different universe. Stems bark; -ਓ invites.

Giving and forgiving show the range: ਦਿਓ (please give) versus ਦੇ (give, familiar), ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰੋ (please forgive) versus ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰ (forgive, familiar). Beggars and kings both hear ਦਿਓ — politeness in Punjabi costs nothing and buys everything.

Strangers get titles: ਸਰਦਾਰ ਜੀ (sir), ਵੀਰ (brother), ਭੈਣ (sister). ਸਰਦਾਰ ਜੀ, ਮਦਦ ਕਰੋ (sir, please help) opens any door; barking orders at strangers closes them. Punjabi warmth is earned with two syllables of respect.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on -ਓ. The -ਓ ending (o) is Punjabi's respect rocket — ਆਓ (do come), ਵੇਖੋ (please see), ਦੱਸੋ (please tell). One sound that upgrades every request instantly. Stem-alone orders bark; -ਓ invites.

Questions learners ask

When do I use ਤੁਸੀਂ?

ਤੁਸੀਂ for every adult you don't know well — shopkeepers, officials, elders, strangers. ਤੂੰ waits for close friends, children and family. Wrong-side errors embarrass; learners are forgiven, but respect first is always safest.

How do I make a command polite?

Use the -ਓ form: ਆ becomes ਆਓ, ਜਾ becomes ਜਾਓ, ਦੱਸ becomes ਦੱਸੋ. One ending, instant politeness — the highest-value sound in Punjabi.

How do I address strangers?

ਸਰਦਾਰ ਜੀ (sir) and ਭੈਣ ਜੀ for slightly-older strangers. ਸਰਦਾਰ ਜੀ, ਸਮਾਂ ਕੀ ਹੈ? (sir, what time is it?). Better than pointing and shouting in any language.
