Punjabi for absolute beginners

Zero to first conversation — your first week of Punjabi, step by step.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Day one speaks: ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ (hello), ਹਾਂ (yes), ਨਹੀਂ (no) — say them to mirrors, pets, shopkeepers. Pronunciation need not be perfect; attempts earn smiles and corrections. Speaking starts speaking; nothing else does.

Days two to four add survival: ਧੰਨਵਾਦ (thanks), ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ (sorry), ਪਾਣੀ (water), ਕਿੰਨੇ? (how much?). Four days, seven words, infinite transactions. Survival Punjabi fits in a pocket and works everywhere.

Days five to seven build sentences: ਮੈਂ ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਹਾਂ (I am a teacher), ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚਾਹ ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ (I want tea), ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੋ? (how are you?). Frames plus words equal conversation — slots fill fast when frames stand ready.

Week two opens the script: ੳ ਅ ੲ, then ਕ ਖ ਗ ਘ — Gurmukhi through familiar words. Read what you already say; letters become friends, not walls. Speak-first learners read faster — motivation decodes.

A note on ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ. ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ (shuruaat — beginning) is the bravest word in Punjabi — ਅੱਜ ਤੋਂ ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ (starting from today). Every fluent speaker once said their first broken ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ. Begin badly; continue daily; arrive surely.

Questions learners ask

What are my first seven words?

ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ, ਹਾਂ, ਨਹੀਂ, ਧੰਨਵਾਦ, ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ, ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਮੈਂ — seven words that open every door in Punjab. Speak them from day one.

Script first or speech first?

No — speak first in Roman letters, add Gurmukhi in week two. Speakers stay motivated; script-drillers quit. Talk today, read tomorrow.

How much daily practice?

Twenty focused minutes: five words, one dialogue, one song line. Daily beats marathon — streaks compound into fluency.
