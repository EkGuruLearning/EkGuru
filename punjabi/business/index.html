Punjabi for work and business

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Meetings, customers, contracts — professional Punjabi for offices.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Workplaces run on roles: ਨੌਕਰੀ (job), ਦਫ਼ਤਰ (office), ਬੌਸ (boss), ਗਾਹਕ (customer), ਸਹਿਕਰਮੀ (colleague). ਮੇਰੀ ਨੌਕਰੀ (my job is) introduces your profession; ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ? finds any office. Roles first, tasks second.

Meetings move business: ਮੀਟਿੰਗ (meeting), ਇਕਰਾਰ (contract/deal), ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ (signature). ਮੀਟਿੰਗ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ (meeting tomorrow morning) — confirm with ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਆਵਾਂਗਾ (okay, I'll come). Punjabi meetings open with ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ and close with ਧੰਨਵਾਦ.

Money talks plainly: ਮੁਨਾਫ਼ਾ (profit), ਘਾਟਾ (loss), ਤਨਖ਼ਾਹ (salary), ਛੁੱਟੀ (leave). ਤਨਖ਼ਾਹ ਕਦੋਂ ਮਿਲਦੀ ਹੈ? (when is payday?) — asked smiling, everywhere. Business Punjabi respects directness wrapped in politeness.

Digital tools connect all: ਈਮੇਲ (email), ਫ਼ੋਨ (phone), ਸੁਨੇਹਾ (message). ਈਮੇਲ ਵੇਖੋ (please check email) — then ਸਫ਼ਲਤਾ (success) follows effort. Professional Punjabi: formal words, warm heart.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ਗਾਹਕ. ਗਾਹਕ (gaahak — customer) rules Punjabi business — ਗਾਹਕ ਹੀ ਰਾਜਾ (the customer is king). Master it syllable by syllable; clients notice the effort and reward it.

Transfer practice. Practise one workplace request as a complete message: name the task, make the request, and confirm the next step. Keep a respectful opening and closing. Office conventions differ by team and region, so use the examples as a starting point and observe the register your colleagues prefer. Start from ਨੌਕਰੀ (naukari) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Questions learners ask

How do I introduce myself at work?

ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ, ਮੈਂ (hello, I'm) — then ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ ਮਿਲਕੇ ਖੁਸ਼ੀ ਹੋਈ (pleased to meet you). Business Punjabi opens formal and warms slowly.

How do I ask about meetings?

ਮੀਟਿੰਗ ਕਦੋਂ ਹੈ? (when is the meeting?) and ਮੀਟਿੰਗ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ? (where?). Meetings run Punjabi offices — never miss one for language reasons.

Email and phone in Punjabi?

ਈਮੇਲ ਭੇਜਾਂਗਾ (I'll send an email) and ਫ਼ੋਨ ਤੇ ਗੱਲ ਕਰੀਏ (let's talk on the phone). Digital Punjabi borrows freely — clarity beats purity in business.
