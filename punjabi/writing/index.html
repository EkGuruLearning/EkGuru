Punjabi

Writing Punjabi

Letters to lists to letters — writing Punjabi from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with curves: Gurmukhi letters round and flow — ਲਿਖੋ (please write) circles and hooks before full letters. Trace ੳ ਅ ੲ ਕ ਖ big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: ਕਲਮ (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Punjabi children trace in notebooks, slates and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: ਸੂਚੀ (list) — ਨੋਟ (notes), ਪਤਾ (addresses), ਤਾਰੀਖ (dates). Copy your own ਨਾਮ (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: ਚਿੱਠੀ (letters), ਅਰਜ਼ੀ (applications), ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ (exams), ਘਰ ਦਾ ਕੰਮ (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Punjabi — short, joyful, posted. First posted Punjabi is never forgotten.

Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Punjabi?

Rounded strokes first: Gurmukhi letters curve gently — ੳ, ਅ, ਕ, ਮ. Trace big, shrink slowly; ਲਿਖਾਈ (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

ਸੂਚੀ (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

ਚਿੱਠੀ (letters) to friends, ਨੋਟ (notes) to self, ਘਰ ਦਾ ਕੰਮ (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
