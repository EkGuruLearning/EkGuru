Time and dates in Punjabi

ਕਿੰਨੇ ਵੱਜੇ? ਸੋਮਵਾਰ to ਐਤਵਾਰ — time, days and dates.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Clock time strikes: ਕਿੰਨੇ ਵੱਜੇ? (what time?), ਇੱਕ ਵੱਜਿਆ (one o'clock), ਪੰਜ ਵੱਜੇ (five o'clock). ਸਾਢੇ ਪੰਜ (5:30), ਪੌਣੇ ਛੇ (5:45), ਸਵਾ ਛੇ (6:15) — halves and quarters sing. Ask time daily; numbers stick.

Day parts flow: ਸਵੇਰ (morning), ਦੁਪਹਿਰ (afternoon), ਸ਼ਾਮ (evening), ਰਾਤ (night). ਸਵੇਰੇ (in the morning), ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ (at night) — suffixes time the day. Parts first, precision after.

Weeks cycle planet-wise: ਸੋਮਵਾਰ (Monday) through ਐਤਵਾਰ (Sunday) — Moon-day to Sun-day. ਅੱਜ ਸੋਮਵਾਰ ਹੈ (today is Monday) — date yourself daily. Weekdays rhythm life.

Dates frame plans: ਅੱਜ (today), ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (tomorrow), ਬੀਤਿਆ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (yesterday), ਤਾਰੀਖ (date). ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮਿਲੀਏ? (shall we meet tomorrow?) — propose, confirm, arrive. Plans in Punjabi keep in Punjabi.

A note on ਵੱਜੇ. ਵੱਜੇ (vajje — has struck) makes time musical — ਕਿੰਨੇ ਵੱਜੇ? (how much has struck?), ਪੰਜ ਵੱਜੇ (five has struck). Clocks strike, bells ring, Punjabis ask poetically. Time doesn't pass; it chimes.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask the time?

ਕਿੰਨੇ ਵੱਜੇ? (how much has struck?) — answer ਪੰਜ ਵੱਜੇ (five o'clock). Time strikes in Punjabi; ask it everywhere.

Days of the week?

ਸੋਮਵਾਰ ਮੰਗਲਵਾਰ ਬੁੱਧਵਾਰ ਵੀਰਵਾਰ ਸ਼ੁੱਕਰਵਾਰ ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ ਐਤਵਾਰ — chant the week; planet-days roll musically.

Today, tomorrow, yesterday?

ਅੱਜ (today), ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (tomorrow), ਬੀਤਿਆ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (yesterday), ਪਰਸੋਂ (day after/before). Four words frame every plan.
