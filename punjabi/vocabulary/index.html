Core Punjabi vocabulary

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

ਪਾਣੀ, ਘਰ, ਕੰਮ, ਪਿਆਰ — the words Punjabi daily life runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Survival nouns anchor reality: ਪਾਣੀ (water), ਖਾਣਾ (food), ਘਰ (house), ਰਸਤਾ (road). Point at each, say it aloud, use it today — ਪਾਣੀ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ (I want water). Concrete first, abstract later.

People words warm speech: ਬੰਦਾ (person), ਦੋਸਤ (friend), ਮਾਂ (mother), ਬੱਚਾ (child). ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ (my friend) introduces proudly; ਮਾਂ calls home. People before things, always.

Describing words add colour: ਵੱਡਾ (big), ਛੋਟਾ (small), ਚੰਗਾ (good), ਨਵਾਂ (new). Adjectives dance with gender — ਵੱਡਾ ਭਰਾ (big brother), ਵੱਡੀ ਭੈਣ (big sister). Pair-learn each one.

Time and feeling complete the core: ਅੱਜ (today), ਪਿਆਰ (love), ਖੁਸ਼ੀ (joy), ਕੰਮ (work). ਅੱਜ ਬਹੁਤ ਕੰਮ ਹੈ (there's lots of work today) — then ਪਿਆਰ ਅਤੇ ਖੁਸ਼ੀ balance it. Words for living, not just talking.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ਵਧੀਆ. ਵਧੀਆ (vadheeyaa — nice/great) is Punjab's universal praise — ਵਧੀਆ ਹੈ (it's nice), ਬਹੁਤ ਵਧੀਆ (very nice). Food, films, friends' work — praise everything. Generous ਵਧੀਆ earns generous friendship.

Transfer practice. Group the words by the company they keep: notice which word belongs with a person, action, place, or polite reply. Make one short sentence using a pair from the table, then recall the pair tomorrow without looking. Remembering a usable phrase is more valuable than reciting an isolated translation. Start from ਪਾਣੀ (paani) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Context check. For Core Punjabi vocabulary, “Nice / great” belongs to a particular setting. A common mistake is to treat an English gloss as universal; read the language-specific note above and ask a fluent speaker when local usage differs.

Questions learners ask

Which words give fastest fluency?

Body, home, food, people: ਪਾਣੀ (water), ਘਰ (house), ਮਾਂ (mother), ਦੋਸਤ (friend). Concrete nouns first — point, name, own.

How do adjectives work?

Adjectives agree with gender: ਚੰਗਾ (good-m), ਚੰਗੀ (f). Learn pairs together — agreement becomes music.

Essential time words?

ਅੱਜ (today), ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (tomorrow), ਬੀਤਿਆ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (yesterday), ਹੁਣ (now). Time words frame every plan — learn them this week.
