Punjabi

Flashcards by topic

Spaced repetition, topic decks, daily streaks — flashcards that work.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Cards carry memory: ਕਾਰਡ (card) — one word, one side; meaning, the other. ਯਾਦਦਾਸ਼ਤ (memory) strengthens with every recall; ਭੁੱਲਣਾ (forgetting) is just unreviewed memory. Build decks per topic — greetings, food, verbs — and review each separately.

Spacing schedules success: ਵਕਫ਼ਾ (interval) — review new cards tomorrow, then in three days, seven, thirty. ਫਿਰ ਫਿਰ (again and again), but wider each time. Cramming fills hours; spacing fills years.

Rhythms sustain streaks: ਰੋਜ਼ (daily) quick decks, ਹਫ਼ਤਾਵਾਰੀ (weekly) full reviews, ਮਹੀਨਾਵਾਰੀ (monthly) championships. Ten daily minutes beat Sunday marathons; streaks turn study into ਆਦਤ (habit).

Scores gamify growth: ਨੰਬਰ (marks), ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ (contest), ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤੀ (achievement) — even ਹਾਰ (defeat) teaches. Quiz yourself, beat yesterday's score, celebrate loudly. Flashcards are games that pay in fluency.

A note on ਦੁਹਰਾਅ. ਦੁਹਰਾਅ (duhraa — revision, repeating) is Punjabi's own word for spaced review — recalling again, and again, at widening intervals. The language itself teaches the method: revisit, widen, remember.

Questions learners ask

How often should I review?

ਫਿਰ ਫਿਰ (again and again) — but spaced: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 30 days. ਵਕਫ਼ਾ (spacing) beats cramming; ਯਾਦਦਾਸ਼ਤ (memory) loves intervals.

How many cards per topic?

Five to ten per topic — quality over quantity. Master one ਕਾਰਡ deck fully before opening the next; half-known decks rot.

Daily or weekly review?

ਰੋਜ਼ (daily) short sessions — ten minutes, every day, no exceptions. Streaks compound; ਭੁੱਲਣਾ (forgetting) loses to routine.
