Punjabi alphabet

ੳ ਅ ੲ to ਕ ਖ ਗ — Gurmukhi vowels, consonants and rows, made friendly.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Vowels anchor everything: ਅ ਆ ਇ ਈ ਉ ਊ ਏ ਐ ਓ ਔ — ten pure sounds. Long-short pairs matter — ਇ vs ਈ changes meaning. Chant them in order daily; the mouth memorises what the ear repeats.

Consonants march in families: ਕ ਖ ਗ ਘ (throat), ਚ ਛ ਜ ਝ (palate), ਟ ਠ ਡ ਢ (roof), ਤ ਥ ਦ ਧ (teeth), ਪ ਫ ਬ ਭ (lips). Family by family, feel where each sound lives — Punjabi pronunciation is geography you can touch.

Special letters crown the set: ਯ ਰ ਲ ਵ ੜ ਸ਼ ਖ਼ ਗ਼ ਜ਼ ਫ਼ ਲ਼ — with ੜ (retroflex R) and dotted Persian loans. ਸਕੂਲ (school) and ਖ਼ਬਰ (news) show loans in action. Special letters, everyday words.

Rows grid it all: ਕ ਕਾ ਕਿ ਕੀ ਕੁ ਕੂ ਕੇ ਕੈ ਕੋ ਕੌ — each consonant through every vowel. One row mastered teaches all rows. Read rows aloud for a month; Gurmukhi becomes a friend for life.

Questions learners ask

Is Gurmukhi like Hindi script?

Cousins — Gurmukhi shares Devanagari's order and logic with its own rounded shapes. Hindi readers learn Gurmukhi in days.

What is the Gurmukhi order?

The first row: ੳ ਅ ੲ (vowel-bearers) then ਸ ਹ — the alphabet's name-order. Chant ੳ ਅ ੲ ਸ ਹ ਕ ਖ ਗ ਘ ਙ daily and letters stick.

Which letters first?

Vowels first: ਅ ਆ ਇ ਈ ਉ ਊ ਏ ਐ ਓ ਔ — then ਕ ਖ ਗ ਘ ਚ ਛ ਜ ਝ. Sounds before shapes; reading follows speaking.
