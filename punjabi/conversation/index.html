Punjabi

Real Punjabi conversation

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

ਮੈਂ, ਤੁਸੀਂ, ਹਾਂ, ਨਹੀਂ — hold your first real Punjabi conversations.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

People anchor talk: ਮੈਂ (I), ਤੁਸੀਂ (you formal), ਉਹ (he/she), ਉਹ (they). ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਸਿੱਖਦਾ ਹਾਂ (I'm learning Punjabi) — gender shows on verbs, proudly. Introduce yourself; conversations ignite.

Questions drive dialogue: ਕੀ? (what?), ਕਿੱਥੇ? (where?), ਕਦੋਂ? (when?), ਕਿਉਂ? (why?), ਕਿਵੇਂ? (how?). ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਰਹਿੰਦੇ ਹੋ? (where do you live?) — one question word plus any verb equals conversation.

Politeness oils everything: ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਕੇ (please) and ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ (sorry/excuse me). ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ, ਹੌਲੀ ਬੋਲੋ (sorry, speak slowly) — apologies plus requests, the learner's armour.

Repair keeps talk alive: ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਇਆ (I didn't understand), ਫਿਰ ਦੱਸੋ (please say again), ਇਸਦਾ ਮਤਲਬ ਕੀ ਹੈ? (what does this mean?). Broken Punjabi beats silent perfection — stumble forward daily.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ਮੈਨੂੰ. ਮੈਨੂੰ (mainun — to-me) is Punjabi's magic word — ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ (I want), ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਆਇਆ (I understood), ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪਤਾ ਹੈ (I know). Experiences flow TOWARD you in Punjabi; master ਮੈਨੂੰ and fifty sentences unlock.

Practice: recall, then use

Use this quick recall drill for Real Punjabi conversation. Hide the script and romanisation columns; say the Punjabi form for each cue before revealing its row, then check spelling and sound. The phrase-table speaker buttons provide a model when a matching browser voice is available.

  1. I
  2. What is your name?
  3. Very nice! / great!

Transfer practice. Turn two rows into a brief exchange: open, answer, and add one follow-up question of your own. Keep the turns short enough to say without reading. In a real conversation, listen for the other person’s pace and leave room for a reply rather than delivering a memorised speech. Start from ਮੈਂ (main) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Context check. For Real Punjabi conversation, “Very nice! / great!” belongs to a particular setting. A common mistake is to treat an English gloss as universal; read the language-specific note above and ask a fluent speaker when local usage differs.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask someone's name?

ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ? (what's your name?) — answer ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਰਾਹੁਲ ਹੈ (my name is Rahul). Names first, friendship follows.

Yes and no in Punjabi?

ਹਾਂ (yes), ਨਹੀਂ (no) — short, clear, universal. ਹਾਂ ਜੀ adds respect; ਨਾ is casual. Yes-no fluency first.

When I don't understand?

ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਇਆ (I didn't understand) and ਹੌਲੀ ਬੋਲੋ (please speak slowly). Honesty tunes every speaker to your level.
