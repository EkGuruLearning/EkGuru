Questions about this course

Must I learn Gurmukhi before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Punjabi in Roman letters, and let Gurmukhi come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

ਸਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ for respect, ਹਾਂ and ਨਹੀਂ, ਧੰਨਵਾਦ, ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ, ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, and ਮੈਂ — I. With those seven you can open any interaction in Punjab politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Punjabi?

The three tones — high, low, level — on voiced aspirates, plus retroflexes. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each one.
