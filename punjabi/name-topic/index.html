Write your name in Punjabi

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Rahul becomes ਰਾਹੁਲ — spell, write and own your name in Gurmukhi.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Names transliterate by ear: break yours into sounds, match each to Gurmukhi letters — ਰਵੀ (Ravi), ਲਕਸ਼ਮੀ (Lakshmi), ਕਿਰਨ (Kiran) show the pattern. Say it slow, write it true — ਸਪੈਲਿੰਗ (spelling) by sound, not by English letters.

Foreign sounds borrow dotted letters: ਖ਼ for khabar-sounds (ਖ਼ਬਰ), ਫ਼ for phone (ਫ਼ੋਨ), ਜ਼ for azure (ਅਜ਼ਰ). These spotted letters are Punjabi citizens now — use them proudly for every imported sound.

Introductions pair speech and script: ਜਾਣ-ਪਛਾਣ (introduction) — ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ (my name is), spoken, then written. ਨਾਮ ਵਾਲੇ ਬੋਰਡ (name boards) on doors and desks teach daily — read every board, collect every name.

Nicknames warm everything: ਪਿਆਰ ਦਾ ਨਾਮ (nickname) — ਬੱਬੂ (dear child), ਸੋਨਾ (golden one), ਗੁੱਡੀ (little doll). Punjabi shortens with love: Ravi becomes ਰਵੀ, Simran becomes ਸਿੰਮੀ. Earn a Punjabi nickname and you have arrived.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ਰਾਹੁਲ. Foreign names glide in Gurmukhi — Rahul appears as ਰਾਹੁਲ, sometimes ਰਾਹੂਲ with a longer vowel. All are read Rahul; none is wrong. Punjabi welcomes foreign names with flexible spelling — pick the shape you love and own it.

Questions learners ask

How do I write my name in Punjabi?

Sound it out in Gurmukhi letters: Rahul → ਰਾਹੁਲ, Priya → ਪ੍ਰਿਯਾ, John → ਜਾਨ. Punjabi spells by ear — say your name slowly, catch each sound, write each letter.

What about sounds Punjabi lacks?

Dotted letters — ਖ਼ (kha), ਗ਼ (gha), ਜ਼ (za), ਫ਼ (fa) — handle foreign sounds. ਫ਼ੋਨ (phone), ਖ਼ਬਰ (news) — spotted letters for imported sounds, fully Punjabi now.

How do I introduce my name?

ਪਹਿਲਾ ਅੱਖਰ (first letter) starts it, ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਅੱਖਰ (last letter) ends it. Introduce with ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ (my name is), then write it — introductions stick twice as well written.
