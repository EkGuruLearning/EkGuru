Talking about relationships in Punjabi

Friends, lovers, weddings — the Punjabi of hearts, spoken truly.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Punjabi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Friendship opens hearts: ਦੋਸਤ (male friend), ਸਹੇਲੀ (female friend) — ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ (my friend) introduces proudly. Punjabi friendships run lifelong and deep; ਦੋਸਤੀ (friendship) is celebrated in poetry, films and daily chai. Make one Punjabi friend and gain a hundred phrases.

Romance speaks softly: ਪ੍ਰੇਮੀ (boyfriend), ਪ੍ਰੇਮਿਕਾ (girlfriend), ਪਿਆਰ (love). Punjabi romance favours poetry over bluntness — Heer-Ranjha teaches the register better than textbooks. Learn gently; speak truly.

Weddings unite families: ਵਿਆਹ (wedding spoken), ਸ਼ਾਦੀ (wedding grand), ਲਾੜਾ (groom), ਲਾੜੀ (bride). Punjabi weddings run for days — bhangra, food, rituals, blessings. ਵਿਆਹ ਦੀਆਂ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ (wedding congratulations) is your ticket to the feast.

Bonds deepen and mend: ਪਿਆਰ (love), ਮੁਹੱਬਤ (affection), ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ (life) — ਮੇਰੀ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ (my life) means my beloved. And when bonds strain: ਮੁਆਫ਼ੀ (forgiveness), ਸ਼ਾਂਤੀ (peace). Punjabi hearts forgive spaciously; ask with folded hands.

A note on ਪਿਆਰ vs ਮੁਹੱਬਤ. ਪਿਆਰ (piyaar) is broad love — family, friends, humanity; ਮੁਹੱਬਤ (muhabbat) leans romantic. ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਪਿਆਰ ਲਈ ਧੰਨਵਾਦ (thanks for your love) suits everyone; ਮੁਹੱਬਤ belongs to one. Punjabi loves precisely.

Questions learners ask

Friendship words first?

ਦੋਸਤ (male friend), ਸਹੇਲੀ (female friend) — then ਪਿਆਰ (love) and ਮੁਹੱਬਤ (affection) as bonds deepen. Friendship words first; romance follows naturally.

How do I talk about weddings?

ਵਿਆਹ (spoken wedding), ਸ਼ਾਦੀ (grand wedding) — ਲਾੜਾ (groom), ਲਾੜੀ (bride). ਵਿਆਹ ਦੀਆਂ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ (wedding greetings) blesses every couple.

How do I apologise deeply?

ਮੁਆਫ਼ੀ (forgiveness) — ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮੁਆਫ਼ ਕਰੋ (please forgive me), then ਸ਼ਾਂਤੀ (peace). Punjabi reconciliations run deep and warm; ask sincerely, receive fully.
