Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026 · Applies to https://ekguru.shop/ and every page on it.

The short version: EkGuru has no database and no accounts. Nothing you do here is stored on this website, because there is nowhere on this website to store it. The only personal information that exists is what you type into a form and send us on purpose.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Prakash, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, trading as EkGuru. For anything on this page, write to EkGuruLearning@gmail.com or use the contact form. A person answers, usually within a day.

What this site does not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up anywhere on EkGuru.
  • No tracking cookies. Our analytics (GoatCounter) is cookieless by design — it sets no cookie and creates no cross-site identifier. That is why you are not being shown a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.
  • No mailing list. There is no newsletter. Your address is used to reply to you and for nothing else.
  • No selling or sharing of your information, to anyone, ever.
  • No database. EkGuru is a set of static files. There is no server-side storage of any kind.

Who processes your message

Because there is no server here, forms are delivered by third-party email relays. Your name, address and message pass through whichever of these handles your submission, and each has its own privacy policy:

ServiceWhat it does for us
FormSubmitDelivers the contact form and the copy you receive of your own message.
Web3FormsDelivers booking requests to tutors.
StaticFormsA backup, used only if the two above are unavailable.

Nothing else is sent to them, and they are not permitted to use your details for their own purposes.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, which counts page views without cookies, without a cross-site identifier and without collecting anything that identifies you. It records the page, the referrer, the country and the browser type. It cannot follow you to another site and neither can we.

Advertising

EkGuru may display advertising to help keep the guides, tools and lessons on this site free. When advertising is enabled:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, at youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads on this site; you may visit their websites to opt out of their use of cookies for personalised advertising, where they offer that option.

Advertising cookies are set by the advertising network, not by EkGuru. We do not receive, and cannot see, any personal information they collect.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (if you are in the EU or UK) and the DPDP Act 2023 (if you are in India), you may ask us for a copy of anything we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. In practice the answer is usually short: the only thing we hold is the email you sent us, sitting in an inbox. Write to EkGuruLearning@gmail.com and say what you want done. There is no charge and no form to fill in.

Children

EkGuru is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Lessons for a child are booked by a parent or guardian, who is the person we correspond with. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to us and it will be deleted.

Data outside your country

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the mail relays listed above operate internationally, so your message may be processed outside the country you are in. Tutors are in India, so a booking request is by definition read there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify, which is rather the point.
