Learn Hindi for Polish speakers

Your first language decides which parts of Hindi are easy and which are slow, and almost no course takes account of it. This page does. It is written for someone who already speaks Polish and wants to know what that is worth before spending a year finding out.

Where the verb goes

Polish word order is relatively free because case endings carry the roles. Hindi works on the same principle with postpositions instead of endings, so Polish speakers adapt to Hindi word order faster than speakers of any western European language.

What Polish gives you for free

Case, and a great deal of it. Seven Polish cases have thoroughly trained the instinct that a noun's role is marked morphologically rather than implied by position — which is precisely what Hindi's postpositions do. Polish also has consonant clusters that make Hindi's look mild, and its sz, cz and ż are produced in a region close enough to Hindi's retroflex series that ट ठ ड ढ cause Polish speakers noticeably less trouble than they cause everyone else. Grammatical gender you already handle without thinking, including in the past tense, which is the closest European analogue to Hindi's verb agreement.

What it does not prepare you for

Aspiration, absent from Polish. And Polish palatalisation should be left behind entirely — softening consonants before front vowels is a Polish habit with no Hindi counterpart, and carrying it across marks your accent immediately.

Sounds to work on

A realistic first thirty days

  1. Days 1–5: aspiration, and consciously dropping palatalisation.
  2. Days 6–12: Devanagari. Polish speakers who learned Cyrillic at school find a third alphabet routine.
  3. Days 13–20: postpositions, framed explicitly as cases. This is the fastest route for you and no standard course will do it unless you ask.
  4. Days 21–30: verb agreement, which your past-tense instinct half-prepares you for.

Notice that the order is not the order a general course would use. That is the whole point of organising this by your first language: the sequence that works depends on what you already have.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed — it takes a moment and a real person reads it. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard: there is no single official way to write Hindi in Latin letters.

Questions Polish speakers ask

Is Hindi hard for Polish speakers?

It depends entirely on what you are comparing. Case, and a great deal of it. Seven Polish cases have thoroughly trained the instinct that a noun's role is marked morphologically rather than implied by position — which is precisely what Hindi's postpositions do. Polish also has consonant clusters that make Hindi's look mild, and its sz, cz and ż are produced in a region close enough to Hindi's retroflex series that ट ठ ड ढ cause Polish speakers noticeably less trouble than they cause everyone else. Grammatical gender you already handle without thinking, including in the past tense, which is the closest European analogue to Hindi's verb agreement.

What is the hardest part of Hindi for a Polish speaker?

Aspiration, absent from Polish. And Polish palatalisation should be left behind entirely — softening consonants before front vowels is a Polish habit with no Hindi counterpart, and carrying it across marks your accent immediately.

Do Polish speakers need to learn Devanagari?

Not to speak. Many learners work in romanised Hindi for months. If you want to read signs, messages or anything written, the script is phonetic and consistent, and most learners read simple words within a few weeks.

How long does Hindi take from Polish?

Nobody can give you an honest number, because it depends on hours per week far more than on your first language. What this page can tell you is which parts will be quick for you and which will be slow, which is more useful than a figure.

The part you cannot do alone

Everything above can be read. The sound work in particular cannot be self-assessed — you cannot hear your own errors, which is precisely why they fossilise. A tutor is the only mechanism that fixes that, and an hour a week is enough.
