How to find the right Hindi tutor

Pick the tutor whose profile matches the goal you can describe. The questions below are the ones people actually ask before booking, and answering them here is the difference between a tutor you keep and one you drop after a lesson.

Start from your goal, not the tutor

Decide what you want first: conversation, reading and the Devanagari script, school support, heritage learning, or something specific. Then compare that goal with each profile's subjects, levels and teaching description. A listing is not a guarantee that a tutor currently has the right slot or accepts every request — confirm directly.

Check the lesson time against your day

For "near me", the lesson time is what really matters. Each tutor location page shows the lesson hours in your own timezone, so you can see whether a slot lands in your morning, evening or late night. See tutors by location →

Check trial, price and terms before committing

Do not assume every tutor offers the same trial, lesson duration, payment or cancellation terms. Each profile shows the tutor's base lesson price; EkGuru takes no commission on tutor lessons and adds no platform fee to the displayed price in this release, and EkGuru does not collect tutor-lesson payments. Any converted amount is an estimate based on an exchange rate. Check the profile and confirm the amount, payment method and terms directly before you commit. A lesson request is an enquiry until current availability and terms are confirmed in a reply.

Language background and qualifications

Profiles contain tutor-provided statements about language background, experience and qualifications. EkGuru has not independently verified every statement; ask how the tutor would teach your particular goal before booking.

Common questions

Can I find a Hindi tutor physically near me?

EkGuru tutors teach online only — there is no in-person tutor or local branch to visit. Every lesson is taken over the internet, so "near me" means the lesson time and timezone fit your day rather than a location you travel to.

Do the tutors speak Hindi natively?

Every tutor on EkGuru lives in India and uses Hindi daily. Profiles state the languages each tutor teaches in, so you can check before booking whether they will explain grammar in English or in Hindi.

Can a complete beginner book a tutor?

Yes, and beginners are the most common starting point. Choose a profile that explicitly lists beginners or your current level, and state what you already know. The free self-study course is also a good place to start before your first lesson.

How long until I can hold a conversation?

There is no reliable universal timetable. It depends on prior languages, speaking practice, lesson frequency, feedback and the kind of conversation you mean. Ask a prospective tutor for a plan tied to your goal rather than a guaranteed date.

Can I learn without a tutor first?

Yes. The free Hindi course, the practice tools and the questions section need no payment and no sign-up. Book a tutor when you want conversation and correction.
