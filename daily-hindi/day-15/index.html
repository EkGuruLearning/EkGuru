Day 15 — Asking how much

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The idea

यह कितने का है — the sentence you will use more than any other in India. Note the structure: this how-much of is. Word for word it makes no sense in English, which is the point at which you stop translating.

Today's task

Do this, not just read it

Say it fifty times. It should be a single reflex, not four words assembled.

Words for today

EnglishHindiSounds like
How much is this?यह कितने का है?yeh kitne ka hai
That's too expensiveयह बहुत महँगा हैyeh bahut mehanga hai
Please reduce the price a littleथोड़ा कम कीजिएthoda kam keejiye

The mistake to avoid

Watch for this

Assembling it word by word in the moment. It has to be automatic or it arrives too late.

Where this sits in the thirty days

This is day 1 of 8 in the grammar that carries weight phase (days 15–22), whose job is gender, postpositions and the present tense working together. It assumes you have done day 14. It is also the first day of a new phase, so expect the ground to shift: the previous phase's habits stay, but the thing being practised changes. Day 16 continues from here.

How long today should take

About 21 to 31 minutes if you do the task properly rather than reading past it. The speaking part is not optional decoration — saying it aloud badly is worth more than reading it correctly, because your mouth learns nothing from your eyes. Missing a day is not a reason to stop — the sequence matters, the calendar does not.

A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed — it takes a moment and a real person reads it. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard: there is no single official way to write Hindi in Latin letters.
