What this is, and what it is not

It is a structured beginner path with a specific opinion: sound before script, script before grammar, gender from the first noun, and speaking earlier than is comfortable. Each day names the mistake learners usually make on that topic, which is the part textbooks leave out because it is not the rule — it is what happens to the rule in a real mouth.

It is not a course with a teacher, a certificate or feedback. Thirty days of this will give you the sound system, the script, three tenses, gender and a few hundred words. That is roughly a third of the way to simple conversation, and the remaining two thirds are speaking hours, which no website can supply.

How to actually finish it

  1. Same time every day. Twenty minutes daily beats two hours on Sunday, and it is not close — memory consolidates between sessions.
  2. Do the task, not just the reading. Every day has one and it is always the part that produces the learning.
  3. Say everything aloud. Silent reading builds recognition; only speech builds speech.
  4. Miss a day and carry on. Restarting from day 1 is the commonest way this ends.
Around day 20, this stops being enough

Days 21 to 30 cover the past tense, the ने construction and listening at real speed. All three are things you cannot self-correct, because the errors are inaudible from the inside. That is the point to add one hour a week with a person, not before and not much later.
