How long does it take to learn Hindi?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Around 4–6 months of consistent study for simple conversation, and 1–2 years for comfortable everyday fluency. The Foreign Service Institute's full-time estimate is 1,100 hours to professional working proficiency.

What each stage looks like

What actually changes the timeline

Speaking practice, more than anything else. Two learners studying the same number of hours will end up in very different places if one speaks weekly with a person and the other only uses an app. Comprehension grows either way; production only grows when you produce.

Twenty minutes daily beats three hours on Sunday. This is not motivational advice, it is how memory consolidation works.

Having a reason. Learners with a specific person to talk to, a trip booked, or a film they love tend to finish. Learners who want to "learn a language" mostly do not.

If you already speak an Indian language

Halve everything above. Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Nepali and Punjabi speakers share script, vocabulary or grammar with Hindi and typically reach conversation in two or three months.
