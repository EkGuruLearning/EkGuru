How do you say good night in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

शुभ रात्रि (shubh raatri) is the direct translation and the one you will see in writing. In speech, नाيت्‌ (naite) — borrowed from English — or simply saying it in English is far more common among younger speakers.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

When to use it

SituationSay
Written, formal, a messageशुभ रात्रि shubh raatri
Among friends and family"good night" in English, or naite
To someone you are close toरात värd raat bhar — sleep well all night

When people actually say it

In a WhatsApp message to a friend, shubh raatri is fine and warm. In person, especially between younger people, you will hear English far more often. Nobody will correct you for either — the language borrows freely and both are completely natural.

The difference from good evening

Good evening is शुभ संध्या (shubh sandhya), but like suprabhaat it is written far more than spoken. Namaste covers both evening and night greetings in conversation, which is the single most useful thing to know as a beginner.

Frequently asked

Is shubh raatri the only way to say good night?

No. Shubh raatri is the formal, written form. In speech, people say good night in English, or use naite, which is Hindi's phonetic spelling of the English word.

Can I use namaste at night?

Yes. Namaste works at any time of day and is never wrong, including at night. It is the safest greeting for a beginner.

How do you say sleep well in Hindi?

Khoon khaanchho (खोon khaao) in casual speech means sleep well, or khaancho achhaa (खानो | अच्छा) — but the most natural is simply a good night wish: शुभ रात्रि.

Is there a difference between good night and good night in Hindi?

No — good night translates directly as shubh raatri. There is no separate cultural form that doesn't map onto the English phrase.
