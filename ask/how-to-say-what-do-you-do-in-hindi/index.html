How do you say what do you do in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

आप क्या करते हैं? (aap kyaa karte hain?) is the direct, polite form — "you what do are?" In casual conversation, people often shorten it to क्या करते हैं? (kyaa karte hain?).
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Kyaa karte hain — the standard question

Kyaa karte hain literally means "what do (you) do?" It is the standard, polite way to ask what someone does — for a job, a profession, or how they spend their time. It is one of the most common small-talk questions in Hindi and works with strangers, colleagues, and new acquaintances.

Naukarii kyaa hai? — "what is your job?"

Aapkii naukarii kyaa hai? asks more specifically "what is your job?" Naukarii means "job, employment." It is a slightly more direct question about employment. Both kyaa karte hain and naukarii kyaa hai are common; the first is broader (what do you do), the second is more specific (what is your job).

Frequently asked

What is the most common way to say what do you do in Hindi?

आप क्या करते हैं? (Aap kyaa karte hain?) is the standard, polite form. In casual conversation, people often shorten it to क्या करते हैं? (Kyaa karte hain?).

What is the difference between aap kyaa karte hain and aapki naukarii kyaa hai?

Aap kyaa karte hain means "what do you do?" — broader, covering job or activities. Aapki naukarii kyaa hai means "what is your job?" — more specific to employment.

How do you answer what do you do in Hindi?

मैं ... करता हूँ (Main ... kartaa hoon) for a male speaker, or ... करती हूँ (kartii hoon) for a female. For example: मैं शिक्षक हूँ (Main shikshak hoon) — "I am a teacher."

How do you say what is your work in Hindi?

आपका काम क्या है? (Aapkaa kaam kyaa hai?) — "what is your work?" Kaam means work, and aapkaa means your (polite). This is a slightly more direct way to ask about employment.
