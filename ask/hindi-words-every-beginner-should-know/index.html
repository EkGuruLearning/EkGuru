What are the most useful Hindi words for a beginner?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

About twenty words carry most everyday conversation: हाँ (yes), नहीं (no), क्या (what), कहाँ (where), कितना (how much), पानी (water), खाना (food), अच्छा (good), मैं (I), आप (you).
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Why these and not a list of 500

Question words plus chahiye get you through almost any transaction. "पानी चाहिए" — water, needed — is a complete, correct request. You do not need a single verb conjugation to ask for something, which is why this short list punches so far above its size.
