Is italki or Preply better for learning Hindi?

Both work. Preply has more Hindi tutors and a stricter scheduling system; italki takes a smaller cut and gives tutors more freedom, so prices are often slightly lower. For Hindi specifically the tutor matters far more than the platform.

Why we are telling you this

We run a small Hindi tutoring site, so we have an obvious interest in you not using either. You should still know how they work, because for most learners a big marketplace is a perfectly sensible place to start — the choice is wide and the reviews are real.

The thing both platforms cost you

Commission comes out of the tutor's earnings, not off your bill. A tutor keeping 67% of $6 earns $6. That is why the same person is often cheaper booked directly, and why many tutors quietly prefer it.

What actually decides whether you learn

Not the platform. It is whether the tutor makes you speak from the first lesson instead of lecturing you about grammar, and whether you like them enough to keep showing up on a Tuesday when you are tired.

Take a trial with two or three tutors anywhere — including ours — and pick the person, not the site.
