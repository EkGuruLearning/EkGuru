How much does a Hindi tutor cost?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Online Hindi tutors cost roughly $6 to $6 an hour. Tutors based in India typically charge $6–$6; tutors in the US, UK or Europe charge $6–$6 for the same lesson. The gap is cost of living, not teaching quality.

What the price actually reflects

Per hourUsually means
$6 – $6A native speaker in India, often a qualified teacher
$6 – $6Formal teaching qualifications, exam preparation
$6 – $6A tutor living in a high-cost country, or a niche specialism

A tutor in Jaipur charging $6 is earning a reasonable local wage. The same lesson from someone in London has to cover London rent. You are very often paying for geography.

The number that matters more than the hourly rate

Total monthly cost. One lesson a week at $6 is $6 a month. Three lessons a week at $6 is $6 — more teaching, less money. Learners who compare only hourly rates routinely pick the more expensive arrangement.

What you should not pay for

  • Large prepaid packages before a trial lesson. If a tutor suits you, they will still suit you next month.
  • Platform fees on top — some marketplaces take 18–33% of what you pay, which is why the same tutor is often cheaper direct.
  • Daily lessons on day one. Most people cannot absorb that much, and stop after three weeks.

A realistic budget

One or two lessons a week, plus free study in between, works for almost everyone. Our tutors start at $6 a lesson ($6–$6 across the whole list), with prices shown in your own currency.

Practise this with a teacher

Reading an answer is one thing; being corrected while you say it is another. EkGuru tutors teach one to one from $6 a lesson, and most offer a trial.
