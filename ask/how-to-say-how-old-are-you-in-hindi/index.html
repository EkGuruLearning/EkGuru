How do you say how old are you in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

आपकी आयु क्या है (aapkii aayu kyaa hai) is the direct, polite form. In casual speech, आयु (aayu) is often replaced by the simpler उम्र (umr), so you will hear आपकी उम्र क्या है (aapki umr kyaa hai) far more often in everyday conversation.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

When to use it

SituationSay
Polite, formalआपकी आयु क्या है? aapkii aayu kyaa hai?
Everyday, most commonआपकी उम्र क्या है? aapki umr kyaa hai?
Casual, informalतुमकी उम्र क्या है? tumki umr kyaa hai?
My age is...मेरी उम्र है... meri umr hai...

Umr — the everyday word

While aayu is the formal word for "age," umr is what you will hear in daily conversation. Aapki umr kyaa hai? is the standard way to ask someone their age politely. It works with strangers, colleagues, and acquaintances.

Aap vs tum — the politeness level

Aap is the polite, formal "you" and should be your default. Tum is the informal "you" used with close friends, children, or people younger than you. If you are unsure, use aap — it is never wrong, and using tum with the wrong person can sound rude.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between aayu and umr?

Aayu (आयु) is the formal, written word for 'age.' Umr (उम्र) is the everyday, spoken word. In conversation, umr is far more common — you will rarely hear aayu outside of formal contexts.

How do you say my age is in Hindi?

मेरी उम्र है (Meri umr hai) — 'my age is.' You would follow it with the number: मेरी उम्र २५ वर्ष है (Meri umr 25 varsh hai) — 'my age is 25 years.'

How do you say how old is he in Hindi?

ئۇنىڭ يېشى क्या है (Uski umr kyaa hai?) — 'his/her age is what?' The pronoun changed from aapki/tumki to uski (his/her), but umr kyaa hai stays the same.

Is it polite to ask someone's age in Hindi?

It depends on the context and the person. Asking an older person's age can be considered rude in some contexts, but asking a peer or a shopkeeper their age in casual conversation is generally fine, especially if you are learning Hindi.
