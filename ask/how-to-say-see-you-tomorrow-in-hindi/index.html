How do you say see you tomorrow in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

कल मिलते हैं (kal milte hain) is the most natural — "we meet tomorrow." फिर कल मिलते हैं (phir kal milte hain) adds "again" for warmth.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

When to use it

SituationSay
Most common, all-purposeकल मिलते हैं kal milte hain
Warm, "we meet again tomorrow"फिर कल मिलते हैं phir kal milte hain
Casual, between friendsकल मिलते हैं kal milte hain
Take care, see you tomorrowकल मिलते हैं, ख्याल रखें kal milte hain, khayaal rakhein

Kal milte hain — the standard farewell

Kal means "tomorrow" and milte hain means "we meet." Kal milte hain is the natural, everyday way to say "see you tomorrow." It works with friends, colleagues, and acquaintances. It is one of the most common, useful farewell phrases in Hindi.

Phir kal milte hain — extra warmth

Phir means "again." Phir kal milte hain adds a touch of warmth — "we meet again tomorrow." It is slightly more meaningful than the basic form and is used when you want the farewell to feel a little warmer, especially with close friends or family.

Frequently asked

What is the most common way to say see you tomorrow in Hindi?

कल मिलते हैं (Kal milte hain) — "we meet tomorrow." This is the standard, natural farewell for the next day.

How do you say see you later in Hindi?

बाद में मिलते हैं (Baad meN milte hain) — "we meet later." This is the more general form when you don't specify when.

How do you say see you soon in Hindi?

जल्द मिलते हैं (Jaldi milte hain) — "we meet soon." Jaldi means "soon/quickly." It is the natural way to say see you soon.

How do you say see you again in Hindi?

फिर मिलते हैं (Phir milte hain) — "we meet again." Phir means "again." This is used when you expect to see the person again but don't specify when.
