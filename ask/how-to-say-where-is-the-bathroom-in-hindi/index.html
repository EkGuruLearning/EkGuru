How do you say where is the bathroom in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

शौचालय कहाँ है? (shouchaalay kahaan hai?) is the direct, polite form. In casual speech, especially in a restaurant or shop, शौचालय (shouchaalay) is universally understood, and many people simply say टपट (tapp) or बathroom in informal contexts.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Shouchaalay — the universal word

Shouchaalay (शौचालय) is the standard, polite word for "bathroom/toilet" and is understood everywhere in India. Combine it with kahaan hai? for "where is the bathroom?" It is the safe, correct choice in any context — a restaurant, a hotel, a public building. Nobody will misunderstand it.

Tapp — the informal alternative

In informal settings, especially in some regions, you may hear tapp (टपट) or other colloquial words for bathroom. These are regional and informal — use shouchaalay with strangers or in any context where you want to be polite. The informal words are fine between friends or in casual settings but can be crude in formal contexts.

Frequently asked

What is the most polite way to ask where the bathroom is in Hindi?

शौचालय कहाँ है? (Shouchaalay kahaan hai?) is the most polite and universal. Shouchalay means 'bathroom/toilet' and kahaan hai means 'where is.'

Is there a casual word for bathroom in Hindi?

Yes. In some regions, people use टपट (tapp) or other informal words. However, shouchalay is universally understood and polite. Use shouchalay in any situation where you want to be respectful.

How do you say toilet in Hindi?

शौचालय (Shouchaalay) is the polite, standard word. In formal written contexts, you may also see पचनाय (pachaanāy), but shouchalay is the everyday spoken form.

How do you say can I use the bathroom in Hindi?

मैं शौचालय सकता हूँ? (Main shouchalay saktaa hoon?) is a direct way. More politely, you can say शौचालय इस्तेमाल कर सकता हूँ? (Shouchaalay istemaal kar saktaa hoon?) — 'can I use the bathroom?'
