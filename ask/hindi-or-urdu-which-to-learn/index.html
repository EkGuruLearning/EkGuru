Should I learn Hindi or Urdu?

Spoken, they are close enough that learning one gives you most of the other. Choose by script and destination: Hindi uses Devanagari and is what you need for India; Urdu uses a Perso-Arabic script and is what you need for Pakistan.

The practical difference

HindiUrdu
ScriptDevanagari, left to rightNastaliq, right to left
Main countryIndiaPakistan
Formal vocabulary fromSanskritPersian and Arabic
Everyday speechVery largely the same

Where they genuinely diverge

Not in the market or the taxi — there you would struggle to tell them apart. They separate in formal registers: a news broadcast, a legal document, a poem. Literary Hindi reaches for Sanskrit, literary Urdu for Persian, and at that level they stop being mutually intelligible.

Choose Hindi if

  • You are travelling to, working with or have family in India
  • You want the larger pool of learning material and tutors
  • You watch Bollywood — the dialogue is closer to Hindi-Urdu common ground, but the titles and captions are Devanagari

Choose Urdu if

  • Pakistan, or a Pakistani community
  • You already read Arabic or Persian script — that is a real head start
  • You are drawn to Urdu poetry, which is a genuinely good reason

The honest answer for most people

Learn Hindi first. More material, more tutors, and the spoken language transfers. Picking up Urdu afterwards is mostly learning a new script for a language you already speak — a matter of weeks, not years.
