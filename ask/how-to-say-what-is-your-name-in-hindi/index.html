How do you say what is your name in Hindi?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

आपका नाम क्या है? (aapka naam kya hai?) politely, or तुम्हारा नाम क्या है? (tumhara naam kya hai?) to a friend or a child.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Word by word

HindiMeans
आपका aapkayour (polite)
नाम naamname
क्या kyawhat
है haiis

Literally that is "your name what is" — the verb lands at the end, which is the biggest structural difference between Hindi and English. Why the verb goes last.

Answering

मेरा नाम ___ है (mera naam ___ hai). Not just your name on its own — that sounds like an announcement rather than a reply.
