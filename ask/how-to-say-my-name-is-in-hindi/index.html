How do you say my name is in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

मेरा नाम ... है (meraa naam ... hai) is the direct, standard pattern — "my name is [name]." मैं ... कहलाता हूँ (main ... kahlaataa hoon) is the alternative "I am called [name]."
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

When to use it

SituationSay
Standard, all-purposeमेरा नाम ... है meraa naam ... hai
My name is Johnमेरा नाम जॉन है meraa naam John hai
I am called ...मैं ... कहलाता हूँ main ... kahlaataa hoon
Polite, formalमेरे नाम ... हैं mere naam ... hain

Mera naam ... hai — the core pattern

Mera means "my," naam means "name," and hai means "is." Mera naam [name] hai is the standard, universal way to introduce yourself by name. It works in every context — casual, formal, with strangers or friends. It is one of the first sentences every Hindi learner should master.

Kahlaataa hoon — "I am called"

Main [name] kahlaataa hoon means "I am called [name]." It is slightly more formal and is used when you want to describe how people address you, rather than simply stating your name. The feminine form is kahlaatii hoon. Both are correct; mera naam ... hai is the more common introduction.

Frequently asked

What is the most common way to say my name is in Hindi?

मेरा नाम ... है (Mera naam ... hai) is the standard, all-purpose form. Fill in the name where the ... is.

How do you say my name is John in Hindi?

मेरा नाम जॉन है (Mera naam John hai). The name can be in Hindi script or the Latin alphabet — both are understood.

How do you introduce yourself in Hindi?

Start with नमस्ते (Namaste), then say मेरा नाम ... है (Mera naam ... hai). For example: नमस्ते, मेरा नाम John है (Namaste, mera naam John hai).

How do you say what is your name in Hindi?

आपका नाम क्या है? (Aapkaa naam kyaa hai?) — "what is your name?" The polite form uses aapkaa (your, polite) instead of meraa (my).
