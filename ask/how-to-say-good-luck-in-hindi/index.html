How do you say good luck in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

शुभकामनाएँ (shubhkaamanaaen) is the direct translation — "good wishes." For exams, interviews, and big events, वहीं (shubhkaamanaaen) is what you will hear most. The colloquial लगभग form शुभकामनाएँ is universal.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

When to use it

SituationSay
All-purpose, most commonशुभकामनाएँ! shubhkaamanaaen!
Before an exam or interviewशुभकामनाएँ! shubhkaamanaaen!

Wishing someone well on a journeyयात्रा शुभ हो yaatraa shubh ho — may your journey be auspicious

Shubhkaamanaaen — the standard wish

This is the most common, all-purpose way to wish someone good luck. Literally "good wishes," it is used before exams, interviews, journeys, new jobs, and any significant event. It is polite, warm, and understood everywhere. You will hear it constantly in Indian contexts.

Yaatraa shubh ho — for a journey

When someone is travelling — going on a trip, moving to a new city — yaatraa shubh ho is the specific blessing: "may your journey be auspicious." It is the traditional, warm way to wish someone well on a journey and is still used today.

Frequently asked

What is the most common way to say good luck in Hindi?

शुभकामनाएँ (Shubhkaamanaaen) is the most common and all-purpose — literally 'good wishes.' It is used before exams, interviews, journeys, and any significant event.

Is there a Hindi word that means exactly 'good luck'?

There is no single word that translates 'good luck' exactly. Shubhkaamanaaen (good wishes) is the closest and most natural equivalent, and it is universally used in the contexts where English says 'good luck.'

How do you say best of luck in Hindi?

शुभकामनाएँ (Shubhkaamanaaen) covers it. For extra emphasis, you can say बहुत-बहुत शुभकामनाएँ (bahut-bahut shubhkaamanaaen) — 'very good wishes.'

How do you say good luck with your exam in Hindi?

आपकी परीक्षा के लिए शुभकामनाएँ (Aapkii parikhaa ke liye shubhkaamanaaen) — 'good wishes for your exam.' You can also simply say शुभकामनाएँ (shubhkaamanaaen) if the context is clear.
