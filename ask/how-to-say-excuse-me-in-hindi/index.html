How do you say excuse me in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The word that covers almost every situation is क्षमा करें (kshamaa karein) — literally "do forgive me." For getting someone's attention, क्यों (kyoN) — a short, rising "why?" used as a polite attention-getter — is what you will hear most.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

When to use it

SituationSay
To get someone's attention (store, street)क्यों kyoN
To apologise for bumping into someoneक्षमा करें kshamaa karein
To ask to pass by someoneक्षमा करें, मैं өтना चाहता हूँ kshamaa karein, main ootanaa chaahata hoon
To interrupt a conversationक्षमा करें kshamaa karein

KyoN — the everyday attention-getter

KyoN literally means "why," but when said with a rising tone and a polite expression it means "excuse me" in the sense of "may I have your attention." You will hear it constantly in shops, on buses, and on the street. It is informal but never rude.

Kshamaa karein — the formal apology

Kshamaa karein is the polite, safe choice for any situation where you might be at fault — bumping into someone, interrupting, asking to correct a mistake. It is more formal than maaf karo, which is the casual version you hear between friends.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between kshamaa karein and maaf karo?

Kshamaa karein is polite and formal — the safe choice with strangers, elders, or in any situation where you want to be respectful. Maaf karo is casual, used between friends and family.

Can I just say kyoN to a stranger?

Yes. KyoN with a polite expression is the most common way to get a stranger's attention in India. It is informal but universally understood and never considered rude.

How do you say I am sorry in Hindi?

The everyday phrase is माफ़ करें (maaf karein) for polite speech or माफ़ कर (maaf kar) between friends. See the how to say sorry in Hindi page for the full nuance.

How do you say pardon me in Hindi?

You can use क्षमा करें (kshamaa karein) for a polite pardon, or simply say kyoN with a questioning tone. For not hearing someone, kshamaa karein is the safest choice.
