How do you say congratulations in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

शुभकामनाएँ (shubhkaamanaaen) is the direct, all-purpose translation — "good wishes." बधाई (badhaii) is the more celebratory, colloquial form used at weddings, for promotions, and at celebrations.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Shubhkaamanaaen — the formal wish

This is the safe, all-purpose word for "congratulations." It means "good wishes" and works in any context — a promotion, an exam result, a new job, a graduation. It is polite, formal, and never wrong. If you are unsure which word to use, shubhkaamanaaen is the choice.

Badhaii — the celebratory form

Badhaii is the warmer, more colloquial form used at celebrations — weddings, parties, festivals. It carries more of the celebratory, joyful feel. If someone is celebrating something big — a wedding, a birth, a big win — badhaii is the more natural and enthusiastic choice. You will hear it constantly at Indian celebrations.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between shubhkaamanaaen and badhaii?

Shubhkaamanaaen is the formal, all-purpose 'congratulations' — good wishes. Badhaii is the warmer, more celebratory form used at weddings, parties, and festivals. Shubhkaamanaaen is safe in any context; badhaii is more enthusiastic and celebration-specific.

What is the most common way to say congratulations in Hindi?

Both are common. Shubhkaamanaaen is the formal all-purpose form. Badhaii is the celebratory form you will hear at weddings and parties. For a general congratulations, shubhkaamanaaen is the safest choice.

How do you say congratulations on your wedding in Hindi?

शादी के लिए शुभकामनाएँ (Shaadii ke liye shubhkaamanaaen) — 'congratulations for the wedding.' At a wedding, people will also say badhaii! (celebration!) repeatedly.

How do you say congratulations on your success in Hindi?

सफलता के लिए शुभकामनाएँ (Safaltaa ke liye shubhkaamanaaen) — 'congratulations for the success.' Safaltaa means success, ke liye means 'for,' and shubhkaamanaaen means 'good wishes/congratulations.'
