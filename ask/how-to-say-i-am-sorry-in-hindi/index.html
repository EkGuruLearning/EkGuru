How do you say I am sorry in Hindi?

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-21. Editorial policy and corrections.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

माफ़ करें (maaf karein) is the polite, standard form — "forgive (me)." माफ़ कर (maaf kar) is the casual version used between friends. For a more formal apology, क्षमा करें (kshamaa karein) is appropriate.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Maaf karein — the everyday apology

This is the most common, all-purpose way to say "sorry" in Hindi. Maaf means "forgive/pardon" and karein is the polite imperative "do." Together: "please forgive." It is polite, natural, and works in almost every context — bumping into someone, being late, making a mistake. It is the safe default.

Kshamaa karein — the formal apology

Kshamaa karein is a more formal, weightier word for "forgive." It is appropriate when the offence is more significant — a real mistake, a serious inconvenience. It carries more gravity than maaf karein and is the right choice when you want your apology to feel sincere and formal.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between maaf karein and kshamaa karein?

Maaf karein is the everyday, common 'sorry' — polite and all-purpose. Kshamaa karein is more formal and weightier, used for more serious apologies. For most everyday situations, maaf karein is the natural choice.

What is the most common way to say sorry in Hindi?

माफ़ करें (Maaf karein) is the most common. You will hear it constantly in everyday situations — bumping into someone, making a mistake, being late. It is the standard, polite apology.

How do you say I am sorry in Hindi — the statement form?

मैं माफ़ी चाहता हूँ (Main maafii chaahata hoon) — 'I want forgiveness/I apologise.' This is the full statement form, used when you want to express that you are apologising as a complete thought, rather than just saying 'forgive me.'

How do you say sorry in Hindi between friends?

माफ़ कर (Maaf kar) is the casual form used between friends, family, and people you are close to. The ending changes from the polite -ein to the casual -a, which signals informality.
