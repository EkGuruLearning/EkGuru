About EkGuru

Last updated 11 September 2026 · Applies to https://ekguru.shop/ and every page on it.

EkGuru publishes Hindi tutor profiles and a publication-gated library of self-study language material. Current tutor and course details are linked from this page.

Who runs it

EkGuru identifies Prakash as its site operator, based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. For ownership questions or corrections, use the contact page. A public profile is linked for identity context: LinkedIn.

Why it exists

The site combines self-study material with links to individual Hindi tutor profiles. Publication gates are used so incomplete language levels and research-only country relationships are not presented as finished courses.

What is here

  • The course library — the authoritative list of published languages and exact level ranges.
  • Hindi guides and interactive tools.
  • A structured Hindi practice path.
  • Hindi tutor profiles with the details currently supplied for publication.
  • Quarantined country and source-language research, clearly labelled and kept out of search indexes until its evidence and copy pass review.

How tutor contact works

Tutor profiles state the currently published background, subjects and booking details. Confirm price, currency, availability, timezone, lesson format and trial terms directly before booking. A request is not a confirmed lesson until the tutor accepts it. EkGuru does not process tutor-lesson payments in this release; the separate Support page links to a hosted Razorpay page for optional contributions.

How this site is funded

The current and planned channels are stated plainly.

  • Optional support. Visitors can make a one-time contribution on Razorpay's hosted payment page.
  • Tutoring. Tutors earn from lessons they teach; any future EkGuru booking revenue will be disclosed before it is introduced.
  • Advertising and affiliates. Both are disabled in this release. They will not be activated before the required content, consent, account and disclosure checks pass.

Read the full monetization disclosure.

What we will not do: write a page because an advertiser wanted it, rank a tutor higher because they paid, or publish a review nobody wrote.

How the content is made

EkGuru stores lessons and research as structured source data and uses build tools to format some pages consistently. Structural generation is not treated as proof of quality: templated/filler course levels are blocked, research-only language and country pages are noindex and excluded from sitemaps, and only levels that pass the publication checks appear in the course library. Native-speaker and source review remains an ongoing requirement.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, please say so. Devanagari typed by hand goes wrong in small ways and a native speaker spotting it is worth more than any amount of proofreading here. Report it in a moment.

Contact

The contact form, or EkGuruLearning@gmail.com. A person reads everything, usually replying within a day.

Something here unclear or wrong?
