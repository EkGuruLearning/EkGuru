Core Tamil vocabulary

Water, money, time, today — the everyday words that unlock daily life.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Tamil spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Survival runs on nouns: தண்ணீர் (thanneer — water), உணவு (unavu — food), வீடு (veedu — home), பணம் (panam — money), நேரம் (neram — time). Five words, and you can ask for water, find home, handle money and ask the time — the entire first day in a new city.

Movement words come next: சாலை (saalai — road), பேருந்து (perundu — bus), கடை (kadai — shop), பள்ளி (palli — school). Tamil cities move by bus and foot; knowing பேருந்து நிலையம் எங்கே? (where is the bus stand?) saves more time than any app.

Opinions need adjectives: நல்ல (nalla — good), பெரிய (periya — big), சிறிய (siriya — small), புதிய (pudiya — new), பழைய (pazhaya — old). நல்லது (nalladhu — that's good) approves everything from food to plans — the most useful single opinion in Tamil.

Weather and mood close the kit: மழை (mazhai — rain) matters half the year, வெயில் (veyil — sun/heat) the other half. மழை வருகிறது (rain is coming) starts conversations with strangers; in Tamil Nadu, weather talk is friendship's front door.

A note on இன்று, நாளை, நேற்று. Tamil time words pivot around today: இன்று (indru — today), நாளை (naalai — tomorrow), நேற்று (nettru — yesterday). Master this trio and half your daily sentences assemble themselves — நாளை பார்க்கலாம் (see you tomorrow) alone is worth the lesson.

Questions learners ask

Which words pay off fastest?

Time words first: இன்று (today), நாளை (tomorrow), நேற்று (yesterday), இப்போது (now). You will use them ten times a day — shopping, planning, chatting — while rare nouns gather dust.

Is mixing English okay?

Tamil happily absorbs English — பஸ் (bus), டிக்கெட் (ticket), ஆபீஸ் (office). Use the pure Tamil word when you know it (பேருந்து, நுழைவுச்சீட்டு), the loanword when you don't. Fluency first, purity later.

Nouns or verbs first?

Nouns first, verbs second, adjectives third. With fifty nouns and the twelve verbs from the verbs page, you can point, ask and survive — grammar glues them later.
