Write your name in Tamil

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Rahul becomes ராகுல் — spell, write and own your name in Tamil.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Tamil spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Names transliterate by ear: break yours into sounds, match each to Tamil letters — முருகன் (Murugan), லட்சுமி (Lakshmi), கண்ணன் (Kannan) show the pattern. Say it slow, write it true — எழுத்துக்கூட்டு (spelling) by sound, not by English letters.

Foreign sounds borrow Grantha letters: ஜ for John (ஜான்), ஷ for Sharma sounds, ஹ for hotels and Rahuls, ஸ for stars. These Sanskrit-born letters are Tamil citizens now — use them proudly for every imported sound.

Introductions pair speech and script: அறிமுகம் (introduction) — என் பெயர்... (my name is...), spoken, then written. பெயர் பலகை (name boards) on doors and desks teach daily — read every board, collect every name.

Nicknames warm everything: புனைபெயர் (nickname) — செல்லம் (darling), கண்ணா (dear), தங்கம் (gold). Tamil shortens with love: Murugan becomes முருகு, Lakshmi becomes லட்சு. Earn a Tamil nickname and you have arrived.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ராகுல் vs ராஹுல். Foreign h floats in Tamil names — Rahul appears as ராகுல், ராஹுல், even ரகுல். All are read Rahul; none is wrong. Tamil welcomes foreign names with flexible spelling — pick the shape you love and own it.

Questions learners ask

How do I write my name in Tamil?

Sound it out in Tamil letters: Rahul → ராகுல், Priya → பிரியா, John → ஜான். Tamil spells by ear — say your name slowly, catch each sound, write each letter.

What about sounds Tamil lacks?

Grantha letters — ஜ (ja), ஷ (sha), ஸ (sa), ஹ (ha), க்ஷ (ksha) — handle foreign sounds. ஜான் (John), ஷர்மிளா? — borrowed letters for borrowed names, fully Tamil now.

How do I introduce my name?

முதல் எழுத்து (first letter) starts it, கடைசி எழுத்து (last letter) ends it. Introduce with என் பெயர்... (my name is...), then write it — introductions stick twice as well written.
