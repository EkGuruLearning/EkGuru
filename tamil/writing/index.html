Writing Tamil

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Letters to lists to letters — writing Tamil from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Tamil spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with strokes: Tamil letters curve and loop — எழுதுங்கள் (please write) circles, hooks and waves before full letters. Trace அ ஆ இ ஈ உ ஊ big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: எழுதுகோல் (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Tamil children trace in rice, sand and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: பட்டியல் (list) — குறிப்பு (notes), முகவரி (addresses), தேதி (dates). Copy your own பெயர் (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: கடிதம் (letters), விண்ணப்பம் (applications), தேர்வு (exams), வீட்டுப்பாடம் (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Tamil — short, joyful, posted. First posted Tamil is never forgotten.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on கையொப்பம். Your கையொப்பம் (signature) in Tamil script is a milestone — practise it until it flows in one confident motion. Signing Tamil-style on forms, cards and letters declares belonging more than any certificate.

Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Tamil?

Curves before corners: Tamil letters flow round — அ, ஆ, உ, எ — so practise circles and hooks first. Trace big, shrink slowly; கையெழுத்து (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

பட்டியல் (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

கடிதம் (letters) to friends, குறிப்பு (notes) to self, வீட்டுப்பாடம் (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
