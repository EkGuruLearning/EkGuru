Questions about this course

Must I learn the Tamil script before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Tamil in Roman letters, and let the script come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

வணக்கம் for respect, ஆம் and இல்லை, நன்றி, தயவுசெய்து, சரி, and நான் — I. With those seven you can open any interaction in Tamil Nadu politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Tamil?

The Dravidian sounds new to most ears — ழ் (zh), the rolled ர, the three n's and l's. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each one.
