Family words in Tamil

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

அம்மா, அப்பா, அண்ணன், அக்கா — family, including the elder-younger split.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Tamil spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Parents anchor everything: அம்மா (ammaa — mother) and அப்பா (appaa — father) — the first words of every Tamil child and the warmest words in the language. அம்மா calls across market crowds; அப்பா advises over evening coffee. Learn them with love, not just memory.

Siblings split by age, never blend: அண்ணன் (elder brother), தம்பி (younger brother), அக்கா (elder sister), தங்கை (younger sister). There is no plain brother — every brother is elder or younger, and the word you choose declares the hierarchy. Ask ages early; address correctly always.

Grandparents rule the roost: தாத்தா (thaaththaa — grandfather) and பாட்டி (paatti — grandmother) — storytellers, sweet-sneakers, family courts of final appeal. பாட்டி கதை (grandmother's tale) is a whole genre; தாத்தா advice ends arguments. Respect flows upward; treats flow down.

The wider net holds daily life: மாமா (uncle), அத்தை (auntie), கணவர் (husband), மனைவி (wife), குடும்பம் (family itself). என் குடும்பம் (my family) introduces them all — practise the sentence with your own names filled in, because you will say it at every Tamil gathering forever.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on அத்தை, மாமா and the cousin map. Tamil kinship is famously precise: அத்தை (father's sister / mother's brother's wife), மாமா (mother's brother / father's sister's husband), அண்ணி (elder brother's wife), each relation owns its word. Learners need the big six first; the full map unfolds at weddings.

Questions learners ask

Why two words for brother?

Yes — Tamil splits siblings by age: அண்ணன் (elder brother) versus தம்பி (younger brother), அக்கா (elder sister) versus தங்கை (younger sister). Age order is grammar in Tamil families.

Can I call strangers annaa and akkaa?

அண்ணா (annaa) and அக்கா (akkaa) address any slightly-older man or woman — shopkeepers, colleagues, strangers. It borrows family warmth for the whole world.

How do I ask about family?

உங்கள் குடும்பத்தில் எத்தனை பேர்? (how many people in your family?) — then listen proudly to the answer. Family questions are love in Tamil culture.
