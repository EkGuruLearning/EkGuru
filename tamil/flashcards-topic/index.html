Flashcards by topic in Tamil

Spaced repetition, topic decks, daily streaks — flashcards that work.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Tamil spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Cards carry memory: அட்டை (card) — one word, one side; meaning, the other. நினைவு (memory) strengthens with every recall; மறதி (forgetting) is just unreviewed memory. Build decks per topic — greetings, food, verbs — and review each separately.

Spacing schedules success: இடைவெளி (interval) — review new cards tomorrow, then in three days, seven, thirty. மீண்டும் மீண்டும் (again and again), but wider each time. Cramming fills hours; spacing fills years.

Rhythms sustain streaks: தினசரி (daily) quick decks, வாராந்திர (weekly) full reviews, மாதாந்திர (monthly) championships. Ten daily minutes beat Sunday marathons; streaks turn study into வழக்கம் (habit).

Scores gamify growth: மதிப்பெண் (score), போட்டி (contest), சாதனை (achievement) — even தோல்வி (failure) teaches. Quiz yourself, beat yesterday's score, celebrate loudly. Flashcards are games that pay in fluency.

A note on மீள்பார்வை. மீள்பார்வை (meelpaarvai — revision, looking-again) is Tamil's own word for spaced review — looking again, and again, at widening intervals. The language itself teaches the method: revisit, widen, remember.

Questions learners ask

How often should I review?

மீண்டும் மீண்டும் (again and again) — but spaced: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 30 days. இடைவெளி (spacing) beats cramming; நினைவு (memory) loves intervals.

How many cards per topic?

Five to ten per topic — quality over quantity. Master one அட்டை deck fully before opening the next; half-known decks rot.

Daily or weekly review?

தினசரி (daily) short sessions — ten minutes, every day, no exceptions. Streaks compound; மறதி (forgetting) loses to routine.
