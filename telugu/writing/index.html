Writing Telugu

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Letters to lists to letters — writing Telugu from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with strokes: Telugu letters curve and loop — రాయండి (please write) circles, hooks and waves before full letters. Trace అ ఆ ఇ ఈ ఉ ఊ big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: కలం (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Telugu children trace in rice, sand and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: జాబితా (list) — గమనిక (notes), చిరునామా (addresses), తేదీ (dates). Copy your own పేరు (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: ఉత్తరం (letters), దరఖాస్తు (applications), పరీక్ష (exams), ఇంటిపని (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Telugu — short, joyful, posted. First posted Telugu is never forgotten.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on సంతకం. Your సంతకం (signature) in Telugu script is a milestone — practise it until it flows in one confident motion. Signing Telugu-style on forms, cards and letters declares belonging more than any certificate.

Transfer practice. Write three examples from memory, then compare each with the page one character at a time. Correct the shape or mark that changed the meaning instead of rewriting the whole row. Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Telugu?

Round strokes before all: Telugu letters flow circular — అ, ఆ, ఉ, ఎ — so practise circles and hooks first. Trace big, shrink slowly; చేతిరాత (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

జాబితా (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

ఉత్తరం (letters) to friends, గమనిక (notes) to self, ఇంటిపని (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
