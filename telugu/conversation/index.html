Telugu

Real Telugu conversation

Names, homes, small talk — your first real conversations, line by line.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Every first conversation follows the same dance: నమస్కారం, మీ పేరు ఏమిటి? (what is your name?), నా పేరు... (my name is...), మీరు ఎక్కడి నుండి వస్తున్నారు? (where are you from?). Four lines, and you have had a real conversation. Practise them in order until they flow without thinking.

Small talk runs on three questions: మీరు ఏమి చేస్తారు? (what do you do?), తెలుగు మాట్లాడతారా? (do you speak Telugu?), భోజనం చేశారా? (have you eaten? — asked with genuine care). Answer briefly, ask back immediately — మీరు? — because conversations are volleys, not speeches.

When the torrent comes — and Telugu hospitality pours words — deploy the brakes: నెమ్మదిగా మాట్లాడండి (please speak slowly), నాకు అర్థం కాలేదు (I didn't understand), మళ్లీ చెప్పండి (please say it again). Every Telugu speaker honours these instantly; they are the learner's seatbelt.

Close warmly and promise return: మిమ్మల్ని కలవడం సంతోషం (pleased to meet you), మళ్లీ కలుద్దాం (we'll meet again). Leave with a నమస్కారం and the door stays open — Telugu conversations end the way they begin: with folded hands.

A note on మీరు, నువ్వు and గారు. Telugu has two yous where English has one: మీరు (meeru — formal/plural) and నువ్వు (nuvvu — familiar) — plus the magic suffix గారు (gaaru) that elevates any name. Start with మీరు for every adult; let friendship invite you to నువ్వు.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask someone's name?

మీ పేరు ఏమిటి? (mee peru emiti — what is your name?) for respect; నీ పేరు ఏమిటి? (nee peru emiti) for friends and children. Answer with నా పేరు... (naa peru — my name is...). Names first, always.

How do I say where I'm from?

మీరు ఎక్కడి నుండి వస్తున్నారు? (where are you from?) — then answer for yourself: నేను ... నుండి వస్తున్నాను. Origin questions are the universal second question; prepare your answer before you need it.

What do I say when people speak too fast?

నెమ్మదిగా మాట్లాడండి (please speak slowly) and మళ్లీ చెప్పండి (please say it again). Said with a smile, these two sentences make every Telugu speaker slow down and help.
