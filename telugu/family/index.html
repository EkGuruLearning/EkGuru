Family words in Telugu

అమ్మ, నాన్న, అన్నయ్య, అక్క — family, including the elder-younger split.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Parents anchor everything: అమ్మ (amma — mother) and నాన్న (naanna — father) — the first words of every Telugu child and the warmest words in the language. అమ్మ calls across market crowds; నాన్న advises over evening coffee. Learn them with love, not just memory.

Siblings split by age, never blend: అన్నయ్య (elder brother), తమ్ముడు (younger brother), అక్క (elder sister), చెల్లెలు (younger sister). There is no plain brother — every brother is elder or younger, and the word you choose declares the hierarchy. Ask ages early; address correctly always.

Grandparents rule the roost: తాతయ్య (taatayya — grandfather) and అమ్మమ్మ/నానమ్మ (grandmothers) — storytellers, sweet-sneakers, family courts of final appeal. అమ్మమ్మ కథ (grandmother's tale) is a whole genre; తాతయ్య advice ends arguments. Respect flows upward; treats flow down.

The wider net holds daily life: మామయ్య (uncle), అత్తయ్య (auntie), భర్త (husband), భార్య (wife), కుటుంబం (family itself). నా కుటుంబం (my family) introduces them all — practise the sentence with your own names filled in, because you will say it at every Telugu gathering forever.

A note on అమ్మమ్మ vs నానమ్మ. Telugu grandmothers split by side: అమ్మమ్మ (mother's mother) versus నానమ్మ (father's mother) — and తాతయ్య covers grandfathers with side-names too. Learners need the big six first; the full map unfolds at weddings.

Questions learners ask

Why two words for brother?

Yes — Telugu splits siblings by age: అన్నయ్య (elder brother) versus తమ్ముడు (younger brother), అక్క (elder sister) versus చెల్లెలు (younger sister). Age order is grammar in Telugu families.

Can I call strangers annayya and akkayya?

అన్నయ్య (annayya) and అక్కయ్య (akkayya) address any slightly-older man or woman — shopkeepers, colleagues, strangers. It borrows family warmth for the whole world.

How do I ask about family?

మీ కుటుంబంలో ఎంతమంది? (how many people in your family?) — then listen proudly to the answer. Family questions are love in Telugu culture.
