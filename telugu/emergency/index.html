Emergency Telugu

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Help, doctor, hospital — emergency Telugu you hope to never need.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The core three save lives: సహాయం (help), ప్రమాదం (danger), అత్యవసరం (emergency). నాకు సహాయం చేయండి (please help me) — say it to anyone, anywhere. Telugu crowds respond to distress calls faster than any app; your job is only to ask.

Medical words route the rescue: డాక్టర్ (doctor), ఆసుపత్రి (hospital), మందు (medicine), మందుల షాపు (pharmacy). ఆసుపత్రి ఎక్కడ? (where is the hospital?) plus a taxi wave — in cities, help arrives in minutes.

Symptoms speak simply: ఇక్కడ నొప్పిగా ఉంది (it hurts here — point!), ఒంట్లో బాగోలేదు (I feel unwell), తలనొప్పి (headache), జ్వరం (fever). Pain words plus pointing hands diagnose across every language barrier on earth.

Danger and loss close the kit: అగ్ని (fire), దొంగ (thief), పోలీసులు (police), పోయింది (it's lost), ఎంబసీ (embassy). Memorise these cold — emergencies never wait for phrasebooks, and ten minutes of drilling buys total peace of mind.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on సహాయం. సహాయం (sahaayam — help) is Telugu's shortest lifeline — shout it and crowds come running. Telugu bystander culture is famously kind: strangers will escort you to hospitals, translate for doctors, and refuse thanks. Ask loudly; receive gratefully.

Questions learners ask

What is the one emergency sentence?

నాకు సహాయం చేయండి (please help me) — loud, clear, repeated. Add ఆసుపత్రి ఎక్కడ? (where is the hospital?) and Telugu crowds mobilise instantly.

How do I explain pain?

Point and say ఇక్కడ నొప్పిగా ఉంది (it hurts here). Then ఒంట్లో బాగోలేదు (I feel unwell) and డాక్టర్ కావాలి (I need a doctor). Pain plus pointing crosses every language gap.

How do I report something lost?

పోయింది (it is lost) — డబ్బు పోయింది (money lost), బ్యాగ్ పోయింది (bag lost). Then పోలీస్ స్టేషన్ ఎక్కడ? (where is the police station?).
