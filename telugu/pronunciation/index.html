Telugu pronunciation

ర vs ఱ, ళ, క్ష — every tricky sound, one by one, with examples.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The two r's split soft and hard: ర is a light flap — నోరు (noru — mouth), మరి (mari — well then) — while ఱ trills hard — ఱెక్క (rrekka — wing). English ears merge them; Telugu meaning doesn't. Practise pairs daily until your tongue files them separately.

The liquid ళ curls back: పళ్ళు (pallu — teeth), వేళ (vela — time/finger), పల్లె (palle — village) Keep it clean: contrast ల (dental, tongue on teeth) with ళ (retroflex, curled). Glide la-lla daily; the roof-touch becomes reflex.

Dental versus retroflex stops: త (teeth — తలుపు, తెలుగు) against ట (roof — కోట, అట్ట?). Hold a finger to your lips: dentals puff forward, retroflexes pop upward. Ten minutes of త-ట alternation retunes the mouth within a week.

Conjuncts stack kindly: క్ష (ksha — క్షమ, forgiveness), జ్ఞ (jna — జ్ఞానం, knowledge), చ (cha — చెట్టు, tree; softening to sa inside words). Telugu spelling mirrors speech faithfully: pronounce what you see, and you are right nine times in ten.

A note on ఱ (rra). Telugu's warrior trill: roll it hard like Spanish rr — ఱెక్క (wing), కఱకు (rough). Daily speech often softens it toward ర, but names, proverbs and poetry keep the trill. Roll boldly; natives applaud the effort.

Questions learners ask

Do ర and ఱ really differ?

Mostly — modern speech merges them in daily words, but classics, names and careful speech keep ఱ trilled. Learn to hear it first; roll it second. Reading old poetry demands it.

How do I make the ళ sound?

ళ is retroflex — tongue curled back: పళ్ళు (teeth), వేళ (finger/time). ల is dental — tongue on teeth: పలు? Say la-la sliding tongue back and feel the shift.

What are those stacked letters?

Conjuncts: క్ష (ksha — క్షమ), జ్ఞ (jna — జ్ఞానం), త్ర (tra). Pronounce each part quickly in sequence — k+sha, j+na. Conjuncts look fierce and behave kindly.
