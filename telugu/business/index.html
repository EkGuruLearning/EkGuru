Telugu for work and business

Meetings, customers, contracts — professional Telugu for offices.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Workplaces run on roles: పని (work/job), ఆఫీసు (office), బాస్ (boss), కస్టమర్ (customer), సహోద్యోగి (colleague). నా పని... (my work is...) introduces your profession; ఆఫీసు ఎక్కడ? finds any office. Roles first, tasks second.

Meetings move business: సమావేశం (meeting), ఒప్పందం (contract/deal), సంతకం (signature). సమావేశం రేపు ఉదయం (meeting tomorrow morning) — confirm with సరే, వస్తాను (okay, I'll come). Telugu meetings open with నమస్కారం and close with ధన్యవాదాలు.

Money talks plainly: లాభం (profit), నష్టం (loss), జీతం (salary), సెలవు (leave). జీతం ఎప్పుడు? (when is payday?) — asked smiling, everywhere. Business Telugu respects directness wrapped in politeness.

Digital tools connect all: ఈమెయిల్ (email), ఫోన్ (phone), మెసేజ్ (message). ఈమెయిల్ చూడండి (please check email) — then విజయం (success) follows effort. Professional Telugu: formal words, warm heart.

A note on కస్టమర్. కస్టమర్ (customer) rules Telugu business — borrowed, beloved, universal. Formal writing prefers వినియోగదారుడు, but spoken deals run on కస్టమర్. Master the loanword first; the pure form can wait for contracts.

Questions learners ask

How do I introduce myself at work?

నమస్కారం, నేను... (hello, I'm...) — then మిమ్మల్ని కలవడం సంతోషం (pleased to meet you). Business Telugu opens formal and warms slowly.

How do I ask about meetings?

సమావేశం ఎప్పుడు? (when is the meeting?) and సమావేశం ఎక్కడ? (where?). Meetings run Telugu offices — never miss one for language reasons.

Email and phone in Telugu?

ఈమెయిల్ పంపుతాను (I'll send an email) and ఫోన్లో మాట్లాడదాం (let's talk on the phone). Digital Telugu borrows freely — clarity beats purity in business.
