Write your name in Telugu

Rahul becomes రాహుల్ — spell, write and own your name in Telugu.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Telugu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Names transliterate by ear: break yours into sounds, match each to Telugu letters — శ్రీను (Srinu), లక్ష్మి (Lakshmi), వెంకట్ (Venkat) show the pattern. Say it slow, write it true — స్పెల్లింగ్ (spelling) by sound, not by English letters.

Foreign sounds borrow loan letters: జ for John (జాన్), హ for hotels and Rahuls, శ for Sharma sounds. These Sanskrit-born letters are Telugu citizens now — use them proudly for every imported sound.

Introductions pair speech and script: పరిచయం (introduction) — నా పేరు... (my name is...), spoken, then written. పేరు బోర్డులు (name boards) on doors and desks teach daily — read every board, collect every name.

Nicknames warm everything: ముద్దుపేరు (nickname) — బంగారం (gold/darling), కన్నా (dear), చిన్నోడు (little one). Telugu shortens with love: Srinivas becomes శ్రీను, Lakshmi becomes లచ్చి. Earn a Telugu nickname and you have arrived.

A note on రాహుల్ vs రాహుల్. The ha glides in Telugu names — Rahul appears as రాహుల్ or రాహుల్ with a soft హ, sometimes even రావుల్. All are read Rahul; none is wrong. Telugu welcomes foreign names with flexible spelling — pick the shape you love and own it.

Questions learners ask

How do I write my name in Telugu?

Sound it out in Telugu letters: Rahul → రాహుల్, Priya → ప్రియా, John → జాన్. Telugu spells by ear — say your name slowly, catch each sound, write each letter.

What about sounds Telugu lacks?

Loan letters — జ (ja), హ (ha), శ ష స (sha/sa) — handle foreign sounds. జాన్ (John), హోటల్ (hotel) — borrowed letters for borrowed names, fully Telugu now.

How do I introduce my name?

మొదటి అక్షరం (first letter) starts it, చివరి అక్షరం (last letter) ends it. Introduce with నా పేరు... (my name is...), then write it — introductions stick twice as well written.
