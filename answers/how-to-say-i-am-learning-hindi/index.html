How do you say "I am learning Hindi" in Hindi?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

मैं हिंदी सीख रहा हूँ (main hindi seekh raha hoon) if you are male, मैं हिंदी सीख रही हूँ (seekh rahi hoon) if you are female. The verb changes with the gender of the speaker, not the listener — which is the first real grammar shock for most English speakers, and the reason this one sentence teaches more than it looks.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

The sentence, taken apart

  • मैं (main) — I
  • हिंदी — Hindi
  • सीख (seekh) — learn, the verb stem
  • रहा / रही (raha / rahi) — the continuous marker, and this is the part that carries gender
  • हूँ (hoon) — am

Why the gender is on the verb

In Hindi the verb agrees with the subject's gender. A man says karta hoon, a woman says karti hoon, for the identical action. It feels strange for about a week and then becomes automatic.

Get it wrong and you will still be understood perfectly. Nobody will correct you unkindly. But it is the single most audible marker of a beginner, so it is worth fixing early.

Swap the verb, keep the frame

  • मैं खाना बना रहा हूँ — I am cooking
  • मैं किताब पढ़ रही हूँ — I am reading a book
  • मैं काम कर रहा हूँ — I am working

Same skeleton every time: subject + object + verb stem + raha/rahi + hoon. Hindi puts the verb last, and once that clicks, long sentences stop being frightening.

The one that gets you help

मैं हिंदी सीख रहा हूँ, कृपया धीरे बोलिए — I'm learning Hindi, please speak slowly.

Say that and almost anyone in India will slow down, simplify, and start helping you. It is the most useful sentence in this entire section.

Related

  • मुझे हिंदी नहीं आती (mujhe hindi nahin aati) — I don't know Hindi
  • थोड़ी थोड़ी आती है (thodi thodi aati hai) — I know a little
  • मैं समझ सकता हूँ, बोल नहीं सकता — I can understand, I can't speak. True for most learners for a long time, and worth being able to say.
