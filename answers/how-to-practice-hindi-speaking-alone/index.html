How can I practise speaking Hindi alone?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

You can build a great deal alone: shadowing, recording yourself, narrating your day out loud, and talking to a phone's voice keyboard. What you cannot do alone is find out what you are getting wrong — nothing free can hear you and correct you. The realistic plan is daily solo practice with one weekly session where a person interrupts you.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Things that genuinely work alone

Shadowing. Play a clip, then say the sentence along with it, matching speed and intonation. Two minutes a day rebuilds your mouth's habits faster than an hour of reading. This is the highest-value item on the page.

Narrate your day. Out loud, in Hindi, badly. मैं चाय बना रहा हूँ. मैं बाहर जा रहा हूँ. It sounds ridiculous and it converts passive vocabulary into words you can actually produce.

Record yourself. Sixty seconds on any topic, then listen back. It is uncomfortable and it is the fastest way to hear your own filler words and wrong verb endings.

Dictation to your phone. Set the keyboard to Hindi and speak. If the phone types what you meant, your pronunciation was clear enough. Free, instant, honest feedback on sounds — though not on grammar.

Read aloud. Any Devanagari text. It fixes the script and your mouth at once.

What alone cannot do

You cannot correct an error you cannot hear. Learners plateau on the same handful of mistakes — a wrong gender ending, a flat retroflex ट, को where से belongs — and repeat them for months, becoming fluent at being wrong.

Voice apps do not catch this. A person catches it in ten seconds.

The arrangement that works

Twenty minutes a day alone, one conversation a week with someone who interrupts you. Solo practice builds the material; the session finds the faults. Reverse the ratio and you waste the lesson on things you could have drilled yourself.

If you have nobody yet

Language exchange partners are free and genuinely helpful for confidence. They are usually not teachers, so they will not know why something is wrong — but they will tell you when it is, and at the beginning that is most of the value.
