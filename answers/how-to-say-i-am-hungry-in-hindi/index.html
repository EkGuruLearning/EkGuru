How do you say I am hungry in Hindi?

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Hindi spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

मुझे भूख लगी है (mujhe bhookh lagi hai). Literally that is "to me hunger has attached" — Hindi treats hunger as something that happens to you, not something you are. Saying main bhookha hoon is understood but sounds like a translation.
A note on the Hindi spellings

Devanagari is easy to get slightly wrong when it is typed by hand — a missing matra, the wrong nukta. If something here looks off to you, trust a native speaker over this page and please tell us so it gets fixed. Romanised spellings are a guide to the sound, not a standard.

Why the grammar looks backwards

A whole family of everyday feelings works this way in Hindi: hunger, thirst, cold, fever, even liking something. The person is the recipient, not the subject.

  • मुझे भूख लगी है — I am hungry
  • मुझे प्यास लगी है — I am thirsty
  • मुझे ठंड लग रही है — I am cold

Once you notice the shape, all of them arrive at once.

In a restaurant

You will more often need खाना चाहिए (khaana chahiye) — food is needed. Short, polite, and it works anywhere.
