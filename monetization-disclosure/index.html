Monetization disclosure

Current release status: 20 September 2026

This page separates what is active now from what EkGuru may use later. It is not a promise of earnings, discounts, ad approval or future partnerships.

Active now

Optional support payments

The Support EkGuru page links to a Razorpay-hosted payment page for an optional one-time contribution. Razorpay processes the payment information entered there. A contribution does not purchase course access, book a tutor, create a subscription or improve a learner's ranking.

Tutor lessons

Tutors set the prices displayed in their profiles. EkGuru does not collect tutor-lesson payments in this release. A lesson request introduces the learner to the selected tutor; each party must confirm price, timing, cancellation terms and payment arrangements directly.

Disabled in this release

Advertising

Google AdSense is disabled. Public HTML contains no AdSense loader or ad units, and the runtime release gate is off. EkGuru has not treated its local privacy notice as a Google-certified CMP/TCF deployment. No page—including lessons, tests, forms, booking, payment, legal, contact, search, error, thin or research pages—is approved to load ads in this release.

Affiliate links

No affiliate tracking identifier is configured. Links to tutoring marketplaces, books or tools are ordinary links unless they are explicitly labelled as affiliate links beside the link.

If an approved Preply, Amazon India or selected Impact relationship is configured later, only directly relevant recommendations may use it. Each commercial link or group will carry a clear disclosure. EkGuru will not invent discounts, prices, ratings or personal use, and will not build generic product walls.

Editorial independence

  • Payment, sponsorship or commission does not determine course publication, tutor ranking or country/language claims.
  • Research-only and unpublished course material remains noindex, out of sitemaps and ad-ineligible.
  • Reviews and testimonials must come from attributable records; EkGuru will not create synthetic endorsements.
  • Enabling a provider later requires updated configuration, legal copy, consent testing and production verification first.

Contact and corrections

If a commercial relationship is missing a disclosure or any statement here is inaccurate, use the contact form or email EkGuruLearning@gmail.com.

See also the Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and Terms of Use.
