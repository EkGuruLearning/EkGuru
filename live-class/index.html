How a live class works

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Applies to https://ekguru.shop/ and every page on it.

A live class is a one-to-one video lesson with an independent Hindi tutor. You book it with the tutor, agree the time together, and speak Hindi from the first lesson. This page covers what to do before, during and after a class.

Before you book

  • Read the tutor's profile: level, lesson length, price in US dollars, and the times they are free. Times are shown in Indian Standard Time (IST), so convert them to your own time zone before you choose.
  • You must be 18 or older to book a lesson.
  • Ask the tutor about their cancellation and rescheduling rules before you book. Each tutor sets their own rules.
  • Send a booking request with the button on the tutor's profile. A request is not a confirmed lesson. It becomes one only when the tutor replies and agrees a time with you.

Before the lesson

  • Have a stable internet connection, a device with a working camera and microphone, and a quiet place to sit. Headphones help.
  • Your tutor arranges the video call with you and sends the link. EkGuru does not host the call.
  • Agree a backup with your tutor, such as a phone call or a message, in case the video fails.
  • Agree how you will pay, and what happens if a lesson is missed, before the first lesson.

Payment

EkGuru does not collect payments for lessons. Any payment for a lesson is agreed directly with your tutor. The Support page has an optional one-time contribution through Razorpay, but a contribution does not book a lesson.

If anyone asks you to pay for a lesson through a route you did not agree with your tutor, stop and write to us.

During the lesson

  • Join on time. If you are running late, message your tutor.
  • Speak Hindi as much as you can. Your tutor will correct you gently and adjust the pace to your level.
  • Be respectful to your tutor. Either of you can end a lesson that is disrespectful.
  • Ask your tutor for any practice material they plan to use.

After the lesson

  • A review must come from a real lesson you had with that tutor. Send it through the review form on the tutor's page. A review appears on the profile only after EkGuru has checked it and set it live.
  • If something went wrong, write to us with the tutor's name and the date of the lesson. EkGuru is not a party to the lesson itself, but we can look into what happened.
