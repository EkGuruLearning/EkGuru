How to use this

Most learning sites are a pile of articles with a search box. The problem with a pile is that you cannot tell what you have not covered. A map tells you. If you can see that you have read four pages under "The system" and none under "The four skills", that is a genuinely useful thing to know about your own learning.

Each topic page is written to stand on its own — it is not an index with a sentence at the top. Read it, then follow the links underneath into the specific guides, questions and tools on that topic.

Want a real person to practise with?

Reading gets you the grammar. Speaking is the part that only happens with another human. EkGuru tutors are native Hindi speakers in India who teach one to one over video — no group classes, no subscription.
