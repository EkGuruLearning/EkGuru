Devanagari, letter by letter

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear the words on this page spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

44 letters, why they are ordered the way they are, and how the vowel marks work.

Devanagari is not an alphabet in the English sense. It is an abugida: each consonant already contains a short 'a' sound, so क is 'ka' rather than 'k'. To change that vowel you attach a mark called a matra — का, कि, की, कु. To remove it altogether you add a small stroke underneath, the halant: क्. That single fact explains most beginner confusion about why Hindi words look longer than they sound.

The traditional order is a phonetics lesson in disguise. It begins at the back of the mouth with क ख ग घ ङ, moves forward to the palate with च छ ज झ ञ, then the retroflex ट ठ ड ढ ण made with the tongue curled back, then the dental त थ द ध न against the teeth, and finally the lips with प फ ब भ म. Every group follows the same five-step pattern: plain, aspirated, voiced, voiced-aspirated, nasal. Learn that pattern once in the first group and the other four cost you almost nothing.

Eleven vowels, thirty-three consonants. You will also meet five consonants written with a dot underneath — क़ ख़ ग़ ज़ फ़ — used mostly in words borrowed from Persian, Arabic and English, and conjuncts where two consonants join into one shape. Most learners read simple words within four to six weeks.
