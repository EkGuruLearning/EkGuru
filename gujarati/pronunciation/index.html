Gujarati pronunciation

Murmured vowels, soft છ, rhythm — speak Gujarati like a native.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The signature ળ rules: બાળક (child), શાળા (school), કાળ (time) — tongue back, flap forward. Minimal pairs drill it: કાળ (time) vs કાલ (tomorrow). Master ળ and Gujaratis beam; skip it and they still understand, smiling.

Murmur runs through speech: કહેવું (to say), જવું (to go) — vowels soften mid-word, consonants relax. Gujarati sounds gentle because it IS gentle; harsh crispness marks the foreigner. Soften everything.

Length carries meaning: ઇ vs ઈ, ઉ vs ઊ — short and long are different letters, different words. Double the long ones fully; clip the short ones crisp. Ears measure; mouths must comply.

Rhythm ties it together: ધીમે (slowly) first, then natural speed; મોટેથી (loudly) for clarity, શાંત (quiet) for calm. ઉચ્ચાર (pronunciation) improves with echoing — shadow natives daily, record yourself weekly.

Questions learners ask

How do I pronounce ળ?

The retroflex flap: tongue curls back, flaps forward — બાળક (child), શાળા (school). Hindi speakers must learn it fresh; practise flapping daily.

What are murmured vowels?

Vowels half-swallowed mid-word — કહેવું sounds kahevun, soft and quick. Listen for the murmur; imitate the music.

Do vowel lengths matter?

Long-short pairs: ઇ/ઈ, ઉ/ઊ change words. Hold long vowels fully double — Gujarati ears measure length precisely.
