Emergency Gujarati

મદદ, ડૉક્ટર, હોસ્પિટલ — emergency Gujarati you hope to never need.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The core three save lives: મદદ (help), ભય (danger), તાત્કાલિક મદદ (urgent help). મને મદદ કરો (please help me) — say it to anyone, anywhere. Gujarati crowds respond to distress calls faster than any app; your job is only to ask.

Medical words route the rescue: ડૉક્ટર (doctor), હોસ્પિટલ (hospital), દવા (medicine), મેડિકલ (pharmacy). હોસ્પિટલ ક્યાં છે? (where is the hospital?) plus a taxi wave — in cities, help arrives in minutes.

Symptoms speak simply: અહીં દુખે છે (it hurts here, point!), મને સારું નથી લાગતું (I feel unwell), માથું દુખે છે (headache), તાવ (fever). Pain words plus pointing hands diagnose across every language barrier on earth.

Danger and loss close the kit: આગ (fire), ચોર (thief), પોલીસ (police), ખોવાયું (it's lost), દૂતાવાસ (embassy). Memorise these cold — emergencies never wait for phrasebooks, and ten minutes of drilling buys total peace of mind.

A note on મદદ. મદદ (madad — help) is Gujarati's shortest lifeline — shout it and crowds come running. Gujarat's bystander culture is famously kind: strangers will escort you to hospitals, translate for doctors, and refuse thanks. Ask loudly; receive gratefully.

Questions learners ask

What is the one emergency sentence?

મને મદદ કરો (please help me) — loud, clear, repeated. Add હોસ્પિટલ ક્યાં છે? (where is the hospital?) and Gujarati crowds mobilise instantly.

How do I explain pain?

Point and say અહીં દુખે છે (it hurts here). Then મને સારું નથી લાગતું (I feel unwell) and ડૉક્ટરને બોલાવો (call a doctor). Pain plus pointing crosses every language gap.

How do I report something lost?

ખોવાયું (it is lost) — પૈસા ખોવાયા (money lost), બેગ ખોવાઈ (bag lost). Then પોલીસ સ્ટેશન ક્યાં છે? (where is the police station?).
