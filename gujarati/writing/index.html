Writing Gujarati

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Letters to lists to letters — writing Gujarati from first strokes to full pages.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Writing starts with curves: Gujarati letters round and flow — લખો (please write) circles and hooks before full letters. Trace અ આ ઇ ઈ big and slow; the hand learns shapes the eye already knows.

Tools matter little, mileage matters all: પેન (pen), pencil, finger on steamed mirrors — Gujarati children trace in notebooks, slates and air. Write everywhere; medium is vanity, motion is learning.

Lists graduate the hand: યાદી (list) — નોંધ (notes), સરનામું (addresses), તારીખ (dates). Copy your own નામ (name) fifty times, then friends' names, then street names. Familiar words, fresh script: the fastest fluent hand.

Real writing arrives: પત્ર (letters), અરજી (applications), પરીક્ષા (exams), ગૃહકાર્ય (homework). Write a postcard to yourself in Gujarati — short, joyful, posted. First posted Gujarati is never forgotten.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on સહી. Your સહી (signature) in Gujarati script is a milestone — practise it until it flows in one confident motion. Signing Gujarati-style on forms, cards and letters declares belonging more than any certificate.

Transfer practice. Write three examples from memory, then compare each with the page one character at a time. Correct the shape or mark that changed the meaning instead of rewriting the whole row. A photograph of a real label or notebook can become a useful review prompt, provided you have permission to keep it. Questions learners ask

How do I start writing Gujarati?

Round strokes first: Gujarati letters curve without headline bars — અ, આ, ક, મ. Trace big, shrink slowly; અક્ષર (handwriting) beautifies with mileage.

What should I write first?

યાદી (lists): shopping lists, word lists, daily plans. Lists forgive grammar, demand spelling, and get used — the perfect beginner writing.

When real writing?

પત્ર (letters) to friends, નોંધ (notes) to self, ગૃહકાર્ય (homework) for courses. Real writing to real readers beats copied exercises every time.
