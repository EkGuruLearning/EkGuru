Gujarati sentence structure

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Follow a Gujarati sentence from its subject through the object to the verb; the native examples keep each role visible.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Simple sentences stack three blocks: હું શાળાએ જાઉં છું (I — to-school — go), તે ગીત ગાય છે (she — song — sings), અમે ગુજરાતી શીખીએ છીએ (we — Gujarati — study). Subject, object, verb — build a hundred of these and Gujarati skeleton is yours.

Animals make perfect practice: છોકરો હસે છે (the boy laughs), કૂતરો ભસે છે (the dog barks), બિલાડી સૂવે છે (the cat sleeps). Short subjects, vivid verbs, zero ambiguity — describe every creature you meet and SOV becomes reflex.

Time flexes the frame: હું કાલે આવીશ (I'll come tomorrow), ગઈકાલે વરસાદ પડ્યો (it rained yesterday). Time words float — front for emphasis, middle for flow — while the verb holds the end. Tense plus time word: past, present, future, mastered.

Identity and questions close the set: આ મારું ઘર છે (this is my house), આ તારું પુસ્તક છે? (is this your book?), બધું બરાબર છે (all is well). Equate with આ/તે, question with rising tone, reassure with બધું બરાબર છે — sentences for every situation.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on વરસાદ પડે છે. Gujarati weather does things: વરસાદ પડે છે (rain falls), તડકો પડ્યો છે (sun is out), પવન ફૂંકાય છે (wind blows). No empty it — Gujarati skies act. Weather sentences teach SOV with zero confusion: subject, then verb, done.

Questions learners ask

What is Gujarati word order?

Subject-Object-Verb: હું પુસ્તક વાંચું છું (I book read). Endings mark roles, so order flexes for emphasis — but verb-last is home.

How do tenses work?

Tense lives on the verb: વાંચું છું (read-present), વાંચ્યું (read-past), વાંચીશ (will read). Time words — ગઈકાલે, આજે, કાલે — frame it; the verb carries it.

How do I ask questions?

Rise at the end: આ તારું પુસ્તક છે? (is this your book?). Question words — શું, ક્યાં, કોણ — sit where the answer would.
