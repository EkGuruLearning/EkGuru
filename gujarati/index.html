Questions about this course

Must I learn the Gujarati script before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Gujarati in Roman letters, and let the script come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

નમસ્તે for respect, હા and ના, આભાર, માફ કરજો, સારું, and હું — I. With those seven you can open any interaction in Gujarat politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Gujarati?

The murmured vowels, three-gender verbs — કરું છું, કરે છે — and the soft છ sound. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each one.
