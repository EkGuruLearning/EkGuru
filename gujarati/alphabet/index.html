Gujarati alphabet

Devanagari's headline-free cousin — vowels, consonants and barakshari.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Vowels anchor everything: અ આ ઇ ઈ ઉ ઊ એ ઐ ઓ ઔ અં અઃ — thirteen pure sounds. Long-short pairs matter — ઇ vs ઈ changes meaning. Chant them in order daily; the mouth memorises what the ear repeats.

Consonants march in families: ક ખ ગ ઘ (throat), ચ છ જ ઝ (palate), ટ ઠ ડ ઢ (roof), ત થ દ ધ (teeth), પ ફ બ ભ (lips). Family by family, feel where each sound lives — Gujarati pronunciation is geography you can touch.

Special letters crown the set: ય ર લ વ શ ષ સ હ ળ ક્ષ જ્ઞ — with ળ (retroflex L) shared with Marathi. શાળા (school) shows ળ in action; જ્ઞાન (knowledge) shows જ્ઞ. Special letters, everyday words.

Barakshari grids it all: ક કા કિ કી કુ કૂ કે કૈ કો કૌ કં કઃ — each consonant through every vowel. One grid mastered teaches all grids. Read barakshari aloud for a month; Gujarati script becomes a friend for life.

Questions learners ask

Is Gujarati script like Hindi?

Close cousins — Gujarati script drops Devanagari's top headline bar and rounds the letters. Hindi readers recognise most shapes instantly.

What is barakshari?

The vowel-consonant grid: ક કા કિ કી કુ કૂ કે કૈ કો કૌ — every consonant marries every vowel in order. Master one row fully and the whole grid unlocks.

Which letters first?

Vowels first: અ આ ઇ ઈ ઉ ઊ એ ઐ ઓ ઔ — then ક ખ ગ ઘ ચ છ જ ઝ. Sounds before shapes; reading follows speaking.
