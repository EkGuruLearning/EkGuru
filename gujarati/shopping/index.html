Shopping and bargaining in Gujarati

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

કિંમત, ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ, રંગ — shop in Gujarati from first price to final deal.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Prices open every transaction: આની કિંમત શું? (what's this one's price?), કેટલા રૂપિયા? (how many rupees?). Listen for the number, repeat it back — પચાસ? (fifty?) — and you are already shopping in Gujarati.

Bargaining follows the friendly script: ઘણું મોંઘું છે (too costly), ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ આપો (give a discount), થોડું ઓછું કરો (reduce a little). Start walking away slowly — સારું, જોઉં છું (okay, I'll look around) — and the real price often chases you down the street.

Sizes and colours refine the hunt: મોટું આપો (give the big one), નાનું આપો (give the small one), નવું બતાવો (show a new one), બીજો રંગ (another colour). Point, name the change, nod — Gujarati shopkeepers read gestures fluently and reward attempts instantly.

Colours close the sale: લાલ (red), ભૂરો (blue), લીલો (green), પીળો (yellow), સફેદ (white), કાળો (black). લાલ સરસ લાગે છે (the red looks nice) — then લઈશ (I'll take it). Deal done, in Gujarati, with a smile on both sides.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on થોડું ઓછું કરો. થોડું ઓછું કરો (thodun ochhun karo — please reduce a little) is the magic sentence of Gujarat's markets. Said smiling, it opens every negotiation; said with લઈશ (I'll take it) right after, it closes most. Bargaining respects the dance, never the demand.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask the price?

આની કિંમત શું? (what's the price of this?) — then ફક્ત કેટલા? (just how much?) for everything else. Two questions, every shop in Gujarat.

How do I bargain politely?

ઘણું મોંઘું છે (too costly) with a smile, then થોડું ઓછું કરો (reduce a little). Bargaining is friendly theatre — play your part warmly and the price moves.

Which colours first?

Start with five: લાલ (red), ભૂરો (blue), લીલો (green), પીળો (yellow), સફેદ (white). બીજો રંગ બતાવો (show another colour) reveals the rest.
