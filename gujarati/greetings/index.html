Saying hello the Gujarati way

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

નમસ્તે and beyond — greet Gujarat with warmth from your first word.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Greetings open every door: નમસ્તે (respectful hello), જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ (devout hello), હેલો (casual hi). Fold your hands, say નમસ્તે, and Gujarat opens — bazaars, businesses, homes. One word, infinite welcome.

Health-questions show you care: તમે કેમ છો? (how are you, formal), હું મજામાં છું (I'm fine), અને તમે? (and you?). Gujarati small talk runs on genuine enquiry — ask properly, listen properly, belong quickly.

Time splits the day: શુભ સવાર (good morning), શુભ બપોર (good afternoon), શુભ સાંજ (good evening), શુભ રાત્રિ (good night). Morning greetings before noon, રાત્રિ only at parting — timing your શુભ shows cultural fluency.

Partings promise return: આવજો (goodbye, come again), ફરી મળીશું (we'll meet again), કાળજી રાખજો (take care). Gujaratis rarely say goodbye without a next meeting — આવજો assumes friendship continues. Say it warmly; mean it always.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on નમસ્તે. This is a respectful Gujarati greeting. Usage varies by age, region and setting; listen to local speakers and mirror their level of formality.

Questions learners ask

How do I say hello respectfully?

નમસ્તે with folded hands — respectful everywhere, from Ahmedabad markets to village homes. Casual હેલો works with friends; નમસ્તે never fails.

How do I ask how someone is?

તમે કેમ છો? (formal) or તું કેમ છે? (informal) — and answer હું મજામાં છું, આભાર (I'm fine, thanks). Health-questions open Gujarati hearts.

What are the time greetings?

શુભ સવાર (good morning), શુભ બપોર (good afternoon), શુભ સાંજ (good evening), શુભ રાત્રિ (good night). Time-greetings show care; use them generously.
