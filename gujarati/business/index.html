Gujarati for work and business

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Meetings, customers, contracts — professional Gujarati for offices.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Workplaces run on roles: નોકરી (job), ઓફિસ (office), બોસ (boss), ગ્રાહક (customer), સહકર્મી (colleague). મારી નોકરી (my job is) introduces your profession; ઓફિસ ક્યાં છે? finds any office. Roles first, tasks second.

Meetings move business: મીટિંગ (meeting), કરાર (contract/deal), સહી (signature). મીટિંગ કાલે સવારે (meeting tomorrow morning) — confirm with સારું, આવીશ (okay, I'll come). Gujarati meetings open with નમસ્તે and close with આભાર.

Money talks plainly: નફો (profit), ખોટ (loss), પગાર (salary), રજા (leave). પગાર ક્યારે થાય છે? (when is payday?) — asked smiling, everywhere. Business Gujarati respects directness wrapped in politeness.

Digital tools connect all: ઈમેલ (email), ફોન (phone), મેસેજ (message). ઈમેલ જોજો (please check email) — then સફળતા (success) follows effort. Professional Gujarati: formal words, warm heart.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on ગ્રાહક. ગ્રાહક (graahak — customer) rules Gujarati business — ગ્રાહક જ રાજા (the customer is king). Master it syllable by syllable; clients notice the effort and reward it.

Transfer practice. Practise one workplace request as a complete message: name the task, make the request, and confirm the next step. Keep a respectful opening and closing. Questions learners ask

How do I introduce myself at work?

નમસ્તે, હું (hello, I'm) — then તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો (pleased to meet you). Business Gujarati opens formal and warms slowly.

How do I ask about meetings?

મીટિંગ ક્યારે છે? (when is the meeting?) and મીટિંગ ક્યાં છે? (where?). Meetings run Gujarati offices — never miss one for language reasons.

Email and phone in Gujarati?

ઈમેલ મોકલીશ (I'll send an email) and ફોન પર વાત કરીએ (let's talk on the phone). Digital Gujarati borrows freely — clarity beats purity in business.
