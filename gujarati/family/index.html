Family words in Gujarati

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

મા, બાપા, બા, દાદા — family words that open Gujarati homes.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Parents anchor all: મા (mother), બાપા (father) — મારી મા (my mother), મારા બાપા (my father). Gujarati children chant મા first; learners should too. Home begins here.

Siblings share childhood: ભાઈ (brother), બહેન (sister), મોટાભાઈ (elder brother), મોટીબહેન (elder sister). મોટા marks respect for age — birth order lives in address. Call elders rightly; love follows.

Grandparents crown memory: બા (grandmother), દાદા (grandfather) — story-keepers, sweet-givers, wisdom-wells. મારા બા સરસ વાર્તા કહે છે (my grandmother tells lovely stories) — every Gujarati smiles. Honour them in speech.

The household gathers: કુટુંબ (family), ઘર (home), લગ્ન (wedding), બાળક (child). મારું કુટુંબ મોટું છે (my family is big) — then listen; Gujaratis narrate families with epic joy. Share yours; hear theirs.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on બા. બા (baa — grandmother/mother) rules Gujarati hearts — બાની વાર્તાઓ (grandmother's stories) raised generations. Learn બા and દાદા (grandfather); elders glow when learners speak family. Respect the roots; the tree welcomes you.

Transfer practice. Choose one real relationship and practise the matching term in a short introduction. Family titles can carry age, affection, respect, and local habit, so they do not always map neatly onto English labels. When unsure, listen to how the family addresses one another and ask before using an intimate form. Start from મા (maa) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Context check. For Family words, “Wedding / marriage” belongs to a particular setting. A common mistake is to treat an English gloss as universal; read the language-specific note above and ask a fluent speaker when local usage differs.

Questions learners ask

Which family words first?

મા (mother), બાપા (father), ભાઈ (brother), બહેન (sister). Family first — Gujaratis ask about yours within minutes.

Extended family?

કાકા (uncle), કાકી (aunt), બા (grandmother), દાદા (grandfather). Extended family, extended vocabulary.

How do I describe my family?

મારું કુટુંબ મોટું છે (my family is big) — મારી મા શિક્ષક છે (my mother is a teacher). Describe yours; hearts open.
