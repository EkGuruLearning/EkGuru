Talking about relationships in Gujarati

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

Friends, lovers, weddings — the Gujarati of hearts, spoken truly.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Friendship opens hearts: મિત્ર (male friend), બહેનપણી (female friend) — મારો મિત્ર (my friend) introduces proudly. Gujarati friendships run lifelong and deep; મૈત્રી (friendship) is celebrated in poetry, films and daily chai. Make one Gujarati friend and gain a hundred phrases.

Romance speaks softly: પ્રેમી (boyfriend), પ્રેમિકા (girlfriend), પ્રેમ (love). Gujarati romance favours poetry over bluntness — film songs teach the register better than textbooks. Learn gently; speak truly.

Weddings unite families: લગ્ન (wedding spoken), વિવાહ (wedding formal), વરરાજા (groom), કન્યા (bride). Gujarati weddings run for days — garba, food, rituals, blessings. લગ્નની શુભેચ્છાઓ (wedding congratulations) is your ticket to the feast.

Bonds deepen and mend: પ્રેમ (love), લાગણી (affection), જીવન (life) — મારું જીવન (my life) means my beloved. And when bonds strain: માફી (forgiveness), શાંતિ (peace). Gujarati hearts forgive spaciously; ask with folded hands.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on પ્રેમ vs હેત. પ્રેમ (prem) is broad love — family, friends, humanity; હેત (het) is tender affection. તમારા પ્રેમ બદલ આભાર (thanks for your love) suits everyone; હેત belongs to dear ones. Gujarati loves precisely.

Transfer practice. Practise a respectful way to introduce a person or describe a relationship, then check whether the form suits the setting. Terms of affection and respect are personal as well as linguistic. Avoid assuming that a phrase that works among close friends belongs in every family or public conversation. Start from પ્રેમી (premi) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Questions learners ask

Friendship words first?

મિત્ર (male friend), બહેનપણી (female friend) — then પ્રેમ (love) and લાગણી (affection) as bonds deepen. Friendship words first; romance follows naturally.

How do I talk about weddings?

લગ્ન (spoken wedding), વિવાહ (formal wedding) — વરરાજા (groom), કન્યા (bride). લગ્નની શુભેચ્છાઓ (wedding greetings) blesses every couple.

How do I apologise deeply?

માફી (forgiveness) — મને માફ કરજો (please forgive me), then શાંતિ (peace). Gujarati reconciliations run deep and warm; ask sincerely, receive fully.
