Gujarati slang

જોરદાર, ભાઈબંધ, એક નંબર — street Gujarati that fun runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Praise slang opens doors: જોરદાર (awesome), એક નંબર (number one), બહુ જોરદાર (very awesome), રાડો (rocking), કડક (solid). Food, films, friends' outfits — praise everything loudly. Gujaratis praise generously; learners should too.

Friendship words bond crews: ભાઈ (bro), ભાઈબંધ (close friend), દોસ્ત (buddy) — friends-only, always warm. ભાઈ, આવ ને (come on bro) starts a thousand conversations daily. Never aim them upward at elders; sideways at friends, fire freely.

Reaction words colour stories: અરેરે (oh no!), વાહ (wow!), ગપ્પાં ન માર (stop making things up!). Gujarati storytelling runs on reactions — gasp, laugh, disbelieve theatrically. Flat faces kill stories; big reactions feed them.

Playful edges season everything: ચલ નીકળ (get lost — playful among friends, rude otherwise), છી (mild disgust), ટાઈમપાસ (chilling). Learn the edges, respect the edges: slang's power is knowing exactly where fun ends.

A note on ટાઈમપાસ. ટાઈમપાસ (timepass) defies translation — chilling, hanging out, passing time joyfully. શું કરે છે? — ટાઈમપાસ (what are you doing? — timepass). Whole philosophies hide in two syllables; use it freely, smile always.

Questions learners ask

What slang is safe everywhere?

જોરદાર (awesome) and એક નંબર (number one) — positive, universal, safe. Sprinkle them on food, films and friends; instant insider status.

Can I call people bhai?

ભાઈ (bro) and ભાઈબંધ (close friend) — for friends only, never elders or strangers. Friendship slang misused offends; rightly used, it bonds instantly.

What does timepass mean?

Hanging out, passing time joyfully — શું ચાલે છે? — કંઈ નહીં, ટાઈમપાસ (what's up? — nothing, timepass). The most Gujarati answer in existence: relaxed joy, declared proudly.
