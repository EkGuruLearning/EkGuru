Core Gujarati vocabulary

પાણી, ઘર, કામ, પ્રેમ — the words Gujarati daily life runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Gujarati spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Survival nouns anchor reality: પાણી (water), ખાવાનું (food), ઘર (house), રસ્તો (road). Point at each, say it aloud, use it today — પાણી જોઈએ છે (I want water). Concrete first, abstract later.

People words warm speech: માણસ (person), મિત્ર (friend), મા (mother), બાળક (child). મારો મિત્ર (my friend) introduces proudly; મા calls home. People before things, always.

Describing words add colour: મોટું (big), નાનું (small), સારું (good), નવું (new). Adjectives dance with gender — મોટો ભાઈ (big brother), મોટી બહેન (big sister). Triple-learn each one.

Time and feeling complete the core: આજે (today), પ્રેમ (love), આનંદ (joy), કામ (work). આજે ઘણું કામ છે (there's lots of work today) — then પ્રેમ અને આનંદ balance it. Words for living, not just talking.

A note on સરસ. સરસ (saras — nice/great) is Gujarat's universal praise — સરસ છે (it's nice), ખૂબ સરસ (very nice). Food, films, friends' work — praise everything. Generous સરસ earns generous friendship.

Questions learners ask

Which words give fastest fluency?

Body, home, food, people: પાણી (water), ઘર (house), મા (mother), મિત્ર (friend). Concrete nouns first — point, name, own.

How do adjectives work?

Adjectives agree with gender: સારો (good-m), સારી (f), સારું (n). Learn triples together — agreement becomes music.

Essential time words?

આજે (today), કાલે (tomorrow), ગઈકાલે (yesterday), હમણાં (now). Time words frame every plan — learn them this week.
