Languages of Pakistan

The languages of Pakistan at a glance: English, Sindhi, Urdu are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Western Panjabi, Saraiki, Northern Pashto, Balochi. Linguists count roughly 75 living languages in Pakistan; this guide lists the major ones individually.
