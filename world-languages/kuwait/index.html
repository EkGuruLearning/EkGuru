Languages of Kuwait

The languages of Kuwait at a glance: Standard Arabic is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Gulf Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Filipino. Linguists count roughly 18 living languages in Kuwait; this guide lists the major ones individually.
