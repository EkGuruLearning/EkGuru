Languages of Saudi Arabia

The languages of Saudi Arabia at a glance: Standard Arabic is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Najdi Arabic, Hejazi Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Filipino. Linguists count roughly 20 living languages in Saudi Arabia; this guide lists the major ones individually.
