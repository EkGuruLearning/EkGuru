Languages of Iran

The languages of Iran at a glance: Persian is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are South Azerbaijani, Central Kurdish (Sorani), Southern Kurdish, Gilaki, Mazanderani. Linguists count roughly 75 living languages in Iran; this guide lists the major ones individually.
