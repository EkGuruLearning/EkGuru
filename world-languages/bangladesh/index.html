Languages of Bangladesh

The languages of Bangladesh at a glance: Bengali is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Rohingya, Chakma, Urdu, Marma, Rakhine. Linguists count roughly 40 living languages in Bangladesh; this guide lists the major ones individually.
