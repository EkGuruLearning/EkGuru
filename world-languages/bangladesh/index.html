Languages of Bangladesh

The languages of Bangladesh at a glance: Bengali is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Rohingya, Chakma, Urdu, Marma, Rakhine. Linguists count roughly 40 living languages in Bangladesh; this guide lists the major ones individually.

Bangladeshi Sign Language
Sign Language		misunknown—No distinct ISO 639-3 code; related to Indian/Pakistani signing (shared ISL-family). Whether BdSL is a distinct language vs ISL dialect is disputed — do not code as 'ins'. Verify pass 2 (WFD). Separately-coded wbs is the Kolkata/India-side variety, not BdSL — BdSL itself remains uncoded. Pass 2 2026-09-14 [sil-iso639-3-tab-2026]: SIL 639-3 table confirms genuinely uncoded; SIL wbs (West Bengal SL) is IN-side, not BD. code-dispute RESOLVED, confidence stays low; WFD/deaf-org consultation still pending (report §5.3).
