Languages of Tajikistan

The languages of Tajikistan at a glance: Tajik is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Uzbek, Shughni, Russian, Yaghnobi. Linguists count roughly 30 living languages in Tajikistan; this guide lists the major ones individually.

More languages spoken in Tajikistan (below listing threshold)
