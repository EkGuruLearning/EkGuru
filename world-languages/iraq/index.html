Languages of Iraq

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-11-10. Editorial policy and corrections.

The languages of Iraq at a glance: Central Kurdish (Sorani), Northern Kurdish (Kurmanji), Standard Arabic are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Mesopotamian Arabic, South Azerbaijani (Iraqi Turkmen), Assyrian Neo-Aramaic. Linguists count roughly 28 living languages in Iraq; this guide lists the major ones individually.
