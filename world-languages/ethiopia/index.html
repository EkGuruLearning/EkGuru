Languages of Ethiopia

The languages of Ethiopia at a glance: Afar, Amharic, Oromo, Somali, Tigrinya are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Sidamo, Wolaytta. Linguists count roughly 90 living languages in Ethiopia; this guide lists the major ones individually.
