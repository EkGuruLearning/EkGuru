Languages of Georgia

The languages of Georgia at a glance: Georgian is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Mingrelian, Azerbaijani, Armenian, Abkhaz, Russian. Linguists count roughly 12 living languages in Georgia; this guide lists the major ones individually.
