Languages of Afghanistan

The languages of Afghanistan at a glance: Dari, Pashto is the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Southern Uzbek, Turkmen, Balochi, Pashayi languages. Linguists count roughly 40 living languages in Afghanistan; this guide lists the major ones individually.

Nuristani languages
Indigenous		mis≈130k L1—Covers: ask, bsh, prn, trm, wbk. Constitution names one 'Nuristani'; actually a BRANCH (Ashkun ask, Kamkata-viri bsh, Prasuni prn, Tregami trm, Waigali wbk). Group entry per METHOD, not a language.
Pamiri languages
Indigenous		mis≈60k L1—Covers: sgh, wbl, mnj, ydg, isk, sgy. Constitution names one 'Pamiri'; Eastern Iranian cluster (Shughni sgh, Wakhi wbl, Munji mnj, Yidgha ydg, Ishkashimi isk, Sanglechi sgy). Below threshold individually; constitutionally named so listed as group.
Pashayi languages
Indigenous		mis≈400k L1—Covers: aee, glh, psi, psh. Constitution names one 'Pashayi'; ISO splits four (NE aee, NW glh, SE psi, SW psh). Dardic group, Laghman/Kapisa/Kunar valleys.
