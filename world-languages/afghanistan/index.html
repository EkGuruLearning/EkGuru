Languages of Afghanistan

The languages of Afghanistan at a glance: Dari, Pashto is the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Southern Uzbek, Turkmen, Balochi, Pashayi languages. Linguists count roughly 40 living languages in Afghanistan; this guide lists the major ones individually.
