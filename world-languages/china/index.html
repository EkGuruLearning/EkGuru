Languages of China

The languages of China at a glance: Cantonese, Kazakh, Korean, Mandarin, Peripheral Mongolian, Tibetan, Uyghur, Zhuang are among the official languages. Linguists count roughly 300 living languages in China; this guide lists the major ones individually.
