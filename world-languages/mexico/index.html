Languages of Mexico

The languages of Mexico at a glance: Huastec, Huautla Mazatec, Isthmus Zapotec, Mezquital Otomi, Mixtec, Nahuatl, Papantla Totonac, Purepecha, Spanish, Tzeltal, Tzotzil, Yucatec Maya are among the official languages. Linguists count roughly 300 living languages in Mexico; this guide lists the major ones individually.
