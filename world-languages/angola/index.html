Languages of Angola

The languages of Angola at a glance: Chokwe, Kimbundu, Kongo, Kwanyama, Nyemba, Portuguese, Umbundu are among the official languages. Linguists count roughly 50 living languages in Angola; this guide lists the major ones individually.
