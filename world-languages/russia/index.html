Languages of Russia

The languages of Russia at a glance: Adyghe, Bashkir, Buryat, Chechen, Chuvash, Dargwa, Ingush, Kabardian, Kalmyk, Komi-Zyrian, Lezgian, Meadow Mari, Ossetic, Russian, Tatar, Tuvan, Udmurt, Yakut are among the official languages. Linguists count roughly 150 living languages in Russia; this guide lists the major ones individually.
