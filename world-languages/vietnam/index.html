Languages of Vietnam

The languages of Vietnam at a glance: Vietnamese is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Tay (Tày), Muong, Khmer, Nung, Tai Dam (Black Tai). Linguists count roughly 110 living languages in Vietnam; this guide lists the major ones individually.
