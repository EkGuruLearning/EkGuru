Languages of India

The languages of India at a glance: Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri (Meitei), Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi (Eastern), Sanskrit, Santali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu are among the official languages. Linguists count roughly 450 living languages in India; this guide lists the major ones individually.

NationalOfficial · official regional		kok≈2.3M L1Deva8th Schedule; official in Goa (Devanagari script mandated). Macrolanguage 'kok' (individual: gom Goan Konkani). Five-script tradition (Devanagari/Roman/Kannada/Malayalam/Perso-Arabic) — script note essential.

Odia

NationalOfficial · official regional		ory≈38M L1Orya8th Schedule; official in Odisha. Classical language. Renamed Oriya→Odia (2011); code 'ory' unchanged.
Punjabi (Eastern)

NationalOfficial · official regional		pan≈33M L1Guru8th Schedule; official in Punjab (+ Delhi additional). Gurmukhi script. Eastern Punjabi (pan) vs Western Punjabi/Lahnda (pnb, Pakistan) — distinct codes, distinct standards; cross-border note, no merge.
Sanskrit
संस्कृतम्
National · HistoricalOfficial · official regional · Historical		san≈25k L1Deva8th Schedule; 2nd-official in Uttarakhand (+ Himachal). Census L1 (~25k) is symbolic self-report, not a speech community — liturgical/scholarly role is the honest frame. Classical language. SIL type=H Historical (registry fact); 25k L1 + Uttarakhand official status are living-use overlays, honestly framed above.
Santali
ᱥᱟᱱᱛᱟᱲᱤ
National		sat≈7.3M L1Olck8th Schedule (2003); additional-official in Jharkhand. Only Munda language in the Schedule; Ol Chiki script. Largest tribal language of India.
Sindhi
سنڌي
National		snd≈2.7M L1Arab8th Schedule; official NOWHERE in India (partition dispersal) — the Schedule's only stateless major language. Perso-Arabic + Devanagari scripts.
Tamil
தமிழ்
NationalOfficial · official regional		tam≈69M L1Taml8th Schedule; official in Tamil Nadu (+ Puducherry). First-declared Classical language (2004).
Telugu
తెలుగు
NationalOfficial · official regional		tel≈81M L1Telu8th Schedule; official in Andhra Pradesh + Telangana. Classical language.
Urdu
اردو
NationalOfficial · official regional		urd≈51M L1Arab8th Schedule; additional-official in UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, Telangana, West Bengal, Delhi. Hindi–Urdu: one grammar continuum, two literary standards — teach together with script notes, never merge silently.
