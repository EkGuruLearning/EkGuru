Languages of United Arab Emirates

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: last editorial date not recorded. Editorial policy and corrections.

The languages of United Arab Emirates at a glance: Standard Arabic is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Hindi, Gulf Arabic, Malayalam, Filipino, Bengali. Linguists count roughly 25 living languages in United Arab Emirates; this guide lists the major ones individually.
