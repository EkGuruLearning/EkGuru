Languages of Kazakhstan

The languages of Kazakhstan at a glance: Kazakh, Russian are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Uzbek, Uyghur, Tatar, Azerbaijani. Linguists count roughly 44 living languages in Kazakhstan; this guide lists the major ones individually.

Uyghur

Regional		uig≈250k L1ArabConcentrated Jetisu/Almaty region (Zharkent/Panfilov). SIL Ref_Name keeps old spelling 'Uighur'. Soviet-era Cyrillic publishing, now mostly Perso-Arabic; exile-Latin exists — script choice is political, note it.
Uzbek

Regional		uzn≈600k L1LatnConcentrated south (Turkestan region, Shymkent). Code 'uzn' = NORTHERN Uzbek (SIL identity audit 2026-09-14 — not uzs). Latin in principle, Cyrillic common in practice.
