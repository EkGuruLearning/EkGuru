Languages of Nepal

The languages of Nepal at a glance: Bhojpuri, Maithili, Nepali are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Nepali, Nepali, Nepali, Maithili, Maithili. Linguists count roughly 124 living languages in Nepal; this guide lists the major ones individually.
