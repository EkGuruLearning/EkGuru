Languages of Syria

The languages of Syria at a glance: Standard Arabic is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Levantine Arabic, Mesopotamian Arabic, Northern Kurdish (Kurmanji). Linguists count roughly 20 living languages in Syria; this guide lists the major ones individually.
