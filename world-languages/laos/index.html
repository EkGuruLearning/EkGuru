Languages of Laos

The languages of Laos at a glance: Lao is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Khmu, Hmong Njua (Green Mong), White Hmong, Phu Thai. Linguists count roughly 80 living languages in Laos; this guide lists the major ones individually.
