Languages of Myanmar

The languages of Myanmar at a glance: Burmese is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Shan, S'gaw Karen, Rakhine (Arakanese), Chin languages, Jingpho (Kachin). Linguists count roughly 100 living languages in Myanmar; this guide lists the major ones individually.
