Languages of Philippines

The languages of Philippines at a glance: English, Filipino are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Cebuano, Ilokano, Hiligaynon, Bikol, Waray. Linguists count roughly 180 living languages in Philippines; this guide lists the major ones individually.
