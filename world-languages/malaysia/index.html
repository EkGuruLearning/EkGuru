Languages of Malaysia

The languages of Malaysia at a glance: Standard Malay is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Hokkien (Min Nan), Tamil, Cantonese (Yue), Hakka, Iban. Linguists count roughly 130 living languages in Malaysia; this guide lists the major ones individually.
