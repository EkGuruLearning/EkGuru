Languages of Sri Lanka

The languages of Sri Lanka at a glance: Sinhala, Tamil is the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Sri Lankan Creole Malay, Vedda. Linguists count roughly 8 living languages in Sri Lanka; this guide lists the major ones individually.
