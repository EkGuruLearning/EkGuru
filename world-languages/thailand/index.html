Languages of Thailand

The languages of Thailand at a glance: Thai is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Northeastern Thai (Isan), Northern Thai (Lanna), Southern Thai (Dambro), Pattani Malay, Northern Khmer. Linguists count roughly 70 living languages in Thailand; this guide lists the major ones individually.
