Languages of Qatar

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: last editorial date not recorded. Editorial policy and corrections.

The languages of Qatar at a glance: Standard Arabic is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Hindi, Nepali, Bengali, Gulf Arabic, Urdu. Linguists count roughly 20 living languages in Qatar; this guide lists the major ones individually.
