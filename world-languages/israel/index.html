Languages of Israel

The languages of Israel at a glance: Hebrew, Standard Arabic are among the official languages. The largest mother tongues here are Levantine Arabic, Russian, Eastern Yiddish, Amharic. Linguists count roughly 35 living languages in Israel; this guide lists the major ones individually.
