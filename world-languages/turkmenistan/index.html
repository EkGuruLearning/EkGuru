Languages of Turkmenistan

Author: not separately recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: last editorial date not recorded. Editorial policy and corrections.

The languages of Turkmenistan at a glance: Turkmen is the official language. The largest mother tongues here are Uzbek, Russian. Linguists count roughly 32 living languages in Turkmenistan; this guide lists the major ones individually.
