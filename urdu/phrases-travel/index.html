Travel phrases in Urdu

ٹکٹ, اسٹیشن, کہاں؟ — travel by train, bus and word.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Stations start journeys: ریلوے اسٹیشن (railway station), بس اڈا (bus stand), ہوائی اڈا (airport). اسٹیشن کہاں ہے؟ (where is the station?) — ask anyone; people direct travellers with pride.

Tickets buy passage: ٹکٹ (ticket) — ایک ٹکٹ دیجیے (give one ticket), ٹکٹ کتنے کا؟ (how much?). گاڑی کب آئے گی؟ (when will the train come?) — گاڑی means both train and car; context steers.

Directions decode cities: دائیں (right), بائیں (left), سیدھا (straight), نزدیک (near), دور (far). سیدھا جائیے، پھر دائیں (go straight, then right) — follow, arrive, thank.

Stays shelter nights: ہوٹل (hotel), کمرہ (room), پتہ (address). کمرہ کتنے کا؟ (how much for a room?) — bargain smiling, sleep soundly. سفر ends in rest; rest remakes travellers.

A note on سفر. سفر (safar — journey) is pure romance — ریل کا سفر (train journey) through plains and old cities, winter fog on windows. سفر بخیر (happy journey) blesses every traveller. Journey well; arrive storied.

Questions learners ask

How do I find stations?

ریلوے اسٹیشن کہاں ہے؟ (where is the railway station?) — swap in بس اڈا, ہوائی اڈا. Where-questions navigate nations.

How do I buy tickets?

ٹکٹ کتنے کا؟ (how much is the ticket?) and ایک ٹکٹ دیجیے (give one ticket). Tickets first, journeys after.

Left, right, straight?

دائیں (right), بائیں (left), سیدھا (straight). دائیں مڑیے (turn right) — direct and be directed.
