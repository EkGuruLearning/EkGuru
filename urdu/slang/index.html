Urdu slang

Written by: authorship not independently recorded. Maintained by Prakash. Reviewed by: not recorded. Updated: 2026-10-04. Editorial policy and corrections.

زبردست, یار, ایک نمبر — street Urdu that fun runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Praise slang opens doors: زبردست (awesome), ایک نمبر (number one), چھا گئے (you rock), فرسٹ کلاس (first class), کمال (wonderful). Food, films, friends' outfits — praise everything loudly. Urdu speakers praise generously; learners should too.

Friendship words bond crews: یار (buddy), بھائی (bro), دوست (friend) — friends-only, always warm. یار، آؤ نا (come on buddy) starts a thousand conversations daily. Never aim them upward at elders; sideways at friends, fire freely.

Reaction words colour stories: ہائے اللہ (oh my God!), واہ جی واہ (wow!), گپیں نہ مارو (stop making things up!). Urdu storytelling runs on reactions — gasp, laugh, disbelieve theatrically. Flat faces kill stories; big reactions feed them.

Playful edges season everything: چل نکل (get lost — playful among friends, rude otherwise), چھی (mild disgust), ٹائم پاس (chilling). Learn the edges, respect the edges: slang's power is knowing exactly where fun ends.

Language, culture, and common traps

A note on چھا گئے. چھا گئے (chhaa gaye — you've overshadowed everyone, you rock!) is the ultimate praise — shouted at brilliant performances, clever jokes, winning goals. Praise loudly; belong instantly.

Transfer practice. Before repeating a slang expression, identify who said it, to whom, and in what mood. Practise recognising the phrase before trying it yourself. Slang can sound friendly in one group and rude in another; avoid using it with elders, customers, or strangers until a trusted speaker confirms the setting. Start from زبردست (zabardast) and decide whether it fits the situation before using it elsewhere.

Context check. For Urdu slang, “Stop making things up” belongs to a particular setting. A common mistake is to treat an English gloss as universal; read the language-specific note above and ask a fluent speaker when local usage differs.

Questions learners ask

What slang is safe everywhere?

زبردست (awesome) and ایک نمبر (number one) — positive, universal, safe. Sprinkle them on food, films and friends; instant insider status.

Can I call people yaar?

یار (buddy) and بھائی (bro) — for friends only, never elders or strangers. Friendship slang misused offends; rightly used, it bonds instantly.

What does timepass mean?

Hanging out, passing time joyfully — کیا چل رہا ہے؟ — کچھ نہیں، ٹائم پاس (what's up? — nothing, timepass). The most casual answer in existence: relaxed joy, declared proudly.
