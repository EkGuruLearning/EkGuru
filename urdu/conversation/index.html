Urdu

Real Urdu conversation

میں, آپ, ہاں, نہیں — hold your first real Urdu conversations.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

People anchor talk: میں (I), آپ (you formal), وہ (he/she/they). میں اردو سیکھتا ہوں (I'm learning Urdu) — gender shows on verbs, proudly. Introduce yourself; conversations ignite.

Questions drive dialogue: کیا؟ (what?), کہاں؟ (where?), کب؟ (when?), کیوں؟ (why?), کیسے؟ (how?). آپ کہاں رہتے ہیں؟ (where do you live?) — one question word plus any verb equals conversation.

Politeness oils everything: براہ کرم (please) and معاف کیجیے (sorry/excuse me). معاف کیجیے، آہستہ بولیے (sorry, speak slowly) — apologies plus requests, the learner's armour.

Repair keeps talk alive: مجھے سمجھ نہیں آیا (I didn't understand), پھر کہیے (please say again), اس کا مطلب کیا ہے؟ (what does this mean?). Broken Urdu beats silent perfection — stumble forward daily.

Questions learners ask

How do I ask someone's name?

آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (what's your name?) — answer میرا نام راہل ہے (my name is Rahul). Names first, friendship follows.

Yes and no in Urdu?

ہاں (yes), نہیں (no) — short, clear, universal. جی ہاں adds respect; نا is casual. Yes-no fluency first.

When I don't understand?

مجھے سمجھ نہیں آیا (I didn't understand) and آہستہ بولیے (please speak slowly). Honesty tunes every speaker to your level.
