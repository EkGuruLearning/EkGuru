Family words in Urdu

ماں, ابا, دادی, دادا — family words that open homes.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Parents anchor all: ماں (mother), ابا (father) — میری ماں (my mother), میرے ابا (my father). Children chant ماں first; learners should too. Home begins here.

Siblings share childhood: بھائی (brother), بہن (sister), بڑا بھائی (elder brother), بڑی بہن (elder sister). بڑا marks respect for age — birth order lives in address. Call elders rightly; love follows.

Grandparents crown memory: دادی (grandmother), دادا (grandfather) — story-keepers, sweet-givers, wisdom-wells. میری دادی پیاری کہانی سناتی ہے (my grandmother tells lovely stories) — everyone smiles. Honour them in speech.

The household gathers: خاندان (family), گھر (home), شادی (wedding), بچہ (child). میرا خاندان بڑا ہے (my family is big) — then listen; people narrate families with epic joy. Share yours; hear theirs.

A note on دادی. دادی (daadi — grandmother) rules hearts — دادی کی کہانیاں (grandmother's stories) raised generations. Learn دادی and دادا (grandfather); elders glow when learners speak family. Respect the roots; the tree welcomes you.

Questions learners ask

Which family words first?

ماں (mother), ابا (father), بھائی (brother), بہن (sister). Family first — people ask about yours within minutes.

Extended family?

چچا (uncle), چچی (aunt), دادی (grandmother), دادا (grandfather). Extended family, extended vocabulary.

How do I describe my family?

میرا خاندان بڑا ہے (my family is big) — میری ماں استانی ہے (my mother is a teacher). Describe yours; hearts open.
