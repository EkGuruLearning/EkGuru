Emergency Urdu

مدد, ڈاکٹر, ہسپتال — emergency Urdu you hope to never need.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

The core three save lives: مدد (help), خطرہ (danger), فوری مدد (urgent help). مجھے مدد کیجیے (please help me) — say it to anyone, anywhere. Crowds respond to distress calls faster than any app; your job is only to ask.

Medical words route the rescue: ڈاکٹر (doctor), ہسپتال (hospital), دوا (medicine), میڈیکل اسٹور (pharmacy). ہسپتال کہاں ہے؟ (where is the hospital?) plus a taxi wave — in cities, help arrives in minutes.

Symptoms speak simply: یہاں درد ہے (it hurts here, point!), مجھے اچھا نہیں لگ رہا (I feel unwell), سر درد (headache), بخار (fever). Pain words plus pointing hands diagnose across every language barrier on earth.

Danger and loss close the kit: آگ (fire), چور (thief), پولیس (police), کھو گیا (it's lost), سفارتخانہ (embassy). Memorise these cold — emergencies never wait for phrasebooks, and ten minutes of drilling buys total peace of mind.

A note on مدد. مدد (madad — help) is Urdu's shortest lifeline — shout it and crowds come running. Bystander culture is famously kind: strangers will escort you to hospitals, translate for doctors, and refuse thanks. Ask loudly; receive gratefully.

Questions learners ask

What is the one emergency sentence?

مجھے مدد کیجیے (please help me) — loud, clear, repeated. Add ہسپتال کہاں ہے؟ (where is the hospital?) and crowds mobilise instantly.

How do I explain pain?

Point and say یہاں درد ہے (it hurts here). Then مجھے اچھا نہیں لگ رہا (I feel unwell) and ڈاکٹر کو بلائیے (call a doctor). Pain plus pointing crosses every language gap.

How do I report something lost?

کھو گیا (it is lost) — پیسے کھو گئے (money lost), بیگ کھو گیا (bag lost). Then پولیس اسٹیشن کہاں ہے؟ (where is the police station?).
