Core Urdu vocabulary

پانی, گھر, کام, پیار — the words Urdu daily life runs on.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Survival nouns anchor reality: پانی (water), کھانا (food), گھر (house), راستہ (road). Point at each, say it aloud, use it today — پانی چاہیے (I want water). Concrete first, abstract later.

People words warm speech: آدمی (person), دوست (friend), ماں (mother), بچہ (child). میرا دوست (my friend) introduces proudly; ماں calls home. People before things, always.

Describing words add colour: بڑا (big), چھوٹا (small), اچھا (good), نیا (new). Adjectives dance with gender — بڑا بھائی (big brother), بڑی بہن (big sister). Pair-learn each one.

Time and feeling complete the core: آج (today), پیار (love), خوشی (joy), کام (work). آج بہت کام ہے (there's lots of work today) — then پیار اور خوشی balance it. Words for living, not just talking.

A note on خوب. خوب (khoob — nice/great) is the universal praise — خوب ہے (it's nice), بہت خوب (very nice). Food, films, friends' work — praise everything. Generous خوب earns generous friendship.

Questions learners ask

Which words give fastest fluency?

Body, home, food, people: پانی (water), گھر (house), ماں (mother), دوست (friend). Concrete nouns first — point, name, own.

How do adjectives work?

Adjectives agree with gender: اچھا (good-m), اچھی (f). Learn pairs together — agreement becomes music.

Essential time words?

آج (today), کل (tomorrow), گذشتہ کل (yesterday), اب (now). Time words frame every plan — learn them this week.
