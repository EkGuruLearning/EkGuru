Urdu

Urdu among world languages

Indo-Aryan roots, Persian soul, Hindi twin — Urdu's family place.

🔊 Tap any speaker button to hear Urdu spoken. Too fast or slow? Use the speed button at the bottom-right of the page.

Urdu anchors the north: ہندی (Hindi), پنجابی (Punjabi), بنگالی (Bengali) — cousins, not strangers. Shared roots, shared daily words, proud separate literatures. Learn Urdu and Hindi speech starts echoing.

Persian crowns the register: فارسی lent Urdu thousands of formal words — ادب vocabulary crowns poetry and prose. Daily speech mixes happily. Both registers are real Urdu; learners need both.

Arabic threads the sacred: عربی lent religious and scholarly words — علم، کتاب، دنیا. Three fountains feed one river; drink from all.

Unity crowns diversity: اتحاد (unity) — hundreds of mother tongues, one conversation. Urdu speakers learn English for globe, Urdu for soul. Every language you meet makes your Urdu richer.

Questions learners ask

Is Urdu related to Hindi?

Twins, nearly — Urdu and Hindi share grammar and daily words almost fully. Script differs, formal vocabulary differs, hearts are one.

Which language is closest to Urdu?

ہندی for daily near-identity, پنجابی next door, then فارسی above all. The urdu-vs-hindi page maps the closest pair.

Why so much Persian in Urdu?

Centuries of poetry, courts and culture — فارسی words fill formal Urdu while grammar stays Indo-Aryan. Persian lends soul; Urdu keeps its heart.
