Questions about this course

Must I learn the Urdu script before speaking?

No. Start with spoken Urdu in Roman letters, and let the script come second. Learners who speak first stay motivated far longer than learners who drill letters for a month before saying a word.

Which words should I learn in week one?

السلام علیکم for respect, ہاں and نہیں, شکریہ, معاف کیجیے, ٹھیک ہے, and میں — I. With those seven you can open any interaction politely while your vocabulary grows underneath.

What is the hardest part of Urdu?

The retroflexes and Persian-Arabic sounds — ع غ ق خ — plus reading right-to-left. Ten minutes of daily listening retunes your ears within weeks; the pronunciation page walks you through each one.
